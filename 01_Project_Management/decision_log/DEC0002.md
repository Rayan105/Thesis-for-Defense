### Option A: Approve `RQ0/RQ1–RQ4`, Null Policy & `C1–C4` Now on Bounded Google Scholar Corpus (Supplementary Multi-DB Required at `STEP-023` & `STEP-048`)
- **Pros & Scientific Tradeoffs:** Allows immediate progression into hospital IRB/site feasibility (`STEP-010–013`, preserving academic Summer 2026 schedule) because `83` candidate PDFs and `42` full-text extractions already surface `14` strong contradicting $\ge 3$-modality comparators (`P0002, P0013, P0016, P0020–P0023, P0025, P0030–P0032, P0036–P0038`) and recent `2025–2026` Q1 systematic reviews (`P0010, P0017, P0019, P0026, P0027, P0034, P0035, P0040–P0042`). Literature-gap risks are contained by transparently documenting Google Scholar coverage and committing to full multi-database update runs at `STEP-023` and `STEP-048`.
- **Risks & Limitations:** Single-database initial retrieval leaves `protocol.md`'s formal multi-database PRISMA criterion partially deferred; Q1 journal reviewers at submission will strictly expect PubMed/IEEE/Scopus numbers prior to `STEP-048` (`G16 / paper`).

### Option B: Execute Supplementary PubMed, IEEE Xplore & Scopus Searches Prior to Signing `G03 PASS`
- **Pros & Scientific Tradeoffs:** Satisfies **OS §14.2 `G03`** criterion ("multi-database search logs") at 100% literal strictness before hospital data architecture freezes, catching any indexed biomedical/engineering 4-modality papers missed by Google Scholar ranking.
- **Risks & Limitations:** Adds 2–5 days of structured API/portal exports, deduplication against `P0001–P0083`, screening, and incremental `T05` extraction prior to closing `STEP-009 / G03`.

### Option C: Revise Framing of `RQ1–RQ4` or Broaden/Narrow Modalities Before `G03`
- **Pros & Scientific Tradeoffs:** Permits supervisor adjustments (e.g., clinical risk scores prioritization in `RQ1`, lifestyle sub-variables, or specific Bangladesh target endpoint hints prior to `STEP-014`).
- **Risks & Limitations:** Any alteration to `RQ1–RQ4` triggers a `v1.1.0` update of `research_questions.md` and `G03` reassessment.
