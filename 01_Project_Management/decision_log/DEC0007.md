## 1. Decision Question (`UD12`)
Approve the **two-stage decoupled preprocessing contract** governing Stage `S09` (`STEP-019`–`STEP-022`):
1. Strict separation of **Stage A (record-local deterministic: units, `50 Hz` biquad notch $Q=30$, $\le 0.5\text{ Hz}$ Butterworth zero-phase high-pass, PNG `90°` CCW rotation & red-grid suppression)** BEFORE splitting vs **Stage B (Train-only fitted MICE/median imputation + `RobustScaler` + binary MNAR `_isnan` masks + `OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore')`)** AFTER `STEP-020` (`G07`).
2. Zero pre-split SMOTE / oversampling (`OS §4.5 #4`); use Train-derived class weighting (`pos_weight`) for imbalanced strata cleanly preserving probability calibration.
3. Explicit confirmation at `G08` (`STEP-022`) that `0.5 Hz` high-pass & `50 Hz` notch filtering preserve diagnostic ST-T, J-point, Q-wave, and P-wave waveform morphology.
