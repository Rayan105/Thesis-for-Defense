## 1. Decision Question
The team has elected to work exclusively with **secondary public/credentialed datasets (sourced from Kaggle, Mendeley Data, and/or PhysioNet)** rather than pursuing prospective or retrospective primary hospital data collection and hospital IRB submission in Bangladesh (`RF09`, p. 4).

This decision record establishes:
1. Formal Supervisor ratification preserving the verbatim approved title (`RF01`) while amending `scope.md` and `research_questions.md` (`RQ4`) from primary Bangladesh hospital data to **secondary benchmark datasets** (framing prospective Bangladesh clinical validation explicitly as Future Work).
2. Selection of a **scientifically valid, patient-aligned secondary dataset architecture** avoiding Kaggle pitfalls (synthetic data, scalar-only CSVs lacking ECG waveforms, unlinked patient row-stitching, and unauthorized PhysioNet re-uploads).
3. Replacement of `STEP-011` hospital IRB collection with **Secondary Dataset License, CITI/DUA Compliance, and Source Provenance Verification**.

---

## 2. Proposal Facts & Methodological Guardrails
- **`RF01` (p. 1):** Approved title remains verbatim (*"...Using Clinical, ECG, Laboratory, and Lifestyle Data"*). No title change is proposed or required provided the chosen datasets cover the four modalities cleanly or test missing-modality fusion honestly.
- **`RF09` (p. 4):** Original proposal anticipated primary hospital/diagnostic-center collection post-ethics approval. Changing to secondary open/credentialed data requires formal **Supervisor sign-off** (`DEC0004`) to prevent scope-drift penalties during DIU CSE499 defense or Q1 review.
- **Zero-Stitching Scientific Rule (`EX-UNIMODAL` / `P0022` Lesson):** Combining ECG rows from Patient Set $A$ (`e.g., PTB-XL`) with clinical/lab/lifestyle rows from unrelated Patient Set $B$ (`e.g., Kaggle Heart Disease CSV`) by row index or random matching is **strictly prohibited**. All modalities fused per patient sample MUST share verified same-patient linkage, OR distinct cohorts with different available subsets must be modeled natively via **missing-modality tokens (`[MISSING_MODALITY]`)**.

---

## 3. Secondary Dataset Architecture Options Considered

### Option A (`Recommended for Q1 Publishability`): Official PhysioNet Credentialed + Open Multi-Cohort (`MIMIC-IV-ECG / MDS-ED + PTB-XL + CAIR-CVD-2025`)
- **Structure:**
  1. Primary same-patient 3-modality anchor (`Clinical + Raw 12-lead 500 Hz Digital ECG + Laboratory Assays [+ Clinical Notes/Smoking]`): **`MIMIC-IV + MIMIC-IV-ECG`** (`SITE0002`, `127,994` pts) and/or **`MIMIC-IV-Ext-MDS-ED`** (`SITE0010`, built by Strodthoff et al. on PhysioNet).
  2. Open ECG Pretraining / Unimodal-Bimodal Benchmark: **`PTB-XL`** (`SITE0001`, Open CC-BY 4.0; `21,837` 12-lead ECGs; available on PhysioNet & Kaggle).
  3. Optional Missing-Modality South Asian Lifestyle+Clinical+Lab Branch (`C+L+S`, `0` ECG): **`CAIR-CVD-2025`** (`SITE0007`, Mendeley CC BY 4.0, `N=1,529` Bangladesh records) tested cleanly inside a **Missing-Modality Transformer** (`RQ2/RQ4`) without fake patient stitching!
- **Pros:** Enormous patient-aligned $N$ satisfying Riley EPV with `100%` digital raw waveforms, high Q1 reviewer credibility, and clean legal compliance via free PhysioNet CITI training + DUA (`STEP-011`).
- **Cons:** Requires `3–7` days for team members to complete PhysioNet CITI ethics certification (`Credentialed Access`); MIMIC lacks structured diet/activity questionnaires (mitigated transparently via multi-cohort missing-modality modeling with `CAIR-CVD-2025` or structured lifestyle proxies).

### Option B (`Open Kaggle / Mendeley CC-BY Track — Zero CITI Turnaround`): `CARDIO-ML-ED` + `PTB-XL` + `CAIR-CVD-2025` (Missing-Modality Multi-Cohort)
- **Structure:**
  1. Primary same-patient anchor: **`CARDIO-ML-ED`** (`SITE0009`, hosted on Mendeley CC BY 4.0 & mirrored on Kaggle `zara2099/multimodal-cardiovascular-risk-dataset`, `~582` ED patients with **same-patient Clinical/Vitals + 82 Lab Assays + Medical History + Digitized 12-lead ECG Images**; falls under pre-registered `UD26` Tier B `500–1,499` regime!).
  2. Pretraining / Waveform Anchor: **`PTB-XL`** (`SITE0001`, Kaggle/PhysioNet CC BY 4.0).
  3. Bangladeshi Missing-ECG Lifestyle Anchor: **`CAIR-CVD-2025`** (`SITE0007`, Mendeley CC BY 4.0, `N=1,529`, `C+L+S`).
- **Pros:** 100% immediately downloadable from Kaggle/Mendeley under CC BY 4.0; includes digitized 12-lead ECGs + 82 same-patient clinical/lab features; clean `582`-subject fit for our `UD26` Tier B compact transfer-learning + 5-fold patient CV protocol (`STEP-010`).
- **Cons / Mandatory Pre-Approval Check (`STEP-011`):** Must inspect `CARDIO-ML-ED` CSVs locally in `STEP-012` to verify: (a) genuine non-synthetic clinical distributions, (b) exact CVD endpoint/horizon (`UD02`) and pediatric exclusion (`aged 6–98` noted in summary -> must filter adults $\ge 18$ yr per `protocol.md`!), (c) absence of incorporation leakage among the 82 labs, and (d) whether `10_Comprehensive_Medical_History.csv` captures smoking/lifestyle.

### Option C: Team-Selected Alternative Kaggle Dataset(s)
- Researchers supply specific Kaggle URLs/slugs; accepted ONLY after passing the `STEP-011/012` **Kaggle Provenance, Non-Synthetic & Patient-Linkage Audit** (`zero pirated PhysioNet mirrors`, `zero synthetic toy tables`, `zero cross-dataset row-stitching`).

---

## 4. Recommendation & Rejected Alternatives
- **Recommendation:** Adopt **Option A** if researchers can complete PhysioNet CITI training during `STEP-011` (strongest Q1 journal defense), OR **Option B (`CARDIO-ML-ED + PTB-XL + CAIR-CVD-2025`)** as an immediate CC BY 4.0 open Kaggle/Mendeley pathway — **subject to passing `STEP-011` provenance/adult-filtering audit and explicit Supervisor sign-off**.
- **Rejected Alternatives:**
  1. Using a tabular-only Kaggle dataset (`UCI Heart`, `Kaggle 70K CVD CSV`, `CAIR-CVD-2025` alone) with zero ECG waveforms/images;
  2. Row-concatenating unrelated Kaggle tabular CSVs with unrelated Kaggle ECG image folders across different individuals;
  3. Downloading credentialed datasets (e.g., MIMIC) from unauthorized third-party Kaggle mirrors without PhysioNet CITI/DUA compliance.
