## 1. Decision Question (`UD14`)
Lock the **four-tier model comparator matrix**, clinical risk-score boundary, ECG dual-format encoders (`WFDB 1D` + `CARDIO-ML-ED PNG 2D`), and parameter capacity of our **Proposed Multimodal Transformer Fusion Network (`CVD-MM-Transformer`)** matching `REL0001`'s empirical EPV across `DS0001–DS0004`.

---

## 2. Options Considered
- **Option A (`Selected — Compact Modality-Masked Cross-Modal Transformer + Complete 4-Tier Comparator Suite`):**
  - Covers every proposal model family (`LR`, `RF`, `SVM`, `XGBoost`, `1D-CNN/ResNet`, `CNN-BiLSTM`, `Transformer`) across `Tier 1 (Unimodal)` + `Tier 2 (Contextual WHO/Framingham Covariate Score)` + `Tier 3 (Early/Late/Gated Fusion)` + `Tier 4 (CVD-MM-Transformer)`.
  - Uses **compact EPV-safe dimensions (`d_model=64`, `2` Transformer layers, `4` heads, `< 250K` core parameters)** paired with **per-cohort tabular schema projections** (`11/15/31/25`-dim -> `[C, L, S]` tokens) and dual **WFDB `ResNet-1D` + `CARDIO-ML-ED 2D-CNN`** ECG tokenizers, masked natively by **`ModalityMask_CELS` key-padding mask (`~mask`)**.
  - Keeps `DS0002` (`390` Train encounters / `233` positive events) regularized via compact bottleneck + dropout (`0.25–0.35`) + label/class `pos_weight`, avoiding catastrophic overfitting of multi-million-parameter foundation backbones (`UD26`).
- **Option B (`Heavy Multi-Million-Parameter ViT/LLM Transformer Trained End-to-End from Scratch`):** Rejected (`Severe EPV violation on DS0002 CARDIO-ML-ED N_train=390 and high memorization risk`).
