## 1. Decision Summary (`UD15–UD18`, `UD24`)
Locks every controlled comparison rule **before any training or test metric is seen**:
1. **`UD15` Experiment Matrix & Fairness (`experiment_plan.md`):** Six blocks (`A–F`) covering Tiers `1–4`, progressive/LOMO lifestyle ablations, `S = 5` deterministic seeds (`20261127–20261201`), matched validation tuning budgets, and `Train pos_weight` without pre-split SMOTE.
2. **`UD16` & `UD24` Metrics, Calibration & Thresholds (`09_Evaluation_Stats/protocol.md`):** Evaluates all `6` mandatory metrics (`Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC, Confusion Matrix`) + `PR-AUC, Specificity, NPV, Balanced Accuracy, MCC, Brier Score, ECE [15 quantile bins], Calibration Slope & Intercept`. Post-hoc calibration (`Temperature / Platt / Isotonic`) and operating thresholds ($\tau_J$ Youden's $J$ & $\tau_{0.85}$ high-sensitivity rule-out) fit **strictly on `Validation`** before `G11` unsealing.
3. **`UD17` Decision Curve Analysis (`DCA`):** Preregistered threshold sweep $p_t \in [0.05, 0.50]$ (`0.01` step) comparing Champion vs Best Unimodal vs Simple Fusion vs Treat-All/None (`confirmed clinically prior to G14`).
4. **`UD18` Statistical Inference & Multiplicity:** **`B = 2,000` patient-level stratified bootstrap `95%` CIs**, paired two-sided **DeLong** tests, paired bootstrap difference tests ($\Delta$ PR-AUC / Brier / DCA Net Benefit), **McNemar** exact test, and **Holm-Bonferroni** adjusted $p$-values across comparison blocks.
