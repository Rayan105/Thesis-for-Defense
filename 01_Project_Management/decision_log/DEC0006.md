## 1. Decision Question (`UD10`)
Across our frozen releases **`ADJ0001–ADJ0004`** (`91,781` verified adult encounters out of `93,947` screened):
1. Should we **retain partial-modality cohorts (`ADJ0001`: `C_partial+E_wfdb`; `ADJ0002`: `C+E_png+L` [`329`] + `C+L` [`226`]; `ADJ0003`: `C+L+S`; `ADJ0004`: `C+L_ord+S`)** alongside explicit binary feature-level MNAR missingness masks and modality presence masks (`[has_clinical, has_ecg, has_lab, has_lifestyle]`), OR drop all partial rows cleanly keeping only sub-subsets?
2. Should `CARDIO-ML-ED` (`ADJ0002`, `555` eligible across `543` unique `Col1` patients, incl. repeated records like `1560/1561` on `PatientFileNo=167233`) group strictly by connected `PatientGroupID` across every split/CV fold (`0` same-patient leakage)?

---

## 2. Empirical Release Matrix (`ADJ0001–ADJ0004`)
- **`ADJ0001` (`DS0001 PTB-XL`):** `21,198` encounters (`18,434` unique patients); `0` cross-fold patients; `68.0%` missing `height_cm`, `56.7%` missing `weight_kg`, `0` missing ECG waveforms or age/sex.
- **`ADJ0002` (`DS0002 CARDIO-ML-ED`):** `555` eligible (`543` unique patients): `329` (`323` unique patients) have paired **`C + E_png + L`**; `226` (`220` pts) have **`C + L [MISSING_ECG]`**; `32.4%` missing Troponin (`Tier B` only), `20.4%` missing Creatinine, `17.1%` missing WBC.
- **`ADJ0003` (`DS0003 CAIR-CVD-2025`):** `1,453` unique Bangladeshi outpatients (`C + L + S [MISSING_ECG]`), `4.1–5.6%` missing labs/anthropometrics.
- **`ADJ0004` (`DS0004 Kaggle sulianova`):** `68,575` clean deduplicated adults (`C + L_ord + S [MISSING_ECG]`), `0%` missingness.

---

## 3. Options Considered
- **Option A (`Recommended — Missing-Modality Partial Retention + Connected Patient Grouping`):**
  - Retain all `4` clean cohorts in their native modality profiles with explicit modality masks + MNAR missingness indicators (`RQ2` / `RQ4` missing-modality modeling);
  - Keep `ADJ0002`'s `329` `C+E_png+L` rows as the primary 3-modality anchor while evaluating `226` `[MISSING_ECG]` rows cleanly as an internal missing-ECG degradation test (`Block F`);
  - Enforce 100% `PatientGroupID` containment inside splits/CV folds (`0` leakage across repeated encounters like `Col1=167233`);
  - Impute numeric missing values (`height/weight/Cr/WBC/CAIR labs`) strictly on Train partitions inside Stage `S09` (`STEP-019`–`021`).
- **Option B (`Strict Multimodal Only — Drop ADJ0002 [MISSING_ECG] & Missing Lab Cases`):** Drops `226` `CARDIO-ML-ED` cases and complete-case-drops any missing Cr/WBC, shrinking same-patient multimodal anchor power below `UD26` Tier B stability (`<250` rows).
