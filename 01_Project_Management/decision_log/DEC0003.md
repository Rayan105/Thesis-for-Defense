## 1. Decision Question
To unlock institutional IRB/ethics filing (`STEP-011`) and prevent spectrum bias or small-$N$ overfitting (`RISK0001, RISK0004, RISK0005`), three coupled feasibility parameters must be decided by Researchers and Supervisor:
1. **`UD04` (Primary Bangladesh Sites & Target Recruitment):** Which participating Bangladesh hospital(s) / diagnostic center(s) (`SITE0009+`) will be approached for ethics/MOU filing, using what recruitment mode (consecutive outpatient/cardiac screening vs multi-center), and confirming digital 12-lead ECG vs scanned-strip availability (`target N ≥ 1,250–1,500`, `E ≥ 300` where feasible)?
2. **`UD06` (External Public/Credentialed Cohort Adoption):** Should the framework utilize only the primary Bangladesh hospital cohort (`Option A`), add open **`PTB-XL` (`SITE0001`)** for ECG encoder pretraining (`Option B`), or additionally adopt credentialed **`MIMIC-IV + MIMIC-IV-ECG` (`SITE0002`, subject to CITI + DUA)** for partial-overlap (`Clinical+ECG+Lab`) cross-cohort benchmarking (`Option C`)?
3. **`UD26` (Small-$N$ & Compute Contingency Contract):** Confirm binding pre-specified cutoffs (`Tier A: N≥1,500`, `Tier B: 500≤N<1,500` using PTB-XL pretraining + compact cross-attention + 5-fold patient CV, `Tier C: N<500` triggering multi-center/MIMIC expansion or frozen-embedding fusion).

## 2. Proposal Facts & Feasibility Locators
- **`RF09` (p. 4):** Primary dataset must be collected from hospitals or diagnostic centers **after obtaining required ethical approval**.
- **`RF14` (p. 5):** Proposal does not fix sample size, prevalence, split proportions, or ECG acquisition format.
- **Riley et al. (2020) Math ([feasibility_epv_study.md](../../03_Datasets_Cohort/reports/feasibility_epv_study.md)):** At $\phi = 0.25$ and leakage-free anticipated $\text{AUC} = 0.80$, $P=25$ parameters require **$N_{\text{dev}} = 952$ ($E_{\text{dev}} = 238$ events)** for global shrinkage $S \ge 0.90$, plus $N_{\text{test}} = 300\text{–}400$ ($\ge 100$ test events) for Hanley-McNeil AUC 95% CI width $\le \pm 0.056\text{–}0.071$.
- **Screened Cohorts ([sites.csv](../../03_Datasets_Cohort/sites.csv)):** `PTB-XL` (`21,837` digital 12L ECGs, CC-BY 4.0) offers immediate lawful ECG pretraining; `MIMIC-IV + MIMIC-IV-ECG` (`127,994` pts) covers 3/4 modalities (lacks Lifestyle) under CITI/DUA; `CAIR-CVD-2025` has zero raw ECG waveforms and cannot replace primary ECG acquisition.

## 3. Options Considered & Q1/Clinical Tradeoffs

### 3.1 `UD06` External Benchmark Options
- **Option A — Primary Bangladeshi Hospital Cohort Only (No External Public Data):**
  - *Pros:* Purest focus on local Bangladeshi clinical workflow and full 4 modalities (`C+E+L+S`).
  - *Cons:* Zero external validation cohort and high overfitting risk (`RISK0005`) if hospital $N < 1{,}000$; `P0017/P0027/P0041` show single-center internal-only models face strong Q1 rejection (`17%` external rate cited as weakness).
- **Option B — Primary Bangladesh Cohort + Open `PTB-XL` Pretraining (`SITE0001`) + Optional Second BD Center (`LOCO`):**
  - *Pros:* Zero extra IRB/DUA delay (`CC-BY 4.0`); unlocks deep 12-lead ECG representation learning for `UD26` Tier B cohorts while keeping multi-center Bangladesh evaluation (`G07/G12`) clean.
  - *Cons:* Requires careful `PTB-XL`-to-Bangladesh calibration/domain check (`P0018 §4.4` notes demographic shift).
- **Option C — Primary Bangladesh Cohort + `PTB-XL` Pretraining + Credentialed `MIMIC-IV + MIMIC-IV-ECG` (`SITE0002`) Benchmarking:**
  - *Pros:* Provides massive external multi-modality benchmark (`Clinical + 12-lead ECG + Lab`) proving generalization of 3-modality sub-branch (`RQ4`) AND insulates FYDP timeline against primary hospital delays (`RISK0001`).
  - *Cons:* Requires researchers to complete PhysioNet CITI Human Subjects Training + DUA (`STEP-011`) and careful harmonization of ICD endpoints, `60 Hz` US vs `50 Hz` BD powerline filtering, and absence of Lifestyle in MIMIC.

### 3.2 `UD04` Recruitment Modes (Primary BD Sites)
- **Preferred Mode:** Consecutive or matched-pathway outpatient cardiology / preventive health-checkup enrollment (`CVD+` and clinically verified `CVD−` from the same clinic workflow), **digital 12-lead ECG prioritized**.
- **Rejected Mode:** Extreme case-control pooling (e.g., acute inpatient CCU MI cases paired with healthy student/staff volunteers), which caused `High PROBAST` bias in `P0001, P0006, P0021, P0032`.

---

## 4. Recommendation & Rejected Alternatives
- **Recommendation:** Adopt **Option B or Option C** for `UD06` (definitely leverage open `PTB-XL` pretraining for small-$N$ ECG robustness, and initiate PhysioNet CITI training early in `STEP-011` so `MIMIC-IV-ECG` backup/external validation stays open), prioritize consecutive site-matched digital-ECG recruitment in Bangladesh (`UD04`), and lock the three-tier **`UD26` (`N ≥ 1,500` / `500–1,499` / `<500`) contingency matrix** prospectively.
- **Rejected Alternatives:** Treating open tabular datasets (`CAIR-CVD-2025`, `UCI`) without raw ECG as sufficient for multimodal ECG+Clinical+Lab+Lifestyle claims (`CLAIM0003`); selecting hospitals without checking ECG digital export vs thermal paper beforehand; training an unregularized Transformer from scratch on $N < 500$.
