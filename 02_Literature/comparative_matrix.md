## 1. Executive Synthesis of Full-Text Audit Findings (`n = 42`)

Full-text reading of all 42 included studies (`29` Original Research + `13` Reviews/Meta-Analyses) uncovered critical divergences between preliminary intake tags in `papers.csv` and verified scientific evidence:

1. **PROBAST Risk of Bias (`29` Original Studies):**
   - **Low Risk:** **`1 / 29` (`3.4%`)** — `P0038` (`Rui2026_P0038`, CMR+EHR+Text STEMI MACE, 4-center LOCO, blinded 3-cardiologist review & DCA).
   - **Unclear Risk:** **`3 / 29` (`10.3%`)** — `P0005` (`Hughes2023_P0005`), `P0023` (`Zhou2025_P0023`), `P0025` (`Yao2025_P0025`) — strict patient-level splitting without leakage, downgraded for missing calibration plots, DCA, or main-text event counts.
   - **High Risk:** **`25 / 29` (`86.2%`)** — caused by pre-split SMOTE/MICE/feature selection (`P0002, P0013, P0022, P0031`), recording/segment/image-level splitting (`P0001, P0004, P0007, P0012, P0015, P0028`), test-set tuning/selection (`P0003, P0004`), target/post-diagnosis leakage (`P0002, P0011, P0014, P0036, P0037`), case-control spectrum bias (`P0001, P0006, P0021, P0032`), or severe metric/provenance inconsistencies (`P0016, P0029, P0030`).
2. **XAI Faithfulness Audit (`XAIEvaluated` Correction):**
   - Preliminary intake tagged `24` papers as `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`. **Full-text reading proved `20 / 24` false or overstated.**
   - Only **`4 / 42`** papers evaluated genuine XAI faithfulness or blinded clinical plausibility (`P0028` EAT sanity/NMI/adversarial perturbation; `P0029` CXR Grad-CAM AOPC where insertion succeeded [`1.807>1.759`] but deletion failed vs random [`1.803 vs 1.846`]; `P0032` linear-perceptron top-3 `MEMC`; `P0038` blinded 3-cardiologist review `ICC=0.91` + rationale shuffling).
   - **Zero (`0 / 42`)** studies combined all four accessible screening modalities (**Clinical + 12-lead ECG + Laboratory + Lifestyle**) in a leakage-free Transformer fusion framework audited for **multimodal SHAP deletion/insertion AOPC vs random controls, multi-seed stability, calibration/DCA, and cardiologist plausibility**.
3. **South Asian & Bangladeshi Cohort Evidence:**
   - **0 / 29** original studies tested a clean, leakage-free Bangladeshi or South Asian multimodal cohort combining 12-lead ECG, Clinical, Lab, and Lifestyle data. (`P0018 §4.4 p. 34` confirms PTB-XL/MIMIC-IV lack South Asian diversity; `P0041` is Bangladeshi-led yet finds `0/85` Bangladeshi cohorts; `P0022` touches Bangladeshi `CAIR-CVD-2025` without ECG waveforms under High pre-split leakage; `P0030` claims Singapore `SEED` Indian subgroup alongside severe provenance failures.)
