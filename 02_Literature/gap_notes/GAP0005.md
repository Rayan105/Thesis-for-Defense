# GAP0005 — Absence of an End-to-End Leakage-Free Explainable Multimodal Screening Framework Validated in South Asia

## 1. Proposal Gap Baseline
- Proposal Gap Identifier: **PG5**
- Proposal Statement (Verbatim): "Few studies build an explainable multimodal framework"

## 2. Systematic Evidence Audit
- **Search Coverage:** Google Scholar (`5` queries: `SEARCH-SCHOLAR-Q1`..`Q5`; `2018–2026` protocol window, `2022–2026` retrieved; `2026-11-25`), `83` screened $\rightarrow$ `42` included.
- **Supporting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **CLAIM0001 / P0018 / P0026 / P0040 / P0041:** Severe under-representation of South Asian/Bangladeshi cohorts (`P0018 §4.4 p. 34` on PTB-XL/MIMIC-IV; `P0026 p. 12`: `29%` race reporting, `89%` White, `0` South Asian; `P0041 Fig. 1e p. 5`: Bangladeshi-led SR yet `0/85` Bangladeshi primary studies and `0/85` UQ; `P0040 p. 3`: `91.2%` projected Asian CVD death rise `2025–2050` with `0` BD/SA cohorts).
  - **CLAIM0003 / P0022:** Only primary paper citing Bangladeshi `CAIR-CVD-2025` omits raw ECG waveforms and exhibits High PROBAST pre-split SMOTE/KNN/Z-score leakage (`pp. 5–11`).
  - **CLAIM0004 / CLAIM0005 / P0042:** `25/29` (`86.2%`) original studies in our corpus rate High PROBAST Risk (echoing `P0042` Table 3: only `12/108` [`11.1%`] AI-ECG studies Low Risk, and `P0010`: `34.5%` EHR-ML High Risk).
  - **CLAIM0011:** Formal DCA reported in only `2/29` (`P0007` suppl., `P0038`) + `1` malformed (`P0016`); calibration omitted in `18/29` studies.
  - **Missing-modality robustness (`P0036, P0038, P0039`):** Tested in only `2/29` original studies (`P0036` CAD + CCTA, `P0038` STEMI + CMR).
- **Contradicting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **P0038** (`MACE-MAIS`, **Low PROBAST**): End-to-end missing-modality-aware interpretable framework (`patient split + 4-center LOCO + IBS calibration + 8-panel DCA + blinded 3-cardiologist ICC=0.91 + shuffled-rationale controls`), **but** uses tertiary `3T CMR + EHR + Text` on Chinese STEMI patients (`0` 12-lead ECG waveforms, `0` structured lifestyle modality, `0` SHAP AOPC, `0` South Asian validation, `pp. 3–16`).
  - **P0036** (`CGH`): End-to-end missing-modality Transformer framework with external cohort and SHAP/Grad-CAM, **but** requires `CCTA` imaging, shows internal AUC/figure contradictions and `±30-day` post-CCTA leakage (`High PROBAST`), omits calibration/DCA, omits ECG XAI, and has `0` South Asian data (`pp. 2–12`).
  - **P0016** (`ChemoCardioNet`): Claims multimodal explainable Transformer with Brier/DCA, **but** exhibits severe cohort provenance and internal metric contradictions (`AUC 0.92 vs 0.997 vs 1.00`, malformed DCA `-80 to 0`), requires Echo, and has zero AOPC (`High PROBAST`, `pp. 3–11`).

## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** End-to-end explainable multimodal frameworks do exist for tertiary imaging (`P0038` CMR, `P0036` CCTA). However, **within our 42-study verified corpus, zero studies (`0/42`) jointly satisfy all five Q1-translational requirements simultaneously:**
  1. Fuse the **4 accessible primary screening modalities** (`Clinical + 12-lead ECG + Lab + Lifestyle`) without tertiary imaging (`CMR/CCTA/Echo`) or genomic dependencies;
  2. Enforce **leakage-free patient-level splitting** (`Low PROBAST-AI` design), paired with **post-hoc calibration** (`Brier/ECE/slope`) and **Decision Curve Analysis**;
  3. Validate **multimodal SHAP explanations quantitatively** (`deletion/insertion AOPC vs random`, multi-seed rank stability, shortcut audit, and blinded cardiologist review);
  4. Evaluate **inference-time missing-modality degradation** stress tests alongside progressive/LOMO ablations;
  5. Test on a **Bangladeshi / South Asian clinically adjudicated cohort**.
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ1–RQ4** (Overarching Primary Question & End-to-End Framework)
- **Feasibility Assessment:** Feasible subject to strict gate discipline: IRB/hospital agreements (`STEP-010–011`, `G04`), ≥2 cardiologists for endpoint adjudication and XAI plausibility (`G05–G06`, `G14`), sample-size/EPV sizing (`UD04/UD26`), and multi-seed compute allocation.
