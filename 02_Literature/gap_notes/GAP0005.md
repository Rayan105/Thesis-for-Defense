# GAP0005 — Absence of an End-to-End Explainable Multimodal Framework Combining Accessible Screening Modalities on a South Asian Cohort

## 1. Proposal Gap Baseline
- Proposal Gap Identifier: **PG5**
- Proposal Statement (Verbatim): "Few studies build an explainable multimodal framework"

## 2. Systematic Evidence Audit
- **Search Coverage:** Google Scholar (6 queries, 2020–2026), 83 screened → 42 included.
- **Supporting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **CLAIM0001:** "Benchmark ECG/multimodal CVD datasets severely under-represent or exclude South Asian and Bangladeshi cohorts" — supported by P0018 (§4.4 p. 34), P0025 (pp. 2–3), P0026 (p. 12), P0040 (pp. 13–16), P0041 (Fig. 1e p. 5).
  - **CLAIM0002:** P0041 is Bangladeshi-led yet finds 0/85 Bangladeshi cohorts and 0% UQ (pp. 1, 5, 28, 33).
  - **CLAIM0003:** P0022 references CAIR-CVD-2025 (Bangladesh) but without raw ECG waveforms and with severe pre-split leakage (**High PROBAST**) (pp. 5–11).
  - **CLAIM0005:** 25/29 (86.2%) original studies in our portfolio have High PROBAST Risk.
  - **CLAIM0009:** 0/42 combined 4-modality C+E+L+S with multimodal SHAP AOPC + calibration + cardiologist review.
  - **P0040** (Umbrella Review): 91.2% projected rise in Asian CVD deaths 2025–2050; >80% CVD deaths in LMICs; 0 Bangladeshi/SA cohorts in reviewed studies (pp. 3, 13–16).
  - **P0018** (ECG Transformers Survey): §4.4 (p. 34) explicitly states South Asian scarcity in PTB-XL and MIMIC-IV.
  - **P0026** (AI-ECG SR): 29% report race; 89% of reported races are White; 0 South Asian cohorts (p. 12).
  - **P0042** (PROBAST SR): Only 11.1% (12/108) AI-ECG studies achieved Low PROBAST Risk (Table 3 p. 9).
- **Contradicting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **P0038** (MAIS STEMI): The **only Low PROBAST** study — demonstrates a complete explainable multimodal framework with 4-center LOCO, blinded cardiologist review (ICC=0.91), DCA, and missing-modality handling. However, modalities are CMR+EHR+Text (not ECG+Lab+Lifestyle), population is Chinese STEMI (not South Asian screening), and XAI uses rationale shuffling (not SHAP AOPC) (pp. 3–12).
  - **P0036** (CGH): 5-modality framework with cross-modal fusion and missing-modality handling, including external validation and SHAP — but requires CCTA, used only 460 patients, and has ±30-day alignment window leakage; **High PROBAST** (pp. 5–8).
  - **P0030** (SMM/CHRC-Net): Claims broad multimodal framework with Brier and ECE metrics — but severe data/provenance anomalies render results unverifiable; **High PROBAST** (pp. 10–16).

## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** No prior study has delivered an **end-to-end explainable multimodal framework** that:
  1. Fuses 4 accessible screening modalities (**Clinical + 12-lead ECG waveforms + Laboratory + Lifestyle**) through learned cross-modal attention;
  2. Maintains **leakage-free patient-level splitting** with calibration (Platt/Temperature) and Decision Curve Analysis;
  3. Provides **quantitatively verified XAI** (multimodal SHAP AOPC deletion/insertion vs random, multi-seed Spearman ρ stability, and cardiologist plausibility review);
  4. Evaluates on a **South Asian / Bangladeshi primary clinical cohort** or demonstrates cross-site generalization to such a cohort;
  5. Reports **missing-modality degradation** stress tests for realistic clinical deployment.
  
  **P0038 satisfies criteria 2–3 (partially, without SHAP AOPC) and 5, but not 1 or 4. P0036 satisfies 5 partially but not 1, 2, 3, or 4. No single study covers all five criteria.**
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ1–RQ4** (End-to-end framework)
- **Feasibility Assessment:** Feasible but resource-intensive — requires: (a) hospital data agreement(s) in Bangladesh for 12-lead ECG + clinical + lab + lifestyle data, (b) ≥2 blinded cardiologists for endpoint adjudication and XAI plausibility review, (c) IRB/Ethics clearance, (d) compute for Transformer training and SHAP computation. The framework does **not** depend on MRI/CT/Echo, making it accessible for resource-limited settings.
