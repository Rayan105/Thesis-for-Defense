# GAP0003 — Quantitative XAI Faithfulness, Stability & Clinical Plausibility Gap

## 1. Proposal Gap Baseline
- Proposal Gap Identifier: **PG3**
- Proposal Statement (Verbatim): "Many AI models do not explain their predictions"

## 2. Systematic Evidence Audit
- **Search Coverage:** Google Scholar (`5` queries: `SEARCH-SCHOLAR-Q1`..`Q5`; `2018–2026` protocol window, `2022–2026` retrieved; `2026-11-25`), `83` screened $\rightarrow$ `42` included.
- **Supporting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **CLAIM0009 (Internal synthesis):** Full-text reading disproved `20 / 24` preliminary `XAIEvaluated = Yes (quantitative faithfulness)` tags in `papers.csv`. **0/42 studies** evaluated multimodal SHAP deletion/insertion AOPC vs random controls + multi-seed rank stability + cardiologist plausibility jointly on `C+E+L+S` data.
  - **P0034** (SR of `65` healthcare XAI studies, `47` cardiac): `61.5%` (`40/65`) included no XAI; `0` attention-based models verified faithfulness; only `2/25` XAI studies conducted clinician plausibility evaluation (`pp. 11–16`).
  - **P0035** (SR of `45` ECG-XAI studies): confirms evaluation fragmentation and lack of consensus faithfulness benchmarks (`pp. 16–18`).
  - **P0041** (Bangladeshi-led SR of `85` DL-ECG studies): only `8/85` (`9.4%`) used any XAI and `0/85` (`0%`) quantified uncertainty (`pp. 29–33`).
  - **P0003** (Mayo PCI): explicitly states LIME/SHAP could not be applied to the joint multimodal fusion model (`p. 10`).
  - **P0020** (Mayo ICI): ran SHAP **only on the RF tabular baseline**, explicitly noting SHAP could not interpret the joint fusion framework (`pp. 7, 10`).
  - **P0001, P0002, P0004, P0006, P0007, P0011, P0012, P0013, P0014, P0016, P0021, P0022, P0023, P0030, P0031, P0033, P0036:** Qualitative plots/ranking/weights only (no AOPC, no seed-stability correlation, no clinician plausibility), or **None** (`P0005, P0008, P0015, P0024, P0025, P0037`).
- **Contradicting Literature (`P####` IDs — `4 / 42` with partial or clinical quantitative XAI):**
  - **P0028** (ECG images): EAT sanity checks, NMI=`0.52`, FGSM/PGD perturbation on `n=186` traces — partial quantitative XAI, but unimodal ECG images only, no deletion/insertion AOPC curves, no clinician review; **High PROBAST** (`pp. 26–29`).
  - **P0029** (ECG+CXR): Evaluates CXR Grad-CAM AOPC where insertion outperformed random (`1.807 > 1.759`) yet **deletion failed vs random masking (`1.803 vs 1.846`)** — proving unfaithful saliency even when AOPC was tested; zero ECG AOPC; **High PROBAST** (`pp. 5–8`).
  - **P0032** (CVES physiological signals): Linear-perceptron `MEMC` top-3 deletion (`SHAP 8.76 > Anchors 6.11 > LIME 4.13`) — partial quantitative check on chronic stroke `n=64`, no full AOPC ROC/probability curve vs random or clinician review; **High PROBAST** (`pp. 16–17`).
  - **P0038** (`MACE-MAIS`, **Low PROBAST**): Rigorous blinded 3-cardiologist review (`n=150`, `ICC=0.91`) + shuffled-rationale ablation (`0.6910 -> 0.6238`) — strongest XAI evaluation in corpus, but covers CMR+EHR+Text rationales without ECG waveforms or SHAP AOPC curves (`pp. 10–12`).

## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** Claiming *"AI models do not explain predictions"* is overly broad because decorative SHAP/Grad-CAM plots are common (`17/29` studies show qualitative plots) and `4/42` test partial quantitative or clinician plausibility criteria (`P0028, P0029, P0032, P0038`). The true Q1 scientific gap is the **"Qualitative SHAP Illusion"**: **0/42 studies jointly evaluate (1) multimodal SHAP deletion/insertion AOPC vs random and reverse controls, (2) multi-seed attribution rank stability (`Spearman ρ`, `top-k Jaccard`), (3) shortcut auditing (site/missingness/filter artifacts), and (4) blinded cardiologist plausibility scoring** on a 4-modality `Clinical + 12-lead ECG + Lab + Lifestyle` fusion network.
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ3** (Quantitative XAI Faithfulness, Stability & Clinical Plausibility)
- **Feasibility Assessment:** High — Perturbation AOPC, multi-seed SHAP stability, and LOMO-vs-SHAP concordance are computationally tractable (`GradientSHAP/DeepSHAP/KernelSHAP` on train-only background); cardiologist review requires structured scoring packets (`n ≥ 2` clinicians).
