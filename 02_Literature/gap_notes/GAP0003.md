# GAP0003 — Lack of Quantitative XAI Faithfulness Verification in Multimodal CVD Models

## 1. Proposal Gap Baseline
- Proposal Gap Identifier: **PG3**
- Proposal Statement (Verbatim): "Many AI models do not explain their predictions"

## 2. Systematic Evidence Audit
- **Search Coverage:** Google Scholar (6 queries, 2020–2026), 83 screened → 42 included.
- **Supporting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **CLAIM0009 (Internal synthesis):** Full-text verification disproved 20/24 preliminary XAI tags. **0/42 studies** combined all four screening modalities with multimodal SHAP AOPC + multi-seed stability + cardiologist plausibility jointly.
  - **P0034** (Healthcare XAI SR): 61.5% (40/65) of healthcare AI studies included **no** explainability; 0 attention-based studies tested attribution faithfulness; only 2/25 XAI studies conducted clinician plausibility evaluation (pp. 11–16).
  - **P0035** (ECG-XAI SR): 45 ECG-XAI studies revealed fragmented evaluation with no consensus faithfulness benchmarks (pp. 16–18).
  - **P0041** (Bangladeshi SR): Only 8/85 (9.4%) DL-ECG studies used any XAI; 0/85 (0%) quantified prediction uncertainty (pp. 29–33).
  - **P0003** (Mayo PCI): Authors explicitly stated "LIME/SHAP could not be applied to the multimodal fusion model" (p. 10).
  - **P0001, P0002, P0004, P0006, P0007, P0011, P0012, P0013, P0014, P0016, P0020, P0021, P0022, P0031, P0036, P0037:** All used SHAP/Grad-CAM/IG/LIME **qualitatively only** (summary plots, bar charts, heatmaps) without deletion/insertion AOPC or stability metrics.
- **Contradicting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **P0028** (Khan ECG imgs): EAT sanity checks, NMI=0.52, FGSM/PGD adversarial perturbation on N=186 test traces — **partial quantitative XAI** but unimodal ECG images only, no AOPC curves, no clinician evaluation, **High PROBAST** (pp. 26–29).
  - **P0029** (Kobe CXR+ECG): CXR Grad-CAM AOPC where insertion succeeded (1.807>1.759) but **deletion failed** vs random (1.803 vs 1.846) — mixed/negative result for XAI faithfulness; no ECG AOPC, **High PROBAST** (pp. 5–8).
  - **P0032** (CVES Stroke): Linear perceptron MEMC top-3 deletion (SHAP 8.76 > LIME 4.13) — **partial quantitative** but unimodal physiological signals, no AUC/ROC-based AOPC, N=64, **High PROBAST** (pp. 16–17).
  - **P0038** (MAIS STEMI): Blinded 3-cardiologist review (ICC=0.91) + rationale-shuffling ablation (AUC 0.6910→0.6238) — **genuine clinical plausibility evaluation and faithfulness ablation**, but modalities are CMR+EHR+Text (no ECG, no SHAP AOPC), **Low PROBAST** (pp. 10–12).

## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** The broad claim "models do not explain predictions" is **overstated** — 4/42 studies demonstrate some quantitative XAI evaluation. However, **0/42 combined (a) multimodal SHAP deletion/insertion AOPC vs random controls, (b) multi-seed attribution stability (Spearman ρ / Jaccard), (c) calibration/DCA, and (d) structured cardiologist plausibility review** on a 4-modality Clinical+ECG+Lab+Lifestyle fusion model. P0038 comes closest but uses CMR/Text modalities and lacks SHAP AOPC. **PG3 is sharpened to: "No prior study jointly evaluates multimodal SHAP AOPC faithfulness, multi-seed stability, and cardiologist plausibility on a leakage-free 4-modality ECG+Clinical+Lab+Lifestyle deep fusion model."**
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ3**
- **Feasibility Assessment:** High — SHAP (DeepExplainer/KernelExplainer), AOPC deletion/insertion curves, and multi-seed stability analyses are computationally standard. Cardiologist plausibility review requires a panel of ≥2 blinded cardiologists at the hospital site(s).
