## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** The broad claim *"most studies use only one healthcare data type"* is **narrowed**: multimodal CVD modeling has expanded rapidly (`26/29` original studies in our focused multimodal search use $\ge 2$ modalities, and `14/29` combine $\ge 3$ streams). However, **within our 42-study verified corpus, 0/29 studies combine the four accessible non-imaging primary screening modalities (`Clinical + 12-lead ECG waveforms + Laboratory assays + Structured Lifestyle`)** in a leakage-free cross-modal Transformer framework with patient-level splitting, probability calibration, DCA, quantitative SHAP faithfulness, and Bangladeshi/South Asian clinical validation. Competing 4-modality studies rely on tertiary imaging (`P0016, P0031, P0036, P0038`), genomics (`P0023, P0025`), clinical text (`P0002, P0013`), lack raw ECG (`P0022, P0038`), or exhibit High PROBAST leakage (`P0002, P0013, P0016, P0020, P0022, P0036`).
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ1** (Multimodal Synergy & Discriminative Superiority)
- **Feasibility Assessment:** Feasible — Clinical vitals, 12-lead ECG, routine serum labs, and structured lifestyle questionnaires are collectible at Bangladeshi tertiary cardiac centers/diagnostic clinics without MRI, CCTA, or genomics infrastructure.
