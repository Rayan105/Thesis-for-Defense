# GAP0001 — Unimodal Dominance in CVD Prediction Models

## 1. Proposal Gap Baseline
- Proposal Gap Identifier: **PG1**
- Proposal Statement (Verbatim): "Most studies use only one healthcare data type"

## 2. Systematic Evidence Audit
- **Search Coverage:** Google Scholar (6 queries, 2020–2026), screened 83 → 42 included. No PubMed/Scopus/IEEE/Cochrane (search limitation).
- **Supporting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **P0010** (SR/MA of 21 EHR-ML CVD studies): 34.5% High PROBAST Risk; all models were EHR-only tabular (p. 10).
  - **P0017** (SR of 15 multimodal HF studies): 0/15 combined ≥3 modalities; 10/15 used late fusion of only 2 data types (pp. 5, 14).
  - **P0001** (MIMIC HF): ECG (V4) + Labs (3 biomarkers) only — 2 modalities.
  - **P0003** (Mayo PCI): ECG + EHR only — 2 modalities.
  - **P0004** (MITHSDB): ECG + PCG only — 2 modalities.
  - **P0007** (Swedish AMI): ECG + Age/Sex only — 2 modalities.
  - **P0008** (CardioCLIP): ECG + CXR only — 2 modalities.
  - **P0019** (SR of 85 DL-ECG): Confirms fragmented single-modality focus across 85 studies (pp. 32, 63).
  - **P0027** (SR of 69 DL-CVD): Only 17% external validation, 45% calibration (pp. 28, 30).
- **Contradicting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **P0016** (ChemoCardioNet): Claims 4 modalities (C+E+L+Echo) but with severe provenance/metric anomalies (AUC 1.00 in Fig. 4); **High PROBAST** (pp. 3, 6, 9).
  - **P0025** (ARIC AF): 4 modalities (C+E+L/Proteomics+PRS) in a strict patient-level split design (**Unclear PROBAST**), but targets AF only, uses ridge logistic regression (not deep fusion), and excludes South Asian participants entirely (pp. 1–5).
  - **P0031** (DEEP RISK): 4 modalities (C+E+L+LGE-MRI) but N=186, only 26 events, with SMOTE + frozen β-VAE; **High PROBAST** (pp. 4–5).
  - **P0036** (CGH Cross-modal): 5 modalities (C+E+L+S+CCTA) with dedicated missing-modality fusion and external validation — the **strongest 3+ modality contradicting evidence**, but requires CCTA (tertiary imaging), enrolled only 460 patients (83.4% excluded), and has ±30-day ECG–CCTA alignment window leakage; **High PROBAST** (pp. 5–8).
  - **P0038** (MAIS STEMI): 4 modalities (C+L+Text+CMR) with patient-level + 4-center LOCO + 3-cardiologist adjudication, achieving **Low PROBAST** — but modalities are CMR/Text-based tertiary data, not accessible primary screening (Clinical+ECG+Lab+Lifestyle) (pp. 3–12).

## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** While 5 studies demonstrate 3–4 modality integration (`P0016, P0025, P0031, P0036, P0038`), **none combines the four accessible primary screening modalities (Clinical vitals + 12-lead ECG waveforms + Laboratory assays + Lifestyle behaviors)** in a leakage-free deep fusion framework with patient-level splitting, calibration/DCA reporting, and external or multi-center validation. The 3–4 modality studies either (a) rely on tertiary imaging (CMR, CCTA, Echo) unavailable in resource-limited settings, (b) suffer from High PROBAST bias, (c) exclude South Asian populations, or (d) use classical linear models without cross-modal attention.
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ1** (Multimodal Synergy & Discriminative Superiority)
- **Feasibility Assessment:** Feasible — Clinical vitals, 12-lead ECG, standard labs (CBC, lipid panel, HbA1c, troponin), and lifestyle questionnaires are routinely collected at Bangladeshi tertiary cardiac hospitals. No MRI/CT/Echo dependency.
