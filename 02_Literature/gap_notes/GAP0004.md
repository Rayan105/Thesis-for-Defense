  - **P0020** (Mayo ICI): includes unquantified self-reported **smoking** inside the tabular RF/MLP block alongside 2-lead ECG and labs (`pp. 3, 7`), but performs zero LOMO ablation and zero fusion SHAP (`High PROBAST`).
  - **P0021** (Mayo pericarditis `p-Cal`): includes binary **smoking** in a 16-dim EHR RF vector alongside 12-lead ECG image ViT, but omits laboratory biomarkers and conducts zero lifestyle LOMO ablation (`High PROBAST`, `pp. 1–4`).

  - **P0036** (`CGH` CAD): includes **smoking** among 20–23 clinical/lab tabular inputs alongside CCTA + 12-lead ECG (`Fig. 5B`), with zero isolated lifestyle ablation and tertiary CCTA requirement (`High PROBAST`, `pp. 7–10`).
  - **P0038** (`MACE-MAIS`, **Low PROBAST**): captures binary **smoking** within structured EHR risk factors (`Table 1`), without broader diet/activity/sleep profiling, ECG waveforms, or lifestyle LOMO ablation (`p. 3`).

## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** Although `6/29` studies include a binary smoking flag (`P0020, P0021, P0025, P0036, P0038`) or leaked tabular lifestyle variables without raw ECG (`P0022`), **0/29 studies model a dedicated multi-item structured Lifestyle modality (smoking status/pack-years, physical activity, dietary pattern, sleep, family history per `RF10`) alongside 12-lead ECG waveforms + Clinical + Labs AND test its causal-free incremental value via controlled Leave-One-Modality-Out (LOMO) ablation and Decision Curve Analysis ($\Delta\text{Net Benefit}$)**. Whether lifestyle adds statistically meaningful gain beyond ECG and serum biomarkers remains an **untested falsifiable empirical question** (`RQ2`).
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ2** (Incremental Value of Lifestyle & Progressive/LOMO Ablations)
- **Feasibility Assessment:** Feasible — structured intake CRF (`T11`) can standardize lifestyle fields cleanly during primary hospital/clinic enrollment with strict coding separating `missing/not asked` from numeric `0`.
