# GAP0004 — Lifestyle Modality Rarely Integrated and Never Ablation-Quantified in Multimodal ECG+Lab CVD Models

## 1. Proposal Gap Baseline
- Proposal Gap Identifier: **PG4**
- Proposal Statement (Verbatim): "Lifestyle information is often ignored"

## 2. Systematic Evidence Audit
- **Search Coverage:** Google Scholar (6 queries, 2020–2026), 83 screened → 42 included.
- **Supporting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **CLAIM0007 (Internal synthesis):** "None of the reviewed multimodal ECG+tabular studies quantified lifestyle incremental gain via controlled LOMO ablation."
  - **P0017** (HF SR): 0/15 HF multimodal studies combined lifestyle with clinical/ECG (pp. 5, 14).
  - **P0027** (DL-CVD SR): Notes lifestyle factors are "routinely collected yet rarely modeled" in deep learning pipelines (pp. 3, 28).
  - **P0001, P0002, P0003, P0005, P0007, P0008, P0011, P0012, P0013, P0014, P0023, P0024, P0028, P0029, P0033:** Zero lifestyle variables included.
  - **P0025** (ARIC AF): Includes clinical + ECG + lab + PRS but **excludes lifestyle variables** despite ARIC collecting diet/exercise/smoking data (pp. 3–4).
  - **P0031** (DEEP RISK): Includes clinical + ECG + labs + MRI but **no lifestyle** (pp. 4–5).
- **Contradicting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **P0020** (Mayo ICI): Includes smoking history as a binary EHR covariate alongside 2-lead ECG and labs, but SHAP was only applied to the RF baseline (not fusion model), and no LOMO ablation was performed to isolate lifestyle incremental value; **High PROBAST** (pp. 3, 7, 10).
  - **P0021** (p-Cal): Includes smoking (binary) alongside ECG + EHR, but **excludes laboratory tests**, and no ablation quantifies smoking's incremental contribution; **High PROBAST** (pp. 1–4).
  - **P0022** (QST-Transformer): Includes lifestyle indicators (smoking, alcohol, physical activity) alongside clinical/lab/wearable HR, but **no raw ECG waveforms**, severe pre-split leakage (SMOTE/KNN/Z-score before split), and no LOMO ablation; **High PROBAST** (pp. 5–11).
  - **P0036** (CGH): Includes smoking history alongside C+E+L+CCTA, but requires CCTA, and lifestyle is a single binary indicator; no LOMO ablation reported for lifestyle specifically; **High PROBAST** (Fig. 5 p. 10).

## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** Four studies include at least one lifestyle indicator (`P0020, P0021, P0022, P0036`), but: (a) **none uses a comprehensive lifestyle vector** (diet score + physical activity level + smoking pack-years) as a distinct modality alongside 12-lead ECG waveforms and serum biomarkers, and (b) **none conducts controlled Leave-One-Modality-Out (LOMO) ablation** to quantify lifestyle's incremental predictive and clinical utility (ΔNet Benefit via DCA). The lifestyle features used are limited to binary smoking or single indicators, embedded as EHR columns, without modality-level attribution. **PG4 is supported: "No prior leakage-free multimodal study has quantified the incremental discrimination and clinical utility of a structured lifestyle modality (diet, activity, smoking) via LOMO ablation alongside 12-lead ECG and laboratory data."**
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ2** (Incremental Value of Lifestyle Modality)
- **Feasibility Assessment:** Feasible — standardized lifestyle questionnaires (WHO STEPS, GPAQ, PREDIMED short screener) can be administered during hospital intake alongside ECG and lab tests. The lifestyle modality requires no additional instrumentation.
