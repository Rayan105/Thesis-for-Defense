## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** The literal claim *"combine only two sources"* is **narrowed / partially refuted by 14 studies** integrating 3–5 data streams. However, **0/29 original studies integrate 12-lead ECG waveforms + Clinical EHR + Laboratory assays + Structured Lifestyle via cross-modal Transformer attention** under a leakage-free patient-level split. All 14 multi-source comparators either require unavailable tertiary imaging/genomics (`P0016, P0023, P0025, P0031, P0036, P0038`), use text notes instead of structured lifestyle (`P0002, P0013, P0037`), omit raw ECG waveforms (`P0022, P0038`), restrict to 2 ECG leads/cancer patients (`P0020`), omit labs (`P0021`), or suffer High PROBAST risk (`12/14`). **PG2 is sharpened from "only two sources" to "absence of leakage-free, accessible 4-modality (`C+E+L+S`) Transformer fusion."**
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ1**, **RQ2**
- **Feasibility Assessment:** Feasible — standard hospital clinical/lab/12-lead ECG records paired with a structured lifestyle CRF require zero specialized imaging (`MRI/CCTA/Echo`) or genomic arrays.
