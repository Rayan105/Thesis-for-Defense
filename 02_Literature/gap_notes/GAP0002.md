# GAP0002 — Two-Modality Ceiling in Existing Multimodal CVD Systems

## 1. Proposal Gap Baseline
- Proposal Gap Identifier: **PG2**
- Proposal Statement (Verbatim): "Existing multimodal systems combine only two sources"

## 2. Systematic Evidence Audit
- **Search Coverage:** Google Scholar (6 queries, 2020–2026), 83 screened → 42 included. Single database limitation.
- **Supporting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **P0017** (HF SR): "0/15 included studies combined ≥3 modalities or paired clinical/ECG with lifestyle inputs" (pp. 4–5, 14). **Direct quantitative evidence.**
  - **P0001** (2 mod), **P0003** (2 mod), **P0004** (2 mod), **P0005** (1 mod + PCE composite), **P0006** (2 mod), **P0007** (2 mod), **P0008** (2 mod), **P0011** (2 mod), **P0012** (2 mod), **P0014** (2 mod), **P0015** (2 mod), **P0024** (2 mod), **P0028** (1 mod), **P0029** (2 mod), **P0032** (4 signal channels but all physiological — not Clinical/Lab/Lifestyle), **P0033** (1 mod).
  - **P0040** (Umbrella Review): 0 South Asian/Bangladeshi cohorts with ≥3 modalities; confirms 91.2% CVD death rise in Asia 2025–2050 (pp. 3, 13–16).
- **Contradicting Literature (`P####` IDs):**
  - **P0002** (CaCHD): 4 types (C+E+L+Text) but **High PROBAST** due to pre-split MICE, post-admission ECG, and unclear splitting (pp. 4–8).
  - **P0013** (MULTINet TAVR): 4 types (C+E+L+Text) but **High PROBAST** due to pre-CV MICE, N=761, only 32 events (4.2%), no external validation (pp. 8–10).
  - **P0016** (ChemoCardioNet): 4 types (C+E+L+Echo) but severely anomalous metrics (AUC 1.00), **High PROBAST** (pp. 3–9).
  - **P0020** (Mayo ICI): 4 types (C+E+L+Smoking) but only 2 ECG leads (II, V1), no SHAP on fusion model, 58.3% troponin selection bias, **High PROBAST** (pp. 1–10).
  - **P0025** (ARIC AF): 4 types (C+E+L+PRS) with **Unclear PROBAST** — closest clean counterexample, but uses ridge logistic (not deep fusion), European-only, no lifestyle (pp. 1–5).
  - **P0036** (CGH): 5 types (C+E+L+S+CCTA) but requires CCTA tertiary imaging, **High PROBAST** (pp. 5–8).
  - **P0038** (MAIS): 4 types (C+L+Text+CMR) with **Low PROBAST** — gold-standard design but modalities are CMR-based tertiary, not ECG+Lab+Lifestyle screening (pp. 3–12).

## 3. Sharpened Q1 Scientific Positioning
- **Refined Q1 Research Gap:** The broad claim "only two sources" is **narrowed** — 7 studies integrate 3–4 modalities. However, **none combines 12-lead ECG waveforms + Clinical + Lab + Lifestyle in a cross-modal attention Transformer** with leakage-free patient-level splitting. The ≥3 modality studies either: (a) depend on CMR/CCTA/Echo (tertiary), (b) have High PROBAST bias, (c) use only 2 ECG leads or no ECG waveforms, or (d) employ classical models without learned cross-modal attention. **PG2 is narrowed from "no prior ≥3 modality" to "no accessible 4-modality (C+E+L+S) deep fusion with leakage-free design and ECG waveform processing."**
- **Primary Research Question Link:** **RQ1**, **RQ2**
- **Feasibility Assessment:** Feasible — lifestyle questionnaire (diet, activity, smoking) can be administered alongside routine clinical/ECG/lab workups at Bangladeshi hospitals. No imaging dependency.
