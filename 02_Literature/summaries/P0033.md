## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:**
  - **Primary Secondary-Care Cohort (`BIDMC`):** Retrospective clinical dataset from Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston, USA (`2000–2023`, `MUSE version 9; GE Healthcare`; used for $\beta$-VAE development and all supervised ECG decoding benchmarks, pp. 2, 9–10).
  - **Primary-Care Pretraining Cohort (`CODE`):** Telehealth Network of Minas Gerais (`TNMG`) across 811 counties in Minas Gerais, Brazil (`2010–2016`, `TEB ECGPC`; combined with BIDMC solely for unsupervised $\beta$-VAE training, pp. 2, 9–10).
  - **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** (no race/ethnicity breakdown reported in text or tables; US Boston and Brazilian cohorts only, p. 9).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - **`BIDMC`:** `1,169,387` raw 12-lead ECGs from **`189,542` patients** (`48.71%` female, age `63.73 ± 16.36` years, pp. 2, 9). After `BRAVEHEART` QC and median-beat anomaly rejection (`6.74%` drop rate), final sample size is **`1,090,617` ECGs** (p. 9; flat/`>12 mV` noisy ECGs additionally excluded from training set, exact count `not reported`, p. 10).
  - **`CODE`:** `2,202,555` raw 12-lead ECGs from **`1,503,229` patients** (`39.89%` female, age `53.54 ± 17.40` years, pp. 2, 9). After QC exclusion (`1.92%` drop rate), final sample size is **`2,160,342` ECGs** (p. 9).
  - **Combined Post-QC Corpus:** `3,250,959` median-beat ECGs across `1,692,771` total raw patients (`100k+ / 200k+` hold-out test ECGs per dataset for $\beta$-VAE evaluation, p. 3). Exact patient/ECG counts in the `50/10/40` supervised BIDMC test split are `not reported` (p. 10).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Raw `5-year` mortality rate is **`20.7%`** in `BIDMC` (`189,542` patients) and **`3.5%`** in `CODE` (`1,503,229` patients, p. 9); sex is `48.71%` female in `BIDMC` (p. 9). Exact post-QC positive event counts and split-specific prevalences are `not reported`.
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Three unimodal ECG decoding tasks evaluated on `BIDMC` (pp. 3–4, 10):
  1. **Age:** Continuous regression (`MAE` and `MSE` loss, p. 10).
  2. **Sex:** Binary classification (`female = 1`, Fig. 4 p. 7; binary cross-entropy loss, `AUROC`, p. 10).
  3. **5-Year Mortality Risk:** Binary death within `5 years` from ECG recording date via Massachusetts Department of Public Health (`DPH`) and/or `BIDMC` EHR review (`AUROC`, p. 10). Supplementary qualitative `VAE-SCAN` traversals for cardiac/non-cardiac ICD conditions (`Supplementary Figs. 16–17`, p. 4) are unbundled.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (`1` of `4` target modalities — Unimodal ECG Only):**
  - `12-lead ECG` reduced to **`8` independent leads** (`I, II, V1, V2, V3, V4, V5, V6`, as `III, aVR, aVF, aVL` are linear combinations of I and II, p. 9).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** Clinical/EHR inputs, Laboratory/Biomarkers, and **Lifestyle:** `none / not reported` (`age`, `sex`, and `5-year mortality` serve strictly as prediction targets/clinical concepts rather than fused inputs, pp. 3–4, 10).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** Resting-state `500 Hz` digital 12-lead ECGs (`10 s` in BIDMC; `7 s` or `10 s` in CODE) processed offline via `BRAVEHEART v1.1.1` (MATLAB) to align QRS beats around the vectorcardiogram magnitude R-peak (`VMR-peak`) into representative `1200 ms` median beats (`480 ms` pre-R-peak, p. 9). Band-pass filtered (`0.5–100 Hz`, 3rd-order zero-phase IIR Butterworth), `60 Hz` notch filtered, downsampled to `400 Hz` (`480` samples), and symmetrically zero-padded to **`(512, 8)`** (p. 9). The `10-s` ResNet comparator uses `(4096, 8)` rhythm strips (p. 5).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** `Not applicable` (unimodal ECG; ECGs with `>150 ms` R-peak deviation in `>30%` of leads rejected, p. 9; flat/`>12 mV` noisy ECGs excluded from training set, p. 10).
- **Model Families & Baselines (Progressively Ordered on Performance–Explainability Spectrum, pp. 4–5, 8–11):**
  1. **Annealed $\beta$-VAE (`pp. 2, 9–10`):** 6-block convolutional encoder (`1,533,888` params) and symmetric decoder (`1,283,976` params) with $N_z = 256$ latent units ($\beta = 10$, MAE reconstruction loss, capacity $C_{\max} \in \{25, 50, 100, 200\}$ nats on BIDMC and `250` nats on BIDMC+CODE yielding `80` informative latent factors with $KL > 0.1$).
  2. **`ResNet (10-s)`:** Unconstrained 10-s black-box baseline (`[39]`, `(1024, 256, 64)` output stages, p. 11).
  3. **`ResNet (Median)`:** Adjusted ResNet (`(128, 32, 16)`) trained on median beats (p. 11).
  4. **`ResNet (VAE Rec.)`:** Adjusted ResNet trained on $\beta$-VAE-reconstructed median beats (pp. 4, 8, 11).
  5. **`MLP` ($\beta$-VAE features):** 2-layer MLP (`256`, `128` units, Dropout `0.2`) on the `80` BIDMC+CODE $\beta$-VAE latent means (pp. 5, 8, 11).
  6. **`Linear` ($\beta$-VAE features):** `LassoCV` (age) / `LogisticRegressionCV` (sex, mortality) with L1/hyperparameter CV (`scikit-learn v1.4.2`, p. 11).
  7. **`VAE-SCAN` (`pp. 4–5, 10–11`):** Symbol-Concept Association Network (symmetrical MLP encoder `128-256` [`74,400` params] & decoder `256-128` [`53,761` params], $\lambda=10$, `BCE × 10,000`, trained separately per target) anchoring label posteriors $q_\psi(z_y|y)$ to frozen $\beta$-VAE latents $q_\phi(z_x|x)$ for bi-directional ECG decoding and concept visualization.
- **Fusion Strategy:** `None` (unimodal ECG representation learning & decoding; multi-factor SCAN mentioned as future/exploratory capability on p. 7).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** **Patient-ID-grouped** fixed splits (`85/5/10` train/val/test for unsupervised $\beta$-VAE; `50/10/40` train/val/test for supervised baselines and SCAN, stratified for mortality in SCAN, pp. 10–11).
- **Annealed $\beta$-VAE Reconstruction Performance (`Table 1`, p. 2, Test Set):**
  - `Low capacity` ($C=25$, `18` factors): MAE `0.014 mV`, Pearson's $R$ `0.98`
  - `Medium capacity` ($C=50$, `23` factors): MAE `0.011 mV`, Pearson's $R$ `0.98`
  - `High capacity` ($C=100$, `29` factors): MAE `0.010 mV`, Pearson's $R$ `0.99`
  - `Full capacity` ($C=200$, `51` factors, BIDMC): MAE `0.007 mV`, Pearson's $R$ `0.99`
  - `*Full capacity (+CODE)` ($C=250$, **`80` factors**, BIDMC+CODE): MAE **`0.006 mV`**, Pearson's $R$ **`1.00`**
- **$\beta$-VAE Encoding Capacity Trade-Off (`Table 2`, p. 4, BIDMC Test Set — BIDMC-Only $\beta$-VAE):**
  - `Low capacity` (`Linear` / `MLP`): Age MAE `10.85` / `9.71`, Sex AUC `0.80` / `0.86`, Mortality AUC `0.75` / `0.79`
  - `Medium capacity` (`Linear` / `MLP`): Age MAE `10.49` / `9.29`, Sex AUC `0.82` / `0.88`, Mortality AUC `0.75` / `0.79`
  - `High capacity` (`Linear` / `MLP`): Age MAE `10.17` / `8.93`, Sex AUC `0.82` / `0.89`, Mortality AUC `0.76` / `0.80`
  - `Full capacity` ($200$ nats, `Linear` / `MLP`): Age MAE `10.07` / `8.52`, Sex AUC `0.84` / `0.90`, Mortality AUC `0.77` / `0.81`
- **Performance–Explainability Trade-Off Across Model Families (`Table 3` & `Fig. 5`, p. 8, BIDMC `40%` Test Set, using `*Full capacity (+CODE)` 80 features):**
  - `ResNet (10-s)` [black-box]: Age MAE **`7.50`**, Sex AUC **`0.94`**, Mortality AUC **`0.87`**
  - `ResNet (Median)`: Age MAE `7.60` (`-1.33%`), Sex AUC `0.93` (`-1.06%`), Mortality AUC `0.84` (`-3.45%`)
  - `ResNet (VAE Rec.)`: Age MAE `8.33`, Sex AUC `0.90`, Mortality AUC `0.81` (`5.19%` average drop vs `ResNet (Median)`, p. 4)
  - `MLP (*β-VAE)`: Age MAE `8.38` (`-10.26%` vs ResNet Median in Fig. 5), Sex AUC `0.91` (`-2.15%`), Mortality AUC `0.81` (`-3.57%`)
  - `Linear (*β-VAE)` [sample interpretable]: Age MAE `10.04` (`-19.81%` vs MLP), Sex AUC `0.84` (`-7.69%`), Mortality AUC `0.76` (`-6.17%`; `11.22%` avg drop vs MLP, p. 5)
  - `SCAN (*β-VAE)` [sample + model interpretable]: Age MAE `10.76` (`-7.17%` vs Linear), Sex AUC `0.81` (`-3.57%`), Mortality AUC `0.72` (`-5.26%`; `5.33%` additional avg drop vs Linear, p. 5)
- **Cross-Signal Prediction Stability (`pp. 4–5`):** `ResNet (Median)` lost `9.15%` average performance when tested on VAE-reconstructed signals (`Pearson's R = 0.85`), whereas `ResNet (VAE Rec.)` gained `+0.73%` on original median beats with higher prediction correlation (`Pearson's R = 0.98`).
- **Missing Metrics (`Tables 1–3`):** `95% CIs`, `PR-AUC`, `Sensitivity`, `Specificity`, `Precision`, `F1-score`: `not reported` in main PDF (mortality classification metrics deferred to unbundled `Supplementary Table 1`, p. 4).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported` — no calibration curve, slope, Brier score, or ECE).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `No` (`not reported` — no DeLong test, bootstrap CIs, or paired model p-values).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — LOW RISK (Internally) / UNCLEAR (External):** Large consecutive cohorts (`BIDMC` `189,542` patients; `CODE` `1,503,229` patients, p. 9) grouped strictly by `patient ID` across fixed `85/5/10` and `50/10/40` splits (`p. 10`), preventing cross-split patient leakage. However, supervised decoding models were evaluated **only on BIDMC internal holdout** (CODE used solely for $\beta$-VAE pretraining, pp. 2–4, 10), and `~5.75` ECGs/patient (`1,090,617 / 189,542`) are evaluated without single-ECG-per-patient sampling confirmation.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW RISK:** Automated, standardized 8-lead median-beat pipeline (`BRAVEHEART v1.1.1`, Butterworth `0.5–100 Hz`, `60 Hz` notch, `400 Hz`, `(512, 8)`, p. 9); flat/`>12 mV` exclusion applied to training set only (p. 10).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Age, sex, and 5-year mortality via Massachusetts DPH / BIDMC EHR (p. 10). However, BIDMC spans `2000–2023` (p. 9) with no stated exclusion or censoring handling for ECGs acquired in `2019–2023` lacking 5 full years of follow-up, and no competing-risk handling.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Complete absence of calibration** (`Brier/ECE/calibration curve`), **`95% CIs`**, **`DCA`**, and **statistical comparison tests** (`Tables 1–3`, pp. 2, 4, 8).
  - Unbundled mortality classification metrics (`Supplementary Table 1`, p. 4), unaddressed 5-year censoring for recent `2019–2023` ECGs, repeated-ECG clustering in test evaluation, and no external test evaluation for downstream predictive tasks (`BIDMC` only, `Table 3 p. 8`). *(Mitigating strengths: patient-ID splits, threshold-free AUROC, independent baseline tuning, public GitHub code `konspatl/vae_scan`, pp. 10–11.)*
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:**
  1. **Annealed $\beta$-VAE Disentangled Latent Factor Traversals (`-5` to `+5`, step `1`, `p. 10`):** Visualizes individual PQRST morphological axes (`Fig. 1a` p. 3) and signal reconstructions (`Fig. 1b`).
  2. **Linear / Logistic Weights (`LassoCV` / `LogisticRegressionCV`, pp. 4, 11):** Sample-level additive feature weights over the `80` $\beta$-VAE factors.
  3. **`VAE-SCAN` Bi-Directional Concept Traversals (`Fig. 3` p. 6, `Fig. 4` p. 7):** Visualizes $p_\gamma(y|z_y)$ (sample-level) and $q_\psi(z_y|y)$ (model/population-level) morphological variations across `age`, `sex`, and `5-year mortality`, qualitatively corroborated against published cardiology ECG biomarkers (`LAD`, `PR/QRS/QT` prolongation, `ST` depression, `T-wave` flattening/inversion, pathological `Q` waves, loss of `P` waves; refs `[35–38]`, p. 4).
- **Rigor Audit:** Although P0033 explicitly frames fidelity and robustness as core XAI criteria (`pp. 2, 6`), fidelity is argued *architecturally* (white-box linear/SCAN decoder mapping) plus via **ECG signal reconstruction accuracy** (`MAE 0.006 mV`, `R = 1.00`, `Table 1` p. 2), and robustness is tested on **predictive output correlation** across raw vs reconstructed median beats (`R = 0.98 vs 0.85`, p. 5) and **downstream predictive performance drops** (`Table 3`, `Fig. 5`, p. 8). There are **zero attribution-faithfulness perturbation tests (`AOPC` deletion/insertion, comprehensiveness/sufficiency)**, **zero multi-seed explanation stability correlations** (`Spearman ρ`, `Jaccard`), and **zero formal clinician plausibility ratings** (`0` reader-study data).
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **Qualitative / Visual / Intrinsic Architecture Only (No Deletion/Insertion AOPC Faithfulness, Multi-Seed Stability, or Clinician Review)** *(Corrects `papers.csv` tag `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`, which conflates quantitative VAE reconstruction fidelity [`MAE/R`] and accuracy–explainability trade-off drops [`-11.22%` / `-5.33%`] with quantitative XAI attribution faithfulness).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (`pp. 6–8`):** Findings rely on three general tasks (`age`, `sex`, `5-year mortality`) and may not generalize to all cardiac/non-cardiac conditions; median-beat extraction discards rhythmic/HRV variability needed for mortality prediction (`-3.45%`); $\beta$-VAE bottleneck discards rare/subtle signals (e.g., pacing spikes or high-frequency artifacts) exploited by unconstrained ResNets (`-5.19%`); latent factors remain partially entangled (shown by MLP vs Linear gap `-11.22%`); and confounding clinical factor correlations persist unless jointly modeled (`p. 6`).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Landmark methodological paper (`npj Digital Medicine`, 2025) empirically quantifying the **accuracy–explainability trade-off in AI-ECG** (`ResNet 10-s` mortality AUC `0.87` $\rightarrow$ `ResNet Median` `0.84` $\rightarrow$ `MLP` `0.81` $\rightarrow$ `Linear` `0.76` $\rightarrow$ `SCAN` `0.72`) and warning against post-hoc saliency unreliability (`p. 2`). Directly motivates our FYDP design (`PG3`, `PG5`, `RQ3`): rather than sacrificing `-0.15` AUC via an unsupervised median-beat $\beta$-VAE bottleneck or trusting unvalidated heatmaps, our FYDP preserves full discriminative performance via multimodal Transformer fusion while auditing post-hoc multimodal SHAP explanations quantitatively with **AOPC deletion/insertion faithfulness** and **multi-seed stability**. Key gaps in `P0033` left open for our FYDP: unimodal ECG-only design (`0` Clinical/Lab/Lifestyle fusion, `PG2`, `PG4`), `0` South Asian/Bangladeshi evaluation (`PG1`), absent calibration/DCA/CIs, and no external supervised testing.
