## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Retrospective secondary analysis of the **EchoNext Mini-Model Dataset** (`v1.1.0`, Elias & Finer 2025, `PhysioNet`, `10.13026/3ykd-bf14`), collected from adult patients ($\ge 18$ years) at **Columbia University Irving Medical Center (CUIMC)**, New York, USA, between `2008` and `2022` (p. 8 §4.1, p. 19 ref 10). Subgroup fairness charts plot only `Asian`, `Black`, and `Hispanic` groups (Fig. 4.2, p. 13; White and subgroup sample counts omitted). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported**.
- **Sample Size ($N$):** `100,000` 12-lead ECGs paired with echocardiogram-derived Structural Heart Disease (SHD) labels and clinical metadata (pp. 8–10, Table 4.1 on p. 10). Mean age `61.18 ± 16.08` years (text p. 9: `61.2 ± 16.1`, range `18–90`, Table 4.1 p. 10); sex breakdown is `not reported` in Table 4.1. **Important:** Unique patient count is `not reported`, and `most_recent_ecg` has mean `0.36286` (Table 4.1, p. 10), confirming multiple ECGs per patient exist in the 100,000-record dataset.
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Composite `SHD` positive in `52.19%` (`mean = 0.52188`, `52,188 / 100,000` ECGs, Table 4.1 p. 10). Component abnormalities (p. 9 §4.1.3 & Table 4.1 p. 10): LVEF $\le 45\%$ (`23.89%`), LV wall thickness $\ge 13$ mm (`24.22%`), pulmonary hypertension PASP $\ge 45$ mmHg (`18.99%`), RV systolic dysfunction (`13.24%`), tricuspid regurgitation (`10.65%`), TR max velocity $\ge 32$ (`10.21%`), mitral regurgitation (`8.45%`), aortic stenosis (`4.05%`), pericardial effusion (`3.02%`), aortic regurgitation (`1.26%`), pulmonary regurgitation (`0.82%`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Binary diagnostic detection of echocardiogram-confirmed composite **Structural Heart Disease (SHD)** from 12-lead ECGs and clinical metadata collected *"within a one-year interval"* (p. 8 §4.1). Exact temporal direction/gap between ECG and echocardiogram ($\Delta t$) is `not reported`.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (2 of 4 target modalities + Potential Target-Leakage Ambiguity):**
  - **12-lead ECG waveforms:** `100,000` 12-lead ECGs (`12 × 2,500` time points, p. 9 §4.1.1; exact sampling frequency in Hz and filtering `not reported`). Training-set stochastic augmentations: time shifting, amplitude scaling, additive Gaussian noise, and temporal masking (p. 9 §4.1.2).
  - **Tabular Clinical & ECG Metadata + Missingness Indicators:** §4.1 (p. 8) lists GE MUSE Nx 10.2 metadata (`age`, `sex`, `heart rate`, `PR interval`, `QRS duration`, `QT interval`, `corrected QT interval`), whereas §4.1.2 (p. 9) explicitly describes appending missingness masks *"so that the model understands when some clinical information is missing from the echocardiographic variables"*, and Table 4.1 (p. 10) lists echocardiographic measurements (`ivs_measurement`, `lvpw_measurement`, `pasp_value`, `tr_max_velocity_value`, `lvef_value`) alongside ECG intervals without specifying the exact subset passed into `TabMLP Encoder`.
  - **Excluded target modalities:** No dedicated Laboratory/Biomarker panel (and if echo variables entered tabular input, that constitutes direct label leakage); **Lifestyle:** `none / not reported`.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `12 × 2,500` time points (p. 9 §4.1.1; sampling rate Hz and duration in seconds `not reported`, though standard EchoNext is 250 Hz $\times$ 10 s).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Mask-aware modelling (`Mask Modelling`) appending a binary missingness indicator per missing tabular variable $j$ to the tabular feature vector before `TabMLP Encoder` (p. 9 §4.1.2, Fig. 4.1 p. 11).
- **Model Families & Baselines:** Compares 5 ECG waveform backbones inside the identical `TabMLP + GatedLateFusion` pipeline (pp. 5–6, 10–12, Fig. 4.1): `Simple CNN-1D`, `ResNet1d18`, `TCN` (Temporal Convolutional Network), `Light Transformer`, and `Hybrid` (CNN-Stem + Transformer encoders). **Critical omission:** Zero **unimodal baselines** (`ECG-only` or `Tabular-only`), zero **non-gated fusion baselines**, and zero comparisons against the original EchoNext reference model are reported.
- **Fusion Strategy:** `Adaptive Gated Late-Fusion` (`GatedLateFusion`, pp. 10–11 & Fig. 4.1): ECG backbone embedding and `TabMLP` embedding (mapped to identical latent dimensionality) are concatenated and fed to a gating network producing modality-specific weights, which are normalized, fused, and passed to a binary MLP-Logit-Sigmoid classifier head. Exact layer widths, latent dimensions, and gating equations are `not reported`.

Models trained from scratch over `3` independent random seeds (`batch size = 64`, `AdamW`, `lr = 3e-4`, `wd = 1e-4`, cosine warmup, `60` epochs, early stopping on val AUC, BCEWithLogitsLoss with positive-class weight, p. 11 §4.2).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — UNCLEAR / HIGH RISK:** Dataset (`100,000` ECGs) contains multiple ECGs per patient (`most_recent_ecg = 0.36286`, Table 4.1 p. 10), yet unique patient counts, train/val/test sizes, and confirmation of patient-level split separation are omitted (`not reported`). Sex distribution and White demographic subgroup are also omitted from Table 4.1 and Fig. 4.2.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH RISK:** Section 4.1.2 (p. 9) explicitly states missingness masks are appended *"so that the model understands when some clinical information is missing from the echocardiographic variables"*, while Table 4.1 (p. 10) lists echocardiographic ground-truth measurements (`lvef_value`, `pasp_value`, `ivs_measurement`, `lvpw_measurement`, `tr_max_velocity_value`) alongside ECG intervals without specifying the exact input features fed to `TabMLP`—raising a major **target-leakage ambiguity** if echo variables entered the predictors. Sampling rate, preprocessing normalization isolation, and gating/MLP hyperparameters are also `not reported`.
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Echo-confirmed composite SHD labels (`52.19%`) derive from PhysioNet EchoNext, but ECG-to-echo temporal direction and exact adjudication window beyond `"within a one-year interval"` (p. 8) are `not reported`.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Zero unimodal baselines** (`ECG-only`, `Tabular-only`) and **zero non-gated fusion ablations**, making it impossible to verify whether `GatedLateFusion` adds any value over unimodal models.
  - **Zero calibration** (`Brier/ECE/plot`), **zero DCA**, and **unspecified classification threshold** for ACC/F1/BACC/SENS/SPEC in Table 4.2.
  - **Multiple preprint quality/reporting errors:** Misreferencing Table 4.3 (bootstrap CIs) as fairness metrics (p. 13), claiming TCN BACC `0.7652` beats Hybrid `0.7662` (p. 12), duplicating section number `4.2.3` (pp. 14–15), claiming "equity-scaled AUC" while plotting raw AUCs (pp. 13–14), and misattributing EchoNext in Literature Review to Ghorbani et al. 2020 (p. 4, actually *EchoNet*).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Not yet peer-reviewed (`medRxiv`, p. 1); single-cohort `100,000`-sample dataset (`CUIMC`, p. 18 §5.1) requiring external multicenter datasets for generalizability (p. 18).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Directly relevant benchmark for `GatedLateFusion` vs Transformer ECG architectures (`P0011`) on EchoNext (`100K` 12-lead ECGs), showing how a TCN + TabMLP gated model (`AUC 0.8626`) edges out Light Transformer (`0.8539`) and Hybrid (`0.8619`) across 3 seeds (`PG1`, `PG2`). However, it illustrates critical pitfalls our FYDP explicitly solves (`PG2–PG5`): uses only 2 modalities (no Lab biomarkers or Lifestyle, `PG4`), lacks unimodal/ablation baselines, fails to verify calibration/DCA, exhibits severe tabular/echo-variable leakage ambiguity, and omits quantitative SHAP faithfulness (`AOPC`) and multi-seed SHAP stability (`PG3`, `PG5`).
