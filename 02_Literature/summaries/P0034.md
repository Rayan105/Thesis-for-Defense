## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country (Systematic Review Scope):** PRISMA 2020 systematic review (no primary patient recruitment and no statistical meta-analysis) led by researchers at Sultan Moulay Slimane University, Beni Mellal, Morocco (p. 1). Searched `PubMed`, `IEEE Xplore`, `Web of Science`, `Scopus`, `ACM Digital Library`, and `Google Scholar` for English peer-reviewed studies (`January 2018 – August 2025`, pp. 1, 3; note `Table A1` also incorporates `2026` additions added during peer review, pp. 10, 28, 30). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** — review explicitly omitted systematic extraction of cohort geography, race/ethnicity, and fairness/equity subgroup metrics across included studies (`pp. 7, 22`).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - **PRISMA Funnel (`§2.3` pp. 4–5, `Fig. 1` p. 5):** `1,250` records identified $\rightarrow$ `270` duplicates removed $\rightarrow$ `980` screened (title/abstract by `H.N.` and `R.A.`, tie-break `M.E.`) $\rightarrow$ `755` excluded $\rightarrow$ `225` full-text assessed (`Cohen's κ = 0.84`, p. 5) $\rightarrow$ `160` excluded (`75` not predictive studies, `55` no XAI or multimodal model, `30` insufficient data) $\rightarrow$ **`65` included studies** (`Table A1`, pp. 26–29).
  - **Cardiac vs Non-Cardiac Composition (`p. 10` §4, `Table 3` pp. 11–12, `p. 17` §4.4, `p. 21` §5.2):** Of the `65` included items, **`47` (`72.3%`)** predicted primary cardiovascular outcomes (only `20/47` met both CVD prediction and XAI/multimodal criteria, p. 10), while **`~18` (`~27.7%`)** targeted non-cardiac outcomes (stroke, stress, seizure, cognitive load, hydration, insomnia, diabetes, fall risk, driver state, user recognition, pp. 4, 11–12). Furthermore, `p. 17` notes `49` primary empirical studies after excluding `16` reviews, editorials, framework papers, and text/LLM-only items contained in `Table A1` (`pp. 26–29`). Patient sample sizes of individual studies are `not tabulated` in `Table A1` (except `[84]` `79,226` scanned ECG images, p. 28).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Target condition distribution across `65` studies (`Table 3`, p. 11): `Atrial Fibrillation (AF)` `~18/65 (~27.7%)`, `Arrhythmia` `~14/65 (~21.5%)`, `Other CVD` (`CAD, LVEF, LQTS, AV block, HTN`) `~15/65 (~23%)`, and `Non-cardiac` `~18/65 (~27.7%)`. Class-imbalance handling was `not reported` in **`50/65 (76.9%)`** studies (`8/65 [12.3%]` used oversampling/SMOTE, `3/65 [4.6%]` undersampling, `4/65 [6.2%]` class-weighted/cost-sensitive loss, `Table 3` p. 11, p. 14).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Prospective cardiovascular prediction/risk stratification (incident AF, low LVEF, CAD, SCD/arrhythmic death, mortality, LQTS/AV block) vs diagnostic/non-cardiac tasks (`pp. 4, 10–11, Table A1`); follow-up horizons across included studies are `not systematically tabulated`.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Synthesized (Review — No Primary Modeling):**
  - Across `65` studies (`Table 3` p. 11, `§4.2` p. 14): **`ECG-only` `47/65 (72.3%)`** vs **`Multi-modal` `18/65 (27.7%)`**: `ECG + biosignals (PPG/IMU)` `8`, `ECG + clinical/demographic` `4`, `ECG + EEG` `3`, `ECG + genomics` `1`, `ECG + imaging` `1`, `other combinations` `1` (note `Table A1` also lists `ECG + clinical + genomics [40]`, `ECG + imaging + clinical [34]`, `ECG + genomics + imaging [44]`, `ECG + EHR [35]`, pp. 27–29). **Lifestyle modality:** `none / not systematically extracted`.
- **ECG Format & Preprocessing Audit (`p. 17` §4.4):** No primary ECG modeling. Across `49` primary empirical studies, non-reporting rates were severe: sampling rate unreported in `42/49 (85.7%)`, band-pass filter in `38/49 (77.6%)`, denoising in `41/49 (83.7%)`, baseline drift correction in `44/49 (89.8%)`, and lead configuration in `35/49 (71.4%)`.
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Reviewed narratively as an integration hurdle (`§4.2`, p. 14), without quantitative extraction of missing-data/imputation methods across the 65 studies.
- **Model Families Synthesized (`Table 3` p. 11, `Fig. 4` p. 13, `p. 13`):** `CNN-based` `28/65 (43%)`, `CNN-LSTM hybrids` `10/65 (15%)`, `Classical ML (SVM, RF, SVR)` `10/65 (15%)`, `Transformer / Attention-based` `7/65 (11%)`, and `Other Architectures` (`RL, generative, miscellaneous`) `10/65 (16%)`; Deep Learning overall `~48/65 (~74%)`; `Federated / Privacy-preserving` `3/65 (4.6%)` (`[31, 35, 36]`, plus post-search `[49, 51]` noted on p. 17). Zero foundation/self-supervised primary ECG models were captured among the `65` studies (`p. 7`).
- **Fusion / Synthesis Strategy:** Qualitative structured synthesis across 5 dimensions (`Accuracy, Explainability, Robustness/Generalizability, Clinical Actionability, Multi-modal Integration`) plus a custom 4-level ordinal **Clinical Actionability Scoring Framework** (`Levels 0–3`, pp. 5–8). No quantitative meta-analysis performed.

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Review Quality Appraisal of Included Studies:** Applied an informal 4-criterion descriptive check (`dataset size/diversity`, `external validation`, `model/XAI clarity`, `clinical setting`, p. 6), with **zero formal Risk-of-Bias tool** (no PROBAST, PROBAST-AI, QUADAS-2, or QUAPAS) and **no per-study quality scores** in `Table A1` (`pp. 26–29`).
- **Review-Level Methodological Flaws Identified:**
  1. **Contradictory Eligibility & Composition (`pp. 4, 10–11, 17, 26–29`):** §2.3 (`p. 4`) explicitly excludes studies *"if they lacked an explainability component"* and excludes *"Review articles, editorials..."*, yet `40/65 (61.5%)` included studies implement **no XAI**, `16/65` are reviews/editorials/frameworks/text-only (`p. 17`), `~18/65` are non-cardiac (`p. 10`), and `Table A1` incorporates post-August-2025 `2026` papers (`[31, 37, 38]`, pp. 28, 30) outside the stated Jan 2018–Aug 2025 search window (`p. 3`).
  2. **Unregistered Protocol & English-Only Search:** No PROSPERO registration (`p. 3, 21`) and exclusion of non-English Asian AI-ECG venues (`p. 21`).
  3. **Level 3 Disaggregation Discrepancy (`p. 11 vs p. 18`):** `Table 3` classifies `[30]` (non-interventional multi-center validation) and `[34]` (prospective observational cohort) under `Level 3 (Demonstrated clinical impact)`, contradicting §2.6's requirement of prospective/interventional outcome impact (`pp. 7–8`), and `p. 18`'s prose disaggregation accounts for `5` (`[2, 9, 32, 33, 30]`) rather than `6` studies.
  4. **Absent Calibration, Fairness, Uncertainty, and Sample Sizes (`pp. 7, 22, 26–29`):** `Table A1` omits dataset names, sample sizes, and numeric AUCs for nearly all 65 entries.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **Not Applicable (PRISMA Systematic Review — High Selection & Synthesis Consistency Risk)**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Synthesis Findings (`Table 3` p. 11, `Fig. 3` p. 12, `§4.3` pp. 15–16):**
  - **`40/65 (61.5%)` implemented NO XAI whatsoever.**
  - Across the **`25` XAI studies (`38.5%`)**: `Attention mechanisms` `7` (`28%` of XAI / `10.8%` of all), `Saliency maps / Grad-CAM` `5` (`20%` / `7.7%`), `SHAP` `4` (`16%` / `6.2%`), `Gini index` `2` (`8%` / `3.1%`), `LIME` `2` (`8%` / `3.1%`), `Multi-layered XAI` `2` (`8%` / `3.1%`), `Other` `3` (`12%` / `4.5%` in Fig. 3).
  - **Key Faithfulness & Clinical Rigor Findings (`pp. 15–16`):**
    1. **`0` attention-based studies formally evaluated attention faithfulness** via perturbation or counterfactual tests (`p. 15`).
    2. **`0/65` studies implemented concept-based (CBM, TCAV) or causal explanation frameworks** (`p. 16`).
    3. Only **`2/25` XAI studies** reported any practicing clinician evaluation of plausibility/utility (`[37, 38]`, p. 16).
    4. Multimodal XAI is fragmented (`SHAP/importance` on EHR vs `saliency` on ECG separately, `p. 19`).
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **None (Systematic Review — No Primary Modeling / No Primary XAI Evaluation)** *(Refines `papers.csv` `No (qualitative/visual only)`: P0034 performs no primary model/XAI experiments, while quantitatively auditing XAI prevalence and actionability across 65 papers).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (`pp. 21–22`):** English-only search bias; reliance on heterogeneous internal reported AUCs; lack of PROSPERO pre-registration; cutoff at August 2025 missing emerging ECG foundation models; rampant primary-literature omission of Precision/Recall/F1 (`84.6%`) and class-imbalance handling (`76.9%`); inclusion of `~18` non-cardiac papers (`27.7%`); and non-extraction of calibration, fairness, and uncertainty metrics.
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Despite internal PRISMA-inclusion inconsistencies (reviews/editorials/non-cardiac/2026 additions mixed into `n=65`, no formal PROBAST appraisal), `P0034` provides high-value 2026 review evidence directly validating our FYDP thesis (`PG2–PG5`): proves `72.3%` of AI-ECG literature remains unimodal, `61.5%` uses zero XAI, **`0` attention studies test perturbation faithfulness** (`PG3`, `RQ3`), `87.7%` lack external validation, `84.6%` omit Precision/Recall/F1, multimodal XAI remains disjointed across modalities, and calibration/fairness/South Asian equity are systematically neglected (`PG1`, `PG5`). *(Note: `P0034` also cites `P0035` [`Taleban et al., ref. 50`] on pp. 29, 31).*
