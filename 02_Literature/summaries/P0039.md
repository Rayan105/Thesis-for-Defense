- **Missing-Modality & Misalignment Synthesis (`§5.1–5.2`, pp. 19–20, Fig. 14):**
  - Quantifies major deployment barriers across `52` studies (Fig. 14 p. 20): **Modality Misalignment `23%`**, **Missing Data `18%`**, **Limited Interpretability `15%`**, **External Validation `12%`**, and **Others (regulatory/privacy) `32%`**.
  - Catalogs practical missing-modality strategies (`p. 20`, `p. 23`): **modality dropout during training**, **late-fusion fallback architectures** permitting unimodal inference, **explicit missingness indicators**, **generative imputation (VAE/GAN)**, and **dynamic time warping / attention-based downweighting / self-supervised cross-modal pretraining** (p. 19).
- **Model Families & Fusion Strategies Synthesized (`§4.1`, p. 16, Fig. 11 p. 17):**
  - Across `52` studies: **Intermediate fusion `60%`** (predominantly CNN/GNN/Transformer encoders fused prior to prediction), **Early fusion `22%`**, **Late fusion `12%`**, **Hybrid fusion `6%`**. ## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Validation Rigor Across Corpus (`§4.5`, p. 18):** **`88%` internal validation only** (train-test splits / k-fold CV); only **`12%` (`6 / 52` studies)** conducted **true external validation** (defined in `§2.4` p. 5 as institutional, temporal, or acquisition-condition independence). Authors warn (`pp. 16, 19, 21–22`) that early/intermediate fusion gains observed internally often degrade externally (e.g., `McLean et al. [78/81]`).
- **Statistical Synthesis of Multimodal ROC-AUC Gain (`§2.7` pp. 6–7, `§4.4` pp. 17–18, Table 7 p. 18):**
  - Across **`28` comparative studies** (weighted by sample size, using strongest unimodal baseline for conservative $\Delta\text{AUC} = \text{AUC}_{\text{multimodal}} - \text{AUC}_{\text{unimodal}}$):
  - **Overall (`n = 28`):** Weighted mean **$\Delta\text{AUC} = +7.3\%$ (`+0.073` AUC points, `95% CI: +5.8% to +8.8%`)** (Table 7 p. 18; Abstract p. 1 states `5–12%`, p. 2 states `~7–8%`).
  - **Domain Breakdown (`Table 7`, p. 18):**
    - `Neurology` (`n = 8`): **`+9.5%`** (`95% CI: +7.0% to +12.0%`)
    - `Oncology` (`n = 10`): **`+8.9%`** (`95% CI: +7.1% to +10.7%`)
    - `Dermatology` (`n = 3`): **`+6.1%`** (`95% CI: +3.8% to +8.4%`)
    - `ICU / Critical Care` (`n = 2`): **`+5.5%`** (`95% CI: +1.2% to +9.8%`)
    - **`Cardiology` (`n = 5`): `+4.2%` (`95% CI: +2.5% to +5.9%`)** *(smallest pooled multimodal AUC gain across all domains)*.
  - *(Note: Table 6 p. 18 reports relative percentage gains `(MM-UM)/UM` mixing AUC and Accuracy, whereas Table 7 reports absolute AUC point gains.)*
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported` or synthesized across studies; only listed as a future recommendation on p. 23).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`; recommended as future work on p. 23).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** Reports sample-size weighted normal-approximation `95% CIs` (`Table 7`, p. 18), but **zero heterogeneity tests ($I^2, Q$), zero publication bias tests (Egger/funnel plot), zero forest plots, and zero study-level effect tables** for the `28` pooled studies.

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Heterogeneity of metrics/endpoints, inconsistent CI/uncertainty reporting limiting synthesis, potential English/peer-reviewed publication bias, lack of standardized reporting checklists, and over-reliance on internal validation (`88%`) with sparse external validation (`12%`) (pp. 22–23).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Useful **conceptual framing survey** (`ACME`, 2026) directly validating `PG1–PG5`: documents intermediate fusion (`60%`) dominance, modest cardiology multimodal gain (`+4.2%` AUC, lowest across domains, Table 7 p. 18), and the exact translational gaps our FYDP targets — **modality misalignment (`23%`)**, **missing modalities (`18%`, cataloging modality dropout & late-fusion fallback)**, **external validation deficit (`12%`)**, **limited interpretability (`15%`)**, and **absent calibration/DCA/clinician validation**. However, due to **severe editorial/citation/counting defects** (missing Cohen's $\kappa$, mismatched Table 3/4 reference numbers, duplicated Table 4/5, opaque 28-study pooling), its quantitative percentages (`60%`, `12%`, `+7.3%` $\Delta$AUC) must be cited cautiously as approximate survey benchmarks rather than exact meta-analytic ground truth. *(Metadata note vs `papers.csv`: `Access` is marked `none`, whereas p. 23 explicitly specifies `Open Access CC-BY 4.0`.)*
