## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Community-based prospective cohort across four US centers (`ARIC` — Atherosclerosis Risk in Communities Study: Forsyth County, NC; Jackson, MS; Minneapolis suburbs, MN; Washington County, MD, `p. 2`).
  - **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **None (`0%`) — Explicitly Excluded.** Methods (`p. 2–3`) explicitly state: *"We excluded participants... who did not identify as either Black or White, and Black participants from Minneapolis or Washington County due to low participant numbers."* `Table 1 (p. 5)` confirms `100%` Black/White enrollment (`80.18%` White / `19.82%` Black at Visit 3; `82.44%` White / `17.56%` Black at Visit 5).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - *Visit 3 (1993–95, Incident AF):* Complete 4-modality intersection **`n = 8,374`** (`7,537` combined training + `837` holdout test across 10 replicates, `p. 1, p. 3–4 Fig. 1A/C, p. 5 Table 1`). Unimodal source pools (`p. 3 Fig. 1A, p. 4 Fig. 1C`): CRS `12,601` (`11,764` train), Prot `11,207` (`10,370` train), ECG `11,561` (`10,724` train), PRS `10,170` (`9,333` train). Mean age `59.98 ± 5.67` yrs; `4,541 (54.23%)` female (`p. 5 Table 1`).
  - *Visit 5 (2011–13, Prevalent/Covert AF):* Complete 4-modality intersection **`n = 3,730`** (`3,357` combined training + `373` holdout test across 10 replicates, `p. 1, p. 3–4 Fig. 1A/C, p. 5 Table 1`). Unimodal source pools (`p. 3 Fig. 1A, p. 4 Fig. 1C`): CRS `6,298` (`5,925` train), Prot `4,997` (`4,624` train), ECG `5,661` (`5,288` train), PRS `5,089` (`4,716` train). Mean age `75.45 ± 5.05` yrs; `2,141 (57.40%)` female (`p. 5 Table 1`).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:**
  - *Visit 3 Incident AF (`median follow-up 15.1 years` through 2019; `12.6 per 1,000 person-years`, `pp. 1, 6`):* **`1,910` events (`22.81%`)** among `8,374` (`230` Black [`2.75%` of cohort], `1,680` White [`20.06%` of cohort]; `<5 yr`: `23 + 207 = 230` events; `>5 yr`: `207 + 1,473 = 1,680` events, `p. 5 Table 1`; `22.8%` mean test prevalence across 10 replicates, `p. 7`).
  - *Visit 5 Prevalent/Covert AF (AF diagnosis on/before Visit 5 without AF rhythm on Visit 5 ECG, `pp. 1, 4`):* **`229` events (`6.14%`)** among `3,730` (`18` Black [`0.48%`], `211` White [`5.66%`], `p. 5 Table 1`; `5.9%` mean test prevalence across 10 replicates, `p. 8`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Multi-source adjudicated Atrial Fibrillation (`AF` — study ECGs visually confirmed by cardiologist, hospital discharge records, death certificates, `p. 4`): (1) **Incident AF** over median `15.1` years (up to ~`25` years, 1993–2019; plus `<5 yr` and `>5 yr` sensitivity splits, `pp. 4, 7, 10`), and (2) **Prevalent (covert) AF** during sinus/non-AF rhythm at Visit 5 (`pp. 1, 4`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (All 4 target domains + Genomics & Proteomics):**
  - **Clinical/EHR + Lifestyle (`CRS`):** `11` CHARGE-AF clinical variables — age, race, height, weight, SBP, DBP, **current smoking (`lifestyle`)**, antihypertensive medication use, prevalent diabetes, prevalent MI, prevalent HF (`pp. 3–4`). Diet, physical activity, and alcohol are not included.
  - **12-lead ECG (`ECG`):** `10-second` resting sinus-rhythm 12-lead ECG (MAC1200 PC; AF rhythm on index ECG excluded, `pp. 2, 4`).
  - **Laboratory/Biomarkers (`Prot`):** `~5,000` plasma proteins quantified via Somalogic 5K aptamer-based proteomics platform (`RFU`, `pp. 4–5`). *(Note: fasting glucose, creatinine, and cystatin C eGFR appear in baseline Table 1 / diabetes definitions on `pp. 4–5` rather than as separate CRS features).*
  - **Genomics (`PRS`):** Pre-weighted polygenic risk score (`1,091,491` SNPs via PRS-CS from external 6-cohort AF GWAS `>1M` individuals; TOPMed freeze 8 imputed Affymetrix 6.0 genotypes, `pp. 4–5`).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `10 s` at `250 Hz` (`2,500 × 12`, Visit 3) and `500 Hz` (`5,000 × 12`, Visit 5), transformed to **`(4096, 12)`** for compatibility with a contrastive-learning pretrained 1D residual CNN (`p. 4–5`).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Combined models used complete 4-modality intersections (`8,374` Visit 3; `3,730` Visit 5, `p. 2, 6`); a sensitivity analysis in replicate 1 using `k = 10` covariate-nearest-neighbor multiple imputation (`5×`) for missing ECG/proteomics (`8%–20%` missingness) showed no significant AUC difference by DeLong test (`p. 10`, unbundled `Table S8`).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - *Clinical:* Original Cox `CHARGE-AF` score vs refitted ARIC logistic `CRS` (`11` raw clinical variables directly outperformed scalar CRS in combined models, `p. 3 Fig. 1B`, `p. 4–5`).
  - *Genomics:* Fixed external `PRS` (`p. 5`).
  - *ECG:* Residual 1D CNN (`4` residual conv blocks + GAP + 2 FC layers) initialized with contrastive weights pretrained on `3.2 million` ECGs (`Diamant et al. [31]`), `90:10` train/holdout split with early stopping patience `3` (`p. 5`).
  - *Proteomics:* L1-regularized (Lasso) logistic regression on `~5,000` proteins (`5-fold CV` over `10` penalty candidates, `pp. 5–6`).
  - *Exhaustive Benchmarking:* All `15` modality combinations (`1, 2, 3, 4` sources) plus original `CHARGE-AF` evaluated head-to-head (`Tables 2–3, pp. 6, 8`).
- **Fusion Strategy:** **Late / score-level fusion** via standardized **L2-regularized (Ridge) logistic regression** (`5-fold CV` over `10` penalty candidates) combining the `11` raw CHARGE-AF variables directly with the scalar `PRS`, `ECG` risk score, and `Prot` risk score (`p. 3 Fig. 1B, p. 6`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** `10` independent random replicates of **`90%` derivation / `10%` holdout test** (`837` Visit 3; `373` Visit 5, `p. 4 Fig. 1C, p. 6`); unimodal `CRS`/`ECG`/`Prot` models trained on all available non-test participants within each replicate (`p. 4 Fig. 1C`).
- **Incident AF Results at Visit 3 (`p. 6 Table 2, p. 7`):**
  - `CHARGE-AF` baseline: AUC `0.660 (95% CI: 0.648, 0.673)`
  - Unimodal models: `CRS` `0.668 (0.657, 0.679)`, `PRS` `0.609 (0.600, 0.617)`, `ECG` `0.639 (0.617, 0.661)`, `Prot` `0.645 (0.637, 0.653)`
  - Best 2-source (`CRS + PRS`): AUC **`0.752 (0.741, 0.763)`**, PR-AUC `0.518 (0.513, 0.524)` (`p. 7`)
  - Best 3-source (`CRS + PRS + ECG`): AUC **`0.761 (0.751, 0.771)`**, PR-AUC `0.525 (0.520, 0.531)` (`+0.009` over `CRS+PRS`, $P=0.001$ paired t-test, $P<0.001$ DeLong, `p. 7`)
  - Full 4-source (`CRS + PRS + ECG + Prot`): AUC **`0.763 (0.753, 0.772)`** ($\Delta = +0.102$ over CHARGE-AF; `+0.002` vs `CRS+PRS+ECG`, **not significant** by one-sided paired t-test or DeLong test, `p. 6 Table 2 footnotes a,b, p. 7`).
- **Prevalent / Covert AF Results at Visit 5 (`p. 8 Table 3`):**
  - `CHARGE-AF` baseline: AUC `0.737 (95% CI: 0.685, 0.789)`
  - Unimodal models: `CRS` `0.742 (0.695, 0.789)`, `PRS` `0.701 (0.682, 0.720)`, `ECG` `0.671 (0.628, 0.713)`, `Prot` `0.717 (0.681, 0.753)`
  - Best 2-source (`CRS + PRS`): AUC **`0.854 (0.828, 0.880)`**, PR-AUC `0.296 (0.277, 0.315)` (`p. 8`)
  - `CRS + PRS + ECG`: AUC `0.855 (0.828, 0.882)`
  - Best 3-source (`CRS + PRS + Prot`): AUC **`0.875 (0.851, 0.898)`** (`Table 3, p. 8`; note text on `p. 8` erroneously prints CI `(0.751–0.771)` copied from Visit 3), PR-AUC **`0.438 (0.416, 0.459)`** ($P=0.005$ paired t-test, $P<0.001$ DeLong vs `CRS+PRS`, `p. 8`)
  - Full 4-source (`CRS + PRS + ECG + Prot`): AUC **`0.875 (0.851, 0.899)`** ($\Delta = +0.138$ over CHARGE-AF, `p. 8 Table 3`).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** **Yes (Hosmer–Lemeshow test only, `pp. 6–8`)** — Visit 3 HL pass counts across 10 replicates: `8/10` (`CRS + PRS`) and `10/10` (`CRS + PRS + ECG`, `p. 7`); Visit 5 HL pass counts: `10/10` (`CRS + PRS`) and `9/10` (`CRS + PRS + ECG`, `p. 8`). Calibration curves, calibration slope/intercept, Brier score, and ECE are `not reported`.
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** **Yes** — one-sided paired t-tests across 10 replicates and one-sided DeLong tests on concatenated 10-replicate test predictions (`pp. 6–8, Table 2`).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Prospective population-based `ARIC` cohort (`8,374` Visit 3; `3,730` Visit 5) with transparent inclusion/exclusion (`pp. 2–4`), complete-case vs kNN sensitivity imputation (`p. 10`), and participant-level `90:10` holdout splits (`p. 4, 6`); downgraded from purely Low due to exclusion of non-Black/non-White ethnicities and Black participants at 2 centers (`p. 2`) and absence of external cohort validation (`p. 10`).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW RISK:** All clinical covariates, genotypes, sinus-rhythm 12-lead ECGs, and plasma proteomics were acquired at Visit 3/5 baseline prior to incident AF follow-up (`pp. 2, 4`), and Ridge standardization + CV tuning occurred strictly within derivation splits (`p. 6`).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Rigorous multi-source AF adjudication (`p. 4`), though `345` censored non-AF participants within 5 years at Visit 3 were treated as binary controls in logistic regression rather than modeled via Cox survival censoring (`p. 10`), and Visit 5 prevalent AF includes resolved paroxysmal cases (`p. 10`).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Very high statistical discipline (`10` repeated random splits, `95% CIs`, `PR-AUC`, `Hosmer–Lemeshow` goodness-of-fit, `DeLong` & paired t-tests, race-stratified and `<5/>5 yr` sensitivity checks, `pp. 6–8`); residual concerns are lack of external validation (`p. 10`), binary logistic simplification of `15.1–25`-year censored survival data (`p. 10`), HL-only calibration without plots/Brier/DCA, and pooling 10 correlated replicates in concatenated DeLong tests (`p. 6`).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **Unclear Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (`p. 10`):** Moderate sample size compared to large ECG-only cohorts (`Attia et al. 2019`); Visit 5 prevalent AF may include prior symptomatic AF; logistic regression treats `345` censored individuals as controls; Cox survival modeling not applied to neural ECG branch; European-ancestry GWAS weights underperformed in Black participants (`Table S3`); no external validation cohort.
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:**
  - Gold-standard four-source multimodal comparative benchmark (`Clinical+Smoking + PRS + 12-lead ECG + 5K Proteomics`) proving rigorous statistical comparisons (`DeLong`, `PR-AUC`, `HL calibration`) and revealing modality synergy vs redundancy (`CRS+PRS` captures most parsimonious gain, while `ECG` significantly boosts incident AF and `Proteomics` doubles prevalent AF PR-AUC `0.296 -> 0.438`). Directly motivates our FYDP design (`PG1–PG5`) by showing: (1) late scalar score fusion leaves cross-modal ECG-clinical-biomarker interactions unexplored (`PG5`), (2) lifestyle is restricted to binary smoking (`PG4`), (3) South Asian/Bangladeshi populations are completely excluded (`PG4`), and (4) zero explainability (`PG3`) or DCA (`RQ4`) is provided.
  - **Discrepancies vs `papers.csv`:** None substantive — `papers.csv` accurately records `DOI` (`10.1093/ehjdh/ztae081`), `Year` (`2025`), `Modalities` (`Clinical/EHR; Genomics; 12-lead ECG; Laboratory/Biomarkers`), `XAIEvaluated` (`No`), and `CitationKey` (`Yao2025_P0025`). Our summary newly captures exact cohort counts (`8,374` Visit 3 / `3,730` Visit 5 + broader pools), smoking as lifestyle in CRS, explicit non-Black/non-White exclusion (`0%` South Asian), HL calibration counts, and the `p. 8` text CI typo `(0.751–0.771)` vs `Table 3` `(0.851, 0.898)`.
