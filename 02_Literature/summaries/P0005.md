# P0005 — A deep learning-based electrocardiogram risk score for long term cardiovascular death and disease

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Multi-center retrospective EHR and resting ECG cohorts across `3` US academic health systems: `Stanford University Medical Center` (CA; `STARR-OMOP` / `Philips TraceMaster`, `March 2008–May 2018`), `Cedars-Sinai Medical Center` (CA; `GE MUSE` / `EPIC Clarity`), and `Columbia University Irving Medical Center` (NY; `GE MUSE` / `OMOP`) (pp. 1–2, 7). South Asian / Bangladeshi representation: **not explicitly reported** (`Asian Patients, N=3,091` shown in Stanford PCE comparison set `Fig. 3E`, p. 5, alongside Non-Hispanic White `10,515`, Hispanic `1,609`, Black `668`, Other/unknown `2,487`; Bangladeshi or disaggregated South Asian counts `not reported`, demographic tables deferred to Supplementary Tables 1–3 not in PDF).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - Stanford development extraction (adults $\ge 18$ yrs): `910,966` ECGs from `307,557` patients (`8:1:1` patient-level split, p. 7).
  - Training / Validation ($\ge 5$ yrs follow-up or CV death within 5 yrs): `311,334` training ECGs and `38,970` validation ECGs (p. 7 Methods; note p. 2 Results cites `312,422` ECGs); `244,839` patients in 8-fold cross-validation (`CV`) set (first ECG per patient, p. 7).
  - Three Held-Out Test Sets (first resting ECG per patient; total **`536,449` patients**, pp. 2, 7): `Stanford` (`n=31,899`), `Cedars-Sinai` (`n=46,095`), and `Columbia` (`n=458,455`).
  - Two PCE Comparison Subsets (screening-eligible non-inpatients without prior CVD/diabetes, `LDL < 190 mg/dL`, BP within 1 year; pp. 2, 7): `Stanford PCE` (`n=18,370` from CV set) and `Cedars-Sinai PCE` (`n=4,065` from Cedars-Sinai test set).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Exact raw event counts (`n`) and prevalences in the test sets are **`not reported`** in the main PDF (deferred to external Supplementary Tables 1–3; top-tertile 5-yr CV mortality PPVs in Table 1 p. 2 are `6.7%` Stanford, `16.2%` Cedars-Sinai, `15.6%` Columbia, and overall 10-yr Kaplan–Meier ASCVD incidence in Stanford PCE set is `9.30%`, `Fig. 2` p. 4).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Primary training target is **5-year cardiovascular mortality** (death in EHR [+ Social Security Death Index at Stanford] within `30 days` of MI, ischemic stroke, intracranial hemorrhage, sudden cardiac death, or HF hospitalization, pp. 2, 7). Downstream endpoints include **5-year and 10-year hard ASCVD** (MI, ischemic stroke, intracranial hemorrhage, sudden cardiac death) plus incident HF, AF, heart block, cardiomyopathy, aortic stenosis, and pulmonary/peripheral vascular disease (pp. 2–3, 7; OMOP concept codes deferred to Supplementary Table 8).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included:**
  - **SEER core model is UNIMODAL (`12-lead ECG` or `Single-lead Lead I ECG` waveforms alone, pp. 1, 8).**
  - **Score-level multimodal composite (`SEER + PCE`):** Evaluated on PCE comparison sets (`Stanford n=18,370`, `Cedars-Sinai n=4,065`) by combining SEER (`ECG`) with the **Pooled Cohort Equations (`PCE`)**, which use `Clinical/EHR` (age, sex, race, systolic BP, antihypertensive treatment, diabetes, smoking flag) and `Laboratory/Biomarkers` (total & HDL cholesterol, `LDL < 190 mg/dL` inclusion criterion; pp. 3–4, 7).
  - **Excluded target modality:** Dedicated `Lifestyle` modality (diet, physical activity, smoking pack-years) `none / not reported` (only binary prior OMOP smoking label inside PCE, p. 7).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** Resting `10 s`, `12-lead` ECG waveforms sampled at `500 Hz`, bandpass & wandering-baseline filtered, normalized per-lead, and downsampled to `250 Hz` (`12 × 2,500`, pp. 7–8). Single-lead ablation uses `Lead I` only with `1×1` convolutions replacing `1×12` convolutions (p. 8).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Low-quality ECGs excluded; absent prior OMOP labels for smoking, diabetes, or antihypertensive medications defaulted to `false` (p. 7); subjects without BP or LDL measurements excluded from PCE comparison sets (pp. 2, 7).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - `SEER`: Ensemble of `9` CNNs (`1` original model trained on train set + `8` cross-validation models across train+val folds; PyTorch `1.11`, Adam `lr=1e-3 -> 1e-6`, batch size `128`, weight decay `1e-4`; layer architecture in Supplementary Fig. 6, pp. 7–8).
  - Baselines: `PCE`, `XGBoost 1.5` random forests trained on `38` standard ECG markers (`AUC 0.68 [0.65–0.70]`, p. 4) or `11` common Philips Tracemaster parameters (`AUC 0.71 [0.66–0.75]`, pp. 5–6), and task-specific ECG CNNs (p. 2).
- **Fusion Strategy:** No joint/intermediate or attention-based deep fusion; `Composite` score is a **post-hoc linear late/score-level weighted average** (`16 parts unnormalized SEER + 1 part unnormalized PCE`, p. 4).

- **Data Split:** Patient-level `8:1:1` split at Stanford (all ECGs per patient in training; strictly first ECG per patient in validation/CV/test evaluation), plus `2` independent external hospital test cohorts (`Cedars-Sinai`, `Columbia`; pp. 2, 7).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Large multi-center clinical cohorts (`536,449` held-out test patients across `3` health systems and `2` ECG vendors) with strict patient-level splitting, first ECG per patient in evaluation, and race/ethnicity/sex subgroup validation (`Fig. 3`, p. 5). However, clinical ECG cohorts incur ascertainment bias (acknowledged on p. 5 for younger adults), and baseline demographic/event tables reside in external Supplementary Tables 1–3 (`not in PDF`).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Standardized `10 s` resting ECG preprocessing; clinical PCE variables constrained strictly to `≤ 1 year` prior to ECG (p. 7). Defaulting unrecorded OMOP comorbidity labels to `false` (p. 7) risks misclassification, and external ECGs used site-specific normalization parameters at Cedars-Sinai (p. 8).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Standardized OMOP mortality/ASCVD definitions (p. 7), but Stanford mortality was supplemented with Social Security Death Index (`SSDI`) whereas Cedars-Sinai (`EPIC Clarity`) and Columbia lacked SSDI linkage (`p. 7`), likely explaining differences in PPV (`6.7%` vs `15.6%–16.2%`) and sensitivity (`51.9%` at Cedars-Sinai, Table 1 p. 2).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — UNCLEAR / HIGH RISK:** Exemplary zero-leakage patient-level split, censoring-aware `Harrell's C-statistic` and `Kaplan–Meier` incidence estimates, pre-specified Stanford CV tertile cutoffs (`1.1%`, `3.9%`), and `95%` bootstrap CIs (`pp. 7–8`). However, **formal probability calibration (`Brier/ECE/calibration slope`) is absent**, `<5 yr` censored records were excluded during binary CNN training (`311,334` of `910,966` ECGs), `Stanford PCE` evaluation relied on `8-fold CV` of training patients rather than the held-out test set, empirical `16:1` composite weights lacked formal calibration or DeLong testing, and minor numeric discrepancies appear (`312,422` vs `311,334` trained ECGs; text vs Table 1 rounded Harrell C and Lead I AUCs).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **Unclear** *(Distinguished from `P0001–P0004` by strict patient-level splitting, zero data leakage, and `N=504,550` external validation across 2 independent centers, while remaining `Unclear`—or `High` under strict calibration-required scoring—because formal calibration metrics, raw event counts, baseline tables, and CNN layer details reside outside the PDF or are omitted.)*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Retrospective observational design requiring prospective clinical trials; single-center training population risk of demographic bias; hospital single-lead ECGs do not directly validate smartwatch ECGs; insufficient 10-year follow-up to compute 10-year AUCs directly (p. 7).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Gold-standard benchmark for multi-center external validation scale (`N=536,449` held-out test patients across 3 hospitals) and clinical reclassification (`NRI 17.8%` over PCE), illustrating the prognostic richness of raw 12-lead ECGs (`PG1`). Simultaneously reinforces our FYDP thesis (`PG2–PG5`): `SEER` itself is strictly unimodal (`ECG-only`), fuses with `PCE` only via an uncalibrated `16:1` linear score average rather than cross-modal Transformer attention over `ECG + Clinical + Lab + Lifestyle`, omits `Lifestyle` beyond binary smoking, lacks `calibration` (`Brier/ECE`) and `DCA`, and provides **zero explainability (`None`)** for its CNN waveform decisions.
