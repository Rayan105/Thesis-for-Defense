## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Synthesis Findings (`pp. 2, 11–18`, `Table 4` p. 31):**
  - Demonstrates that **structured-data perturbation explainers (`LIME`, `SHAP`) treat ECG time points independently and break under ECG temporal dependence and noise** (`pp. 2, 9–10, 15`), while gradient methods risk highlighting overfitting/spurious artifacts (`pp. 11, 16`).
  - Finds ECG XAI evaluation is **"fragmented and lacks standardized benchmarks"**: localization scores, pointing games, degradation curves, and human-in-the-loop concordance lack consensus definitions (`pp. 16–18`), few studies incorporate direct clinician feedback or handle multi-condition confounding (`p. 17`), and emerging counterfactual (`VCCE`) and fiducial-aligned (`glocal`) frameworks remain under-validated (`pp. 15–18`).
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **None (Systematic Review — No Primary Modeling / No Primary Faithfulness Experiments)** *(Refines `papers.csv` `No (qualitative/visual only)`: P0035 is a qualitative taxonomic review with zero primary XAI experiments and no extracted quantitative benchmark table).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (Field-Wide, `§4.2` p. 17):** Predominance of single-center datasets limiting demographic/hardware generalizability; absence of consensus definitions for localization scores, pointing games, and clinician concordance; sparse handling of multi-condition overlapping pathologies; and lack of open cardiologist-annotated ECG XAI benchmarks and process-oriented explanations.
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Useful conceptual taxonomy of 5 ECG XAI families (`Perturbation, Backpropagation, Intrinsic, Sequence-aware, Counterfactual`) highlighting why naive time-series perturbation breaks temporal coherence and why faithfulness + stability + clinician plausibility must be rigorously standardized (`PG3`, `RQ3`). However, `P0035` lacks a master study table, quantitative metric synthesis, multimodal fusion synthesis (`ECG + Clinical + Lab + Lifestyle`), and formal RoB auditing—leaving our FYDP's empirical evaluation of **multimodal SHAP AOPC deletion/insertion faithfulness, multi-seed stability, and cross-modal Transformer attention** (`PG2–PG5`) completely distinct and experimentally grounded.
