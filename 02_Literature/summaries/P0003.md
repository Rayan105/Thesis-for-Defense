# P0003 — Multi-modal fusion model for predicting adverse cardiovascular outcome post percutaneous coronary intervention

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Bhattacharya2022_P0003`
- **DOI:** `10.1088/1361-6579/ac9e8a` (art. p. 1 [PDF p. 2])
- **PubMed ID (PMID):** `not reported`
- **Authors:** Amartya Bhattacharya, Sudarsan Sadasivuni, Chieh-Ju Chao, Pradyumna Agasthi, Chadi Ayoub, David R Holmes, Reza Arsanjani, Arindam Sanyal, Imon Banerjee (art. p. 1 [PDF p. 2])
- **Venue & Year:** *Physiological Measurement*, `43 (2022) 124004`, published `22 December 2022` (received 9 September 2022, revised 18 October 2022, accepted 28 October 2022; art. p. 1 [PDF p. 2])
- **Reading Status:** Full-Text Verified (`PDF pp. 1–12`, article `pp. 1–11`)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Multi-site retrospective institutional registry cohort across `4` Mayo Clinic Health Systems hospitals in the USA (`La Crosse, WI; Mankato, MN; Rochester, MN; Scottsdale, AZ`), `January 2006–December 2018` (art. p. 2 §2.1 [PDF p. 3]). South Asian / Bangladeshi representation: **none / not reported** (race/ethnicity distribution `not reported`).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** `17,356` unique post-PCI patients (`mean age 67.2 ± 12.6 years`, `9,163 [52.7%] male`), retained from `21,872` initially identified PCI patients after excluding `4,516` lost to follow-up (art. p. 1 Abstract, art. p. 2 §2.1 [PDF pp. 2–3]). **Internal reporting discrepancy:** `Table 1` (art. p. 3 [PDF p. 4]) sums across Train (`6,871`: `4,832` male + `2,039` female) and Test (`6,193`: `4,331` male + `1,862` female) to only `13,064` total (`9,163` male = `70.13%`), leaving `4,292` patients unaccounted for in `Table 1`.
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Exact event counts (`n`) and prevalence percentages for the 3 clinical endpoints are **`not reported`** in the PDF (Methods states qualitatively that negative samples *"were present in huge proportions concerning the positive samples"*, art. p. 5 §2.5 [PDF p. 6]).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Three binary endpoints at a **6-month horizon** post-index PCI (`art. p. 2 §2.2 [PDF p. 3]`):
  1. Heart failure hospitalization (`HFH`)
  2. Ischemic stroke
  3. All-cause mortality (supplemented via Social Security Death Index, art. p. 9 [PDF p. 10])
  - Ascertained using `ICD-9/10` and `CPT` codes (exact code list `not reported`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (2 of 4 target modalities):**
  - `12-lead ECG` (pre-PCI standard `10 s` clinical strip images digitized into a `6`-channel array, art. pp. 3–4 §2.3–2.4 [PDF pp. 4–5]).
  - `Clinical/EHR` (`60` physician-curated baseline variables $\rightarrow$ `157` one-hot encoded features covering demographics, past medical history [MI, CABG, CKD, tumors], procedural variables, inpatient/outpatient medications, and binary smoker flag [`19.35%` in Table 1]; art. p. 2 §2.2, art. p. 3 Table 1 [PDF pp. 3–4]).
  - **Excluded / Ambiguous target modalities:** **Lifestyle** beyond binary smoker flag (`diet, physical activity, pack-years`): `none / not reported`. **Laboratory/Biomarkers & Vitals:** §2.2 (art. p. 2) lists only demographics, history, procedures, and medications (no lab/vital variables), yet `Table 3` (art. p. 10 [PDF p. 11]) marks `Vitals = Y` and `Lab = Y` for *"This work"* (Supplemental Table 1 not included in PDF).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** Standard `10 s` 12-lead ECG paper/grid strip images recorded in clinic before PCI (`top 3 rows`: `2.5 s` slices of I, II, III, aVR, aVF, aVL, V1–V6; `bottom 3 rows`: `10 s` rhythm strips), digitized via custom Python module into a `[1691, 6]` 6-channel time-series array (`<1%` data loss on 100 manually checked samples; art. pp. 3–4 §2.3 & Fig. 1–2 [PDF pp. 4–5]).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** `<5%` missing data in clinical variables coded as `-1` prior to categorization and one-hot encoding (art. p. 2 §2.2 [PDF p. 3]). No missing-ECG or missing-modality inference strategy is reported.
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - **Baseline 1 (`ECG only - time domain`):** Signal normalized `[-1, 1]`, median-subtracted baseline removal, `14` first-order R-R peak time-domain features (30% peak threshold) fed into 6 channel-wise 2-layer ANNs (`10` hidden tanh neurons + softmax) and combined via an ANN meta-classifier (art. p. 5 §2.5.1 & Fig. 3 [PDF p. 6]). **Critical omission:** No unimodal raw-waveform `1D-CNN ECG-only` baseline was evaluated.
  - **Baseline 2 (`EMR only`):** Stacked Dense (`157 -> Dropout 0.25 -> 157 -> Dropout 0.25 -> 128 -> Dropout -> Sigmoid`, art. p. 6 §2.5.2 [PDF p. 7]; `Fig. 2` depicts `157 -> 128`, art. p. 4 [PDF p. 5]).
  - **Proposed Joint Fusion:** Two-branch end-to-end DNN trained with focal loss (`Adam lr=1e-4`, early stopping patience `10`, art. pp. 4–5 [PDF pp. 5–6]).
- **Fusion Strategy:** End-to-end **Joint (Intermediate) Fusion**: ECG branch (`5` stacked `Conv1D+BatchNorm+ReLU` blocks $\rightarrow$ `MaxPooling1D` $\rightarrow$ `Dense(256)+Dropout(0.25)+BatchNorm` $\rightarrow$ `Dense(16)+Dropout(0.25)`) concatenated with EMR branch (`Dense(157)+Dropout -> Dense(128)+Dropout`), passed through `Dense(256) -> Dropout -> Dense(1, Sigmoid)` (art. p. 4 §2.4 & Fig. 2 [PDF p. 5]).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — UNCLEAR / HIGH RISK:** Excluded `4,516` (`20.6%`) of `21,872` PCI patients lost to follow-up (attrition/selection bias, art. p. 2 §2.1 [PDF p. 3]); `Table 1` accounts for only `13,064` of `17,356` patients (`4,292` unaccounted, art. p. 3 [PDF p. 4]); race/ethnicity unreported; no external validation outside Mayo Clinic.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH RISK:** Digitizing 6-row ECG strips collapses four `2.5 s` leads per row into single continuous channels in rows 1–3; `-1` missing-value coding and one-hot encoding lack documented train-only isolation; §2.2 omits mention of labs/vitals while `Table 3` ticks `Lab=Y` and `Vitals=Y`; `Fig. 5a` includes post-PCI discharge medications (`Clopidogrel use at discharge`, `Ticlopidine use at discharge`, PDF p. 9).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Exact ICD/CPT criteria for HFH and stroke are `not reported` in PDF; all-cause mortality substituted for cardiovascular mortality (art. p. 9 [PDF p. 10]); event counts and prevalence are `not reported`.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Test-set threshold tuning:** Operating points were selected *"individually ... from the ROCs based on the optimal tradeoff between true positive (high) and false positive (low) rate"* (`art. p. 6 §3.1 [PDF p. 7]`).
  - **Missing metrics & baselines:** Raw event counts, AUROC `95% CIs`, probability calibration (`Brier/ECE/plot`), DCA, and a raw-waveform `1D-CNN ECG-only` baseline are all absent.
  - **Severe metric anomalies:** `Table 2` reports Precision `0.94–0.99` alongside Recall `0.77–0.84` despite severe class imbalance and moderate AUROCs (`0.786–0.897`), mathematically inconsistent with positive-class precision under low prevalence (suggesting negative/majority-class or weighted metrics); `Fig. 6(b)` shows ablating ECG Channels 1–2 yields higher Mortality AUROC (`0.902`, `0.901`) than the unablated Fusion model (`0.897`); `Table 1` vs §2.5 split discrepancy (`52.6%/47.4%` vs `60:20:20`).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:**
  - `LIME` applied **strictly to the unimodal `EMR-only` baseline** (`Fig. 5a`, art. p. 6 §3.2, art. p. 8 [PDF pp. 7, 9]), identifying `prior/postprocedural stroke` (`0.142`) as top predictor.
  - **Multimodal fusion XAI explicitly omitted:** Authors state *"Given the complex multimodal and multidimensional (1D temporal data and tabular data) nature of the study, none of the standard XAI approaches (such as LIME, SHAP) can be used for model interpretation"* (art. p. 10 Limitations [PDF p. 11]).
  - Instead, they conducted a coarse **feature/channel randomization sensitivity ablation** (`Fig. 5b–c`, `Fig. 6`, art. pp. 6, 8–9 [PDF pp. 7, 9–10]), randomizing either the single EMR-LIME top feature or one of the 6 ECG channels (`Channel 4` randomization caused the largest drop: HF `0.863 -> 0.752`, Mortality `0.897 -> 0.852`, Stroke `0.786 -> 0.701`).
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **Qualitative / Visual / Built-in Importance Only** *(Corrects preliminary `papers.csv` tag `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)` which is false upon full-text inspection: LIME was applied ONLY to the EMR-only baseline branch, zero attribution method was applied to the multimodal fusion model, and coarse 1-variable/1-channel ablation is not attribution faithfulness [AOPC deletion/insertion], multi-seed stability, or clinician plausibility evaluation).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Used all-cause mortality rather than cardiovascular mortality (SSDI reliance); lacked coronary anatomical `SYNTAX` score; unable to apply standard XAI (`LIME/SHAP`) to multimodal 1D+tabular fusion (art. pp. 9–10 [PDF pp. 10–11]).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Early landmark proof-of-concept showing raw 12-lead ECG + EHR joint fusion beats EHR-only for cardiac-specific post-PCI outcomes (`HFH +0.10`, `Stroke +0.11` AUROC, DeLong $p < 0.05$), while directly motivating our FYDP's core advances (`PG2–PG5`): incorporates only 2 modalities (no dedicated Lab panel or Lifestyle), uses CNN+MLP concatenation rather than Transformer cross-modal attention, explicitly abandons multimodal XAI (`LIME/SHAP`), omits calibration/DCA/raw-ECG CNN baseline, and carries High PROBAST risk.
