## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Retrospective 2-center consecutive CCTA hospital cohort in **Chongqing, China** (`Chongqing General Hospital [CGH]` internal development/test, Feb 2022–Aug 2023; `Yongchuan Hospital Affiliated to Chongqing Medical University [YHACMU]` external validation, Apr–Oct 2024, pp. 2, 4, 7 Table 1, 12–13; anonymized as `xx Hospital` on p. 2). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** (Chinese regional cohorts; authors explicitly note multi-ethnic cohort validation is needed on p. 12).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - **Internal `CGH` Cohort:** `2,763` consecutive CCTA patients screened $\rightarrow$ excluded `1,482` incomplete clinical, `723` incomplete lab, `18` missing ECG, `43` poor image quality, `24` repeat exams, `37` no CVD (`Fig. 1` p. 3) $\rightarrow$ reported final internal cohort **`460` patients** (mean age `64.23 ± 12.85` yrs, `241` male / `219` female, `Table 1` p. 7). *(Arithmetic error in `Fig. 1` p. 3: `540 - (43 + 24 + 37) = 436 ≠ 460` — an unexplained 24-patient discrepancy.)*
  - **External `YHACMU` Cohort:** `1,205` patients screened $\rightarrow$ **`217` included** (mean age `65.28 ± 10.36` yrs, `98` male / `119` female, `Table 1` p. 7; screening exclusions omitted from `Fig. 1`).
  - **Real-World Missing-Data Test Set:** **`203` patients** retrospectively selected from the `723` CGH patients originally excluded for incomplete laboratory data (`pp. 5, 8`).
- **Event Count & Prevalence (`5` Mutually Exclusive CVD Classes; Healthy Controls Excluded, p. 3, p. 7):**
  - **Internal (`n = 460`, p. 7):** Coronary atherosclerosis (non-obstructive CAD) `84 (18.3%)`, Obstructive CAD `161 (35.0%)`, Heart failure `60 (13.0%)`, Arrhythmia `102 (22.2%)`, Myocardial ischemia `53 (11.5%)`.
  - **External (`n = 217`, p. 7):** Coronary atherosclerosis `38 (17.5%)`, Obstructive CAD `49 (22.6%)`, Heart failure `23 (10.6%)`, Arrhythmia `58 (26.7%)`, Myocardial ischemia `49 (22.6%)`.
  - **Missing-Data Set (`n = 203`):** Per-class breakdown deferred to unbundled `Table S4` (`p. 8`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Cross-sectional 5-class diagnosis assigned via hierarchical priority (`PCI/CABG surgical records` > `ICD-10 four-digit primary discharge diagnosis` > `outpatient follow-up`, double-coded by 2 senior cardiologists with `96%` agreement / `Kappa = 0.94` on 50 random cases and 3rd-physician tie-break, pp. 2–3). *(Contradiction: Discussion p. 12 erroneously refers to the task as `"a binary classification of cardiovascular disease"`.)* Prediction horizon: `not applicable / cross-sectional` (eligible clinical/ECG/lab data collected within `±30 days` before and after CCTA, p. 2).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (`3` of `4` target modalities + CCTA imaging):**
  - **CCTA Images:** Coronary CT angiography acquired on Philips IQon Spectral CT / United Imaging UCT760 (internal) and Philips Brilliance 256 / UCT860 (external), normalized `0–255` with horizontal flip, random crop, and noise augmentation, encoded by `MRSPAN`-block CNN (`pp. 3, 5, Fig. 3A–B` p. 6).
  - **12-lead ECG:** XML recordings (`±30 days` of CCTA, p. 2) decomposed via discrete wavelet transform (`DWT`) into low- and high-frequency channels $\rightarrow$ dual-stream CNN + bilinear pooling (`pp. 5–6, Fig. 3C`).
  - **Clinical/EHR + Laboratory/Biomarkers + Smoking (`CLPs`):** `Table 1` (`p. 7`) states `9 clinical + 11 laboratory = 20` parameters; `Fig. 5B` (`p. 10`) lists `20` variables (`age, gender, BMI, hypertension, dyslipidemia, COPD, fatty liver disease, blood glucose, current smoker` + `IMA, hs-troponin, myoglobin, NT-proBNP, AST, hsCRP, WBC, CK-MB, CK, LDH, α-HBDH`); however, `p. 2` states `"all 23 parameters"` and `Fig. 5A` plots up to `21` variables. Encoded via 1D CNN (`p. 5`).
  - **Lifestyle:** **Current smoker only** (`1` binary variable in `Fig. 5B, D`, p. 10); diet, physical activity, and alcohol `not reported`.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** 12-lead ECG findings in `XML` format (`Table 1` p. 7); exact sampling frequency (Hz), duration (s), lead subset, filtering, and DWT wavelet basis/levels are `not reported` in the main PDF (`pp. 5–7`, deferred to unbundled `Appendix S1`).
- **Missing-Modality / Missing-Data Strategy:** Zero-imputation + binary missingness mask matrix (`p. 7`) evaluated on `10%–50%` random clinical/lab feature omission (`1,000` repeats, p. 7) and on `203` real-world block-missing-lab patients (`pp. 5, 8`). **Note:** Stress tests drop *clinical/laboratory tabular features only*, not entire CCTA or ECG modalities (`p. 7–8`).
- **Model Families & Baselines:** Multimodal fusion (`MRSPAN` CCTA + dual-stream DWT-CNN ECG + 1D-CNN Clinical/Lab $\rightarrow$ MLP Softmax, pp. 5–6) compared against `CCTA Images Only`, `Clinical + Laboratory Parameters (CLPs) Only`, and two senior cardiologists (`2024 ESC guidelines`, pp. 2, 8–9). **`ECG-only` and `CCTA + CLPs` ablations are omitted** (`Tables 2–3`, pp. 8–9).
- **Fusion Strategy:** Deep weighted-average latent fusion (`Feature Weighting Initialization` + linear/Softmax dynamic attention weights $\alpha_i = \exp(e_i)/\sum_{j=1}^M \exp(e_j)$ + $F = \sum_{i=1}^M \alpha_i f_i$, p. 5) $\rightarrow$ MLP classifier (`p. 5`). *(Discrepancy: Discussion p. 12 reports only two modality weights — `68% ± 5%` radiographic + `32% ± 5%` clinical = `100%` — omitting ECG completely).*

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** `70:15:15` internal split (`322` train / `69` val / `69` test, `Fig. 1` p. 3, `Fig. 2A` p. 4) + `217` external test (`YHACMU`) + `203` missingness test (`CGH` exclusions).
- **Overall Discrimination (`p. 7`, `Table 3` p. 8, `Fig. 4A` p. 9):**
  - **Internal Test (`n = 69`, `Table 3` & `Fig. 4A`):** Multimodal **AUC = `0.86`** (`95% CI: 0.863–0.877` in text p. 7 & Fig. 4A; printed `0.86, 0.88` in Table 3 — note `0.860 < 0.863`), Sensitivity `86.7% (82.1–88.9)`, Specificity `79.3% (74.5–81.6)`, PPV `72.6% (67.6–75.6)`, NPV `89.6% (86.4–92.2)`, F1 `0.85 (0.83–0.87)` vs Imaging-only `0.83 (0.833–0.846)` (`F1 0.80`) and Clinical+Lab `0.79 (0.784–0.802)` (`F1 0.77`). *(Alert: Abstract p. 1 states internal AUC `0.89`, which matches `Fig. 4A`'s `Internal training dataset` panel [`0.89, 95% CI 0.891–0.907`], NOT the `0.86` internal test set!)*
  - **External Test (`n = 217`, `p. 7`, `Fig. 4A` p. 9):** Multimodal **AUC = `0.83` (`95% CI: 0.826–0.834`)** vs Imaging-only `0.79 (0.792–0.803)` and Clinical+Lab `0.77 (0.773–0.787)` (`p < 0.001` for both, p. 7).
- **Per-Class AUCs (`Table 2` p. 8 vs `Fig. 4B–D` p. 9 — Severe Internal Contradiction):**
  - `Table 2` Internal Multimodal AUCs: Coronary atherosclerosis `0.89 (0.88, 0.94)`, CAD `0.89 (0.86, 0.93)`, HF `0.87 (0.84, 0.92)`, Arrhythmia `0.87 (0.82, 0.91)`, Myocardial ischemia `0.86 (0.83, 0.98 [typo])`. `Table 2` External Multimodal AUCs: `0.83 (0.78, 0.0.86 [typo])`, `0.84 (0.80, 0.87)`, `0.82 (0.79, 0.85)`, `0.83 (0.81, 0.86)`, `0.86 (0.81, 0.89)` (all `p ≤ 0.004` vs unimodal CCTA or CLPs).
  - `Fig. 4B–D` (`p. 9`) prints completely different per-class AUCs (and replaces `Arrhythmia` with `Myocardial injury` in the legends): External Multimodal `0.89, 0.87, 0.88, 0.91, 0.87`; External Clinical `0.89, 0.86, 0.85, 0.90, 0.84`; External Image `0.89, 0.88, 0.88, 0.91, 0.86`.
- **Missing-Data Robustness (`pp. 8, 10–12`):** `AUC = 0.76 ± 0.03` reported for `40%` simulated clinical/lab missingness (`p. 8`), `AUC = 0.76 (95% CI: 0.72–0.80)` for `50%` missingness (`p. 12`), and `AUC = 0.76 ± 0.03` on the `203` real-world incomplete-lab test cohort (`pp. 8, 11`). `Fig. 5A` (`p. 10`) plots degradation as clinical inputs drop from `21` down to `13`.
- **Clinician Comparison (`p. 9`):** Two specialists achieved `Kappa = 0.92` and `0.89`; Multimodal AUC `0.86` vs manual `0.78`, sensitivity `88%` vs `77%`, `+10.43` percentage points (`+44.0%` relative) CHD diagnosis rate gain.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported` — no Brier score, ECE, or calibration curve).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `Yes` — `DeLong` test for paired ROC curves (`Table 2`, p. 8; `pp. 7, 11`).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:** Excludes `83.4%` (`2,303 / 2,763`) of screened CGH patients leaving `460` complete-case subjects (`Fig. 1` has an unresolved `24`-patient mismatch: `540 - 104 = 436 ≠ 460`), excludes healthy controls (`p. 3`), evaluates an undersized `n = 69` internal test split across `5` classes (`~8–24` events/class), and restricts external testing (`n = 217`) to a second hospital in the same Chinese city (`p. 12`).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH / UNCLEAR RISK:** Allows ECG and laboratory tests up to **`30 days after` CCTA** (`p. 2`), risking post-diagnostic/post-PCI biomarker and treatment leakage. Feature counts conflict across text (`23`, p. 2), `Table 1` (`9 + 11 = 20`, p. 7), and `Fig. 5A` (`21`, p. 10), and raw ECG acquisition/DWT parameters are absent (`pp. 5–7`).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Multiclass forced primary label assignment among comorbid patients (`p. 3`) creates label ambiguity; adjudicator blinding to predictors is `not reported` (comparison specialists viewed all predictors, p. 2).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Zero calibration** (`Brier/ECE/slope`) and **zero DCA**; operating threshold tuned via Youden index on the `69`-patient validation set (`p. 7`).
  - **Pervasive internal inconsistencies:** Abstract (`0.89`) vs text/Table 3 (`0.86` with CI `0.863–0.877`), `Table 2` vs `Fig. 4B–D` per-class AUCs, `Arrhythmia` vs `Myocardial injury` legend swap (`Fig. 4`), `2` modality weights (`68% + 32%`) omitting ECG (`p. 12`), and Discussion claiming binary CVD classification (`p. 12`).
  - Missing `ECG-only` and `CCTA+CLPs` ablations, and unbundled `Appendix S1`.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (`p. 12`):** Single geographic region (Chongqing, China) limiting multi-ethnic generalizability; lack of longitudinal/continuous laboratory modeling; need for prospective clinical utility and cost-effectiveness evaluation; and erroneous claim of binary-only CVD classification (`p. 12`).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Relevant benchmark for missing-modality/missing-feature masking (`RQ4`) using real-world incomplete clinical records (`n = 203`) alongside `4` modality domains (CCTA + ECG + Clinical/Lab + Smoking). However, `P0036` suffers severe **High PROBAST risk** (post-CCTA `±30-day` leakage, `83.4%` exclusion, `n = 69` test set, pervasive Abstract/Table/Figure numerical contradictions), omits `ECG-only` ablations, omits calibration and DCA, excludes healthy controls, has `0` South Asian representation (`PG1`), and fails to report or substantiate quantitative XAI faithfulness (`PG3`, `PG5`).
