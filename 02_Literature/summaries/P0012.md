## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Retrospective secondary analysis of ICU/hospital diagnostic ECGs from **Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center (BIDMC)**, Boston, Massachusetts, USA (`MIMIC-IV-ECG` Matched Subset on PhysioNet, pp. 1, 2, 16). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** (race/ethnicity is not reported in Table 1, p. 3).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** `384,877` standard 12-lead ECG **recordings** (pp. 1, 2, 3 Table 1): Male `201,804` (`52.4%`), Female `183,073` (`47.6%`), mean age `64.8` years (`SD 17`, Table 1 p. 3). **Critical omission:** Unique *patient* count (`subject_id`) is `not reported`, and all splits are conducted at the **record level** (pp. 5, 7).
- **Event Count & Prevalence (Multilabel across 13 Classes, Table 1 p. 3 & Table 2 p. 6):**
  - `Normal`: `151,476` (`39.4%`); `Atrial fibrillation`: `123,898` (`32.2%`); `Heart failure`: `122,681` (`31.9%`); `STEMI/NSTEMI`: `45,116` (`11.7%`); `Pulmonary hypertension`: `29,018` (`7.5%`); `SVT/VT`: `24,980` (`6.5%`); `Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy`: `21,398` (`5.6%`); `Aortic stenosis/insufficiency`: `19,682` (`5.1%`); `Mitral valve prolapse/stenosis`: `18,716` (`4.9%`); `Atrioventricular block`: `18,057` (`4.7%`); `Unstable angina`: `13,067` (`3.4%`); `Pulmonary embolism`: `8,524` (`2.2%`); `Cardiac arrest`: `7,593` (`2.0%`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Cross-sectional multilabel classification across `13` diagnostic categories (`1` Normal rhythm + `12` CVD classes) derived from ICD-10 codes associated with each ECG recording (pp. 2–3). Exact ICD-10 code list and longitudinal prediction horizon ($\Delta t$) are `not reported`.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (ECG + ECG-Derived Features + Demographics; Lacks Independent Clinical EHR/Labs/Lifestyle):**
  - **Raw 12-lead ECG waveforms:** `5,000 × 12` (`10 s` at `500 Hz`), zero-filled missing values, truncated/zero-padded to 5,000 samples, per-lead z-score normalized ($\mu=0, \sigma=1$), segmented into non-overlapping patches (`p. 6` text states `50`-sample patches $\rightarrow$ `100` patches; `Fig. 1` p. 4 depicts `W: 100, H: 12`) mapped via 1D CNN to `768`-dim patch tokens (pp. 3–6).
  - **Structured machine-measured ECG features (`9` features):** `rr_interval`, `p_onset`, `p_end`, `qrs_onset`, `qrs_end`, `t_end`, `p_axis`, `qrs_axis`, `t_axis` (Fig. 1 Note, p. 4), standardized on training-set statistics only, zero-imputed with binary validity masks (p. 6). *Note: These 9 features are derived from the ECG itself rather than independent EHR or lab modalities.*
  - **Demographics:** `Age` (normalized) and `Sex` embedded as metadata tokens (pp. 3–4, 6).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** Independent Clinical EHR labs/vitals/history, Laboratory biomarkers, and **Lifestyle:** `none / not reported`.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `10 s` standard 12-lead ECG at `500 Hz` (`5,000 × 12`, p. 6).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Zero-imputation + binary validity masks for the 9 structured ECG features (`p. 6`), explicitly stress-tested by zero-masking all 9 structured features at inference (`CaMPNet_Feature_Masked`, pp. 13–14).
- **Model Families & Baselines:** `CaMPNet` (`6`-layer Transformer encoder, `8` heads/layer, `768`-dim embeddings, adaptive class-weighted BCE loss Eq. 4, pp. 6–7) benchmarked against `ResNet-ECG` (Kalmady et al. [29]), `ECG_Waveform` (waveform-only), `ECG_Features_XGBoost` (9 structured features only), `CaMPNet_Feature_Masked`, and `CaMPNet_Without_Age_Sex` (Table 3, p. 13).
- **Fusion Strategy:** Query-conditioned early cross-attention fusion before the main Transformer encoder (Eqs. 1–3, Figs. 1–2, pp. 4–5): the structured ECG feature token acts as Query ($Q$) attending to raw ECG patch tokens ($K, V$) via scaled dot-product cross-attention + FFN + LayerNorm to produce a context-enhanced `Fusion token`, concatenated with `[CLS] + Patch tokens + Age/Sex Meta tokens + Fusion token` + positional embeddings prior to the 6-layer Transformer encoder.

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Chronological outer split of the `384,877` **records**: earliest `90%` for development (randomly split `7:1:1` into train/val/internal test, i.e., `~70% / 10% / 10%` of total, Table 2 p. 6), and most recent `10%` withheld as **"temporal external validation"** (single-hospital temporal holdout, pp. 5, 7). Trained up to `200` epochs with early stopping on `0.5 × AUC + 0.5 × accuracy` (pp. 6–7).
- **Internal Test Set (Macro-averaged across 13 labels, Table 3 p. 13 & Table 4 p. 8):**
  - **`CaMPNet`:** AUC **`0.845` (`SD 0.04`)**, AUPRC **`0.489` (`SD 0.03`)**, F1-score **`0.501` (`SD 0.04`)**, Recall `0.493` (`SD 0.02`), Precision **`0.515` (`SD 0.03`)**, Accuracy **`0.923` (`SD 0.07`)**, Specificity **`0.949` (`SD 0.06`)**.
    - Top per-class performance (Table 4, p. 8): Normal (`AUC 0.963`, `AUPRC 0.931`, `F1 0.877`), AF (`AUC 0.907`, `AUPRC 0.836`, `F1 0.761`), AV block (`AUC 0.888`), HF (`AUC 0.887`, `AUPRC 0.781`, `F1 0.727`), STEMI/NSTEMI (`AUC 0.880`, `AUPRC 0.620`).
    - Lowest per-class performance (Table 4, p. 8): Mitral valve prolapse/stenosis (`AUC 0.771`, `AUPRC 0.300`), PE (`AUC 0.794`, `AUPRC 0.217`, `F1 0.295`), Pulmonary hypertension (`AUC 0.796`), Cardiac arrest (`AUC 0.797`, `AUPRC 0.276`).
  - **Baselines & Ablations (Table 3, p. 13):**
    - `ResNet-ECG` [29]: AUC **`0.848`** *(marginally higher AUC than CaMPNet's `0.845`)*, AUPRC `0.398`, F1 `0.152`, Recall `0.691`, Precision `0.098`, Accuracy `0.842`, Specificity `0.845`.
    - `ECG_Waveform`: AUC `0.794`, AUPRC `0.302`, F1 `0.311`, Recall `0.792`, Precision `0.215`, Accuracy `0.667`, Specificity `0.631`.
    - `ECG_Features_XGBoost`: AUC `0.782`, AUPRC `0.293`, F1 `0.316`, Recall `0.686`, Precision `0.241`, Accuracy `0.744`, Specificity `0.720`.
    - `CaMPNet_Feature_Masked`: AUC `0.839`, AUPRC `0.432`, F1 `0.442`, Recall `0.531`, Precision `0.411`, Accuracy `0.889`, Specificity `0.911`.
    - `CaMPNet_Without_Age_Sex`: AUC `0.842`, AUPRC `0.411`, F1 `0.412`, Recall `0.601`, Precision `0.332`, Accuracy `0.878`, Specificity `0.880`.
- **Temporal Holdout ("Temporal External Validation", recent 10%, Table 5 p. 15):** Mean AUC fell to **`0.715` (`SD 0.03`)**, AUPRC **`0.298` (`SD 0.02`)**, F1 **`0.322` (`SD 0.03`)**, Recall `0.317` (`SD 0.03`), Precision `0.332` (`SD 0.04`), Accuracy `0.906` (`SD 0.05`), Specificity `0.941` (`SD 0.06`). Common labels stayed robust (Normal `0.969`, AF `0.862`, HF `0.845`), whereas rare disorders collapsed (Cardiac arrest `0.569`, Mitral valve `0.578`, SVT/VT `0.625`, PHTN `0.641`, PE `0.654`).
- **Subgroup Analysis (pp. 11–12, Figs. 5–6):** Text reports Female AUC `0.838` vs Male `0.848` and Age quartiles Q1 (`≤54`) `0.872` vs Q4 (`>78`) `0.811` (Abstract says Q1 `0.884`). **Discrepancy alert:** Figure 5 bar plot displays Female AUC `0.863` vs Male `0.865`, and Figure 6 displays Q1 `0.900`, Q2 `0.874`, Q3 `0.848`, Q4 `0.812`—not matching the text numbers!
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `Yes (Visual + Platt Scaling Only — No Quantitative Brier/ECE)` — Methods describe Platt scaling (p. 7) and Figure 4 (p. 10) plots 13 calibration curves showing visible deviations from the diagonal in mid/high probability bins (e.g., `Normal`, `PE`, `MVP`), with **zero numerical Brier score, ECE, or calibration slope/intercept reported**.
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `No` (`not reported` — no DeLong test, p-values, 95% CIs, or multi-seed runs).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:** Chronological `90%/10%` and random `7:1:1` splits were performed on `384,877` **ECG records** without patient-ID (`subject_id`) grouping or deduplication (pp. 2, 5, 7), allowing repeated recordings from the same ICU patient to span train/val/internal-test partitions and inflate internal metrics (consistent with the steep `-0.130` AUC drop from internal `0.845` to temporal `0.715`). Single-center ICU cohort (`MIMIC-IV-ECG`) limits outpatient/community generalizability.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Structured features standardized strictly on training-set statistics (`p. 6`) with missingness masks; however, missing raw ECG samples were zero-filled, and structured features depend on vendor-specific measurement pipelines (p. 16).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:** ICD-10 diagnostic codes linked to ECG recordings lack exact code lists, temporal alignment rules, and blinding details (pp. 2–3).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Record-level split leakage risk; no multi-seed variance, 95% CIs, or formal significance tests (DeLong/bootstrap), even though `ResNet-ECG` had slightly higher AUC (`0.848` vs `0.845`).
  - Lacks quantitative calibration metrics (`Brier/ECE/slope`), lacks DCA, and omits threshold selection details for F1/Recall/Precision/Accuracy/Specificity.
  - Multiple internal numeric/label inconsistencies across text vs Figures 5–6 (`0.838/0.848` vs `0.863/0.865`; `0.872` vs `0.884` vs `0.900`), patch width (`50` vs `100` in Fig. 1), and Figure 7's misexpansion of CaMPNet (`"consistency-aware multi-prior network"`, p. 13).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Rare-label scarcity (`PE`, `cardiac arrest`, `MVP`) and overlapping comorbidity errors; single-center North American ICU cohort (`MIMIC-IV-ECG`); `-0.130` AUC drop on temporal drift (`0.845` $\rightarrow$ `0.715`); vendor dependence of the 9 structured ECG features; opaque internal transformer reasoning not always matching clinical logic; and untested generalizability across sampling rates (`250 Hz`/`1000 Hz`), demographics, or pediatric cohorts (pp. 16–17).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Direct architectural precedent (`CaMPNet`) for query-conditioned cross-attention between structured features and 12-lead ECG patch tokens (`PG2`, `PG5`), demonstrating notable AUPRC (`0.489` vs `0.398`) and F1 (`0.501` vs `0.152`) gains over ResNet-ECG on MIMIC-IV-ECG and validating structured-feature missingness tolerance. However, `CaMPNet` fuses only ECG waveforms with ECG-derived intervals and age/sex—omitting true EHR clinical histories, Laboratory assays, and Lifestyle factors (`PG2`, `PG4`)—suffers High PROBAST ROB from record-level splitting and text/figure discrepancies, lacks quantitative calibration/DCA, and omits quantitative XAI faithfulness (`AOPC`) and stability testing (`PG3`, `PG5`).
