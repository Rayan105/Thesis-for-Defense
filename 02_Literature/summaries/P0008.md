## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:**
  - **Development/Internal Cohort:** Retrospective single-centre study at the Department of Cardiology, **Kobe University Hospital**, Kobe, Japan (January 2012 – February 2022, pp. 1–3, 8).
  - **External Validation Cohort:** Public credentialed US critical-care/hospital EHR dataset (**MIMIC-IV**, **MIMIC-IV-CXR**, and **MIMIC-IV-ECG**, Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston, USA, pp. 1, 5, 8, 12).
  - **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** (Japanese single-centre internal cohort with no racial/ethnic breakdown; MIMIC-IV external cohort ethnicity demographics not reported in PDF).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - **Internal Kobe Cohort:** `9,458` unique patients contributing `24,135` same-day paired ECG–CXR examinations (pp. 1, 3, Table 1 p. 4, Fig. 2 p. 5). All `9,458` patients had laboratory outcomes; `8,262` patients had eligible echocardiography within `±14` days (p. 3). Mean age `69.6 ± 22.1` yrs, `60.4%` (`5,717`) male (Table 1 p. 4).
  - **External MIMIC-IV Cohort:** `40,285` patients contributing `94,105` paired same-day ECG–CXR–NT-proBNP samples (pp. 5, 9).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Exact positive event counts and class prevalences (`%`) for the binary classification tasks are **`not reported`** in the main PDF (Table 1 p. 4 reports continuous baseline means ± SD: BNP `69.0 ± 32.17 pg/mL`, LVEF `60.8 ± 14.0%`, E/e' `12.5 ± 9.01`; external MIMIC-IV median NT-proBNP = `1,249 pg/mL`, IQR `294–4,177 pg/mL`, p. 5).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:**
  - **Primary cross-sectional surrogate endpoints:** `BNP > 100 pg/mL` (same day), `LVEF < 50%`, and `E/e' > 15` (closest echo within `±14` days; median interval `0` days [IQR `0–0` for LVEF, `0–1` for E/e'], pp. 3, 5, 8).
  - **Prognostic longitudinal endpoint:** Subsequent severe BNP elevation (`max follow-up BNP > 400 pg/mL` within `3 years` among patients with baseline `BNP < 400 pg/mL`, p. 8).
  - **19 additional binary clinical/echo/lab indicators (Table 2, pp. 6–7; Fig. 6, p. 11):** `AR ≥ moderate`, `AS ≥ moderate`, `DBP > 90`, `SBP > 140`, `eGFR < 60`, `HbA1c ≥ 6.5`, `Hb < 12`, `K > 5.5`, `Na > 145`, `LAVI > 34`, `K < 3.4`, `Na < 135`, `LVDd > 55`, `LVOT VTI < 15`, `MR ≥ moderate`, `BMI > 25`, `SVI < 35` (included in Table 2 despite LVEF replacing SVI in primary analysis, p. 5), `TRPG > 30`, and `BUN > 20` (`23` total binary targets in Table 2).
  - **External MIMIC-IV endpoint:** Abnormal `NT-proBNP` elevation (exact threshold cutoff in pg/mL deferred to external Supplementary Material, p. 5).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (1 of 4 target modalities + 1 thoracic imaging modality):**
  - `12-lead ECG` (raw waveforms, p. 3).
  - `Chest X-ray (CXR)` (frontal greyscale radiographs, p. 3).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** Clinical/EHR (age, sex, BP, BMI used only descriptively or as targets—not model inputs), Laboratory/Biomarkers (used strictly as prediction targets, not inputs), Lifestyle (`none / not reported`).
- **ECG & CXR Format & Sampling:**
  - `12-lead ECG`: `10 s` at `500 Hz` (`5,000` samples/lead, `1.25 μV` resolution); during training, a random continuous `2-s` segment (`12 × 1,000`) extracted with zero-centring baseline correction (p. 3).
  - `CXR`: Frontal greyscale JPEG (`>2000 px` original), resized to `1 × 224 × 224`, normalized (`mean=0.5`, `std=0.5`), augmented via random cropping, rotation, and erasing (p. 3).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** **Native order-flexible & unimodal/sequential inference** — Step 1 embeds ECG and CXR into an L2-normalized shared space; Step 2 updates a `GRU` hidden state initialized from a zero prior (`h_prior`), yielding predictions from ECG alone (`p_ECG`), CXR alone (`p_CXR`), `ECG → CXR`, or `CXR → ECG` without retraining (pp. 2–4).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - Step 1 Encoders: `1D ResNet-Transformer` (ECG encoder, ref 24) + `ViT-B/16` pretrained on ImageNet-1K (CXR encoder), trained via contrastive `L_CLIP-Switch` loss (CLIP symmetric softmax + Switch loss generating pseudo-negative intra-batch swaps to reduce modality-specific clustering, pp. 3–4, Fig. 3 p. 5).
  - Step 2 Predictor: `GRU` + linear binary classification head (p. 4).
  - Baselines: Unimodal Step-2 `ECG`-only and `CXR`-only models (pp. 6–8). **No simultaneous multimodal fusion baseline (e.g., late concatenation or cross-attention), clinical risk score, or tabular baseline evaluated.**
- **Fusion Strategy:** Two-stage shared contrastive embedding (`L_CLIP-Switch`) followed by **sequential GRU state-updating fusion** across arbitrary arrival order (`ECG → CXR` / `CXR → ECG`, pp. 3–4).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** `70:15:15` **patient-level** random split on Kobe cohort: Train `6,620` patients (`16,971` pairs), Validation `1,419` patients (`3,494` pairs), Test `1,419` patients (`3,670` pairs) (Fig. 2 p. 5, text p. 8). External MIMIC-IV held out entirely (`40,285` patients, p. 5).
- **Primary Internal Test Discrimination (pp. 1, 2, 8, Fig. 5A p. 10, Table 2 p. 6):**
  - **`BNP > 100 pg/mL`:**
    - `ECG → CXR`: **ROC-AUC = `0.90` (95% CI: `0.89–0.92`)** (beats both unimodal models: `ΔAUC vs ECG = [0.00423, 0.0221]`, `ΔAUC vs CXR = [0.0397, 0.0616]`)
    - `CXR → ECG`: **ROC-AUC = `0.90` (95% CI: `0.89–0.92`)** (beats CXR `[0.0369, 0.0568]`, not significant vs ECG `[-0.00016, 0.0191]`)
    - `ECG-only`: `0.89` (`0.87–0.91`); `CXR-only`: `0.85` (`0.83–0.87`)
  - **`LVEF < 50%`:**
    - `ECG → CXR`: **ROC-AUC = `0.87` (95% CI: `0.82–0.91`)** (beats ECG `[0.0181, 0.0881]` and CXR `[0.0188, 0.0763]`)
    - `CXR → ECG`: **ROC-AUC = `0.86` (95% CI: `0.82–0.91`)** (beats ECG `[0.0190, 0.0801]` and CXR `[0.0111, 0.0751]`)
    - `ECG-only`: `0.82` (`0.77–0.87`); `CXR-only`: `0.82` (`0.77–0.87`)
    - Sensitivity: `0.789` for both multimodal orders (p. 8; full threshold table in external Table S2).
  - **`E/e' > 15`:**
    - `ECG → CXR`: **ROC-AUC = `0.80` (95% CI: `0.75–0.84`)**; `CXR → ECG`: `0.79` (`0.75–0.84`); `ECG-only`: `0.79` (`0.74–0.83`); `CXR-only`: `0.75` (`0.70–0.80`) (beats CXR only; not significant vs ECG, Table 2 p. 6).
  - **3-Year Prognostic Severe BNP (`> 400 pg/mL`, Fig. 5B p. 10, text p. 8):**
    - `CXR → ECG`: **ROC-AUC = `0.79` (95% CI: `0.74–0.83`)** (beats both ECG `[0.00159, 0.0489]` and CXR `[0.0129, 0.0579]`); `ECG → CXR`: `0.78` (`0.73–0.83`, ns vs ECG `[-0.00306, 0.0439]`); `ECG-only`: `0.76` (`0.71–0.81`); `CXR-only`: `0.75` (`0.70–0.80`).
- **External Validation (`MIMIC-IV`, `N=40,285` patients / `94,105` samples, abnormal `NT-proBNP`, pp. 1, 2, 9, Fig. 5D p. 10):**
  - `ECG → CXR`: **ROC-AUC = `0.89` (95% CI: `0.88–0.90`)**
  - `CXR → ECG`: **ROC-AUC = `0.89` (95% CI: `0.88–0.89`)**
  - `ECG-only`: `0.84` (`0.83–0.85`); `CXR-only`: `0.85` (`0.84–0.86`)
- **Cross-Modal Retrieval (Fig. 4, p. 9; text p. 8):** Bidirectional Top-1 retrieval accuracy `~40%` at batch size `30`.
- **Secondary Metrics (`PR-AUC`, `F1`, `Brier Score`, `PPV/NPV`):** `not reported` in main PDF (threshold metrics deferred to external Table S2, p. 8).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — UNCLEAR / MODERATE RISK:**
  - Rigorous **patient-level** `70:15:15` split (`6,620 / 1,419 / 1,419` unique patients, Fig. 2 p. 5) prevents internal patient leakage, and MIMIC-IV external validation (`N=40,285` patients) is exceptionally large.
  - However, single-centre cardiology selection requiring same-day ECG+CXR and exclusion of patients without follow-up BNP in the 3-year prognostic subanalysis (`p. 8`) risk selection and informative-censoring bias.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - Standardized ECG zero-centring and fixed ImageNet-style CXR scaling (`0.5/0.5`) avoid cross-split normalization leakage; however, test-time cropping protocol for the `2-s` ECG window (e.g., center vs averaged crops) and exact intra-day ECG-vs-CXR timing are unknown (`p. 3, 11`).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - Relies on surrogate laboratory/echo cutoffs (`BNP > 100`, `LVEF < 50`, `E/e' > 15`) rather than adjudicated HF diagnoses or invasive hemodynamics (`p. 5, 11`); echo allowed within `±14` days of ECG/CXR (`p. 3`); external MIMIC-IV NT-proBNP threshold is omitted from main text (`p. 5`).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Complete absence of calibration** (Brier, ECE, calibration curves), **PR-AUC**, **DCA**, and **event counts/prevalences** in the main PDF (`pp. 5–11`).
  - Lacks comparison against simultaneous multimodal fusion architectures or clinical baselines.
  - 3-year prognostic scatter classification (`Fig. 5C`, p. 8, 10) uses a post-hoc Youden threshold without accounting for competing mortality risk beyond excluding post-death labs.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Modality-specific noise; CLIP favors shared cross-modal features over modality-specific signals; surrogate biomarkers rather than invasive hemodynamics; unknown intra-day ECG/CXR timestamps (`computational sequential updating` rather than verified clinical sequence); single-centre Japanese training cohort; no prospective reader study or DCA/clinical utility validation (`p. 11`).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Highly relevant MIMIC-IV external comparator (`N=40,285`) demonstrating how `L_CLIP-Switch` contrastive embeddings + GRU state updating support **order-flexible and missing-modality inference** (`RQ4`). However, CardioCLIP restricts inputs to ECG + CXR (missing Clinical EHR, Lab assays as inputs, and Lifestyle factors—`PG2`, `PG4`), predicts surrogate biomarkers rather than CVD events, omits calibration/PR-AUC/DCA, and provides **zero explainability (`PG3`)**—gaps our FYDP directly addresses through 4-modality fusion, full calibration/DCA reporting, and quantitative SHAP AOPC faithfulness.
