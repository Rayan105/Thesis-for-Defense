## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Surveyed (mapped against 4 target modalities):**
  - **ECG:** 1D raw/time-series single-lead and 12-lead ECGs (`Table 1`, pp. 16–32); 2D time-frequency/image/GAF/RP/MTF transforms (`Table 2`, pp. 33–40); and homogeneous ECG-derived multimodal inputs (`Table 3`, pp. 41–50: e.g., ECG + GAF/RP/MTF images, ECG + RR intervals + entropy/HFD, ECG + wavelet features).
  - **Clinical/EHR, Clinical Text & Imaging (in multimodal subset, Table 3, pp. 41–50):** Synthesizes cross-modal studies combining ECG with echocardiography video (`LVH-Fusion [76]`), EMR/clinical indicators (`MF-CADNet [78]`, `Qiu et al. 2024 [80]`, `ECG-LM [20]`), Chest X-rays + diagnostic reports (`MoRE [82]`), and clinical text reports (`Qiu et al. 2023 [85]`, `Yu et al. 2023 [18]`, `MERL [79]`, `TAC-ECG [19]`). Also notes PPG/BP/EDA conceptually in §5.1 (p. 12) and MIMIC-III waveform ECG+PPG in `[19]` (p. 49).
  - **Laboratory/Biomarkers & Lifestyle:** **None / not modeled** across the `17` multimodal studies in Table 3 (pp. 41–50) — confirming zero 4-modality ECG+Clinical+Lab+Lifestyle fusion systems among the reviewed architectures.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `No primary ECG modeling` (synthesizes heterogeneous durations—single beat around R-peak, 2 s, 3 s, 10 s, 30 min, 24 h Holter—and sampling rates such as 250/300/360/500 Hz across reviewed studies, §5.4.2 pp. 59–60, Tables 1–4).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** `Not applicable` (critiques that strict ECG-text/echo pairing is brittle and recommends decision-level fusion, calibrated lag, uncertainty-aware reweighting, and explicit missing-modality documentation, §5.1–5.2 pp. 13–14).

  - *1D Evolution (pp. 3–8, Fig. 2, Table 1):* Shallow 1D CNNs, DBNs, stacked sparse autoencoders $\rightarrow$ CNN-LSTM/BiLSTM/GRU, multi-scale/residual 1D CNNs, Wavelet-BiLSTM $\rightarrow$ SE-ResNet, 1D ShuffleNet, Self-ONN, ECG-DETR, CAT-Net, ECGTransForm, ECGBert, CardioPatternFormer, ECGFM, 1DCNN-ECG-Mamba.

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Primary Empirical Results:** `None` (systematic review; no primary model trained and no formal meta-analysis performed due to heterogeneity of tasks, metrics, and split protocols, §7 p. 63).
- **Key Cross-Paradigm Synthesis Findings (`§5.3, pp. 15, 32, 50, 58` & `Table 4, pp. 51–58`):**
  - **Protocol Leakage Sensitivity (`§5.3.1, p. 32`):** Highlights Li et al. (2022 `[54]`), where 10-fold beat-level (patient-dependent) accuracy of `99.61%` drops to **`94.80%`** under patient-independent (`DS1` $\rightarrow$ `DS2`) splitting (`pp. 32, 53, 56`), and Di Paolo & Castro (2024 `[81]`), where intra-patient F1 `97.64%` drops to **`81.91%`** inter-patient (`p. 46`).
  - **Selected Cross-Modal Benchmarks (`Table 3–4, pp. 41–58`):**
    - `Qiu et al. (2024) [80]` (920 AF patients, ablation recurrence): ECG-only AUROC `0.769` (`Acc 65.2%`) $\rightarrow$ ECG + clinical features AUROC **`0.899`** (`Acc 81.5%`, `F1 71.7%`) (`p. 13, p. 44, p. 57`).
    - `Torres Soto et al. (2022) [76]` (`LVH-Fusion`, ECG + Echo video, patient-level hold-out): AUROC `0.92`, AUPRC `0.80`, Spec `0.96`, F1 `0.73` (`p. 13–14, p. 41, p. 56`).
    - `Wang et al. (2023) [78]` (`MF-CADNet`, ECG+VCG+spectrogram+EMR): PTB F1 `98.41%`, PKUSZ F1 `93.49%` (`p. 13–14, p. 42, p. 57`).
    - `Thapa et al. (2024) [82]` (`MoRE`, ECG+CXR+reports): Zero-shot PTB-XL AUROC `0.50–0.77`, Fine-tuned PTB-XL AUROC `0.82–0.91`, MIMIC-IV $\rightarrow$ CheXpert external AUROC `91.00%` (`p. 44, p. 57`).
    - `Han et al. (2023) [77]` (`MAMIL`, ECG+GAF): Patient-external INCART AUROC `74.95%`, F1 `69.10%` (`p. 43, p. 57–58`).
    - `Liu et al. (2024) [79]` (`MERL`, ECG+text reports): Mean zero-shot AUROC `75.24%` across PTB-XL/CPSC2018/CSN (`p. 13, p. 45, p. 57`).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported` as primary analysis; explicitly flags in `§7, p. 63` and `§8, p. 63` that *"uncertainty quantification and model calibration remain underreported"* across primary studies).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `No` (`not reported` — explicitly critiques primary studies for ignoring within-subject correlation when segmenting beats/windows and calls for patient-level bootstrapping and hierarchical statistical comparisons, §5.4.1 p. 59).
