## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Study Type & Setting:** Systematic literature review (qualitative synthesis only; **no primary experiments** and **no meta-analysis** conducted by the authors; pp. 2–4, Fig. 1 p. 4). PROSPERO registration ID: `not reported`.
- **Search Scope & PRISMA Funnel (pp. 2–4, Fig. 1 p. 4):**
  - Search window: `1 January 2019–15 March 2025` across `4` databases: PubMed (`n = 1,102`), Scopus (`n = 520`), Web of Science (`n = 250`), and arXiv (`n = 170`), totaling `2,042` identified records (Fig. 1, p. 4).
  - Duplicate removal: `738` duplicates removed $\rightarrow$ `1,304` records screened $\rightarrow$ `987` excluded by title/abstract $\rightarrow$ `317` full-text reports assessed (`0` not retrieved) $\rightarrow$ `215` excluded $\rightarrow$ **`102` studies included** in qualitative synthesis (Fig. 1, p. 4).
  - **PRISMA Arithmetic Discrepancy:** Fig. 1 (p. 4) lists 6 full-text exclusion reasons: Commentary/editorial (`11`), No performance metrics (`31`), Insufficient outcome data (`47`), Non-ECG signal (`30`), No AI (`25`), Duplicate cohort (`23`), which sum to `167` rather than `215` (`48` excluded reports unaccounted for). Furthermore, §2.1 excludes non-English articles whereas §2.2 permits English or Spanish (p. 3), and Fig. 1 invokes PRISMA 2020 while citing Moher et al. 2009 [19] (pp. 4, 29).
- **Target Population & South Asian Representation:** Adults ($\ge 18$ years) undergoing 12-lead, single-lead, or wearable ECG evaluation for cardiovascular disease diagnosis (p. 3). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **None / 0 cohorts evaluated**. The review explicitly documents severe global dataset bias: only `29%` of surveyed datasets report race/ethnicity, `89%` of recorded populations are White/Caucasian, and `<30%` (`28%`) of datasets are publicly accessible (p. 12, Table 4 p. 18, p. 22, citing [4, 12, 13]). Page 27 explicitly calls for future validation data collection across *"Latin America, Africa, and parts of Asia"*.
- **Target Clinical Endpoints:** Atrial fibrillation (AF), myocardial infarction / occlusion MI (MI/OMI), heart failure / reduced LVEF (HF), arrhythmias, ventricular tachycardia, coronary artery disease (CAD), stress/anomaly detection, and signal denoising/synthetic ECG generation (pp. 6–14, Tables 1–3 pp. 8–11).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Primary Synthesis Protocol:** Narrative qualitative synthesis across `102` included studies (no pooled quantitative meta-analysis performed by `P0026`, p. 4). Moreover, **no master per-study extraction table** listing all `102` studies, cohorts, sample sizes, or metrics is included in the PDF.
- **Representative Reported Literature Benchmarks:**
  - **Atrial Fibrillation (Wearables, p. 6):** Cites Shahid et al. [7] meta-analysis of Apple Watch ECG achieving pooled sensitivity `94.8%` (`95% CI: 91.7–96.8%`) and specificity `95%` (`95% CI: 88.6–97.8%`).
  - **MI / Occlusion MI (`Table 1` p. 8, p. 6):** Notes up to `56%` of OMIs lack ST-segment elevation; rule-based+AI [4] sensitivity `43.6%` (misses `>50%` of occlusions), whereas `CNN + Imaging` [18] achieves sensitivity `>70%`.
  - **Heart Failure / Reduced LVEF (`Table 2` p. 9):** Hou et al. [1] (`DenseNet-121`, `136,775` ECG–echo pairs) internal AUC `0.965` dropping to external AUC `0.848`; Seoni et al. [37] (`CNN-BiLSTM`, `67,332` ECGs) internal AUC `0.943` dropping to external AUC `0.867`; Cheng et al. [38] (`CNN-BiLSTM`, `145,220` ECGs) internal AUC `0.958` dropping to external AUC `0.823`.
  - **CAD & Stress (`pp. 13–14, 26`):** `CAD` detection `>0.96` accuracy/sensitivity/specificity ([37], p. 26); `SWEET` wearable stress `Random Forest` / `XGBoost` `>98%` accuracy ([11], pp. 13–14).
  - **ECG Denoising (`Table 3` p. 11):** `WT+ACF` [9] on MIT-BIH (`+6.5 dB` SNR); `DP-IDAE` [8] on MIT-BIH/PTB-XL (`+7.06 dB` SNR); classic FIR/WT (`<1 dB`).
