## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** PRISMA systematic review and random-effects AUROC meta-analysis led by researchers at Queen Mary University of London / Barts Health NHS Trust (UK, p. 1). Searched Web of Science Core Collection, Embase, PubMed, and IEEE Xplore on `7 November 2024` (10-year window: November 2014 – November 2024, pp. 1–2). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none (`0/15` included studies)** — Discussion explicitly notes included studies used predominantly Western populations (e.g., `9/15` incorporating MIMIC, plus UK, US, Italy, Germany, Spain, China, Taiwan, Japan, Table 2 pp. 4–5, p. 14).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - **Screening Funnel (Fig. 1 p. 6; §3.1 p. 3):** `372` records identified (`PubMed=44`, `Web of Science=132`, `IEEE Xplore=30`, `Embase=166`), `88` duplicates removed $\rightarrow$ `284` unique records screened $\rightarrow$ `237` excluded at title/abstract $\rightarrow$ `47` full-text assessed $\rightarrow$ `32` excluded (`18` single-modality, `8` insufficient ML details, `3` non-HF outcomes, `2` non-classification/prediction, `1` background registry) $\rightarrow$ **`15` included studies** (`10` HF classification, `4` HF prognosis, `1` both, p. 3).
  - **Included Study Sample Sizes (Table 2 pp. 4–5; §3.2 p. 3):** Span `91` (`Vahabi et al.`) to `54,462` patients (`Postiglione et al.`), with `1` study omitting patient count (`Lu Yuan et al.`, reporting `16,120` CXR-text pairs). *(Note arithmetic discrepancy in text §3.2 p. 3 & §4 p. 14 claiming `9/15 (60%)` studies had `100–1,000` participants, whereas Table 2 shows `8` studies had `1,017–9,789` participants and only `1` study [Liu Yi et al., n=440] fell between `100` and `1,000` — likely an author typo for `100–10,000`).* Note that **`Botros et al. (2025/2024)` [Ref. 57] = `P0001`** in our corpus (Table 2 p. 5, Table 3 p. 9).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Synthesizes `10` HF classification studies (`HF` vs `non-HF`, `HFpEF` vs `HFrEF`, acute HF), `4` HF prognosis studies (survival time, 30-day readmission, 30-day/in-hospital mortality), and `1` dual-objective study (`González et al. [60]`) (p. 3, Table 3 pp. 8–11).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Heterogeneous HF diagnostic definitions (ICD-9/10, LVEF thresholds, NT-proBNP `>300 ng/L`, ESC criteria, PheKB, or physician/unreported) and prognostic endpoints (`30-day` mortality/readmission, survival time; Table 2–3 pp. 4–5, 8–11).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Synthesized (Review — No Primary Modeling):**
  - Across `15` included studies (p. 3 §3.3, Fig. 3 p. 7): `Tabular` (`11/15`, `73.3%`), `Imaging` (combining CXRs and ECGs under imaging, `8/15`, `53.3%`), `Clinical Text` (`7/15`, `46.7%`), `Sound/PCG` (`1/15`), `Tabular time-series` (`1/15`), and `Genomics/Omics` (`1/15`, multi-omics only).
  - **Key Gap Documented (`p. 5`):** `0/15` studies integrated genetic data with clinical/imaging modalities, and authors conclude **no included study integrated three or more distinct modalities** in their modality taxonomy (note: `Liu Yi et al. [53]` used 2nd-lead ECG + CXR + structured text, with ECG+CXR grouped under Imaging). **Lifestyle modality:** `none / not reported`.
- **ECG Format & Sampling (Reviewed Studies):** No primary ECG modeling. `4/15` included studies incorporated ECG/HRV: `González et al. [60]` (short ECG + sampled long-term HRV), `Liu Yi et al. [53]` (2nd-lead ECG + CXR + structured text), `Botros et al. [57] = P0001` (ECG + blood tests), and `Shiraga et al. [55]` (12-lead ECG + PCG) (Table 3 pp. 9–10).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Not systematically synthesized; notes `Ma Meikun et al. [63]` used VRNN imputation for missing longitudinal lab time-series (p. 7 §3.4).
- **Model Families & Fusion Strategies Synthesized (`§3.3–3.4`, pp. 3, 5, 7, Fig. 3):**
  - **Fusion Strategies (some studies tested >1):** Late fusion `10/15` (`66.7%`), Early fusion `7/15` (`46.7%`), Middle fusion `5/15` (`33.3%`); `2` classification studies (`Botros et al. [57]`, `Lee et al. [64]`) and `1` prognosis study (`González et al. [60]`) compared strategies and found late fusion outperformed early/middle fusion (pp. 5–6).
  - **Algorithms:** CNNs for image/ECG (`7` studies: DenseNet, ResNet, EfficientNet), BERT-variant Transformers for clinical text (`3` studies: BERT, BlueBERT, BioBERT) plus SE+Transformer for ECG (`1` study), and ensemble GBDTs/RF for tabular data (`6` studies, p. 7 §3.4).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Validation Partitioning Across Corpus (Table 2 pp. 4–5; p. 14):** `13/15` (`86.7%`) evaluated on **internal holdout sets only**; only **`2/15` (`13.3%`)** conducted external validation (`Gao et al. [59]`, `González et al. [60]`), both exhibiting sharp external performance drops (e.g., Gao internal AUROC `83.9` $\rightarrow$ external `76.7`, Table 3 p. 8; p. 14). **`0/15` prospective interventional clinical deployments** (p. 14).
- **Random-Effects AUROC Meta-Analysis (`12/15` reported AUROC, `8/12` with 95% CIs, p. 7 §3.5, Fig. 5 p. 13):**
  - **HF Classification Pooled AUROC:** **`92.83` (`95% CI: 88.91–96.73`, $p < 0.001$)**; heterogeneity **$I^2 = 100\%$ ($p < 0.001$)** (p. 7 §3.5, Fig. 5 p. 13).
  - **HF Prognosis Pooled AUROC:** **`84.00` (`95% CI: 72.86–95.14`, $p < 0.001$)**; heterogeneity **$I^2 = 99.94\%$ ($p < 0.001$)** (p. 7 §3.5, Fig. 5 p. 13).
  - **Reporting Completeness Audit (`p. 7`, `Fig. 4` p. 12):** AUROC reported in `12/15` (`8` with CIs), F1 in `8/15` (`6` with CIs), Accuracy in `8/15` (`3` with CIs), Recall in `9/15` (`4` with CIs), Precision in `7/15` (`3` with CIs), Specificity in `6/15` (`3` with CIs).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` — explicitly finds that **calibration across decision thresholds and cost-effectiveness analyses were NOT systematically evaluated in any of the reviewed studies** (`p. 14`).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not evaluated` across the reviewed corpus, p. 14).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** Meta-analytic random-effects $p < 0.001$ and heterogeneity $I^2$ tests reported (`p. 7`). **Formal publication bias testing (e.g., funnel plot / Egger's test) was NOT conducted** (`p. 14`).

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Possible publication and English-language bias (no formal publication bias assessment); deliberately broad HF definition causing metric heterogeneity; `60%` small-study share (typo `"100–1,000"` vs `<10,000` in Table 2); pooled AUROC ($I^2 = 100\%$) represents an upper bound rather than clinical expectation; computational complexity reported by only `1/15` study (`Lee et al. [64]`, p. 14).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** High-value 2026 systematic review (`CRD420250654631`) directly validating **every major gap** (`PG1–PG5`) our FYDP addresses in CVD/HF multimodal learning: documents that (i) `0/15` studies integrated $\ge 3$ distinct modalities or combined Lifestyle/Genetics with ECG+Clinical (`PG2`, `PG4`); (ii) `9/15` relied on MIMIC Western cohorts with **zero South Asian/Bangladeshi studies**; (iii) `13/15` lacked external validation and `0/15` were prospective; (iv) **zero** studies systematically evaluated calibration across decision thresholds or cost-effectiveness/DCA; and (v) XAI in `7/15` studies was *"only superficially addressed"* without quantitative faithfulness (`PG3`, `PG5`).
