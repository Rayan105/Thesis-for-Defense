# P0013 — The Application of Multimodal Data Fusion Algorithm MULTINet in Postoperative Risk Assessment of TAVR

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `He2025_P0013`
- **DOI:** `10.3390/jcm14248620` (`https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm14248620`, p. 1)

- **Authors:** Wei He, Jiawei Luo, Xiaoyan Yang (p. 1)
- **Venue & Year:** *Journal of Clinical Medicine*, Vol. 14, Issue 24, `8620`, 2025 (Received 31 October 2025; revised 27 November 2025; accepted 2 December 2025; published 5 December 2025, p. 1)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Retrospective secondary analysis of ICU/hospital records from **Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center (BIDMC)**, Boston, Massachusetts, USA (`MIMIC-IV v3.0`, 2008–2019; TAVR procedures screened via ICD-9/ICD-10 `procedures_icd` [printed `"proceres_icd"`], pp. 3, 14). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** (ethnicity is not reported).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** `773` TAVR procedure records screened $\rightarrow$ after excluding repeated hospitalizations and repeated surgeries, **`761` unique TAVR patients/records** (`subject_id`) included (p. 8 §3). Demographics: mean age `79.6` years (`SD 9.33`, p. 8; note p. 10 states *"median age of 82 years (SD: 9.33)"*; `<80`: `307 [40%]`, `≥80`: `454 [60%]`, Table 3 p. 10); Male `402` (`52.8%`), Female `359` (`47.2%`, p. 8, Table 3 p. 10).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** **`32` deaths** within 30 days post-TAVR out of `761` patients (**`4.2%`** mortality rate, p. 8 §3, p. 14 §4.4).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Binary postoperative **30-day all-cause mortality** following Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement (TAVR) (pp. 1, 3, 4).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (3 of 4 target modalities + Clinical Text; NO Lifestyle; NO Echocardiography):**
  - **Laboratory Tests ($x_l$):** Most recent preoperative laboratory results imputed via MICE + Rubin's rules (p. 3 §2.2), linear projection to `768`-dim.
  - **Clinical / ICU Time-Series Monitoring ($x_t$):** Asynchronous ICU monitoring data aligned onto uniform temporal grid via cross-cohort average interval $\bar{\Delta}_j$ (Eq. 1, pp. 3–4), aggregated within window $\delta$ (mean/mode), filled via LOCF + linear interpolation + null for discrete (p. 4), linear projection to `768`-dim. *(Note: Demographics + machine-measured ECG metrics like `p_end`, `t_end`, `p_axis` and ICU scores like `CAM-ICU Inattention` and `ventilator flow rate` appear in IG feature rankings, Fig. 4 p. 11.)*
  - **12-lead ECG Waveforms ($x_e$):** Last preoperative clean 12-lead ECG (`500 Hz`, `10 s`; replaced with prior clean ECG if abnormal, p. 4), embedded via frozen pretrained **ECG-FM** (Wav2Vec 2.0-style foundation model, McKeen et al. 2024, pp. 4, 14) to `768`-dim (fine-tuned ECG-FM evaluated in Table 2, p. 10).
  - **Clinical Text ($x_n$):** Machine-generated ECG summary reports and clinicians' narrative CT/MRI reports encoded via **Clinical ModernBERT** (Lee et al. 2025, pp. 4, 14–15).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** **Lifestyle:** `none / not reported`; **Echocardiography:** explicitly omitted (noted on p. 14).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** Raw 12-lead ECG at `500 Hz` over `10 s` (`p. 4`), encoded via frozen ECG-FM (plus machine-measured ECG interval parameters in structured features, p. 4, Fig. 4 p. 11).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Dual mechanism: MICE/LOCF/interpolation at variable level (pp. 3–4) + **Masked Multi-Head Cross-Attention (`M-MHCA`)** with binary modality mask matrix $M_u \in \{0,1\}^{B \times L \times D}$ gating latent state updates (Eqs. 2–4, pp. 4–6) and **Attention Pooling Fusion** zeroing absent modalities while amplifying surviving modalities and fused latent tokens (demonstrated on missing ICU time-series, Fig. 3B, pp. 10–11).
- **Model Families & Baselines:** `MULTINet` (`768`-dim projections, 1-layer 8-head Transformer, $256 \times 768$ learnable latent matrix, dropout `0.1`, LayerNorm, Dual Focal Loss $\alpha=0.25, \gamma=3$ [Eq. 5], Adam `lr=1e-4`, `20` epochs, L2 reg, p. 6) compared against `HAIM-XGBoost` [13] and `MedFuse` (LSTM fusion [27], Table 1 p. 8), plus internal ablations (`Median interpolation`, `Uni-modality` without cross-attention, `Multi-modality` latent-only, `Mean pooling`, `ECG-FM finetuned`, Table 2 p. 10). **Omission:** Zero single-modality baselines (`ECG-only`, `Lab-only`, `Time-series-only`, `Text-only`).
- **Fusion Strategy:** Dual-branch architecture combining parallel unimodal embeddings with iterative latent cross-modal interaction (`M-MHCA` updating $H_{\text{latent}}$), unified via masked **Attention Pooling Fusion** (Fig. 1, pp. 4–6).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** **`5`-fold cross-validation** (`random seed = 2025`) producing concatenated out-of-fold (OOF) predictions, with `95% CIs` via `1,000`-sample bootstrapping; thresholds chosen by maximizing Youden Index ($\text{TPR}-\text{FPR}$, pp. 6–7). No external validation cohort.
- **Main Multimodal Benchmark (5-fold CV OOF + 95% CI, Table 1 on p. 8):**
  - **`MULTINet` (frozen ECG-FM):** AUC **`0.9153` (`0.87–0.95`)**, AUPR **`0.5708` (`0.48–0.66`)**, Recall **`0.8051` (`0.71–0.90`)**, Brier **`0.0269` (`0.02–0.03`)**.
  - **`XGBoost` (`HAIM`):** AUC `0.8958` (`0.85–0.93`), AUPR `0.4053` (`0.26–0.57`), Recall `0.7793` (`0.62–0.90`), Brier `0.0343` (`0.03–0.04`).
  - **`MedFuse`:** AUC `0.5571` (`0.53–0.61`), AUPR `0.2487` (`0.15–0.32`), Recall `0.3089` (`0.15–0.47`), Brier `0.2496` (`0.24–0.25`).
- **Ablation Results (Table 2 on p. 10):**
  - `ECG-FM finetuned`: AUC **`0.9286` (`0.88–0.96`)** *(higher AUC & Recall than frozen MULTINet)*, AUPR `0.5325` (`0.38–0.70`), Recall **`0.8463` (`0.71–0.96`)**, Brier `0.0287` (`0.02–0.03`).
  - `Uni-modality`: AUC `0.9015` (`0.85–0.94`), AUPR `0.5553` (`0.39–0.70`), Recall `0.7491` (`0.59–0.89`), Brier `0.0329` (`0.03–0.04`).
  - `Mean pooling`: AUC `0.8977` (`0.84–0.94`), AUPR `0.4385` (`0.28–0.61`), Recall `0.7790` (`0.63–0.90`), Brier `0.0328` (`0.03–0.04`).
  - `Multi-modality` (fusion only): AUC `0.8715` (`0.86–0.88`), AUPR `0.4192` (`0.31–0.50`), Recall `0.6845` (`0.53–0.83`), Brier `0.0389` (`0.03–0.04`).
  - `Median interpolation`: AUC `0.7242` (`0.62–0.81`), AUPR `0.2517` (`0.11–0.41`), Recall `0.6586` (`0.50–0.81`), Brier `0.0377` (`0.03–0.04`).
  - **Missing metrics:** F1-score, Precision, Specificity, Accuracy, and single-modality ablations are `not reported`.
- **Subgroup Analysis (Table 3 on p. 10):**
  - Age `<80` (`307, 40%`): AUC `0.8808` (`0.78–0.96`), AUPR `0.5907` (`0.52–0.64`), Recall `0.8165` (`0.62–0.98`), Brier `0.0268` (`0.02–0.03`).
  - Age `≥80` (`454, 60%`): AUC `0.8779` (`0.76–0.97`), AUPR `0.5565` (`0.55–0.56`), Recall `0.8541` (`0.64–0.99`), Brier `0.0269` (`0.02–0.03`).
  - Male (`402, 53%`): AUC `0.8812` (`0.83–0.93`), AUPR `0.5108` (`0.37–0.65`), Recall `0.8208` (`0.72–0.92`), Brier `0.0361` (`0.03–0.04`).
  - Female (`359, 47%`): AUC `0.8803` (`0.80–0.94`), AUPR `0.5715` (`0.36–0.78`), Recall `0.8013` (`0.78–0.82`), Brier `0.0336` (`0.03–0.04`).
  - **Mathematical Anomaly Warning:** All 4 subgroup AUCs (`~0.88`) sit well below the full-cohort AUC (`0.9153`), and BOTH Male (`0.0361`) and Female (`0.0336`) Brier scores are substantially worse than the reported overall cohort Brier score (`0.0269`)—a mathematical impossibility for a single unified set of OOF predictions unless computed on inconsistent subsets/folds.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** **`Yes`** — Calibration curves (Fig. 2 left, p. 9) and Brier score (`0.0269`, Table 1 p. 8). ECE and calibration slope/intercept are `not reported`.
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** **`Yes`** — DCA net-benefit curve (Fig. 2 right, p. 9) demonstrating positive net benefit superior to XGBoost, MedFuse, Treat All, and Treat None.
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** **`No`** (`not reported` — no DeLong test or p-value comparing `MULTINet` `0.9153 [0.87–0.95]` vs `XGBoost` `0.8958 [0.85–0.93]`, whose 95% CIs overlap substantially).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — UNCLEAR / HIGH RISK:** Small single-center ICU cohort (`N = 761`, only **`32` events [4.2%]**), no external validation, and inclusion of parallel/combined surgeries (p. 14).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH RISK:** Data preprocessing (§2.2, pp. 3–4) applies **MICE imputation** across laboratory features and computes cohort-wide mean observation intervals $\bar{\Delta}_j$ (`Eq. 1`, summed over all $i=1\dots N$ patients) **prior to** 5-fold CV splitting (§2.4, p. 6), with no confirmation of fold-isolated fitting (pre-split imputation/resampling leakage risk). Replacing abnormal preoperative ECGs with prior clean ECGs (p. 4) introduces manual selection bias. Furthermore, ICU time-series predictors appearing in top IG features (`CAM-ICU Inattention`, `ventilator flow rate`, `BiPap O2 Flow`, Fig. 4 p. 11) lack explicit cutoff timestamps proving strict pre-TAVR isolation.
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW RISK:** 30-day all-cause mortality in MIMIC-IV is objective (`32/761`, p. 8).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Severe **events-per-variable (EPV) overfitting risk**: `32` positive deaths (~`6–7` deaths per fold) trained on a `768`-dim dual-branch Transformer (`256 × 768` latent matrix) over a single seed (`2025`); authors themselves admit low event rate amplifies variance and *"may lead to overly optimistic results"* (p. 14).
  - Threshold tuned by Youden Index (p. 7) without confirming fold-strict isolation; zero single-modality baselines; zero DeLong tests; and unexplained **Table 3 mathematical discrepancy** (both Male/Female Brier `0.0361/0.0336` worse than whole-cohort `0.0269`).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Single-center MIMIC-IV dataset lacking external validation; small positive sample size (`32` deaths, `4.2%`) increasing overfitting/variance risk; multimodal missingness; potential confounding by combined surgeries and unmeasured perioperative management; and absence of transthoracic echocardiography (`AVA`, peak jet velocity, mean gradient, pp. 13–14).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Methodologically informative blueprint (`J. Clin. Med.`, 2025) showing how **masked cross-attention latent fusion (`M-MHCA`)** and **Attention Pooling Fusion** dynamically handle missing modalities (`Fig. 3B`) while combining calibration (`Brier`, calibration plots) and clinical utility (`DCA`) (`PG1`, `PG2`, `PG5`). However, it cannot serve as an unbiased baseline due to **High PROBAST ROB** (`32` events in a `768`-dim Transformer, potential pre-CV MICE/$\bar{\Delta}_j$ leakage, Table 3 Brier anomaly), omits Lifestyle (`PG4`), omits single-modality ablations, and lacks quantitative XAI faithfulness (`AOPC`) or stability validation (`PG3`, `PG5`).
