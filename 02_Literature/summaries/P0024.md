# P0024 — Abnormality prediction and forecasting of laboratory values from electrocardiogram signals using multimodal deep learning

- **Citation Key:** `Alcaraz2025_P0024`
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-025-26715-5` (`https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-26715-5`, pp. 1, 9)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Retrospective secondary analysis of US critical care/emergency department EHR and ECG data from Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston, USA (`MIMIC-IV`, `MIMIC-IV-ECG`, `MIMIC-IV-ECG-ICD`, `p. 2`).
  - **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **Not specifically reported / disaggregated.** `Table 1 (p. 5)` and text (`p. 4`) report ethnicity across samples as Caucasian `251,872 (65.33%)`, African `64,733 (16.79%)`, Hispanic `22,509 (5.83%)`, **Asian `11,795 (3.05%)`**, and Other `28,633 (7.42%)` — South Asian or Bangladeshi ancestry is not broken out within the `3.05%` Asian category.
- **Sample Size ($N$):** **`385,480` samples from `127,994` adult patients** ($\ge 18$ years, `p. 2`, `p. 3 Fig. 2`). Sex: `194,343 (50.41%)` male, `191,137 (49.58%)` female (`p. 4, p. 5 Table 1`). Median age `64` years (`IQR: 25`, quantile span `18–91` years, `p. 4, p. 5 Table 1`).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Covers **`560` distinct binary abnormality labels** (`341` abnormally high, `219` abnormally low) derived from **`102` unique laboratory tests** (`p. 2, p. 3 Fig. 2`). Requiring $\ge 10$ positive and $\ge 10$ negative cases per fold/val/test leaves **`137` abnormalities** for abnormality prediction (`±60 min`) and **`116`**, **`126`**, and **`135` abnormalities** for `30-`, `60-`, and `120-minute` forecasting, respectively (`p. 2`). Exact per-label prevalence and positive counts reside in unbundled online `Supplementary Tables S3–S4` (`not reported` in main PDF, `p. 4`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon (`pp. 2–3, Fig. 1`):**
  - *Abnormality prediction (`Fig. 1B, p. 3`):* Binary classification (normal vs abnormal) of the closest laboratory measurement within `±60 minutes` of the ECG using ECG + vital signs within `±30 minutes` (`p. 2`).
  - *Abnormality forecasting (`Fig. 1C, p. 3`):* Binary classification of whether *any* abnormal lab value occurs within future horizons of **`+30`**, **`+60`**, or **`+120` minutes** post-ECG (`p. 2`).
  - *Thresholding:* Defined using the dataset-wide **median** of MIMIC-IV patient-specific lab reference thresholds (`p. 2`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (2 of 4 target modalities; Labs are prediction targets, NOT predictors):**
  - **12-lead ECG:** Raw `10-second` 12-lead ECG waveforms (`p. 2, p. 4`).
  - **Clinical/EHR (Demographics, Biometrics, Vital Signs):** Gender, age, race (`Caucasian, African, Asian, Latino, Other`), `6` vital signs (`temperature °F, heart rate, respiration rate, SpO2, SBP, DBP` closest within `±30 min` of ECG), and `3` biometrics (`BMI, weight, height`) (`p. 2, p. 5 Table 1`).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** **Lifestyle:** `none / not reported`; **Laboratory/Biomarkers:** modeled solely as multi-label binary **output targets**, not input features (`pp. 1–3`).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** Original `10 s` at `500 Hz` downsampled to **`100 Hz`** (`p. 4`). Trained on random `2.5-second` segments; test predictions averaged over four non-overlapping `2.5-second` segments (`p. 4`).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** For tabular metadata, **training-set median imputation** paired with a **binary missingness indicator column** per feature (`p. 2, p. 3 Fig. 2`). For unmeasured target lab outcomes, a special mask token excludes missing targets from loss and evaluation (`p. 2, p. 3 Fig. 2`).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - *ECG Branch:* **S4 structured state space classifier** (`4` bidirectional S4 blocks, dimension `512`, state size `8`), chosen based on prior benchmarking against ResNets, CNNs, RNNs, and Transformers (`p. 4`).
  - *Tabular Branch:* **3-layer MLP** with learned embedding layers for categorical features (gender, ethnicity) (`p. 4`).
  - **Critical Omission:** **Zero unimodal baselines** (`ECG-only S4` vs `Tabular-only MLP`) or competing multimodal fusion benchmarks are evaluated on this task within the paper (`pp. 4–7`).
- **Fusion Strategy:** **Late concatenation fusion** combining output representations from the S4 ECG encoder and 3-layer tabular MLP (`p. 4`). Trained with `AdamW` (`lr = 0.001`, weight decay `0.001`), batch size `64`, `20` epochs, binary cross-entropy loss, selecting best validation checkpoint (`p. 4`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** `20` stratified folds adopted from `MIMIC-IV-ECG-ICD` (`Strodthoff et al. [11]`), allocated **`18:1:1`** (train:validation:test, `p. 2, p. 4`). Note: P0024 cites balanced stratification across gender, age groups, and diagnoses (`p. 4`), without explicitly stating in the PDF text whether the `385,480` samples from `127,994` patients (`~3.01` ECGs/patient) are strictly patient-disjoint across folds.

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Large hospital/ICU cohort (`385,480` samples, `127,994` patients, `p. 2`) with minimal selection exclusions beyond $\ge 18$ yrs and $\ge 1$ available lab label; however, single-center US ICU/ED source with **zero external validation** (`pp. 2, 7`), and patient-level exclusivity of the inherited `Strodthoff et al. [11]` splits is not explicitly re-verified in P0024 (`p. 4`).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — UNCLEAR / HIGH RISK:** Train-only median imputation + missingness indicator masks are clean (`p. 2`), **but predictor timing introduces potential leakage:** Abnormality Prediction (`Fig. 1B, p. 3`) uses vital signs up to `±30 min` *around* (including post-) ECG to estimate labs `±60 min` *before or after* ECG, and Forecasting (`Fig. 1C, p. 3`) also allows vital signs up to **`+30 min` after ECG** while forecasting labs within `[0, +30]` min (`p. 2`).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — HIGH / UNCLEAR RISK:** Reference thresholds were defined by taking a **dataset-wide global median** of patient-specific MIMIC-IV thresholds (`p. 2, p. 7 Limitations`), ignoring age/sex reference ranges, and forecasting evaluates whether *any* abnormal lab occurs in the horizon without conditioning on normal-to-abnormal transitions (`pp. 7–8`).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **No unimodal ablation** (`ECG-only` vs `Vitals/Demographics-only` vs `Multimodal`) to quantify how much predictive signal actually comes from the ECG waveform (`pp. 4–7`).
  - **No calibration** (`Brier/ECE/calibration slope`), **no DCA**, **no external validation** (`p. 7`), and selective main-text reporting of only the `24` top labels (`CI > 0.70`, `pp. 6–7`) while omitting macro AUROC and ~`113` other evaluated abnormalities (`p. 2`).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (`p. 7`):** Global median reference thresholds ignore demographic variation (age/sex); binary high/low framing lacks ordinal or continuous regression granularity; forecasting window does not pinpoint exact transition time or restrict to normal-to-abnormal transitions (`pp. 7–8`); single-center US ICU population requires diverse external cohort testing (`pp. 2, 7`).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:**
  - High-scale MIMIC-IV-ECG benchmark (`385,480` samples, `127,994` patients) establishing that 12-lead ECG + clinical metadata strongly encodes cardiovascular (`NTproBNP AUROC 0.903–0.916`) and renal/hematological biomarkers (`Creatinine`, `Hemoglobin`), supporting our ECG-Lab physiological cross-talk premise (`PG1, PG2`). However, P0024 predicts labs *from* ECG rather than fusing `ECG + Clinical + Labs + Lifestyle` for CVD event prediction, uses late concatenation rather than cross-modal attention (`PG5`), omits unimodal ablations, calibration (`RQ4`), external validation, South Asian representation (`PG4`), and all explainability (`PG3, RQ3`).
  - **Discrepancies vs `papers.csv`:** None substantive — `papers.csv` accurately records `DOI` (`10.1038/s41598-025-26715-5`), `Year` (`2025`), `Modalities` (`12-lead ECG; Clinical/EHR`), `XAIEvaluated` (`No`), and `CitationKey` (`Alcaraz2025_P0024`), while our extraction newly specifies `385,480` samples / `127,994` patients, `3.05%` Asian aggregate ethnicity (`p. 5 Table 1`), missing unimodal ablation, and `High` PROBAST risk.
