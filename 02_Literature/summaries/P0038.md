## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Multi-center retrospective consecutive cohort across **4 regional tertiary cardiovascular referral centers in China**, coordinated by **Renji Hospital, Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine** (`Center 1`, Apr 2017–Jul 2023; `Center 2`, Sep 2015–Sep 2023; `Center 3`, May 2018–Jun 2022; `Center 4`, Mar 2017–Jul 2024; central IRB `RJRE[2018]093`, pp. 1–2, 15, 20). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** (East Asian Chinese hospital cohorts; authors acknowledge multi-national/diverse demographic external validation is still needed, p. 15).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** **`2,137` consecutive first-time STEMI patients** (`840,835` CMR images and `29,741` EHR items, p. 2): `Center 1` (`n = 413`), `Center 2` (`n = 871`), `Center 3` (`n = 282`), `Center 4` (`n = 571`) (Table 1 p. 3). Inclusion: first-time STEMI treated with primary PCI (`PPCI`) within `12 h` of symptom onset and `3-T` CMR within `7 days` post-reperfusion (median `4 days`, IQR `3–6`, p. 15). Cohort demographics (Table 1 p. 3): mean age `57.9 ± 11.8` years, `1,771` (`82.9%`) male, BMI `25.3 ± 3.7` kg/m² (`7.4%` missing).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** **`279` MACE events (`13.1%`)** overall over a median follow-up of `37.0` months (`IQR: 21.0–51.2`, followed up to December 31, 2024; Table 1 p. 3, p. 16):
  - `Center 1`: `66 / 413` (`16.0%`), median f/u `36.0` mo (`19.0–52.0`)
  - `Center 2`: `104 / 871` (`11.9%`), median f/u `32.0` mo (`17.0–49.0`)
  - `Center 3`: `43 / 282` (`15.2%`), median f/u `39.0` mo (`30.0–45.0`)
  - `Center 4`: `66 / 571` (`11.6%`), median f/u `43.0` mo (`26.2–59.0`)
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** **Time-to-event Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events (MACE)** from discharge after index STEMI (events prior to CMR excluded, p. 16): composite of all-cause mortality, myocardial reinfarction (4th Universal Definition), unplanned revascularisation (not index/staged within `60` days), or heart failure hospitalization (clinical consensus or symptomatic fluid overload + diuretics + LVEF `<50%` and/or elevated natriuretic peptides), centrally adjudicated by **3 independent cardiologists** ($\ge 10$ years experience) **blinded to all predictions and research imaging** (p. 16). Continuous horizon is discretized into $K_t = 4$ quartile intervals derived solely from uncensored training patients (pp. 2, 18–19).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (3 data streams; 2 of 4 target FYDP modalities + 6-sequence CMR, NO ECG):**
  - **Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance (`CMR`, 6 intra-modal sequences):** `CINE-SA` (`100%` available across all 4 centers), `LGE` (`96.1%–100%`), `T2WI`, `native T1 mapping (nT1m)`, `enhanced T1 mapping (eT1m)`, and `T2 mapping (T2m)` (`3-T` Philips/GE/Siemens/United Imaging scanners; ROI-cropped `224 × 224`, pp. 2–3, 15–16). Realistic center-dependent missingness: e.g., `T2m` available in `76.2%` of Center 3 vs `7.6%` of Center 2; `eT1m + T2m` co-occurrence $\le 5.1\%$ (p. 3).
  - **Clinical/EHR + Laboratory/Biomarkers + Clinical Text (`EHR` serialized text branch):** Age, sex, BMI, cardiovascular risk factors (`smoking` `50.4%`, `hypertension` `54.6%`, `diabetes` `31.7%`, `dyslipidaemia` `41.3%`), reperfusion (`pre/post-PCI TIMI flow`, `18.0%–22.7%` miss), biomarkers (`hsCRP` `25.4%` miss, `HbA1c` `19.5%` miss, `NT-proBNP` `27.2%` miss, harmonized `Peak troponin I` `4.6%` miss), concomitant medications (unstructured text, p. 15), structured CMR read-outs (`LVEF/RVEF/LGE burden` at Center 1 only), and **CMR radiology reports** (junior/senior dual-reviewed CINE, T2WI, first-pass perfusion, and LGE findings/impressions; Table 1 p. 3, pp. 15–16).
  - **Clinical Reasoning Branch (`LRM`):** Fine-tuned `LLaMA3.1-8B-Instruct` (`LoRA r=32`) generates patient-specific chain-of-thought rationales from EHR text alone, concatenated back to raw EHR text before encoding via `FLAN-T5 (base)` (`LoRA r=16`, `768-d`, pp. 2, 17–18).
  - **Excluded Target Modalities:** **ECG:** `None` (ECG ST-elevation was only an inclusion criterion, p. 15). **Lifestyle:** Smoking status only (`50.4%`, Table 1 p. 3); diet, physical activity, alcohol, and sleep are `none / not reported`.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `None` (no ECG waveforms or ECG features modeled).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy (`pp. 3, 9, 16–18`):**
  - **CMR Intra-Modal Missingness:** Self-supervised `3D Uniformer (small)` encoder pre-trained directly on **naturally incomplete clinical sequences** (no synthetic random dropout) with two-stage attention pooling (aggregating $K$ available non-cine embeddings into $\tilde{z}^{\text{non}}_i$, then fusing with universal $z^{\text{cine}}_i$ into $z^{\text{img}}_i$), trained via **Center Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{center}}$)** + **Modality Contrastive Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{mod}}$)** ($\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{center}} + \gamma\mathcal{L}_{\text{mod}}$, $\gamma=1.0$, $\tau=0.1$, Eqs. 1–6, p. 17).
  - **Tabular/EHR Missingness:** Encodes structured fields + explicit `Unknown: information not available or not measured` markers + free-text reports directly through `FLAN-T5` without fixed-matrix mean imputation (p. 16, Figs. 7–8 pp. 13–14).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - Survival baselines: `Cox-PH` (tabular EHR only) and `DeepSurv` (multimodal with MACE-MAIS pre-trained image embeddings, p. 5).
  - Self-supervised vision baselines (Table 3 p. 9): `DINOv2`, `MoCov3`, `MAE`, `SwinUnetr`, `BrainMVP`.
  - Reasoning LLM baselines: `GPT-4o` (zero-shot), `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` (fine-tuned), `LLaMA3.1-8B-Instruct` (fine-tuned, final LRM), plus `DeepSeek-R1` and `LLaVA-Med` in expert rating (Tables 7–8 p. 10, Fig. 6 p. 12).
- **Fusion Strategy (`MACE-MAIS`, pp. 2, 18):** Frozen `3D Uniformer` image embedding projected via MLP (`1 × 768`) + `FLAN-T5` combined EHR/rationale embedding (`1 × 768`) fused via **gated-attention pooling** (`Eq. 7`, p. 18) $\rightarrow$ MLP $\rightarrow$ discrete-time survival head (`4` intervals, censorship-rebalanced NLL with $\alpha = 0.4$, Eqs. 8–11, p. 18).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split & Leakage Controls (p. 19):**
  1. **Within-Center Internal Evaluation:** Patient-level `80% / 20%` split (`seed = 42`) per center with 3-fold CV inside the 80% training split for checkpoint selection.
  2. **Leave-One-Center-Out (`LOCO`) External Evaluation:** Train on 3 centers, evaluate on held-out 4th center (`seed-free` cross-hospital transfer).
  3. **Leakage Safeguards (p. 19):** Train-only quartile survival bins; train-only image pre-training (frozen downstream); train-only LRM fine-tuning; fixed hyperparameters (`lr=1e-4`, `wd=1e-5`, `α=0.4`, `γ=1.0`, `40` epochs) across all centers.
- **Internal Evaluation Discrimination (`80/20` patient-level split, pp. 4–5, Table 3 p. 9, Figs. 2–3):**
  - **C-index:** Center 1 `0.6132`, Center 2 `0.7617`, Center 3 `0.7130`, Center 4 `0.6762`, **Mean `0.6910`** (`+0.043` over `DeepSurv`, p. 5).
  - **Time-Dependent Survival AUC (p. 4):** Center 1 `0.6998 (95% CI: 0.6791–0.7173)`, Center 2 `0.7358 (0.7176–0.7462)`, Center 3 `0.7204 (0.6962–0.7346)`, Center 4 `0.7520 (0.7406–0.7601)`, **Mean `0.7270`** (`+0.044` over `DeepSurv`, p. 5).
  - **KM Log-Rank (a priori median cutoff, Fig. 2 p. 4):** C1 $P = 8.54 \times 10^{-2}$ (NS), C2 $P = 3.10 \times 10^{-3}$, C3 $P = 5.89 \times 10^{-3}$, C4 $P = 2.22 \times 10^{-4}$.
- **External LOCO Discrimination (pp. 5–6, Fig. 4):**

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — LOW RISK:** Consecutive multi-center enrollment across 4 Chinese hospitals (`n = 2,137`) with clear eligibility criteria (p. 15), patient-level 80/20 splits, and 4-fold Leave-One-Center-Out external validation (p. 19). Missingness per center (`7` unexported Center 4 rows = `1.2%`) is transparently reported (Table 1 p. 3). Narrow clinical spectrum (`first-time STEMI + PPCI + 3T CMR ≤7 days`) is explicitly acknowledged as an applicability scope boundary (p. 15).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Uniform cross-site preprocessing (p. 16), blinded CMR analysis (p. 15), and train-only pretraining/LRM fine-tuning (p. 19). Minor caveats: (i) Stage 2 training-time rationale correction exposed GPT-4o to ground-truth $y$, though shuffled-rationale (`0.6238`) and uncorrected-only LRM (`0.6823`, NS vs `0.6910` at $P > 0.05$, Table 8 p. 10) empirically rule out label leakage; (ii) EHR text includes radiology impressions embedding radiologists' visual readings (Table 1 & pp. 12–13).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW RISK:** Standardized MACE criteria, pre-CMR events excluded, median `37.0-mo` follow-up from discharge, and central blinded 3-cardiologist adjudication (p. 16).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Discrete-time survival modeling with censoring weighting (`α=0.4`), train-only bin boundaries, patient-level bootstrap 95% CIs, Bonferroni corrections, IBS calibration, DCA, and fixed a priori median KM cutoffs (pp. 4–7, 18–19). Minor issues: internal DCA panels (Fig. 5 A, C, E, G) reflect the `80% internal subset` rather than strictly the 20% test fold (though LOCO panels B, D, F, H are external held-out centers); no standalone calibration plot/ECE; minor typographical slips (`-0.04 to -0.007` instead of `-0.004 to -0.007` in Table 6 caption p. 9; total center $n$ printed in Fig. 2 caption p. 4).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **Low Risk** *(with minor caveats on 80%-subset internal DCA panels and Stage 2 rationale correction, rigorously checked via control experiments).*

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:**
  1. **Patient-Specific Chain-of-Thought Clinical Rationales (`LRM`):** Fine-tuned `LLaMA3.1-8B-Instruct` produces structured step-by-step explanations integrating CMR, biomarkers, risk factors, and TIMI perfusion (Figs. 7–8 pp. 13–14).
  2. **Intrinsic Gated-Attention Modality Pooling (`Eqs. 2–3, 7`, pp. 17–18):** Architectural modality weights ($\alpha_{i,m}$), though numeric weight distributions are not reported. **Zero SHAP, Grad-CAM, or Integrated Gradients** on the 3D CMR encoder or survival head.
- **Faithfulness & Clinical Validity Evaluations Conducted:**
  1. **Blinded Multi-Expert Clinical Review (`n = 150`, Fig. 6 p. 12, p. 11):** Three independent senior cardiologists evaluated de-identified, randomly ordered rationales (`XML/markdown/model tags stripped`) across 4 anchored `0–25` criteria (`Efficiency`, `Clinical Validity`, `Completeness`, `Accuracy`). `MACE-MAIS` scored **`94.89%`**, outperforming `GPT-4o` (`+9.53 pp`), `DeepSeek-R1` (`+12.64 pp`), and `LLaVA-Med` (`+41.25 pp`, all $P < 0.001$), with **inter-rater `ICC = 0.91` (`95% CI: 0.87–0.94`)** and length-stratified significance ($P < 0.001$).
  2. **Causal / Perturbation Rationale Controls (`Tables 7–8`, p. 10):** Removing rationale drops C-index `0.6910 → 0.6189`; **shuffling rationales across patients** collapses C-index to `0.6238` (proving instance-level predictive faithfulness).
  3. **Novel Reasoning vs Paraphrase Audit (`Table 9`, p. 11):** `ROUGE-L F1 = 0.17`, `BLEU-4 = 0.07`, `48%` novel unigrams, `SBERT cosine = 0.62`.
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **`Quantitative Faithfulness Evaluated` (Structured Blinded 3-Cardiologist Review [`ICC = 0.91`, `n = 150`] + Shuffled/Ablated Rationale Perturbation Controls; No Pixel/Tabular SHAP/Grad-CAM AOPC)** *(Consistent with `papers.csv` `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`, clarifying that faithfulness is proven via blinded clinician scoring and rationale-shuffling ablations rather than image saliency AOPC).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Restricted to `first-time STEMI + PPCI + CMR ≤7 days` at Chinese tertiary centers (excluding NSTEMI and non-CMR patients); lacks genomics/transcriptomics; composite cause-specific hazard ignores competing risks for specific MACE sub-endpoints; regulatory/prospective trials still needed before deployment (p. 15).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Gold-standard **methodological benchmark (`npj Digital Medicine`, 2026)** for our FYDP (`PG1–PG5`): (1) demonstrates how **missing-modality-aware contrastive + center-loss pretraining** trained on *naturally incomplete* clinical data tolerates systematic real-world modality dropout without synthetic imputation; (2) provides a rigorous **PROBAST-compliant validation template** (patient-level splits, LOCO external validation, train-only quantile binning, IPCW **IBS calibration**, **8-panel DCA**, 1,000-resample bootstrap CIs, Bonferroni adjustments, and blinded cardiologist evaluation with `ICC = 0.91`); and (3) defines clear complementary gaps for our FYDP (`PG2`, `PG4`): `P0038` models **zero ECG waveforms**, **zero Lifestyle modalities** beyond smoking, **zero South Asian / Bangladeshi data**, and **zero pixel/feature-level SHAP AOPC faithfulness**.
