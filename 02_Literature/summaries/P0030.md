Bangladeshi participants specifically: `none / not reported`. *(Important caveat: see severe dataset-provenance red flags below regarding how cardiovascular waveforms and CAC scores were purportedly obtained across SEED, UKBB, ARIC, and MESA.)*

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (1 of 4 target modalities explicitly modeled + Retinal Fundus + PPG; Clinical/Lifestyle gaps):**
  - `Retinal Fundus Imaging` (`3072 × 2048` resolution; U-Net vessel segmentation [Dice `0.92`] converted into hierarchical vessel graphs encoding caliber, tortuosity, arteriovenous ratio [`AVR`], bifurcations, fractal dimension, and vessel density; pp. 7, 9, 15 Table 8).
  - `Cardiovascular Signals (ECG + PPG/PWA)`: `5-minute` windows (`1000 Hz` ECG, `250 Hz` PPG) yielding HRV LF/HF power, arterial stiffness index (`ASI`), mean RR interval, and SBP/DBP (`pp. 7, 9, 15 Table 9`).
  - **Excluded / Ambiguous Target Modalities:** `Laboratory/Biomarkers`: `none` (listed as future scope, p. 16). `Lifestyle`: `none` in model inputs (p. 14 refers vaguely to *"lifestyle constraints"*, and Table 13 mentions lifestyle advice as output, not input). `Clinical/EHR`: hypertension, diabetes, hypercholesterolemia, and CAD history are mentioned as dataset labels/risk factors (`p. 9`), and `EFS-CP` uses undefined "clinical prior constraints" ($\pi_i$, p. 8), but no structured EHR encoder branch is specified in Figs. 1–2 or §III.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** Claimed `1000 Hz` ECG over `5-minute` windows (`pp. 9–10`), detrended and bandpass filtered (`0.04–0.15 Hz` for HRV, p. 9), transformed via Continuous Wavelet Transform (`CWT`, Eq. 4, p. 7). **ECG lead count is `not reported`.**
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Quality filtering via vessel segmentation threshold (`Dice 0.90`, p. 14) to drop noisy images; missing-data imputation and missing-modality handling are `not reported`.
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - Proposed pipeline (`pp. 7–10, 13`): `HRV-GT` (U-Net $\rightarrow$ GCN `64` hidden units + `8`-head `128`-dim Graph Transformer) + `STC-Transformer` (CWT $\rightarrow$ dual spectral/temporal Transformer, `4` layers, `256` hidden units each) $\rightarrow$ `SMM Fusion` (`512`-dim Geometric Preserving Transformation with $\lambda=0.1$, `3`-layer MLP adversarial discriminator with dropout `0.3`, Sparse Autoencoder) $\rightarrow$ `EFS-CP` (Genetic Algorithm: `100` generations, pop `50`, mutation `0.05` + Boltzmann clinical priors + RL ranking) $\rightarrow$ `CHRC-Net` (contrastive head $\tau=0.07$, `2` layers/`128` units + hierarchical 4-class Softmax + entropy uncertainty threshold $H(Z_s) > \delta$, flagging `7.3%` ambiguous cases, p. 14).
  - Baselines (`pp. 10–13, Tables 2–6`): `Method [3]` (re-implemented CNN fundus + LSTM ECG), `Method [8]` (re-implemented XGBoost HRV classifier), `Method [18]` (re-implemented ResNet-50 retinal + feedforward signal early fusion), plus SEED early-fusion `CNN-LSTM`, late-fusion `SVM`, `Logistic Regression`, `Random Forest`, and unimodal/early/late fusion ablations (`p. 13`). *(Severe citation-integrity issue: refs `[8]` and `[18]` are literature review papers—not primary algorithms—yet p. 13 claims they were re-implemented in-house!)*
- **Fusion Strategy:** Intermediate latent-space alignment via **Sparse Manifold Multimodal Fusion (`SMM Fusion`)** (`pp. 8, 10, 13`).

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Computational overhead relative to FRS/ASCVD (`37 ms` inference, `<250 MB` model size), predominantly cross-sectional design without longitudinal EHR progression modeling, need for multi-ethnic fairness regularization, and need for prospective trials plus future SHAP/Grad-CAM incorporation (`pp. 13–14, 16`).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Conceptually targets cross-population generalizability—notably referencing Singapore's `SEED` cohort with a South Asian (`Indian`, `AUC 0.94`) subgroup (`p. 13`)—alongside `PR-AUC`, `Brier`, and `ECE` (`RQ4`). However, **`P0030` exhibits severe data-provenance, reproducibility, and editorial integrity failures** (impossible UKBB/SEED multimodal pairing claims, absent cohort/event tables, missing plots, conflicting numbers across tables/figures, citation mismatches, `"Indian bidding"` prose artifact, and zero verifiable XAI faithfulness), making its reported `0.97` AUC uncredible as an empirical benchmark (`PG1–PG5`).
- **Discrepancies vs `papers.csv`:** `XAIEvaluated` (`Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`) is **false** (unsubstantiated prose-only SHAP/Grad-CAM claims, zero faithfulness metrics); `SampleSize` (`See summary`) comprises claimed `20,000` UKBB + `35,000` SEED = `55,000` (plus unspecified ARIC/MESA validation subsets from `15,792` and `6,814` total cohort sizes) with zero event counts; `PROBASTRisk` assigned **`High Risk`**.
