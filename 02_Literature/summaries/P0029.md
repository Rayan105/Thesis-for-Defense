## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Single-center retrospective cohort from the Department of Cardiology at **Kobe University Hospital**, Kobe, Japan (`January 2012–November 2022`; Ethics approval No. `B230191`, pp. 1–2, 9). **South Asian / Bangladeshi / Singapore representation:** `none / not reported` (single-center Japanese cardiology cohort; race/ethnicity not reported in Table 1, p. 5).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** **`7,483` unique adult patients ($\ge 18$ years) contributing `23,886` paired ECG–CXR records** (`pp. 1, 2, 3 Fig. 1, 5`). *(Note internal wording error on p. 2 Data sources: "The dataset included 23,886 patients", whereas Abstract p. 1, Data preprocessing p. 2, Fig. 1 p. 3, and Results p. 5 clarify `23,886` records from `7,483` patients.)* Baseline cohort characteristics (`Table 1`, p. 5, $N = 7,483$): mean age `66 ± 16` years (AS `79 ± 9.2` vs Non-AS `64 ± 15.9`, $p < 0.01$); female `2,948 (39.4%)`; atrial fibrillation `1,129 (15.0%)`; LVEF `59 ± 12.3%`; AV-Peak V `2.73 ± 1.35 m/s`; AV-mPG `22.9 ± 21.3 mmHg`; AVA `1.4 ± 0.83 cm²`.
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** **`608` of `7,483` unique patients (`8.1%`)** diagnosed with AS (`6,875` non-AS [`91.9%`], pp. 1, 5 Table 1). Record-level split breakdown (`p. 5`):
  - Training: `16,720` records from `4,489` unique patients (`846` AS [`5.06%`], `15,874` non-AS).
  - Validation: `3,583` records from `1,497` unique patients (`308` AS [`8.60%`], `3,275` non-AS).
  - Test: `3,583` records from `1,497` unique patients (`288` AS [`8.04%`], `3,295` non-AS).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Cross-sectional/diagnostic binary **Aortic Stenosis (AS)** classification (`AS` vs `non-AS`, intentionally including moderate AS for earlier screening, pp. 1, 2, 8), defined echocardiographically by $\ge 1$ criterion: aortic valve peak velocity $\ge 2.5\text{ m/s}$, mean pressure gradient ($\text{mPG}$) $\ge 20\text{ mmHg}$, or aortic valve area ($\text{AVA}$) $\le 1.5\text{ cm}^2$ (pp. 1, 2). Linking windows: CXR within $\pm 3$ months ($\pm 90$ days, closest selected) of echocardiography, and ECG within $\pm 1$ week ($\pm 7$ days) of CXR (pp. 2, 3 Fig. 1). Longitudinal horizon ($\Delta t$): `not applicable / not reported`.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (1 of 4 target modalities + 1 imaging modality; 2 total):**
  - `12-lead ECG` raw waveforms (`15-s` recordings sampled at `500 Hz`, cropped to random `2-s` segments `[12 × 1,000]`, pp. 2, 3 Fig. 2).
  - `Chest X-ray (CXR)` frontal images (`DICOM` converted to 8-bit images resized to `224 × 224` pixels, pp. 2, 4).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** `Clinical/EHR` (`none`), `Laboratory/Biomarkers` (`none`), `Lifestyle` (`none / not reported`).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `12-lead ECG` at `500 Hz`, `1.25 µV` resolution, `150 Hz` low-pass filter (`p. 2`). During training, random `2-s` segments are cropped from `15-s` recordings (`12 × 1,000` matrix, Fig. 2 p. 3); augmentation zeroes baseline and adds random noise within `0.1 mV` (p. 2). *(Test-set cropping protocol across the `15-s` ECG—center crop vs multi-crop averaging—is `not reported`.)*
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Complete-case linkage requiring Echo + CXR ($\pm 90$ d) + ECG ($\pm 7$ d); missing-data imputation or inference with missing modalities is `not reported`.
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - **ECG Branch:** Shared `1D ResNet50` extracting `256`-dim features per lead + `[CLS]` token $\rightarrow$ `Transformer encoder` + linear head (p. 4). *(Hyperparameter reporting discrepancy on p. 4: "ECG model" subsection specifies batch size `32` and LR `1e-3` to `1e-7`, whereas "Training and hyperparameter selection" specifies PyTorch Lightning FP16, `CosineAnnealingLR` $T_{\max}=10$, batch size tuned over `{64, 128}`, LR `[1e-6, 1e-3]`, max `100` epochs, patience `20`.)*
  - **CXR Branch:** ImageNet-pretrained `EfficientNet` (`224 × 224`, final sigmoid activation, p. 4, Fig. 1 p. 3). *(Contradiction: p. 6 "Model visualization" text refers to applying Grad-CAM to *"the ResNet architecture for CXR images"* rather than EfficientNet.)*
  - **Benchmarked Baselines:** Unimodal ECG-only Transformer Classifier and unimodal CXR-only model (`Table 2`, p. 6). **Missing Baselines:** Zero early-fusion or late-fusion multimodal baselines are benchmarked in Table 2 despite Discussion (`p. 8`) claiming cooperative learning outperforms early and late fusion; no clinical/EHR baseline.
- **Fusion Strategy:** **Cooperative Learning** (Ding et al. [11], p. 4), minimizing prediction MSE plus an agreement penalty controlled by $\rho \in [0, 1]$ (tuned via Bayesian optimization on validation AUROC; optimal $\rho$ value `not reported`):
  $$\min_{(f_{\text{ecg}}, f_{\text{cxr}})} \mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{2}\left(y - (f_{\text{ecg}}(X_{\text{ecg}}) + f_{\text{cxr}}(X_{\text{cxr}}))\right)^2 + \frac{\rho}{2}\left(f_{\text{ecg}}(X_{\text{ecg}}) - f_{\text{cxr}}(X_{\text{cxr}})\right)^2\right]$$

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Random split **by patient ID** with zero patient overlap (`Fig. 1` p. 3, `p. 2`, `p. 5`): `4,489` patients (`16,720` records) train, `1,497` (`3,583` records) val, `1,497` (`3,583` records) test. *(Note: `16,720/3,583/3,583` reflects a `70%/15%/15%` split of **records**, whereas unique **patients** split `60.0%/20.0%/20.0%`.)* No external validation cohort (p. 9).
- **Held-Out Test Metrics (`Table 2`, p. 6; `1,000` bootstrap iterations, median & `95% CI`):**
  - **ECG Model:** AUROC `0.775 (0.753–0.796)`, AUPRC `0.187 (0.161–0.218)`, Accuracy `0.900 (0.893–0.907)`, Precision `0.130 (0.113–0.161)`, Recall `0.740 (0.635–0.794)`, Specificity `0.681 (0.635–0.798)`, F1 `0.221 (0.196–0.256)`.
  - **CXR Model:** AUROC `0.755 (0.732–0.777)`, AUPRC `0.160 (0.134–0.187)`, Accuracy `0.938 (0.932–0.944)`, Precision `0.121 (0.106–0.140)`, Recall `0.755 (0.680–0.814)`, Specificity **`0.051 (0.046–0.068)`**, F1 `0.209 (0.186–0.234)`.
  - **Multimodal Cooperative Model:** AUROC **`0.812 (0.792–0.832)`** (point bootstrap `0.8111`, p. 6; AUROC diff `95% CI` `0.015–0.058` vs ECG, `0.032–0.080` vs CXR), AUPRC **`0.300 (0.256–0.345)`**, Accuracy `0.859 (0.850–0.868)`, Precision `0.160 (0.139–0.189)`, Recall `0.752 (0.672–0.807)`, Specificity `0.741 (0.704–0.806)`, F1 `0.263 (0.235–0.298)`.
  - *Internal Arithmetic & Figure Discrepancies (`Table 2` & `Fig. 3`, p. 6):* CXR Specificity `0.051` alongside Accuracy `0.938` and Recall `0.755` at `8.04%` test prevalence is mathematically incompatible at any single threshold; similarly ECG Accuracy `0.900` disagrees with Recall `0.740` / Spec `0.681` (implied Acc `~0.686`); Fig. 3 legend mislabels curves (`XP model [AUROC = 0.775]` and `ECG model [AUROC = 0.755]`, swapping ECG and CXR values).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported` — no Brier score, ECE, or calibration curves).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `Yes` — two-tailed bootstrap significance tests comparing multimodal vs unimodal models (Table 2, p. 6: AUROC $p = 0.004$ vs ECG, $p < 0.001$ vs CXR; AUPRC $p < 0.001$ vs both; F1 $p = 0.100$ vs ECG, $p < 0.001$ vs CXR).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH / UNCLEAR RISK:** Patient-ID disjoint splitting (`4,489 / 1,497 / 1,497`, Fig. 1 p. 3, p. 5) prevents direct patient leakage, which is a strength. However, single-institution tertiary cardiology selection requiring Echo + CXR + ECG introduces **referral/indication bias**, CXR–Echo windows span $\pm 90$ days (allowing post-diagnosis imaging), multiple records per patient (`3,583` test records from `1,497` patients) are bootstrapped without explicit patient-clustered resampling, and **zero external validation** exists (p. 9).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — UNCLEAR RISK:** ECG test-crop selection rule across 15-s recordings is unstated; contradictory backbone (`EfficientNet` p. 4 vs `ResNet` p. 6) and ECG optimizer/batch specifications (p. 4) weaken reproducibility.
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Standardized guideline echocardiographic AS criteria (`peak V ≥ 2.5 m/s`, `mPG ≥ 20 mmHg`, `AVA ≤ 1.5 cm²`, p. 2), though temporal ordering (`ECG/CXR` strictly pre-Echo vs post-Echo) and blinding are `not reported`.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Complete omission of **probability calibration** (`Brier/ECE/plot`) and **DCA**.
  - Severe **Table 2 / Fig. 3 reporting/arithmetic anomalies** (CXR Spec `0.051` vs Acc `0.938`; ECG Acc `0.900` vs Recall/Spec `0.740/0.681`; swapped Fig. 3 legend).
  - Omits early/late fusion multimodal comparators, optimal $\rho$, and operating-threshold selection methodology.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:**
  1. **CXR Branch:** `Grad-CAM` spatial heatmaps (`Fig. 4`, p. 7) + **Quantitative Perturbation-Based Faithfulness Ablation** (`pp. 5, 7–8`): progressively deleted/inserted top-$p\%$ Grad-CAM pixels ($p \in \{18, 30, 40, 50\}$) with Gaussian blur, scoring positive-class logit and summarizing by **AOPC** (`1,000`-sample bootstrap 95% CIs) vs area-matched random and uniform map baselines:
     - **Insertion AOPC:** Grad-CAM `1.807 (95% CI: 1.779–1.838)` $>$ area-matched random `1.759 (95% CI: 1.729–1.791)` (positive faithfulness).
     - **Deletion AOPC:** Grad-CAM `1.803 (95% CI: 1.775–1.834)` vs area-matched random `1.846 (95% CI: 1.818–1.878)` (did **not** outperform random; uniform baseline `AOPC ≈ 1.805–1.802` in external `Supplementary Figs. S1A,B`, p. 8).
  2. **ECG Branch:** Two-step visualization combining Transformer lead-level attention weights (Phase 1) and `1D-ResNet50` final-layer Grad-CAM (Phase 2) (`pp. 4–5`, `Fig. 5`, p. 7). **Zero quantitative faithfulness testing (no ECG deletion/insertion AOPC)**, zero cross-modal fusion attribution, zero multi-seed stability, and zero structured clinician plausibility scoring exist.
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **Quantitative Faithfulness Evaluated (Partial — CXR Grad-CAM AOPC Only; Mixed Deletion Result; ECG/Multimodal Visual Only)** *(Clarifies `papers.csv` tag `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`: genuine AOPC deletion/insertion testing with 1,000-bootstrap CIs and random/uniform baselines was executed for the CXR Grad-CAM branch—succeeding on insertion [`1.807` vs `1.759`] but failing to beat random on deletion [`1.803` vs `1.846`]—whereas ECG Transformer+Grad-CAM and multimodal cooperative explanations received visual inspection only.)*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Single-institution homogeneous dataset without external validation; low AS prevalence (`8.1%`) without oversampling/class-weighting exploration; reduced performance in patients with pacemakers or severe chest wall deformities; qualitative ECG/CXR visualization without multicenter confirmation (pp. 8–9).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Direct methodological precedent for **AOPC deletion/insertion faithfulness auditing** (`RQ3`, `PG3`) with bootstrap CIs and random/uniform controls, plus **cooperative multimodal learning** between a `1D-ResNet50 + Transformer` ECG encoder and imaging (`PG1–PG2`, `PG5`). However, `P0029` targets structural AS from `ECG + CXR` only—omitting `Clinical EHR`, `Laboratory biomarkers`, and `Lifestyle` modalities (`PG2`, `PG4`), omitting calibration and DCA (`RQ4`), suffering `High PROBAST risk` from Table 2/Fig. 3 metric contradictions, and evaluating AOPC only on the CXR branch rather than across all fused modalities (`RQ3`).
- **Discrepancies vs `papers.csv`:** `XAIEvaluated` (`Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`) must be qualified as **partial** (CXR Grad-CAM AOPC only, with negative deletion result vs random; ECG/multimodal visual only); internal PDF discrepancies (`23,886 patients` typo p. 2, `EfficientNet` vs `ResNet` p. 6, Table 2 CXR Spec `0.051`, Fig. 3 swapped legend) noted above.
