# P0032 — Explainable multi-modal approach for uncovering key predictors of stroke-risk from ECG, EMG, blood pressure, and respiratory signals

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Krayem2026_P0032`
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-026-37777-4` (`https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-37777-4`, p. 1)
- **PubMed ID (PMID):** `not reported` in PDF
- **Authors:** Jalal Krayem, Lily Wong, Lai Kuan Tham, Shiuan-Ni Liang (Monash University Malaysia & Universiti Malaya, Malaysia, pp. 1, 21)
- **Venue & Year:** *Scientific Reports*, `(2026) 16:6906` (Received 08 November 2025; Accepted 25 January 2026; Published online 02 February 2026, pp. 1, 18)
- **Reading Status:** Full-Text Verified (`pp. 1–22`)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Secondary retrospective analysis of the public PhysioNet **Cerebral Vasoregulation in Elderly with Stroke (`CVES`)** dataset (`Novak et al., Stroke 2010` [61], Boston, USA physiological study; authors affiliated with Malaysian universities, pp. 1, 3, 18, 20). **South Asian / Bangladeshi / Singapore representation:** `none / not reported` (race/ethnicity not reported in Table 2, p. 9).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** Original `CVES` dataset includes `120` subjects (`60` stroke + `60` non-stroke, p. 3). After excluding subjects with missing signals or incomplete tests, **`64` unique subjects** remained (`Table 2`, p. 9):
  - **Stroke group:** `37` subjects (`19` female, `18` male; age `65.16 ± 8.60` yrs; BMI `27.72 ± 5.04` kg/m²).
  - **Control group:** `27` subjects (`15` female, `12` male; age `66.89 ± 6.79` yrs; BMI `25.52 ± 4.53` kg/m²).
  - **Windowed Sample Count:** Signal segmentation (`14-s` windows with `75%` overlap) generated **`1,908` samples** (`p. 14`: *"batch size of 128, 1908 samples (after windowing)"*).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** `37` stroke (`57.8%`) vs `27` non-stroke control (`42.2%`) among `64` subjects (`p. 9`; training SHAP baseline probability `E[f(X)] = 0.571`, Fig. 8 p. 13).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** **Retrospective case-control classification of chronic post-stroke vs non-stroke status** (`p. 3`: *"All stroke patients in this dataset experienced a stroke at least six months prior to their inclusion"*; controls had no stroke history and no focal neurological deficit). Despite the title/abstract phrasing ("stroke-risk prediction"), longitudinal incident stroke prediction horizon ($\Delta t$) is `not applicable` (post-stroke chronic physiology).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (1 of 4 target modalities + 3 other biosignal modalities; 7 physiological channels total):**
  - `2-lead ECG`: `ECG1` (V5/V6-L clavicle) and `ECG2` (V1/V2-L clavicle), recorded at `1000 Hz` and downsampled to `500 Hz` (`pp. 3–4 Table 1, 9`).
  - `EMG`: `EMG1` (right gastrocnemius) and `EMG2` (left gastrocnemius), `1000 Hz` downsampled to `500 Hz` (`pp. 3–4 Table 1, 9`).
  - `Continuous Blood Pressure (BP)`: Finger BP via `Finapres` at `500 Hz` (`pp. 3–4 Table 1`).
  - `Respiratory Signals`: End-tidal $\text{CO}_2$ and respiration flow rate via `Capnomac Ultima` at `500 Hz` (`pp. 3–4 Table 1`).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** `Clinical/EHR` (not fused in model; age/sex/BMI checked only in Table 2), `Laboratory/Biomarkers` (`none`), `Lifestyle` (`none / not reported`).
- **ECG & Signal Format / Feature Engineering:** `10-min` sit-to-stand protocol (`5 min` sit + `5 min` stand) trimmed to `9.5 min` (`570 s`, pp. 3, 9), downsampled/aligned to `500 Hz`, windowed into **`14-s` segments with `75%` overlap** (`p. 9`). From each of the 7 channels, **`18` handcrafted features** were extracted (`14` statistical: mean, SD, variance, median, skewness, kurtosis, `3rd–6th` moments [note p. 4 prose omits `6th` moment while Eq. 6 p. 5 includes $q \in \{3,4,5,6\}$], min, max, zero crossings, mean energy + `4` spectral: FFT energy, spectral entropy, LF power `0.04–0.15 Hz`, HF power `0.15–0.4 Hz`), totaling **`126` features** (`18 × 7 = 126`, confirmed p. 14), z-score normalized (`Eq. 17`, p. 6).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** **Complete-case exclusion** (`120` $\rightarrow$ `64` subjects) and last-`30 s` signal trimming (`p. 9`).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - **Proposed Model:** Single-layer perceptron (`126` inputs $\rightarrow$ linear weighted sum + bias $\rightarrow$ sigmoid with fixed `0.5` threshold, no hidden layers; Adam, `700` epochs, LR `0.03`, batch size `128`, pp. 7, 9, 14).
  - **Baselines (`p. 7, Fig. 5 p. 11, Table 7 p. 17`):** Raw-signal DL models (`CNN`, `GaborCNN`, `LSTM`, `BiLSTM`, `CNN-LSTM`, `CNN-BiLSTM`) and feature-based ML/DL models (`Logistic Regression`, `Voting Classifier`, `Random Forest`, `XGBoost`, `E-ESN`, `KNN`, `Decision Tree`), each trained with and without respiratory signals.
- **Fusion Strategy:** **Early feature-level concatenation** of `126` z-score normalized statistical/spectral features across all 7 channels (`pp. 6–7, 14`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** `80%` train / `20%` stratified test split prior to windowing/feature extraction (`p. 4`, `Fig. 1`), with `5-fold` stratified cross-validation on the `80%` development partition for window/hyperparameter search (`pp. 4, 9`). No external validation cohort (`p. 18`).
- **5-Fold Cross-Validation Accuracy (`p. 9`, `Fig. 4` p. 10):** `84.91% ± 5.48%` with respiratory signals vs `59.36% ± 12.15%` without respiratory signals (paired $t$-test $p = 0.0061$, p. 9). *(Note: Abstract p. 1 cites `84.91%` from CV, whereas test-set tables/plots report `88.29%`.)*
- **Held-Out Test Set Metrics (`Table 4` p. 16, `Fig. 5` p. 11):**
  - **Proposed Single-Layer Perceptron (`All 7 signals`):** Accuracy **`88.29%`** (`66.34%` without respiratory), Precision **`94.94%`**, Recall **`85.53%`**, Specificity **`92.70%`**.
  - **Competing Baselines (`Fig. 5`, p. 11; All signals vs Without respiratory):** `Logistic Regression (F)` `79.87%` vs `64.10%`; `Voting Classifier (F)` `75.13%` vs `65.51%`; `Random Forest (F)` `68.72%` vs `65.64%`; `XGBoost (F)` `67.18%` vs `68.72%`; `E-ESN (F)` `66.49%` vs `69.42%`; `KNN (F)` `66.03%` vs `56.41%`; `CNN (R)` `65.20%` vs `64.42%`; `GaborCNN (R)` `60.31%` vs `58.00%`; `LSTM (R)` `59.80%` vs `56.43%`; `CNN-BiLSTM (R)` `59.79%` vs `58.54%`; `Decision Tree (F)` `59.49%` vs `57.95%`; `CNN-LSTM (R)` `58.06%` vs `56.35%`; `BiLSTM (R)` `52.11%` vs `58.66%`.
  - **Unreported Discrimination Metrics:** **`ROC-AUC`**, **`PR-AUC`**, **`F1-score`**, and **`95% CIs`** are **`not reported`** (explicitly deferred to Future Work on `p. 18`).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `Yes` (partial) — paired $t$-test across 5 CV folds comparing proposed model with vs without respiratory signals ($p = 0.0061$, `p. 9`) and baseline demographic $t$-tests (`Table 2`, `p. 9`); no statistical significance testing against competing ML/DL baselines on the test set.

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:** Very small cohort (`64` subjects retained after excluding `56/120 [46.7%]` subjects for missing/incomplete signals without attrition analysis, `p. 9`), expanded via overlapping `14-s` (`75%` overlap) windows (`1,908` samples). **Case-control / reverse-causality bias:** stroke cases had suffered ischemic stroke **$\ge 6$ months prior** to recording (`p. 3`), capturing chronic post-stroke physiology rather than prospective incident stroke risk. **No external validation** (`p. 18`).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Although `p. 4` & `Fig. 1` state the `80/20` split preceded windowing/feature extraction, Eq. 17 (`p. 6`) computes feature normalization mean/SD *"between all samples"* without explicitly proving train-only scaler fitting; window size (`14 s, 75%`) and optimizer parameters were grid-searched across highly autocorrelated intra-subject overlapping windows (`~51` training subjects).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — HIGH / UNCLEAR RISK:** Labels represent prior chronic stroke ($\ge 6$ months post-event) rather than incident stroke prediction; blinding `not reported`.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Tiny test-set subject count (`20%` of `64` $\approx 13$ subjects) with `126` features on `~51` training subjects (`EPV < 1` at subject level).
  - Omits **`ROC-AUC`**, **`PR-AUC`**, **`F1-score`**, **`95% CIs`** (acknowledged `p. 18`), **calibration** (`Brier/ECE/plot`), and **DCA**; uses fixed `0.5` cutoff despite imbalanced training prior (`0.571`, Fig. 8 p. 13).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:**
  1. **`SHAP` (`Figs. 6–8`, pp. 11–13):** Global mean $|SHAP|$, beeswarm, and 2 local waterfall plots; top-3: `CO2 mean` (`~0.15`), `CO2 variance` (`~0.14`), `BP mean` (`~0.14`).
  2. **`LIME` (`Figs. 9–10`, pp. 14–15):** Aggregated $|weight|$ global plot + 2 local plots; top-3: `CO2 zero crossings`, `Flow rate mean`, `CO2 variance`.
  3. **`Anchors` (`Figs. 11–12`, p. 16):** Global rule-frequency count + 2 local rule sets ($\tau=0.95$); top-3: `CO2 mean` (`61` appearances), `EMG2 median` (`54`), `ECG2 mean` (`42`).
- **Quantitative Faithfulness Evaluation (`MEMC`, Eq. 31 p. 9, `Tables 3–6` pp. 16–17, `Fig. 13` p. 17):**
  - Computed **Model Explanation Metric Consensus (`MEMC`, $V=3$)** by sequentially deleting the top-1, top-2, and top-3 ranked features for each explainer and summing drops in `Accuracy`, `Precision`, `Recall`, and `Specificity`:
    - **`SHAP` (`Table 4` p. 16):** Acc `88.29% → 86.70% → 85.58% → 85.10%` (`-3.19%` at top-3), Prec `94.94% → 92.13%`, Rec `85.53% → 82.86%`, Spec `92.70% → 88.86%` $\rightarrow$ **`MEMC = 8.76`** (`Fig. 13`, p. 17).
    - **`Anchors` (`Table 6` p. 17):** Acc `88.29% → 86.70% → 86.16% → 86.26%` $\rightarrow$ **`MEMC = 6.11`**.
    - **`LIME` (`Table 5` p. 17):** Acc `88.29% → 87.66% → 86.41% → 86.41%` $\rightarrow$ **`MEMC = 4.13`**.
  - *Methodological Limitations of Faithfulness Evaluation:* Evaluates only top-3 features (out of `126`) without full-curve deletion/insertion `AOPC`, lacks a random-feature deletion control, runs on a linear single-layer perceptron (`126` weights + sigmoid), and omits multi-seed stability correlation or clinician plausibility assessment.
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **Quantitative Faithfulness Evaluated (`MEMC` Top-3 Feature Deletion Across `SHAP`, `LIME`, and `Anchors` — No Random Control, Insertion, Stability, or Clinician Audit)** *(Confirms `papers.csv` tag `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)` via MEMC deletion testing, while noting its `V=3` linear-model scope).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Absence of external stroke validation datasets; reliance on post-hoc explainers (`SHAP/LIME/Anchors`) and time-consuming global aggregation of local explainers; missing `95% CIs` and `ROC-AUC` (`p. 18`).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Direct quantitative precedent for **comparative XAI faithfulness benchmarking** (`SHAP` outperforming `Anchors` and `LIME` under perturbation removal, `RQ3`, `PG3`). However, `P0032` studies only `64` chronic post-stroke subjects (`High PROBAST risk`), uses `2-lead` ECG + non-clinical signals (EMG, $\text{CO}_2$, flow, finger BP) without `12-lead` morphology, `Clinical EHR`, `Labs`, or `Lifestyle` (`PG1–PG2`, `PG4`), uses a linear single-layer perceptron rather than cross-modal Transformers (`PG5`), omits `ROC-AUC`, `PR-AUC`, `CIs`, `calibration`, and `DCA` (`RQ4`), and limits faithfulness testing to top-3 `MEMC` without full `AOPC` or random controls (`RQ3`).
- **Discrepancies vs `papers.csv`:** `SampleSize` (`See summary`) is `64` subjects (`37` stroke, `27` control) / `1,908` `14-s` windows; `XAIEvaluated` (`Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`) is **confirmed** via `MEMC` (`SHAP = 8.76` > `Anchors = 6.11` > `LIME = 4.13`); `PROBASTRisk` assigned **`High Risk`**; minor PDF text omission (`p. 4` omits `6th` moment listed in Eq. 6 `p. 5` yielding `18 × 7 = 126` features).
