# P0020 — Multimodal Fusion Artificial Intelligence Model to Predict Risk for MACE and Myocarditis in Cancer Patients Receiving Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Therapy

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Ayoub2025_P0020`
- **DOI:** `10.1016/j.jacadv.2024.101435` (`https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacadv.2024.101435`, p. 1)
- **PubMed ID (PMID):** `not reported`
- **Authors:** Chadi Ayoub, Lalith Appari, Milagros Pereyra, Juan M. Farina, Chieh-Ju Chao, Isabel G. Scalia, Ahmed K. Mahmoud, Mohammed Tiseer Abbas, Nima Ali Baba, Jiwoong Jeong, Steven J. Lester, Bhavik N. Patel, Reza Arsanjani, Imon Banerjee (p. 1)
- **Venue & Year:** *JACC: Advances*, Vol. 4, No. 1, 2025 (`101435`; Received: June 12, 2024; Revised: October 7, 2024; Accepted: October 8, 2024; © 2025 The Authors / ACCF, p. 1)
- **Reading Status:** Full-Text Verified (`pp. 1–11`; note online TRIPOD+AI appendix referenced on p. 11 is not bundled in PDF)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Retrospective cohort across 3 academic Mayo Clinic sites in the USA (`Rochester, MN`; `Phoenix, AZ`; `Jacksonville, FL`, Jan 2011–Feb 2022, p. 2). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** `none / not reported` (no race/ethnicity breakdown in Table 1 on p. 4 or anywhere in text).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** `2,258` unique cancer patients treated with immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICI) who had troponin laboratory testing—selected from `5,413` total ICI-treated patients (`3,155` [`58.3%`] excluded for lacking troponin measurement, p. 2). Mean age `68.5 ± 11.5` years; `1,349` (`59.7%`, rounded `60%` in Table 1) male, `909` (`40%`) female; median follow-up `299` days (`IQR: 800 days`, pp. 1, 4, Table 1).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Composite cardiovascular adverse event outcome occurred in **`264` (`11.7%`) unique patients**, totaling `428` overall events reported in text (`p. 1, p. 4, p. 5 Central Illustration`), while Table 1 (`p. 4`) component counts sum to `424`: ICI-related myocarditis `59 (3%)`, cardiac death `59 (3%)`, heart failure `151 (7%)`, myocardial infarction `84 (4%)`, and stroke `71 (3%)`. Median time to composite onset: `143` days (`IQR: 309` days); ICI-myocarditis: `44` days (`IQR: 74` days); MACE: `177` days (`IQR: 316` days) (`p. 5`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Binary classification at ICI initiation of composite post-ICI cardiovascular adverse events (Bonaca et al.-defined ICI-related myocarditis + MACE [new HF diagnosis, MI, ischemic stroke/TIA, cardiac death]) through census date February 2022 (`p. 2, p. 4`). All-cause mortality was excluded (`p. 2`), and time-to-event modeling was not performed (`p. 9`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (4 of 4 target modalities partially represented, with major gaps):**
  - **2-lead ECG only (subset of 12-lead):** Pre-ICI 12-lead ECG images converted to 1D raw signals for **2 leads only (`Lead II` and `Lead V1`)** (`p. 2, p. 3, Fig. 1`).
  - **Clinical/EHR:** Categorized demographics (age `<40`, `40–60`, `60–80`, `>80`, sex), comorbidities (hypertension, hyperlipidemia, diabetes, stroke/TIA, MI, AF, VT, family history of premature CAD, previous radiation, cancer types; `pp. 3–4, Table 1, Fig. 3`).
  - **Laboratory/Biomarkers:** Categorized normal/abnormal `troponin` (`97.6%` Troponin T, `2.4%` Troponin I, `90.8%` high-sensitivity; median `3` measurements, `IQR: 4`), `creatinine`, `NT-proBNP`, and `CRP` (`p. 3, p. 4, p. 8 Fig. 3`). **Leakage ambiguity:** Methods (`p. 2`) state *"Laboratory testing values both pre- and post-ICI administration were electronically extracted from the EMR"* without confirming strict pre-ICI cutoff for all predictor lab bins.
  - **Lifestyle:** **Smoking only** (self-reported composite of current and previous smoking, `1,285 [57%]`, `p. 4 Table 1, p. 7`). Diet, physical activity, alcohol, and pack-years are absent.
  - *(Ablation modality)*: Baseline echocardiogram report tested as an extra input (`ECG + EMR + echocardiogram` lowered AUC to `0.670`, `p. 3, p. 6`).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `15`-second acquisitions of leads II and V1 digitized from pre-ICI ECG images yielding `2,500 × 2` samples per patient (`~166.7 Hz` effective rate), normalized to `[0, 1]` (`p. 2, p. 3, Fig. 1`).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Drops tabular features with `>5%` missing data and encodes remaining missing categorical values as `-1` in a `32`-dimensional EHR vector (`p. 3`). **Internal contradiction:** `p. 3` states COPD, OSA, and cardiac medications were dropped for `>5%` missingness, yet `p. 7` and `Fig. 3 (p. 8)` show `COPD` (rank 5) and `OSA` (rank 14) active in Baseline 1 SHAP!
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - *Baseline 1 (Tabular EMR + Labs + Smoking):* Calibrated Random Forest (`CaliForest`, Platt scaling, `p. 4`).
  - *Baseline 2 (ECG-only):* 5-layer 1D CNN (Conv1D blocks [outputs `512, 256, 128, 64, 32`; filters `20, 20, 10, 10, 5`], BatchNorm, MaxPooling, Dropout [`0.2` in Fig. 1 p. 3 vs `"2% dropout"` in text p. 4], Global Average Pooling $\rightarrow$ 32-dim embedding, `pp. 3–4`).
  - *Missing Baselines:* No framingham/SCORE2 clinical score, no XGBoost/CatBoost/LightGBM baseline, and no Transformer/cross-attention architecture (`PG1–PG2`, `PG5`).
- **Fusion Strategy:** End-to-end **joint (intermediate) fusion** concatenating the `32`-dim 1D CNN ECG embedding (`X1`) and `32`-dim 2-layer MLP tabular embedding (`X2`) into `Dense(32) -> Dropout(0.2) -> Dense(32) -> Dropout(0.2) -> Dense(1, sigmoid)` (`Fig. 1 p. 3, p. 4`). Trained with batch size `16`, Adam, exponential LR scheduler (`0.001`), early stopping; text inconsistently describes the loss as *"focal categorical cross-entropy loss"* and *"weighted categorical cross-entropy loss"* (`p. 4`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Patient-level random split of `2,258` unique patients: `60%` training (`n = 1,355`), `20%` validation (`n = 452`), `20%` test (`n = 451`) (`p. 5, Central Illustration`). No external validation cohort (`p. 9`).
- **Held-Out Test Results (`Table 2, p. 7`, `Fig. 2, p. 6`, text `p. 6`):**
  - **Baseline 1 (Tabular Calibrated RF):** Mean AUC `0.645` (`95% CI: 0.63–0.64` [point estimate exceeds upper CI bound!]), Accuracy `72%` (`69.1–72%`), NPV `0.9769` (`0.96–0.98`), TPR `57.14%` (`56.2–58%`).
  - **Baseline 2 (ECG-only 1D CNN):** Mean AUC `0.595` (`0.57–0.60`; text `p. 6` rounds to `0.59`), Accuracy `55.19%` (`54.2–55.6%`), NPV `0.9653` (`0.96–0.97`), TPR `50%` (`49.2–50.4%`).
  - **Joint Fusion Model (ECG + EHR/Labs/Smoking):** Mean AUC **`0.717`** (`95% CI: 0.70–0.72`; Abstract reports `0.72`), Accuracy `69.13%` (`68.1–70%`), NPV `0.98` (`0.97–0.98`), TPR `64.28%` (`63.43–64.3%`), PPV `0.08` (`p. 6`).
  - **Ablations (`p. 6`):** Composite (`MACE + myocarditis`) AUC `0.717` vs `MACE alone` AUC `0.672`; adding baseline echocardiogram report (`ECG + EMR + echo`) degraded AUC to `0.670` (`p. 6`).
  - **Unreported Metrics:** `PR-AUC`, `F1-score`, and `Specificity` are `not reported`.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` for the Joint Fusion model (`p. 9` explicitly defers model calibration to future research; only Baseline 1 RF used Platt scaling, `p. 4`, with no Brier score, ECE, or calibration curve reported anywhere).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `No` (`not reported` — no DeLong test or p-value comparing Joint Fusion vs Baseline 1/2 AUCs).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Indication / selection bias:** Excluding `3,155` of `5,413` (`58.3%`) ICI patients lacking troponin tests (`p. 2`) enriches for patients clinically suspected of cardiac symptoms.
  - **Single-system pooling:** Patients from 3 Mayo Clinic sites were pooled and randomly split rather than cross-site validated; no external test cohort (`p. 5, p. 9`).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH / UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - **Temporal lab leakage risk:** Methods (`p. 2`) state pre- and post-ICI lab values were extracted from EMR without proving post-ICI troponin/creatinine/NT-proBNP values were excluded from baseline tabular inputs.
  - **Contradictory feature dropping & coarse binning:** Text (`p. 3`) claims COPD and OSA were dropped (`>5%` missingness), yet both appear in Baseline 1 SHAP (`p. 7, Fig. 3 p. 8`); continuous labs/age are coarsely discretized (`p. 3, p. 9`), and smoking is unquantified self-report (`p. 7`).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Manual chart adjudication using Bonaca et al. myocarditis criteria and specialist guidelines (`p. 2`), though predictor-blinding during adjudication is unstated and unclear causes of death were coded negative (`p. 2`).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **No calibration or DCA** for the fusion model (`p. 9`), despite very low PPV (`0.08`, `p. 6`).
  - **Questionable CI / threshold rigor:** Table 2 (`p. 7`) reports anomalously narrow auto-bootstrapped 95% CIs (`±0.01`) where Baseline 1 AUC `0.645` lies *above* its upper bound `(0.63, 0.64)`; ROC threshold selection (`p. 5`) does not prove strict validation-only locking; missing `PR-AUC`, `F1`, and `DeLong` tests; and minor internal reporting inconsistencies (`428` vs `424` component events in Table 1; `2%` vs `0.2` dropout; focal vs weighted cross-entropy).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (pp. 9–10):** Low event rate precluded single-endpoint prediction, time-to-event modeling, or calibration evaluation; variable binning loses continuous resolution; self-reported smoking has bias (`p. 7`); high NPV (`0.98`) comes with very low PPV (`0.08`); and SHAP applies only to Baseline 1 RF, not the joint fusion model (`p. 10`).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:**
  - Directly demonstrates a real clinical attempt touching all 4 modality domains (2-lead ECG + EHR + Labs + Smoking) in cardio-oncology (`P0020`), yet exposes **all five proposal gaps (`PG1–PG5`)**: uses only 2 ECG leads (`II, V1`) rather than 12-lead waveforms (`PG2`), relies on binary smoking rather than rich lifestyle profiling (`PG4`), uses simple concatenation rather than Transformer cross-modal attention (`PG5`), fails to run SHAP on the joint fusion model or evaluate AOPC faithfulness (`PG3`, `RQ3`), and omits calibration/DCA (`RQ4`).
  - **Discrepancy vs `papers.csv`:**
    1. **`XAIEvaluated` error (Major):** `papers.csv` falsely states `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`, whereas the PDF (`p. 7, p. 10`) explicitly states SHAP was run *only* on Baseline 1 RF with zero faithfulness metrics.
    2. Internal PDF inconsistencies noted: Table 1 component counts sum to `424` vs `428` stated in text (`pp. 1, 4`); `p. 3` says COPD/OSA were dropped while `p. 7–8` include them in SHAP; `p. 4` writes `2% dropout` vs `Dropout (0.2)` in `Fig. 1`; Baseline 1 AUC `0.645` exceeds its Table 2 CI `(0.63, 0.64)`.
