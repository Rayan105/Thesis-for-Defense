# P0007 — Multimodal deep learning for acute myocardial infarction detection from 12-lead electrocardiogram: a multi-centre study with cross-hospital validation

- **Citation Key:** `Gupta2026_P0007`
- **DOI:** `10.1093/ehjdh/ztaf125` (`https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjdh/ztaf125`, p. 1)

- **Authors:** Vibha Gupta, Lukas Hilgendorf, Erik Andersson, Antros Louca, Arman Shahmari, Alfred Hjalmarsson, Rajkumar Saini, Carlo Pirazzi, Monér Alchay, Araz Rawshani (p. 1)
- **Venue & Year:** *European Heart Journal - Digital Health* `(2026) 7, ztaf125` (Received 25 July 2025; revised 1 September 2025; accepted 2 October 2025; online publish-ahead-of-print 27 October 2025; © The Author(s) 2025, p. 1)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Multi-centre retrospective emergency department (ED) cohort across three Swedish hospitals in the Västra Götaland region (1 January 2015 – 30 June 2023): **Sahlgrenska University Hospital (SU)**, **Norra Älvsborgs Länssjukhus (NU)**, and **Skaraborg Hospital (SKAS)** (pp. 1, 3; Swedish Ethical Review Authority `Dnr 2023-03921-01`, pp. 2, 10).
- **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** (authors explicitly note on p. 10: *"All hospitals were located in Sweden, resulting in geographic and demographic homogeneity that may limit broader applicability"*).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** `145,656` standard 12-lead ECGs from `96,813` adult patients presenting with chest pain or dyspnoea within 6 h of ED arrival (pp. 1, 2–3, 5).
  - **Internal Count Discrepancy:** Results text (p. 5) states `145,656` ECGs total alongside site counts `SU = 92,506`, `NU = 19,487`, `SKAS = 30,930` (sum = `142,923`, short by `2,733` ECGs), while **Table 1 (p. 4)** lists `SU = 92,505`, `SKAS = 30,929`, `NU = 19,487` (sum = `142,921` ECGs). Overall patient mean age: `63.5` years, `52.1%` male (p. 5); site-wise mean ages (`± SD`) in Table 1 (p. 4): SU `60.5 ± 19.7` yrs (`51.4%` male), SKAS `72.4 ± 14.8` yrs (`53.6%` male), NU `60.7 ± 19.3` yrs (`53.4%` male).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:**
  - Site-wise AMI ECG counts in Table 1 (p. 4): `SU = 5,427` (`5.9%`), `SKAS = 2,984` (`9.6%`; age-strata sum `96 + 1,017 + 1,872 = 2,985`), `NU = 1,433` (`7.4%`) $\rightarrow$ `9,844` AMI ECGs across the `142,921` tabulated ECGs (`6.89%` pooled; text pp. 1, 3, 5 reports `~6–10%` / `5.9%–9.6%`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:**
  - Diagnostic acute myocardial infarction (**AMI**) at index presentation based on final hospital discharge **ICD-10 code `I21`** for ECGs recorded within 6 h of ED arrival (pp. 2–3, 10). Does **not** separate STEMI from NSTEMI/OMI (p. 10). Longitudinal post-discharge horizon: `none / not reported`.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (2 of 4 target modalities, minimal clinical):**
  - `12-lead ECG` (raw waveforms, p. 3).
  - `Clinical/EHR` (**only 2 demographic variables**: `age` and `sex`, p. 3; structured symptom data existed for only `4.2%` of cases and were excluded, p. 5).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** Laboratory/Biomarkers (e.g., troponin, lipids), Lifestyle (`none / not reported`), vitals/comorbidities (comorbidities summarized descriptively in external Table S3, not used as model inputs, p. 5).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:**
  - Standard `10-s`, `12-lead` ECG decoded from XML/base64 to WFDB, sampled at `500 Hz` and down-sampled to `250 Hz` (`12 × 2,500`, p. 3).
  - Zero-phase first-order Butterworth bandpass filter (`0.5–40 Hz`, `filtfilt`) and per-lead Z-score normalization (p. 3).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Complete-case filtering (`Only ECGs with complete demographic information (age and sex) were included in the analysis, and no data imputation was necessary`, p. 3).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - Proposed: **Conv-BiLSTM-Attn** (1D Conv + BatchNorm + MaxPool + dilated residual block + BiLSTM layers + temporal attention, concatenated with age and sex embeddings $\rightarrow$ FC + SoftMax; binary cross-entropy with label smoothing + Adam + early stopping on validation AUROC, pp. 3–4 Fig. 1).
  - Benchmarks (pp. 3, 5): `Model A` (Han et al. 2020 `ML-ResNet`), `Model B` (Ribeiro et al. 2020 `CODE` ResNet trained from scratch), `Model B-TF` (CODE-pretrained fine-tuned on AMI cohort), and `ECG-SMART-NET` (Riek et al. 2025 CNN for OMI). Unimodal ablation: `Conv-BiLSTM-Attn` without age/sex (p. 6). No clinical-only or GBDT tabular baseline reported.
- **Fusion Strategy:** Intermediate/late vector concatenation (`Patient age and sex are incorporated as auxiliary features by concatenation with the learned ECG representation` prior to final FC/SoftMax layers, p. 3, Fig. 1 p. 4).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** **Recording-level** splitting rather than strictly patient-level (`145,656` ECGs across `96,813` patients $\approx 1.50$ ECGs/patient, p. 3). Two validation protocols (p. 3):
  1. **GAH (Generalization Across Hospitals):** 5-fold stratified CV on pooled 3-hospital data (recording-level split).
  2. **LOHO (Leave-One-Hospital-Out):** Train on 2 hospitals (5-fold CV within train), externally test on the 3rd held-out site (`SU`, `NU`, `SKAS`).
- **GAH Pooled Cross-Validation Results (pp. 1, 5, Fig. 2 p. 6):**
  - Primary Discrimination (`Conv-BiLSTM-Attn`): **ROC-AUC = `0.848` (95% CI: `0.84–0.86`)**, **PR-AUC (AUPRC) = `0.456` (95% CI: `0.44–0.47`)** (Fig. 2 top-left prints `[0.440–0.472]` and per-fold **Brier scores** `0.127–0.141`, p. 6).
  - Per-hospital GAH test performance: `NU` AUROC `0.853` (`0.856 [0.82–0.89]` in Graphical Abstract p. 2 & Fig. 2 p. 6), AUPRC `0.495`; `SU` AUROC `0.851` (`0.853 [0.84–0.86]` in Fig. 2), AUPRC `0.446`; `SKAS` AUROC `0.822` (`0.824 [0.80–0.84]` in Fig. 2), AUPRC `0.456` (p. 5).
  - At Youden-optimal threshold (`0.439`, pp. 1, 5): Sensitivity = `0.736` (`0.71–0.76`), Specificity = `0.793` (`0.76–0.81`), NPV = `0.976` (`0.97–0.98`), Binary F1 = `0.325` (`0.31–0.34`), Weighted F1 = `0.837` (`0.82–0.85`).
- **LOHO External Cross-Hospital Validation (pp. 1, 5, Fig. 3 p. 7):**
  - `NU` held out: **AUROC = `0.849` (`0.84–0.85`)**, AUPRC = `0.469` (`0.46–0.48`), Youden threshold `0.452`, Sensitivity `0.737` (`0.72–0.77`), Specificity `0.789` (`0.75–0.81`), NPV `0.974` (`0.97–0.98`) (p. 5, Fig. 3 p. 7).
  - `SU` held out: **AUROC = `0.819` (`0.80–0.83`)**, AUPRC = `0.380` (`0.36–0.40`), Youden threshold `0.338` (p. 5, Fig. 3 p. 7).
  - `SKAS` held out: **AUROC = `0.801` (`0.79–0.81`)**, AUPRC = `0.405` (`0.39–0.41`), Youden threshold `0.500` (p. 5, Fig. 3 p. 7).
  - Overall LOHO ranges (pp. 1, 5): AUROC `0.801–0.849`, Sensitivity `0.671–0.776`, Specificity `0.651–0.801`, NPV `0.965–0.975`, Binary F1 `0.276–0.337`, Weighted F1 `0.731–0.844`.
- **Comparative Benchmarking (p. 5 & Fig. 2 bottom row p. 6; note: text p. 5 labels this LOHO benchmarking, yet numerical metrics and Fig. 2 title correspond to GAH):**
  - `Conv-BiLSTM-Attn`: AUROC `0.848` (`0.84–0.86`), AUPRC `0.456` (`0.44–0.47`), Weighted F1 `0.837`.
  - `Model B` (`CODE` ResNet): AUROC `0.823` (`0.81–0.83`; Fig. 2 `0.825`), AUPRC `0.430` (`0.41–0.44`), Weighted F1 `0.784`.
  - `ECG-SMART-NET`: AUROC `0.814` (`0.77–0.86`), AUPRC `0.405` (`0.34–0.47`), Weighted F1 `0.789`.
  - `Model A` (`ML-ResNet`): AUROC `0.790` (`0.77–0.83`; Fig. 2 `0.798`), AUPRC `0.350` (`0.33–0.37`), Weighted F1 `0.798`.
  - `Model B-TF`: AUROC `0.750` (`0.67–0.83`; Fig. 2 `0.751`), AUPRC `0.210` (`0.06–0.36`), Weighted F1 `0.454`.
- **Demographic Ablation (ECG + age/sex vs ECG-only, p. 6):** Excluding age and sex reduced AUROC marginally from `0.848` to `0.842` (`0.83–0.85`), AUPRC from `0.456` to `0.455` (`0.446–0.47`), and sensitivity from `0.736` to `0.730` (specificity unchanged; no significance test reported for this ablation).
- **Subgroup Performance (Fig. 4, p. 8):** Across age (`<50`, `50–70`, `>70`) and sex (`Male`, `Female`) across all sites, AUROC ranged `0.761–0.920` (`0.76 [0.75–0.77]` SU `>70` LOHO to `0.92 [0.87–0.95]` NU `<50` GAH & `0.92 [0.81–0.83]` SKAS Female GAH) and AUPRC `0.29–0.54`.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** **Minimal / Partial** — Brier scores per GAH fold (`0.127–0.141`) are printed in Fig. 2 top-left legend (p. 6); calibration plots, ECE, and calibration slope/intercept are `not reported`.
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `Yes` — reported as confirming highest net clinical benefit across `5%–20%` risk thresholds (pp. 4–5; plot in external Supplementary Fig. S3).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `Yes` — paired *t*-tests across CV folds (`t = 3.73–83.10`, `P = 0.020` to `< 0.001`) and DeLong's test (`all P < 0.001`) vs benchmark models (pp. 3–6).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — UNCLEAR / MODERATE RISK:**
  - Large multi-centre ED cohort (`145,656` ECGs, `96,813` patients) preserves realistic AMI prevalence (`5.9%–9.6%`) without artificial case-control balancing.
  - However, **recording-level splitting** (`p. 3`) creates **patient-level overlap** across train and test folds in GAH (and internal LOHO CV), while geographic restriction to Sweden limits broader transportability (p. 10).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - Uniform `0.5–40 Hz` zero-phase filtering and per-lead Z-score normalization avoid cross-split scaling leakage; complete demographics required (`no imputation`, p. 3). Structured symptoms were missing in `95.8%` of cases and omitted (p. 5), and inter-hospital ECG device/acquisition heterogeneity is noted (p. 10).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - Relies on discharge ICD-10 code `I21` without blind adjudication, risking under-coding and failing to separate STEMI vs NSTEMI/OMI (p. 10).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Recording-level leakage** in GAH and internal CV folds (`p. 3`).
  - **Post-hoc threshold optimism:** Youden thresholds were selected *"on each fold"* and differ per held-out site (`0.452` NU, `0.338` SU, `0.500` SKAS, pp. 3, 5), inflating threshold-dependent metrics.
  - **Missing full calibration evaluation** (no calibration curves, slope/intercept, or ECE—only Brier scores in Fig. 2 legend).
  - Multiple internal quantitative inconsistencies (cohort ECG sum `142,921`/`142,923` vs `145,656`; GAH vs LOHO label confusion on p. 5; `ΔAUC` arithmetic mismatches on pp. 5–6).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** ICD-10 `I21` under-coding and inability to distinguish STEMI vs NSTEMI/OMI; Swedish geographic/demographic homogeneity; inter-hospital heterogeneity in size, prevalence, and ECG acquisition; low AMI prevalence and age-related subgroup gaps; `~4%` symptom completeness; retrospective design without prospective workflow impact trials (p. 10).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Strong empirical benchmark showing Leave-One-Hospital-Out (`LOHO`) validation at real-world prevalence (`5.9%–9.6%`) with DCA (`5%–20%`) and PR-AUC. Crucially, fusing only `age + sex` with 12-lead ECG improved AUROC by just `+0.006` (`0.842` $\rightarrow$ `0.848`, p. 6)—directly motivating our FYDP's **4-modality design (`PG2`, `PG4`, `RQ2`)** integrating quantitative **Laboratory biomarkers** and **Lifestyle** factors alongside Clinical EHR and ECG. It also reinforces our strict **patient-level split (`T14`)** and **quantitative SHAP AOPC faithfulness (`PG3`, `RQ3`)** to avoid Gupta et al.'s recording-level leakage and unvalidated ScoreCAM heatmaps.
