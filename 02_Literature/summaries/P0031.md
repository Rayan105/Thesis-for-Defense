# P0031 — Multimodal explainable artificial intelligence identifies patients with non-ischaemic cardiomyopathy at risk of lethal ventricular arrhythmias

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Kolk2024_P0031`
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-024-65357-x` (`https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-65357-x`, p. 1)
- **PubMed ID (PMID):** `not reported` in PDF
- **Authors:** Maarten Z. H. Kolk, Samuel Ruipérez-Campillo, Cornelis P. Allaart, Arthur A. M. Wilde, Reinoud E. Knops, Sanjiv M. Narayan, Fleur V. Y. Tjong & DEEP RISK investigators (pp. 1, 12)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Retrospective bi-center cohort across two tertiary academic hospitals in **Amsterdam, The Netherlands** (`2007–2021`, pp. 1, 3): **Amsterdam UMC Location University of Amsterdam (`AMC`)** as development cohort (IRB No. `21.230`, p. 2) and **Amsterdam UMC Location VU Medical Center (`VUmc`)** as **external validation cohort** (IRB No. `2020.296`, p. 2). **South Asian / Bangladeshi / Singapore representation:** `none / not reported` (Dutch hospital cohorts; race/ethnicity not reported in Table 1, p. 5).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** **`289` patients** with non-ischaemic systolic heart failure (`LVEF ≤ 45%`) receiving a de novo primary-prevention ICD (`≥1` year follow-up, LGE-MRI and 12-lead ECG within `5 years` pre-ICD, pp. 1, 3–5):
  - **Development (`AMC`, Hospital A):** `n = 186` (mean age `63.0 ± 12.5` yrs; `68 [36.6%]` female; Dilated CM `75.8%`; CRT-D `39.8%`; Table 1 p. 5).
  - **External Validation (`VUmc`, Hospital B):** `n = 103` (mean age `53.3 ± 15.7` yrs, $p<0.001$; `25 [24.3%]` female, $p=0.044$; Dilated CM `40.8%`, Hypertrophic `26.2%`, Miscellaneous `29.1%`, $p<0.001$; CRT-D `22.3%`, S-ICD `28.2%`, $p<0.001$; Table 1 p. 5).
  - **Unsupervised $\beta$-VAE Pretraining Corpora (`p. 3`):** `333,304` 12-lead ECG waveforms (`2D-ECG β-VAE`) and `970` LGE-MRI scans (`3D-MRI β-VAE`).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** **`26` of `289` patients (`9.0%`)** experienced malignant ventricular arrhythmia in year 1 (`22` shock and/or ATP, `4` ATP only; p. 4). In the external validation cohort (`n = 103`), Fig. 2B (p. 6) and prose (p. 8) document **`6` events (`5.8%`)** and `97` non-events (`71 TN + 26 FP`), implying `20` events (`10.8%`) in the development cohort (`n = 186`). All-cause 1-year mortality: `9 (4.8%)` dev and `1 (1.0%)` external val (p. 4).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Binary **1-year post-ICD malignant ventricular arrhythmia** (sustained VT or VF treated by appropriate ICD shock and/or ATP), collected from EHR and adjudicated by clinicians (`p. 3`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (3 of 4 target modalities + 1 cardiac imaging modality; 4 total):**
  - `Cardiac MRI (LGE-CMR)`: Short-axis phase-sensitive inversion recovery scans (`median 77 days [IQR 19–211]` before ICD, within 5 years, p. 3); U-Net myocardium segmentation $\rightarrow$ ROI crop (`12 × 64 × 64 × 2` [slices $\times$ H $\times$ W $\times$ mask+scan]), histogram equalization/normalization (p. 3).
  - `12-lead ECG`: Resting `10-s` 12-lead ECGs within 5 years pre-ICD (p. 3).
  - `Clinical/EHR`: Demographics, BMI, underlying pathology, medical history (AA, CVA, COPD, HTN, DM, prior NSVT), device type, and medications ($\beta$-blocker, ARB/ACEi, VKA, sotalol, digoxin, amiodarone, NOAC; `Table 1` p. 5, `Fig. 4` p. 8).
  - `Laboratory/Biomarkers`: `Sodium`, `Potassium`, `Creatinine` (`Table 1` p. 5, `Fig. 4` p. 8).
  - **Excluded target modality:** `Lifestyle`: `none / not reported`.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `10-s` 12-lead resting ECG downsampled to `250 Hz`, denoised (Savitzky-Golay filter) and baseline-wander removed (low-resolution Fourier series subtraction), R-peak aligned to compute **mean P-QRS-T heartbeat waveform per lead**, normalized between `0` and `1` via z-score (`p. 3`).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Complete paired MRI + ECG inclusion (`p. 3`); tabular EHR/lab variables with `>30%` missing excluded, remaining missing values imputed via `missForest` (`v1.1.3`, p. 3). *(Not explicitly stated whether `missForest` was fit solely on Hospital A before transforming Hospital B.)*
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - Unsupervised feature extractors: `3D-ResNet β-VAE` (`256` LGE-MRI latent dims; reconstruction `SSIM = 0.39 ± 0.09`, pp. 3–4) and `2D-ResNet β-VAE` (`32` ECG latent dims; reconstruction `Pearson r = 0.97 ± 0.005`, `RMSE = 0.04 ± 0.003`, pp. 3–4).
  - Downstream supervised predictor: **`XGBoost`** (`stratified k-fold CV` + Bayesian optimization + **`SMOTE`** minority oversampling during development, then retrained on all `n = 186` Hospital A patients, p. 4).
  - Unimodal comparators (`Table 2`, p. 5): `MRI branch` (`256` MRI latents + XGBoost), `ECG branch` (`32` ECG latents + XGBoost), `Clinical branch` (EHR/labs + XGBoost). No guideline clinical score baseline (e.g., LVEF-only or ESC score) benchmarked in Table 2.
- **Fusion Strategy:** **Two-stage feature-level concatenation** (`DEEP RISK`): frozen `3D-ResNet β-VAE` (`256` MRI latents) + frozen `2D-ResNet β-VAE` (`32` ECG latents) + clinical/lab features concatenated as tabular inputs to `XGBoost` (`Fig. 1E–F` p. 2, `p. 4`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** True **inter-hospital external validation** (`AMC` $n=186$ development $\rightarrow$ `VUmc` $n=103$ external test), with `3,000` bootstrap resamples on the external cohort for `95% CIs` (`pp. 4–5`).
- **External Validation Results (`n = 103`, `Table 2` p. 5 & `p. 6`, `3,000` bootstrap iterations):**
  - **`DEEP RISK` (Multimodal):** AUROC **`0.844` (`95% CI: 0.713–0.961`; text `0.84`)**, AUPRC **`0.313` (`0.083–0.652`; Fig. 2D `0.308 ± 0.15`)**, Accuracy `0.741 (0.602–0.961)`, Sensitivity **`0.981` (`0.750–1.000`)**, Specificity **`0.726` (`0.580–0.969`)**, NPV **`99.9%` (`98.6–100.0%`, p. 6)**. Mean bootstrap confusion matrix (`Fig. 2B` p. 6): `TN = 71 (56–96)`, `FP = 26 (3–41)`, `FN = 0 (0–1)`, `TP = 6 (2–11)`.
  - **`MRI branch`:** AUROC `0.801 (0.649–0.936)`, AUPRC `0.232 (0.061–0.499)` (Fig. 2D `0.234 ± 0.12`), Accuracy `0.706 (0.515–0.951)`, Sensitivity `0.955 (0.714–1.000)`, Specificity `0.690 (0.495–0.96)`.
  - **`Clinical branch`:** AUROC `0.639 (0.386–0.867)`, AUPRC `0.131 (0.035–0.294)`, Accuracy `0.625 (0.214–0.922)`, Sensitivity `0.819 (0.364–1.000)`, Specificity `0.613 (0.172–0.938)`.
  - **`ECG branch`:** AUROC `0.544 (0.260–0.816)`, AUPRC `0.099 (0.033–0.206)`, Accuracy `0.643 (0.136–0.864)`, Sensitivity `0.703 (0.333–1.000)`, Specificity `0.641 (0.091–0.878)`.
  - **Unreported Metric & Internal Contradictions:** `F1-score` is stated in Methods (`p. 4`) yet omitted from the main PDF (deferred to external `Supplementary Table 6`, p. 6). Results prose (`p. 6`) swaps Clinical and ECG AUROCs (`"ECG [0.64, 0.39–0.87] and clinical [0.54, 0.26–0.82]"`, contradicting Abstract p. 1 and Table 2 p. 5), while `Fig. 2C` (`p. 6`) prints `Clinical branch (AUROC = 0.580 ± 0.15)` and `Fig. 2D` prints `Clinical branch (AUPRC = 0.104 ± 0.05)`.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `Yes (Visual Slope Referenced — Miscalibrated/Overestimated)` — Methods (`p. 4`) describe calibration curve visualization/slope, and Results (`p. 6`) explicitly report: **`"Calibration curve visualisation (Supplementary Fig. 8) showed overestimated SCD risk prediction"`** (`Brier score`, `ECE`, and exact calibration slope/intercept numerical values are `not reported` in the main PDF).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `Yes` — **DeLong's test** comparing `DEEP RISK` vs `MRI branch` ($p < 0.001$) and `MRI branch` vs `ECG`/`Clinical` branches ($p < 0.001$, p. 6).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH / UNCLEAR RISK:** Inter-hospital external validation (`AMC` $n=186$ $\rightarrow$ `VUmc` $n=103$, pp. 4–5) is a strong design element. However, total sample size is small (`289` patients, only **`26` total events** [`20` dev / **`6` external val**]), producing huge CIs (`AUPRC 0.083–0.652`, `AUROC 0.713–0.961`), far below PROBAST validation thresholds ($\ge 100$ events). Differential LGE-MRI referral rates across hospitals (`p. 10`), major cohort case-mix shift (`Table 1` p. 5), ICD-therapy overestimation of true SCD (`p. 10`), and unverified non-overlap between the `333,304` ECG / `970` MRI VAE pretraining cohorts and the `289` downstream patients add further risk.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — UNCLEAR RISK:** ECG/LGE-MRI scans allowed up to `5 years` prior to ICD implantation (`p. 3`); `missForest` imputation (`p. 3`) does not specify strict dev-only fitting; LGE-MRI VAE reconstruction SSIM was low (`0.39 ± 0.09`, p. 4).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Clinician-adjudicated EHR ICD shock/ATP outcomes over 1 year (`p. 3`), though predictor blinding is unstated and ICD shocks/ATP overestimate true SCD (`p. 10`).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Severe dimensionality-to-event mismatch (`~20` dev events for `256 MRI + 32 ECG + clinical` = `>300` XGBoost features) and **`6` events** in external validation (`n = 103`).
  - **`SMOTE` oversampling** during development (`p. 4`) directly contributes to the documented **overestimated SCD risk calibration** (`Supplementary Fig. 8`, p. 6), with no post-hoc recalibration, `Brier`, `ECE`, or `DCA` reported.
  - Operating cut-point (Youden's $J$, p. 4) yields `0 FN` (`98.1%` sensitivity) on the 6 external events without confirming pre-locking solely on development data; `F1-score` omitted; internal text/Table 2/Fig. 2C contradictions (`Clinical AUROC 0.639 vs 0.54 vs 0.580`).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Appropriate ICD therapy overestimates true SCD (`p. 10`); inter-hospital LGE-MRI referral selection bias; gradient maps from the final conv layer may oversimplify complex spatial relationships; disentangled VAE latents lack direct clinical meaning (`p. 10`); mean P-QRS-T ECG templates drop beat-to-beat autonomic dynamics (`ECG AUROC 0.544`, pp. 9–10).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Strong conceptual reference demonstrating **4-modality clinical integration** (`LGE-MRI + 12-lead ECG + EHR + Labs`) with true **inter-hospital external validation** (`AMC → VUmc`), DeLong testing, and visual calibration auditing (`PG1–PG3`, `RQ4`). Nonetheless, `P0031` uses frozen `β-VAE` concatenation into `XGBoost` rather than end-to-end cross-modal Transformer attention (`PG5`), omits `Lifestyle` (`PG4`), compresses ECGs to single mean-beat templates (`AUROC = 0.544`), suffers `High PROBAST risk` from `SMOTE` miscalibration and extreme event scarcity (`26` total / `6` external events), and performs **zero quantitative XAI faithfulness (`AOPC`) or stability tests** (`PG3`, `RQ3`).
- **Discrepancies vs `papers.csv`:** `XAIEvaluated` (`Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`) is **false** (qualitative/visual SHAP + latent traversal + gradient maps only); `SampleSize` (`See summary`) is `289` NICM patients (`186` dev, `103` ext val; `26` total / `6` ext events) + `333,304` ECG / `970` MRI VAE pretraining; `PROBASTRisk` assigned **`High Risk`**; internal text/table/figure AUROC mismatches (`Clinical 0.639 vs 0.54 vs 0.580`) documented.
