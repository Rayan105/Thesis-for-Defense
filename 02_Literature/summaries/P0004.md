# P0004 — Bimodal ECG and PCG Cardiovascular Disease Detection: Exploring the Potential and Modality Contribution

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Calzoni2025_P0004`
- **DOI:** `10.1007/s10916-025-02245-5` (p. 1)
- **PubMed ID (PMID):** `not reported`
- **Authors:** Alessia Calzoni, Mattia Savardi, Marco Silvestri, Sergio Benini, Alberto Signoroni (p. 1)
- **Venue & Year:** *Journal of Medical Systems*, `(2025) 49:113`, published online `12 September 2025` (received 29 May 2025, accepted 16 August 2025, p. 1)
- **Reading Status:** Full-Text Verified (`pp. 1–11`)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Public benchmark datasets: primary bimodal evaluation on the **Augmented MITHSDB** (`Li et al.`, Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.4263528`, originally from PhysioNet/CinC 2016 Massachusetts Institute of Technology Heart Sounds Database, USA, pp. 2–3, 10); auxiliary unimodal PCG pretraining/fine-tuning on `AudioSet` (`527` audio classes), `PhysioNet/CinC 2016` (`3,153` PCGs from `764` individuals), `CirCor DigiScope` (pediatric cohort, Brazil; `3,163` recordings from `942` subjects), and `Yaseen's database` (`1,000` recordings; excluded after degrading downstream performance) (pp. 2–4, 6). South Asian / Bangladeshi representation: **none / not reported** (demographics/ethnicity of MITHSDB `not reported`, pp. 1–11).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** Original `MITHSDB` comprises `409` PCG recordings (`405` paired with simultaneous single-lead ECG) from `121` subjects (`117` healthy, `288` pathological recordings, p. 3). The **Augmented MITHSDB** segments these into `1,975` fixed `8-second` bimodal windows (`1,009` healthy, `966` pathological) via differential window strides (`8 s` stride for abnormal recordings vs overlapping `3 s` stride for normal recordings, p. 3). Splits: `1,243` training / `337` validation / `395` test segments (p. 3).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** `966` pathological (`48.9%`) vs `1,009` healthy (`51.1%`) across `1,975` segments (p. 3). Test set (`n=395`): `197` Abnormal (`49.9%`) vs `198` Normal (`50.1%`) per confusion matrices (`Fig. 2`, `Fig. 3`, p. 6).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Cross-sectional binary **CVD / cardiac abnormality detection** (`Normal` vs `Abnormal/Pathological`, aggregating mitral valve prolapse [`MVP`], benign murmurs [`Benign`], aortic disease [`AD`], and miscellaneous pathological conditions [`MPC`]; pp. 3, 7, 8 Fig. 5). Longitudinal prediction horizon: `not applicable / not reported`.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (2 of 4 target modalities; non-EHR acoustic-electrical pairing):**
  - `Single-lead ECG` (simultaneous with PCG in MITHSDB, p. 3).
  - `PCG (Phonocardiogram)` (electronic stethoscope heart sound recordings, pp. 2–3).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** `Clinical/EHR` (`none`), `Laboratory/Biomarkers` (`none`), `Lifestyle` (`none / not reported`).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** Fixed `8-second` simultaneous single-lead ECG + PCG segments (p. 3). Exact sampling frequency (`Hz`), digital bandpass filtering, and normalization steps are **`not reported`** in this paper (deferred to preliminary conference paper `[23]`, Calzoni et al. 2024, p. 5). For the transfer-learning PCG branch, `PANNs-CNN14` initial spectrogram/log-mel layers were adapted to `8-second` audio inputs (p. 4).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Complete paired bimodal dataset (`405` paired recordings $\rightarrow$ `1,975` segments, p. 3); missing-modality handling is `not reported`.
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - `1D-CNN` unimodal baselines (`5–8` Conv1D+BatchNorm+ReLU+Pool blocks, `153,923–438,915` parameters, dropout `0/0.2`, early stopping patience `15` on val accuracy; pp. 4 Table 2, 5).
  - Best ECG-only: `6`-layer `1D-CNN` (all avg pooling, `368,707` params for AUROC) and `5`-layer `1D-CNN` (both pooling types for Accuracy) (pp. 4 Table 2, 6–7 Table 3).
  - Best PCG-only without TL: `8`-layer `1D-CNN` (`438,915` params, dropout `0.2`; note p. 6 text & Fig. 2c state `all avg pooling` while Table 3 p. 7 states `both pooling types`).
  - Transfer-learning PCG (`PANNs-CNN14` pretrained on AudioSet, fine-tuned on `PhysioNet/CinC 2016 + CirCor` [excl. Yaseen], then fine-tuned on MITHSDB with first `4` conv blocks frozen; pp. 4, 6).
- **Fusion Strategy:** **Late fusion (frozen embedding concatenation + MLP):** embeddings extracted from the frozen best `6-layer 1D-CNN ECG` branch and frozen `PCG` branch (`8-layer 1D-CNN` or `PANNs-CNN14`) are concatenated and fed into an `MLP` classifier (pp. 4, 5 Fig. 1, 6–7 Table 4; full bimodal fine-tuning was also tested, with frozen-branch results reported in Table 4).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Retained the pre-existing `80/20` Zenodo split (`395` test segments = `20.0%`), then split the `80%` (`1,580` segments) `80/20` at the **patient level** into `1,243` training and `337` validation segments (p. 3). **Critical ambiguity:** Patient-disjointness is explicitly stated only for the train/val split of the `80%` subset, NOT verified for the inherited `20%` (`395`-segment) Zenodo test set.
- **Test Set Metrics (`n=395` segments; mean and `95% CI` via `100`-bootstrap resampling; pp. 6–7 Tables 3–4, p. 9 Table 5):**
  - **Unimodal Baselines (`Table 3`, p. 7):**
    - `1D-CNN ECG-only (6 layers, all avg pool)`: AUROC `93.2% [90.8–95.4]`, Acc `86% [82.5–89.9]`, Prec `89.1% [85.1–93.2]`, Rec `81.9% [76–88.4]`, Spec `90% [86.9–93.1]`.
    - `1D-CNN ECG-only (5 layers, both pool)`: AUROC `92.9% [90.7–95]`, Acc `86.1% [83.8–88.1]`, Prec `85.7% [78.7–89.8]`, Rec `86.8% [83.1–91.2]`, Spec `85.6% [80.7–89.7]`.
    - `1D-CNN PCG-only (8 layers, dropout)`: AUROC `82.8% [78.3–86.7]`, Acc `77.3% [73.9–81.1]`, Prec `74.6% [70.2–79.3]`, Rec `82.8% [77.4–89.1]`, Spec `71.7% [67.7–76.9]`.
    - `Transfer learning PCG (excl. Yaseen)`: AUROC `85.5% [82.3–87.8]`, Acc `76.9% [74.2–80]`, Prec `80.3% [73.8–85]`, Rec `70.7% [65.5–75.1]`, Spec `83% [79.9–86.5]`.
  - **Bimodal Late Fusion (`Table 4`, p. 7):**
    - `ECG 6-layer + PCG 1D-CNN (without TL, both frozen)` (**Best AUROC**): AUROC **`96.4% [94.9–97.4]`**, Acc `90.3% [87.3–92.9]` (raw test `90.6%` in Table 5 p. 9 / Fig. 3a p. 6), Prec `87.5% [82.4–92]`, Rec `93.8% [90.2–97.8]` (`93.9%` raw), Spec `86.9% [83.2–90.2]` (`87.4%` raw).
    - `ECG 6-layer + Transfer learning PCG (both frozen)` (**Best Acc**): AUROC `95.7% [94.2–97.1]`, Acc **`90.5% [87.3–93.2]`**, Prec `89.6% [84.9–93.1]`, Rec `91.5% [88.8–94.5]`, Spec `89.4% [84.6–92.8]`.
    - `F1-score`, `PR-AUC`: `not reported`.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `Yes` — **DeLong's test** comparing bimodal vs all unimodal AUROCs ($p < 0.001$) and between the two bimodal configurations ($p = 0.32$, p. 7); **Wilcoxon signed-rank test** comparing ECG vs PCG mean XAI heatmap distributions (`Fig. 4`, pp. 7–8).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:** Very small cohort (`121` subjects / `405` paired recordings) expanded to `1,975` `8-s` segments using overlapping `3 s` strides exclusively on healthy recordings (`1,009` vs `966` abnormal, p. 3). Patient-level isolation is explicitly enforced only when splitting the `80%` train/val portion (`1,243` / `337`), whereas the `20%` (`395`-segment) test set was inherited directly from Li et al.'s Zenodo split without verifying subject-level independence between train and test (`395 / 1975 = 20.00%` exact, p. 3). No external bimodal validation cohort exists.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — UNCLEAR RISK:** ECG/PCG sampling rate, filtering, and normalization details are omitted from the manuscript (deferred to `[23]`, p. 5); ECG and PCG signals are noted as *"not always temporally aligned"* (pp. 5, 7).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Aggregates heterogeneous cardiac disorders (`MVP`, `benign murmurs`, `aortic disease`, `miscellaneous`) into one binary `Abnormal` label (pp. 3, 7); benign murmurs accounted for `10 of 12` false negatives (`Fig. 5`, p. 7–8).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Model selection directly on the test set:** Text explicitly states *"The optimal models were selected based on their AUROC and accuracy on the test set"* (`p. 6`) and *"These optimal bimodal configurations were selected based on both test accuracy and AUROC"* (`p. 6`) across multiple architectures (`5–8` layers, pooling, dropout, TL datasets).
  - Unverified patient-disjointness of the inherited `20%` test set; minor text/table pooling mismatch (`Fig. 2c` all-avg vs `Table 3` both pooling); missing probability calibration (`Brier/ECE/plot`), DCA, F1, and PR-AUC.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:**
  1. `Grad-CAM` on the final convolutional layer of each branch (p. 5).
  2. `Multi-Layer Grad-CAM` aggregating the last `3` conv layers weighted by absolute Pearson correlation (p. 5).
  3. Time-series `LIME` perturbing half of input segments with noise sampled from local distribution (p. 5).
  4. `UMAP` 2D projection of test-set fused embeddings (`Fig. 5`, pp. 7–8).
  - Because ECG and PCG signals were not temporally aligned, authors did **not** attribute specific waveform events (e.g., S1/S2 or QRS/ST segments) and instead compared the **mean of normalized heatmap values** globally per modality (`Fig. 4`, pp. 5, 7–8) via Wilcoxon signed-rank tests (PCG mean > ECG for Normal across all 3 methods; ECG > PCG for Abnormal in Grad-CAM & Multi-Layer Grad-CAM, while LIME showed PCG > ECG).
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **Qualitative / Visual / Built-in Importance Only** *(Corrects preliminary `papers.csv` tag `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)` which is false upon full-text inspection: comparing mean normalized Grad-CAM/LIME heatmap amplitudes across modalities measures global modality magnitude shift, NOT attribution faithfulness [deletion/insertion AOPC], multi-seed stability correlation, or clinician plausibility review—all of which are absent in pp. 1–11).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Reliance on a single small bimodal dataset (`MITHSDB`) without external validation; potential overfitting despite dropout/early stopping; lack of temporal alignment and synchronized clinical acquisition protocols (pp. 5, 7, 9).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Useful benchmark demonstrating late fusion of `ECG + PCG` beats unimodal models (`+3.2%` AUROC over ECG-only, DeLong $p < 0.001$), but offers limited transferability to our 4-modality clinical/EHR/Lab/Lifestyle CVD prediction task (`PG2–PG5`): relies on `121` subjects without clinical/lab/lifestyle modalities, uses frozen late-fusion MLP concatenation rather than cross-modal Transformers, performs model selection on the test set (`High PROBAST risk`), omits calibration/DCA, and evaluates XAI only via global heatmap means without perturbation faithfulness (`AOPC`) or stability checks.
