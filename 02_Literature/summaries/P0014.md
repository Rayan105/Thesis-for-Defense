# P0014 — MAF-Net: Multimodal cross-attention-based fusion network for cardiovascular disease classification

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Qu2026_P0014`
- **DOI:** `10.1371/journal.pone.0345238` (`https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0345238`, p. 1)

- **Authors:** Chang Qu, Xin Zhang, Yansong Lu, Yi Wang, Chengzhi Su (p. 1)
- **Venue & Year:** *PLOS One* 21(4): e0345238 (Received July 24, 2025; Accepted March 3, 2026; Published April 7, 2026, p. 1)
- **Reading Status:** Full-Text Verified (`pp. 1–19`)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Secondary retrospective analysis of the public `PTB-XL` clinical 12-lead ECG database on PhysioNet (originally collected in Germany/Europe by PTB; study authors affiliated with Changchun University of Science and Technology, Changchun, China; pp. 1, 3 §2.1, Ref. 22). South Asian / Bangladeshi representation: **none / not reported** (ethnicity/country demographics are not analyzed in the paper).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** `21,837` clinical 12-lead ECG recordings from `18,885` patients (p. 3 §2.1). Text reports `17,441` training, `2,193` validation, and `2,203` test samples (`17,441 + 2,193 + 2,203 = 21,837`) after stating *"excluding empty-class samples"* (p. 4 §2.1; exact count of any excluded empty-class records is unclear given the split counts sum exactly to `21,837`).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Multi-label diagnostic assignments across five PTB-XL ECG superclasses (overlapping diagnoses allowed, Table 1, p. 4):
  - `NORM` (Normal ECG): `9,528` records (`43.6%` of `21,837`)
  - `MI` (Myocardial Infarction): `5,486` records (`25.1%`)
  - `STTC` (ST/T Change): `5,250` records (`24.0%`)
  - `CD` (Conduction Disturbance): `4,907` records (`22.5%`)
  - `HYP` (Hypertrophy): `2,655` records (`12.2%`)
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Cross-sectional multi-label (`multi-hot encoding`, sigmoid output, binary cross-entropy loss, p. 4 §2.1) classification across five PTB-XL diagnostic superclasses annotated by up to two cardiologists (p. 3 §2.1). Longitudinal prognostic horizon: **none** (concurrent diagnostic ECG classification only).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (2 of 4 target modalities):**
  - `12-lead ECG` raw waveform signals (`Y` branch, p. 3 §2.1, Fig. 1 p. 5, Fig. 3 p. 7).
  - `Clinical/EHR tabular metadata` (`X` branch, `Dense(7)` input in Fig. 1 p. 5 & Fig. 2 p. 6): explicitly names `age`, `gender`, and `weight` in feature ablation (`§4.2`, Table 4 p. 13), while `§2.1` (p. 3) describes *"demographic information (e.g., gender, age, weight), infarct characteristics, the likelihood of diagnostic ECG statements, and annotations of signal properties"* and **`§3.1.1` (p. 5) explicitly states:** *"The input layer (X-inputs) receives structured feature data including patient information and diagnostic classes."* The full 7-feature schema is never enumerated.
  - **Excluded target modalities:** `Laboratory/Biomarkers`: `none / not reported` (noted as future work, e.g., lipids, p. 17 §5.5); `Lifestyle`: `none / not reported`.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `10 s` clinical 12-lead ECG waveforms (PTB-XL provides `500 Hz` and `100 Hz` rates, p. 3 §2.1; exact chosen sampling rate for experiments is `not reported`). Preprocessing applies wavelet threshold denoising and baseline drift removal (p. 4 §2.1).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Median imputation for numerical clinical features and mode imputation for categorical clinical features (p. 4 §2.1). No missing-modality masking or stress tests (only complete clinical-branch removal evaluated in ablation Table 3/Table 4, pp. 12–13).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - **X Branch (Clinical, pp. 5–6, Fig. 2):** `Dense(7)` $\rightarrow$ FCNN (`Dense(32)->Dense(64)->BatchNorm->Dropout(0.3)`) $\rightarrow$ Quadratic Polynomial Feature Cross Layer (`Dot(axes=1, normalize=True)` $\rightarrow$ `Dense(64)` $\rightarrow$ Concatenate $\rightarrow$ `Dense(128)->BatchNorm`) $\rightarrow$ Feature Attention (`Concatenate` $\rightarrow$ `Dense(64, sigmoid)` $\rightarrow$ `Multiply` $\rightarrow$ `Dropout(0.3)`) $\rightarrow$ `Dense(64)->Dense(32)`.
  - **Y Branch (ECG, pp. 6–8, Fig. 3):** Parallel multi-scale 1D CNNs (`Conv1D(64, k=7)`, `Conv1D(128, k=3)`, `Conv1D(256, k=3)` with ReLU and BatchNorm; concatenated dimension `64+128+256=448` in Eq. 7 vs `512` labeled in Figs. 1 & 3) $\rightarrow$ `Bi-LSTM` (`128` forward + `128` backward = `256`) $\rightarrow$ `Multi-Head Self-Attention` (`4` heads, `head_dim=32`; note Eq. 12 writes `W^O \in \mathbb{R}^{128 \times 256}` outputting `R^{T \times 256}`) + residual LayerNorm $\rightarrow$ concatenated `GlobalAveragePooling` + `GlobalMaxPooling` (`512`-dim) + Dropout.
  - **Baselines:** Literature-copied metrics ONLY (not re-implemented on identical splits, admitted on p. 11 §4.1): `CIGRU-ELM [23]`, `STADSNet [24]`, `STFAC-ECGNet [25]`, `MRF-CNN [26]` (Table 2, p. 11). Internal unimodal ablations: `X (Clinical Data Only)` and `Y (ECG data only)` (Table 3, p. 12).
- **Fusion Strategy:** Bidirectional Multi-Head Cross-Attention Fusion Module (`X→Y` and `Y→X`, `4` heads, Eqs. 17–25, pp. 8–10, Fig. 4 p. 9): concatenates `[x, y_to_x]` and `[y, x_to_y]` (shown as `544 + 544 = 1088` in Fig. 4 p. 9) $\rightarrow$ `Dense(256)->BatchNorm->Dropout->Dense(128)->BatchNorm->Dense(5, Sigmoid)`. Class imbalance handled via inverse-frequency weighted binary cross-entropy loss (p. 4 §2.1).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Patient-stratified PTB-XL 10-fold partition: Folds 1–8 training (`17,441`), Fold 9 validation (`2,193`), Fold 10 test (`2,203`) (p. 4 §2.1). Repeated across `5` random runs (mean $\pm$ SD, p. 11 §4.1).
- **MAF-Net (`X+Y`) Overall Performance (Table 2 p. 11 & Table 3 p. 12):**
  - Accuracy: `90.75% ± 0.32%`
  - Precision: `84.58% ± 0.41%` *(note typographical error in p. 11 prose reading "precision 90.75%±0.32%, recall 84.58%±0.41%, recall 87.12%±0.38%"; Table 2 & Abstract are consistent)*
  - Recall: `87.12% ± 0.38%`
  - F1-score: `0.8069 ± 0.005`
  - Macro ROC-AUC: `0.9407 ± 0.002`
  - PR-AUC & 95% Confidence Intervals: `not reported`.
- **Unimodal & Feature Ablations (Table 3 p. 12, Table 4 & Fig. 5 p. 13):**
  - `X (Clinical Data Only)`: Accuracy `80.80% ± 0.60%`, Precision `75.00% ± 0.80%`, Recall `34.91% ± 1.20%`, F1 `0.2550 ± 0.010`, ROC-AUC `0.7916 ± 0.006`.
  - `Y (ECG Data Only)`: Accuracy `88.86% ± 0.34%`, Precision `80.91% ± 0.43%`, Recall `72.62% ± 0.48%`, F1 `0.7650 ± 0.005`, ROC-AUC `0.9374 ± 0.002` (multimodal $\Delta\text{ROC-AUC} = +0.0033$).
  - Clinical Feature Removal Accuracy (Table 4 p. 13): Full `90.75% ± 0.32%` $\rightarrow$ w/o Weight `90.25% ± 0.31%` $\rightarrow$ w/o Gender `89.95% ± 0.35%` $\rightarrow$ w/o Age `89.55% ± 0.29%` $\rightarrow$ ECG-only `88.86% ± 0.34%`.
- **Per-Class ROC-AUC (`Y-only` vs `MAF-Net`, Fig. 6 pp. 13–14):**
  - `NORM`: `0.9451` $\rightarrow$ `0.9514` | `MI`: `0.9231` $\rightarrow$ `0.9380` | `STTC`: `0.9316` $\rightarrow$ `0.9318` | `CD`: `0.9085` $\rightarrow$ `0.9100` | `HYP`: `0.8960` $\rightarrow$ `0.9013`.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported` — no Brier score, ECE, or calibration curve).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** Claims paired t-tests ($\alpha = 0.05$) against baseline literature models (`§3.2` p. 11, Table 2 footnote p. 11), **but no t-statistics, exact p-values, or DeLong tests are reported anywhere**, and baseline metrics were copied directly from external papers lacking SDs and unified test splits (p. 11 §4.1), invalidating paired hypothesis testing.

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Uses PTB-XL patient-stratified 10-fold partition preventing patient overlap across train/val/test (p. 4 §2.1). However, single-dataset design lacks external validation (acknowledged p. 17 §5.5), and empty-class exclusions are ambiguous relative to total `N=21,837` (p. 4).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH RISK:**
  - The exact 7 features in `X-inputs` (`Dense(7)`, Fig. 1–2) are never fully listed; **§3.1.1 (p. 5) explicitly states X-inputs include *"patient information and diagnostic classes"*** alongside §2.1 (p. 3) mentioning *"infarct characteristics, the likelihood of diagnostic ECG statements"*. Feeding diagnostic statement/class metadata into the predictor branch constitutes severe **target/predictor leakage** (and may explain why X-only achieves `0.7916` ROC-AUC despite basic demographics alone).
  - Median/mode imputation and normalization are not confirmed to be fit strictly on Folds 1–8 prior to Fold 9/10 evaluation (p. 4 §2.1).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW RISK:** Cardiologist-adjudicated PTB-XL superclass labels (p. 3 §2.1).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - External SOTA baselines in Table 2 (p. 11) are copied from published papers without identical-split reproduction yet cited with unverified *"p-value < 0.05"* claims.
  - Complete absence of calibration (`Brier/ECE`), `DCA`, `PR-AUC`, and `95% CIs`.
  - Mathematical/architectural typos (Eq. 29 `TP+TN/(TP+TN+FN+FN)` on p. 10; Eq. 7 `448` channels vs `512` in Figs. 1 & 3; `2,201` total entries in Fig. 7 confusion matrices vs `2,203` test samples).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:** Text in `§4.2` (p. 12) claims cross-attention layer weights were visualized and feature-attention module weights examined, **yet zero attention heatmaps or weight tables appear anywhere in the paper**. Actual reported interpretability consists solely of sequential leave-one-feature-out ablation for `age`, `gender`, and `weight` (`Fig. 5`, `Table 4`, p. 13) and 2D `t-SNE` embedding clustering (`Fig. 10`, pp. 16–17). **Zero SHAP, zero LIME, zero Integrated Gradients, and zero Grad-CAM** are implemented.
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **No — Feature Ablation & t-SNE Clustering Only (Zero Quantitative Faithfulness)** *(Corrects preliminary `papers.csv` tag `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)` which is false upon full-text verification: zero deletion/insertion AOPC curves, zero comprehensiveness/sufficiency, zero seed stability, and zero clinician evaluation exist in pp. 1–19; authors acknowledge in `§5.5` p. 17 that interpretability was only "preliminary").*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Single-dataset training/evaluation on PTB-XL without external validation; only preliminary interpretability lacking full transparency; limited clinical feature diversity (missing lipid levels, blood pressure, etc., p. 17 §5.5); baseline models in Table 2 not reproduced under identical settings (p. 11 §4.1).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Directly relevant cross-attention ECG+Clinical comparator (`PG1–PG2`), demonstrating bidirectional `X↔Y` multi-head cross-attention on PTB-XL, yet illustrating critical methodological gaps targeted by our FYDP (`PG2–PG5`): fuses only 2 modalities (no Lab biomarkers or Lifestyle, `PG2`/`PG4`), exhibits very small incremental AUC over ECG-only (`+0.0033`), suffers High PROBAST risk due to suspected `X-branch` diagnostic metadata leakage and uncalibrated/unreproduced benchmarks, and lacks true post-hoc multimodal SHAP faithfulness (AOPC) or calibration/DCA evaluation (`PG3`/`PG5`).
