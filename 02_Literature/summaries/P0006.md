## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Multi-source retrospective pretraining + clinical downstream CVD screening + external wearable validation + non-CVD out-of-domain testing:
  - Pretraining datasets: **PulseDB–MIMIC** (derived from `MIMIC-III`, Boston, USA), **PulseDB–VitalDB** (derived from `VitalDB`, South Korea), and **MESA** (Multi-Ethnic Study of Atherosclerosis, USA) (pp. 1, 7–8).
  - Downstream CVD screening & 4-class arrhythmia subtyping: **PulseDB–MIMIC** only (`Among these sources, only PulseDB–MIMIC provides diagnostic labels for cardiovascular diseases`, p. 8).
  - External AF validation: Private **BBX46 smart ring** dual-channel PPG–AF cohort collected at **The First Affiliated Hospital of Zhejiang University**, Hangzhou, China (IRB No. `IIT20240813B-R1`, pp. 3, 8, 11).

  - **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported**. Authors explicitly note in Discussion (p. 7) that pretraining cohorts were *"drawn exclusively from U.S. (MIMIC-III, MESA) and South Korean (VitalDB) populations—are geographically and demographically homogeneous, potentially introducing latent biases"*; external AF cohort is from Zhejiang, China (pp. 8, 11).

  - **Downstream 10-CVD 1:1 screening test sizes (`n` shown in Fig. 2a, p. 4):** `PSVT` (`n=204`), `OMI` (`n=418`), `PVT` (`n=422`), `AVB` (`n=401`), `AF` (`n=7` printed in Fig. 2a), `CM` (`n=406`), `HF` (`n=472`), `IHD` (`n=483`), `MAVD` (`n=315`), `CAS` (`n=480`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (1 of 4 target modalities at downstream inference; paired PPG + ECG in pretraining):**
  - `PPG` (single-channel PPG waveform at downstream inference, pp. 2, 11).
  - `Single-lead ECG` (paired with PPG during contrastive pretraining and autoregressive generation target; also serves as `SSL-ECG` upper-bound comparator, pp. 2–3, 8).
  - **Excluded target modalities:** Clinical/EHR (aside from ICD-9 labels), Laboratory/Biomarkers, and Lifestyle (`none / not reported`).
- **ECG & PPG Format & Sampling:**
  - `10-s` windows standardized to `256 Hz` (`2,560` samples; PulseDB–MIMIC/VitalDB upsampled from `125 Hz`, MESA and BBX46 native `256 Hz`, p. 8).
  - Cleaned via NeuroKit2 `nk.ppg_clean` (default filter) and `nk.ecg_clean` (Pan–Tompkins filter) plus `nk.ecg_invert` for ECG polarity correction; normalized to `[-1, 1]` (p. 8).
  - Quality control: template-matching PPG quality score `> 0.85` (excluding `<= 0.85`) and ECG fuzzy-quality index dropping `"Unacceptable"` segments (pp. 7–8). Phase/pulse-transit-time lags between PPG and ECG were intentionally left uncorrected (p. 8).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Unpaired segments were dropped prior to pretraining (`11,710,322` $\rightarrow$ `8,942,348`, p. 8). **At inference, ECG is omitted completely**—downstream classification and ECG synthesis operate from **PPG only** via the ECG-aligned PPG encoder (`Fig. 5d`, p. 9, 11).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - Backbone: Masked Autoencoder (`MAE`) with Vision Transformer (`ViT`: `embed_dim=768`, `depth=12`, `num_heads=12`, `patch_size=(1, 128)`, `masking ratio=0.75`, p. 8) for both PPG and ECG.
  - Contrastive Alignment: CLIP-style symmetric contrastive loss (`τ = 0.05`, Eqs. 1–4, pp. 8, 10).
  - Generative Pathway: `TimeVQVAE` (`STFT n_fft=32`, dual LF/HF encoders with codebooks `256`/`512`, dim `256`) + two 4-layer/4-head bidirectional Transformers (`dim=256`, `t=10` iterative decoding steps, pp. 10–11).
  - Classification baselines: `SSL-PPG` (MAE-pretrained PPG-only ViT) and `SSL-ECG` (MAE-pretrained ECG-only ViT, p. 3). Generation baselines: `RDDM`, `WGAN` (`P2E-WGAN`), `RecQRS` (p. 6). **No clinical risk scores or tabular GBDT baselines evaluated.**
- **Fusion Strategy:** Cross-modal contrastive representation alignment during pretraining (`Fig. 5b`, p. 9), followed by unimodal (`PPG`-only) fine-tuning for CVD screening and `PPG`-conditioned autoregressive token generation for ECG synthesis (`Fig. 5c–d`, pp. 9–11).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Primary 10-CVD screening relies on `1:1` matched case-control cohorts (`50%` prevalence, pp. 3, 8), introducing spectrum distortion (only partially probed via `1:2`–`1:10` sensitivity plots in external Fig. S3).
  - Fig. 2a reports `AF n=7` (p. 4), an extremely small binary AF test size, alongside an Abstract-vs-Methods discrepancy (`86 samples`, p. 1 vs `84 individuals` / `n=40` test batch, pp. 4, 8).
  - Geographic homogeneity (`US MIMIC-III/MESA + South Korea VitalDB` pretraining, p. 7) with zero South Asian representation.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - Strict `70:10:20` **subject-level** split on PulseDB–MIMIC, PPG-DaLiA, and WESAD before pretraining and fine-tuning (p. 8) prevents patient-level leakage; per-segment `[-1, 1]` scaling requires no global statistics. Uncorrected PPG–ECG onset/PTT lag and unharmonized pretraining domain shifts remain minor limitations (p. 8).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - Retrospective ICD-9 administrative billing codes assigned globally at the patient/stay level (`each segment labeled according to the human disease label`, p. 11) without episode-level temporal verification or blind adjudication.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Complete absence of **calibration** (Brier, ECE, calibration curves), **DCA**, and **PR-AUC** (critical for `1:10` imbalance sensitivity claims).
  - External validation restricted to a single disease (**AF** only, `n=40` test individuals at one Chinese hospital, p. 8); the remaining 9 CVDs lack external validation.
  - Main PDF omits exact `95% CIs`, F1, PPV/NPV for the 10 CVDs (deferred to external Table S2) and omits clinical/GBDT baselines.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** US/South Korean dataset homogeneity; inherent opacity of generative ECG synthesis and unclear mechanistic PPG–ECG mapping; uncorrected PPG–ECG timing lags; vulnerability to real-world noise, motion artifacts, skin tone variation, and device heterogeneity; current thresholds intended only for early home-based risk flagging rather than clinical diagnosis (pp. 7–8).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Valuable technical precedent for CLIP-style cross-modal representation transfer (`PG1`, `PG2`) and handling missing modalities at inference (`RQ4`). However, CardioPPG operates on PPG alone at inference (omitting 12-lead clinical ECG waveforms, Clinical EHR, Lab biomarkers, and Lifestyle behaviors—`PG2`, `PG4`), lacks calibration, PR-AUC, and DCA (`PG5`), includes zero South Asian cohorts, and substitutes generative waveform reconstruction metrics for true **XAI faithfulness (`PG3`)**, reinforcing our FYDP's focus on 4-modality Transformer fusion + multimodal SHAP AOPC perturbation faithfulness.
