## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country (Umbrella Review Scope):** PRISMA 2020-guided umbrella review integrating both original primary research and secondary reviews (`PubMed`, `Scopus`, and `Web of Science`; `January 2015 – October 2025`; English peer-reviewed publications only; excluding conference proceedings, preprints, trial registries, editorials, letters; PDF pp. 2, 4 [journal pp. 1, 3]). Included studies were conducted in collaborative programs across **North America, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region**, with participant mean ages `50 to 80 years` and female representation `40% to 60%` (PDF p. 6 [journal p. 5]). Specific Asian/MENA datasets listed in text and Table 1 include China, Japan (`Biobank Japan` in Aragam et al. 2022 GWAS), South Korea (`NHIS-HEALS`, Cho S-Y et al. 2021), and Egypt (private Egyptian dataset `n = 503`, El-Sofany et al. 2024) (PDF pp. 7, 14–15 [journal pp. 6, 13–14]).
- **South Asian / Bangladeshi Representation:** **none / not reported** — despite three Indian clinical co-authors (`Hatwal`, `Batta`, `Mohan`, PDF p. 2), **zero** Bangladeshi or South Asian clinical cohorts are reported among the `48` included studies in text or Table 1 (PDF pp. 13–16). Introduction cites a `91.2%` projected CVD mortality increase in Asia (`2025–2050`) and `>80%` of global CVD deaths occurring in LMICs (`~20.5 million` global CVD deaths in 2023, PDF p. 3 [journal p. 2]), and Limitations explicitly note restriction to *"datasets primarily from high-income countries, potentially restricting generalizability to low-resource settings and underrepresented populations"* (PDF p. 17 [journal p. 16]).
- **Sample Size ($N$) & Screening Funnel (PDF pp. 2, 5–6, 13–16 [journal pp. 1, 4–5, 12–15]):**
  - **PRISMA Funnel (Fig. 1, PDF p. 5 [journal p. 4]):** `3,500` records retrieved from databases (`PubMed = 1,200`, `Scopus = 1,400`, `Web of Science = 900`; caption states `48` additional manual records identified, though not added to the `3,500` box count) $\rightarrow$ `700` duplicates removed $\rightarrow$ `2,800` records screened $\rightarrow$ `2,470` excluded (`1,600` no AI in CVD prediction, `570` short-term/non-clinically relevant outcomes, `300` non-peer-reviewed) $\rightarrow$ `330` sought for retrieval (`20` not retrieved) $\rightarrow$ `310` assessed for eligibility $\rightarrow$ `262` excluded (`162` inappropriate design, `81` no validated AI performance metrics, `19` overlap with larger dataset) $\rightarrow$ **`48` included studies**.
  - **Study-Design Tally Discrepancy:** Abstract (PDF p. 2 [journal p. 1]) states `48` studies comprising **`8 RCTs, 28 cohort studies, and 12 systematic reviews or meta-analyses`** (Results PDF p. 6 omits `"8"` before RCTs). However, Table 1 (PDF pp. 13–16 [journal pp. 12–15]) lists **`48` rows** classified into **`30` Primary** and **`18` Secondary** entries (including narrative/scoping reviews and a GWAS+meta-analysis, with `Elvas et al. 2025 [65]` scoping review labeled `Primary` and only `1` study [`Steinhubl et al. 2018 [34]`] explicitly labeled `RCT`).
  - **Primary Cohort Sample Sizes (Table 1, PDF pp. 13–16):** Reported inconsistently across rows, ranging from `96 ECGs from 42 OMI patients` (`Karim et al. 2025`), `201 patients` (`Mannhart et al. 2023`), and `303` (`Cleveland UCI`, Al-Alshaikh et al. 2024) up to `308,737` (`Kaggle`, Elvas et al. 2025), `375,145` (`UK Biobank`, Dorraki et al. 2024), `423,604` (`UK Biobank`, Alaa et al. 2019), and `1,165,690` GWAS participants (`181,522` CAD cases, Aragam et al. 2022).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** `not reported` at the umbrella synthesis level (only selectively noted in Table 1, e.g., `3.9%` new AF in `Steinhubl et al. 2018` and `181,522` CAD cases in `Aragam et al. 2022`, PDF pp. 13–14).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Broad diagnostic and prognostic CVD endpoints across primary and secondary care: CAD, MI / occlusion MI (OMI), incident HF / HF hospitalization / 30-, 90-, and 365-day HF rehospitalization, occult and incident AF, LV dysfunction / EF estimation, 5-year ASCVD and 10-year CVD / MACE, post-TAVR outcomes, and atherosclerosis (PDF pp. 7–16 [journal pp. 6–15], Table 1).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Synthesized (Umbrella Review — No Primary Modeling, PDF pp. 6–16, Fig. 2–6, Table 1):**
  - **Clinical / EHR (Structured & Unstructured NLP):** UK CPRD, UK Biobank, MESA, CHS, NHIS-HEALS, NHANES, Kaggle, UCI, Egyptian cohorts; NLP extraction from physician notes and discharge summaries (PDF pp. 7, 10, 13–16).
  - **ECG & Wearables:** 12-lead ECGs for occult AF (`Attia 2019`), incident AF (`Raghunath 2021`, `Khurshid 2022`, `Yuan 2023`), OMI (`Karim 2025`), MI scoping review (`Bdir 2025`), wearable patch/smartwatch ECG and PPG (`Steinhubl 2018`, `Mannhart 2023`, `Perez 2019/2023`, `Ding 2023`) (PDF pp. 7, 12–16).
  - **Cardiac Imaging & Retinal:** Echocardiography / videos (`Narula 2016`, `Madani 2018`, `Ouyang 2020`, `Ghorbani 2020`, `Wehbe 2023`), CCTA (`Oikonomou 2019`), SPECT (`Cicek 2024`), CXR (`Weiss 2024`), retinal fundus images (`Hu 2023`) (PDF pp. 9–10, 13–15).
  - **Laboratory / Biomarkers, Genomics, & Microbiome:** Circulating biomarkers (`Farajidavar 2022`), GWAS / polygenic risk scores (`Aragam 2022`, `Krittanawong 2022`), lipidomics/proteomics (`Kasartzian & Tsiampalis 2025`), gut microbiome 16S rRNA / shotgun metagenomics (`Majumder 2025`) (PDF pp. 10–11, 14, 16).
  - **Lifestyle / Physical Activity / Mental Health:** Physical inactivity and psychosocial stress noted in Introduction (PDF p. 3); physical activity patterns from wearables (PDF p. 12); mental health predictors in UK Biobank (`Dorraki 2024`, PDF p. 15); personalized diet/probiotics (`Majumder 2025`, PDF p. 16); *"genetic, clinical, and lifestyle data"* conceptualized (PDF p. 10 [journal p. 9]).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `None` (no primary ECG modeling; signal lengths, lead counts, and sampling rates across reviewed ECG studies are `not reported` in Table 1, PDF pp. 13–16).
- **Missing-Modality / Imbalance Handling:** Missing-modality strategies are `not reported` / not synthesized; notes SMOTE class-imbalance oversampling in `El-Sofany et al. 2024` and `Rehman et al. 2025` (PDF pp. 15–16).
- **Model Families Synthesized:** RF, GBM, XGBoost, SVM, DT, k-NN, Logistic Regression, AutoML, ANN/MLP, PSO-ANN, CNN/DCNN, RNN/LSTM, GAN, Graph-SAGE, NLP, Cox-based ML survival models, and multimodal ensembles (PDF pp. 7, 9–10, 13–16 Table 1).
- **Synthesis Strategy:** **Narrative synthesis only** — quantitative meta-analysis was explicitly precluded by substantial heterogeneity in populations, data sources, AI architectures, validation strategies, and outcome definitions (PDF p. 6 [journal p. 5]). Secondary systematic reviews/meta-analyses were used only for contextual interpretation (PDF pp. 4, 6).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Validation Partitioning Across Included Corpus (PDF p. 7 [journal p. 6]):** Most studies relied **exclusively on internal validation** (random split, chronological split, or cross-validation), with temporal or geographic external validation conducted in only a small minority (`[19–21]`). Sensitivity analyses showed internal stability but frequent performance drops in external/temporal cohorts (`[11, 21]`, e.g., `Bdir et al. 2025` reported $\ge 99\%$ internal MI accuracy on PTB/Chapman-Shaoxing that dropped on external/inter-patient testing, Table 1 p. 16).
- **Descriptive Performance Ranges Reported (No Pooled Primary Meta-Analysis Performed):**
  - **Overall AI Predictive Accuracy:** **`80% to 95%`** across cohort studies and meta-analyses (PDF p. 7 [journal p. 6]).
  - **Imaging-Based AI:** **`>70%`** of studies achieved **`AUC > 0.90`** (Abstract p. 2, Results p. 7, Discussion p. 12; citing `Wehbe et al. 2023 [28]`, Table 1 p. 14); diagnostic accuracy **`85% to 95%`** for LVD and CAD (PDF pp. 9–10, Fig. 4); pooled AUC **`~0.91`** (PDF p. 7).
  - **EHR & Classical ML Models:** Pooled EHR AUC **`0.82 to 0.88`** / **`~0.86`** (`Liu et al. 2025 [12]`, `Al-Khero et al. 2025 [13]`, PDF p. 7, Table 1 p. 15); cohort ML AUCs **`0.82 to 0.91`** (`10–18%` higher than FRS, PDF p. 9, Fig. 3); **`12–25%`** / **`15–25%`** gains over conventional scores (`QRISK3`, `FRS`, PDF pp. 7–8, 12).
  - **NLP-Enhanced EHR:** **`12–20%`** predictive accuracy/AUC gain for MI (`12–18%` for HF onset/readmission, PDF p. 10, Fig. 5 p. 11).
  - **Heart Failure 1-Year Readmission:** AUC **`0.88 to 0.94`** (`15–22%` above conventional scores, PDF p. 10, Fig. 6 p. 11).
  - **ML-Enhanced Polygenic Risk Scores (PRS):** **`20–30%`** improvement in coronary event prediction beyond clinical risk factors (PDF p. 11, Fig. 6).
  - **Wearable Monitoring:** AF sensitivity **`90% to 96%`** (PDF p. 12); AUC **`0.90 to 0.96`** (Fig. 6, PDF p. 11); **`18–25%`** reduction in hospitalization / ED utilization vs usual care (Abstract p. 2, PDF pp. 11–12), plus `~19%` fewer unplanned CV hospitalizations in one prospective study (PDF p. 12).
  - **Individualized Risk Calibration Gain:** **`18–25%`** improvement cited in Personalized Medicine narrative (PDF p. 10, Fig. 6 p. 11), although overall evidence synthesis flags calibration as rarely evaluated.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** **Inconsistently reported / largely absent** — *"most studies presenting discrimination via AUC without corresponding calibration assessment. Only a minority of studies reported comprehensive calibration evaluation, such as calibration plots, Hosmer–Lemeshow (HL) test, calibration slope or intercept, or expected calibration error (ECE)"* (PDF p. 7 [journal p. 6]; Abstract p. 2; RoB summary p. 6).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA) & Health Economics:** `No` / `not reported` — clinical utility (DCA) is not synthesized, and health-economic / cost-effectiveness evaluations are explicitly cited as an unmet future requirement (PDF p. 17 [journal p. 16]).
- **Algorithmic Fairness Audit:** Near absence — subgroup fairness stratified by race/ethnicity, sex, age, SES, or geography was *"rarely conducted"* (PDF pp. 6–7 [journal pp. 5–6]), highlighting `Noseworthy et al. 2020 [25]` showing lower ECG-CNN accuracy in Black vs White patients (Table 1 p. 14).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `No` primary quantitative pooling or hypothesis testing performed (narrative ranges only, PDF p. 6 [journal p. 5]).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI / AMSTAR-2 Risk of Bias Audit
- **Quality Tools Used by `P0040` on Included Studies (PDF pp. 5–6 [journal pp. 4–5]):**
  - **Cochrane RoB 2 (RCTs):** Majority rated *"low risk of bias or some concerns"* due to limitations in outcome-assessment blinding and incomplete model metric reporting (PDF p. 6).
  - **Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS, Cohort Studies):** Mostly `7–9 stars` (*"moderate-to-good methodological quality"*, PDF p. 6).
  - **Critical Omission:** Neither **PROBAST / PROBAST-AI** (required for prediction model studies) nor **AMSTAR-2** (required for the included systematic reviews/meta-analyses) was applied; per-study ratings (`Supplementary Table S1`) are unbundled/not in the PDF (PDF p. 6).
- **Methodological / AMSTAR-2 Audit of `P0040` Itself (Reviewer Critique):**
  1. **No PROSPERO Registration:** Explicitly unregistered (PDF p. 4 [journal p. 3]).
  2. **Suboptimal RoB Appraisal & Missing Per-Study Ratings:** RoB 2 and NOS do not audit prediction-model data leakage, pre-split SMOTE, or calibration; AMSTAR-2 was not run on secondary reviews; `Supplementary Table S1` is absent from the PDF.
  3. **Internal Count & Citation Inconsistencies:** Abstract reports `8 RCTs, 28 cohorts, 12 SRs/MAs` (PDF p. 2), whereas Table 1 (pp. 13–16) shows `30` Primary + `18` Secondary entries (including narrative/scoping reviews, with `Elvas 2025` scoping review labeled `Primary` and only `1` explicit `RCT`). PRISMA Fig. 1 (p. 5) mentions `48` manual records in the caption that are omitted from the `3,500 - 700 = 2,800` box arithmetic. Table 1 also misattributes `Sengupta et al. 2020 [45]` to `EchoNet-Dynamic` HF AUC `0.83` (Ref 45 is a 2016 constrictive pericarditis paper; `EchoNet-Dynamic` is `Ouyang et al. 2020 [30]`) and dates `Perez et al.` NEJM 2019 [Ref 51] as `2023` (PDF pp. 13–14, 19).
  4. **No Primary-Study Overlap Quantification:** Claims to minimize duplication (PDF p. 2), yet reports no Corrected Covered Area (CCA) matrix across the included reviews and primary studies.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **Not Applicable (Umbrella Review) — High / Unclear Methodological Concern** *(Review methodology exhibits Low/Moderate AMSTAR-2 confidence due to unregistered protocol, absent PROBAST/AMSTAR-2 appraisal, missing Table S1, and count/citation discrepancies; synthesized corpus exhibits High/Unclear translational risk due to internal-only validation, missing calibration, and absent fairness audits, PDF pp. 6–7).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (PDF p. 17 [journal p. 16]):** Substantial heterogeneity precluding quantitative meta-analysis; overemphasis on discrimination (AUC) over calibration; English-only search; reliance on high-income country datasets restricting LMIC/underrepresented generalizability; data privacy, acute-care variability, and regulatory gaps.
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Useful broad framing paper (`Vasc Health Risk Manag`, 2026) articulating key translational challenges (`calibration deficit`, `internal-only validation`, `algorithmic fairness bias`, `TRIPOD-AI` compliance, and `LMIC/Asian CVD mortality burden`, PDF pp. 3, 6–7, 17). However, due to internal tally/citation errors, lack of PROSPERO registration, and absence of PROBAST-AI/AMSTAR-2 appraisal, `P0040` should be cited strictly for high-level umbrella context rather than rigorous pooled quantitative benchmarks (unlike `P0010`, `P0041`, and `P0042`). Confirms **zero** South Asian/Bangladeshi clinical cohorts across its 48 reviewed studies.
- **Discrepancies vs `papers.csv`:** Verified DOI (`10.2147/VHRM.S590502`), Year (`2026`), Authors, Title, `XAIUsed`, and `XAIEvaluated = No`. Minor notes in `papers.csv`: `Access` is `none` (whereas the PDF is CC-BY-NC 4.0 Open Access, PDF p. 2); `Modalities` lists `None (review)` while the umbrella synthesis spans EHR, ECG, cardiac imaging, wearables, omics/microbiome, and lifestyle; `PROBASTRisk` is `Pending Step 007`.
