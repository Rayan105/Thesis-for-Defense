# P0021 — Artificial intelligence based multimodal fusion of electronic medical record data and ECG signals for pericarditis prediction: pericarditis calculator (p-Cal) as a screening aid

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Ayoub2026_P0021`
- **DOI:** `10.1136/heartjnl-2025-327090` (`https://doi.org/10.1136/heartjnl-2025-327090`, p. 1)
- **PubMed ID (PMID):** `not reported`

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:**
  - *Internal cohort:* Retrospective single-center cohort at **Mayo Clinic** (USA; pericardial disease subspecialty clinic, pp. 2–3).
  - *External cohort:* Open-source ICU dataset from **Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston, USA** (referred to in text p. 2 as *"Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care (MIMIC) eICU Collaborative Research Database"*, conflating MIMIC and eICU naming, p. 2).
  - **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** `none / not reported` (no race/ethnicity breakdown in Table 1 on p. 4 or text; only PDF margin download stamp reflects Bangladesh reader access).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - *Internal Mayo Clinic cohort:* **`6,508` unique patients** (`902` confirmed pericarditis cases [Aug 2003–Aug 2023] + `5,606` controls [Jan–Dec 2018 with normal ECG, normal TTE, and normal CXR], pp. 1–4, Table 1). Mean age `54.8 ± 16.1` years; `3,309` (`50.85%`) female, `3,199` (`49.15%`) male (`Table 1, p. 4`; Abstract says `50.9%` female).
  - *External MIMIC cohort:* **`140` patients** (`40` pericarditis cases + `100` controls; `81.43%` `<40` years vs `17.32%` internally, `p < 0.001`, pp. 3–4, Table 1).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** Internal: `902 / 6,508` (`13.86%` in Table 1 p. 4; `13.8%` in Abstract p. 1; `13.9%` in text p. 3). External MIMIC: `40 / 140` (`28.57%`, `p < 0.001` vs internal, Table 1 p. 4). Exploratory subtype evaluation: `40` cases each for tamponade, acute, constrictive, and recurrent pericarditis (`p. 4`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Cross-sectional diagnostic screening for presence of pericarditis across all subtypes (acute, recurrent, pericardial effusion, tamponade, effusive-constrictive, constrictive) and all aetiologies (`p. 2`). Internal cases used closest ECG to diagnosis date (`p. 2`); external MIMIC used ICD-coded admission diagnoses with ECGs obtained **within a 1-month window post diagnosis** (`p. 2`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (3 of 4 target modalities — Labs omitted from final model):**
  - **12-lead ECG (as Images):** Standard 12-lead ECG converted to report image format preserving spatial and temporal resolution (`p. 3, p. 6`).
  - **Clinical/EHR (16-dim vector):** Gender (`Male`, `Female`, 2 binary dims), Age Group (`<40`, `40–80`, `>80`, 3 binary dims), and 10 binary comorbidities (`Hypertension`, `Hyperlipidaemia`, `Diabetes`, `CAD`, `Heart failure`, `AF`, `MI`, `Stroke`, `Cancer`, `Autoimmune disease`) (`p. 2, Table 1 p. 4, Fig. 4 p. 8`).
  - **Lifestyle:** **Smoking only** (`1` binary dim; `2,627 [40.37%]` in internal cohort; `--` completely missing in external MIMIC cohort, `Table 1 p. 4, Fig. 4 p. 8`). No diet, exercise, or pack-year history.
  - **Laboratory/Biomarkers:** **Not modeled in final architecture.** Although Methods (`p. 2`) state clinical and laboratory values were collected/extracted, **zero** laboratory assays appear in the `16`-dim tabular vector (`2 sex + 3 age + 1 smoking + 10 comorbidities = 16`, p. 2), Table 1 (`p. 4`), SHAP plots (`Fig. 3 p. 7`), or the `p-Cal` web app (`Fig. 4 p. 8`).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** 12-lead ECG report images (`p. 3, p. 6`; raw sampling frequency/duration not specified). Image quality stress test evaluated variations in brightness, contrast, and color (`p. 6, p. 7`).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Missing tabular entries encoded as `-1` (`p. 2`); authors state the late-fusion framework can operate using ECG alone if EMR tabular data is missing (`p. 6`), though missing clinical entries reduced tabular branch accuracy (`p. 7`) and external MIMIC lacked smoking (`Table 1 p. 4`).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - *Tabular Branch:* Random Forest classifier with cross-validated grid search on training split (`p. 2`).
  - *ECG Branch:* Pretrained **MedCLIP Vision Transformer (`ViT`)** (12 transformer layers, 12 heads, hidden size `768`) fine-tuned on 12-lead ECG images with weighted cross-entropy loss (`p. 3`).
  - *Missing Baselines:* No raw 1D waveform Transformer/CNN numeric comparison reported (`p. 6` states preliminary raw-signal experiments were comparable without reporting metrics), no XGBoost/CatBoost/LightGBM tabular comparison, and no intermediate/cross-modal attention fusion.
- **Fusion Strategy:** Two-step **decision-level late fusion** concatenating predicted class probabilities from independently trained MedCLIP ViT (ECG) and Random Forest (tabular) branches into a **logistic regression metaclassifier** (`Fig. 1 p. 3, p. 3`). Deployed publicly as `p-Cal` Streamlit web app (`p. 6, Fig. 4 p. 8`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Random `80%` train (`n = 5,206`) / `20%` hold-out test (`n = 1,302`) split on internal Mayo cohort (`p. 1, p. 3`; note Fig. 2A–C headers print `sample size: 1304`). External test on MIMIC (`n = 140`, `p. 3`).
- **Internal Hold-Out Test Results (`Table 2, p. 6`, `n = 1,302`):**
  - *Tabular (RF):* AUC `0.744 (0.735, 0.753)`, Precision `0.595`, Recall `0.673`, F1 `0.587`, TPR `0.646`, FPR `0.299`, PPV `0.268`, NPV `0.921`.
  - *ECG (MedCLIP ViT):* AUC `0.847 (0.839, 0.853)`, Precision `0.696`, Recall `0.789`, F1 `0.723`, TPR `0.735`, FPR `0.157`, PPV `0.443`, NPV `0.949`.
  - *Late Fusion (`p-Cal`):* AUC **`0.894 (0.891, 0.902)`**, Precision `0.718 (0.712, 0.725)`, Recall `0.815 (0.810, 0.824)`, F1 `0.748 (0.740, 0.754)`, TPR `0.772 (0.760, 0.788)`, FPR `0.143 (0.137, 0.145)`, PPV `0.479 (0.466, 0.492)`, NPV `0.957 (0.955, 0.960)`.
  - *Internal discrepancy vs `Fig. 2A–C (p. 5)`:* Figure 2 ROC plots (`sample size: 1304`) report lower/different AUCs (`Tabular = 0.762`, `ECG = 0.811`, `Fusion = 0.851`) than Table 2 (`0.744`, `0.847`, `0.894`)!
- **External MIMIC Test Results (`n = 140`, `Table 2, p. 6`, `Fig. 2F–H, p. 5`):**
  - *Late Fusion:* AUC **`0.810 (0.797, 0.823)`**, Precision `0.723`, Recall `0.763`, F1 `0.731`, TPR `0.775`, FPR `0.250`, PPV `0.554`, NPV `0.893` (`Table 2, p. 6`).
  - *Unimodal MIMIC (`p. 4` & `Fig. 2F–H p. 5`):* Tabular AUC `0.62` (`0.617` in Fig. 2F), ECG-only AUC **`0.818`** (`Fig. 2G`) — slightly higher than Fusion `0.810` (`Fig. 2H`) before post-hoc MIMIC calibration (`Fig. 2I–K` shows Fusion `0.823` vs ECG `0.818`).
- **Subtypes (`n = 40` each, `p. 4`):** Fusion FPRs: tamponade `7.5%`, acute `5%`, constrictive `0%`, recurrent `7.5%`.
- **Calibration Evaluated:** **Yes** — calibration plots (`Fig. 2D–E, p. 5`) and Brier scores reported (`0.216` tabular vs `0.184` fusion, `p. 4`; ECE not reported).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `No` (`not reported` for AUC model comparisons — despite Methods p. 3 stating appropriate statistical tests were applied, no DeLong p-values comparing Fusion vs ECG/Tabular are reported; Table 1 only gives t-test p-values for cohort drift).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Severe case-control spectrum bias & temporal mismatch (`p. 2`):** Cases (`n = 902`) spanned `20` years (`Aug 2003–Aug 2023`) from a tertiary pericardial specialty clinic, whereas controls (`n = 5,606`) were drawn from a single year (`Jan–Dec 2018`) and required **normal ECG, normal TTE, and normal CXR** (`p. 2`). Authors explicitly concede this *"may therefore overestimate model performance relative to a real-world differential-diagnosis cohort"* (`p. 7`).
  - **External cohort shift (`Table 1 p. 4`):** Small `n = 140` MIMIC sample with massive age shift (`81.43%` `<40 y` vs `17.32%` internally, `p < 0.001`), missing smoking (`--`), and higher disease prevalence (`28.57%` vs `13.86%`).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH / UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - **Post-diagnosis ECG leakage externally:** MIMIC ECGs were acquired up to `1 month post diagnosis` (`p. 2`).
  - **Inconsistent lab reporting & missingness:** `p. 2` describes extracting lab values, yet the `16`-dim vector omits all labs; smoking is unavailable in MIMIC (`Table 1 p. 4`); age is coarsely discretized (`<40, 40–80, >80`) and missing values coded `-1` (`p. 2`).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW (Internal) / UNCLEAR (External) RISK:** Internal cases confirmed via multiparametric guideline criteria in specialty clinic (blinding not stated, `p. 2`); external MIMIC relies on ICD codes (partially reviewed by cardiologists on representative ECGs, `p. 2`).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH / UNCLEAR RISK:**
  - **Unexplained internal metric & threshold discrepancies:** `Table 2 (p. 6)` reports Fusion AUC `0.894` (`n = 1,302`) while `Fig. 2C (p. 5)` displays `0.851` (`n = 1,304`); operating threshold is `>46%` (`p. 4`), `0.707` (`Fig. 2H p. 5`), and `9.72%` (`Fig. 4B p. 8`).
  - Omits `DeLong` comparison p-values and `DCA`; external ECG-only (`0.818`) slightly outperformed uncalibrated fusion (`0.810`) (`Fig. 2G–H p. 5`). *(Mitigating strengths: calibration curves + Brier scores `0.184` reported [`p. 4–5`], external test attempted, and public code/app released [`p. 6`]).*
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:**
  - *Tabular RF branch:* Global SHAP importance (online supplemental Fig. 3 cited on `p. 4`: top 3 = cancer, AF, autoimmune disease) and instance-level SHAP force plots (`Fig. 3 p. 7`, `Fig. 4B p. 8`).
  - *ECG MedCLIP ViT branch:* `Grad-CAM++` / transformer attention maps highlighting ST elevation and PR depression on 2 illustrative cases (`Fig. 3 p. 7`).
  - *Counterfactual & Image Perturbation Stress Tests (`p. 4, pp. 6–7`):* Inverted high-impact tabular binary flags (cancer, AF, autoimmune disease, CAD) globally across test samples—tabular branch degraded sharply while fusion AUC changed modestly (e.g., `0.894` $\rightarrow$ `0.874` for CAD, `p. 4, p. 6`)—and varied ECG image brightness/contrast/color (`AUROC 0.799–0.853`, `p. 7`).
- **Rigor Audit:** Separate branch-level XAI (no unified cross-modal SHAP); global single-feature counterfactual inversion tests clinical robustness to wrong EHR history rather than instance-level **AOPC deletion/insertion faithfulness** of SHAP or Grad-CAM++ attributions; **zero** multi-seed stability (`Spearman ρ`, `Jaccard`) and **zero** structured clinician evaluation of heatmaps.
- **Actual `XAIEvaluated` Verdict:** **No — Qualitative / Visual + Tabular Sensitivity Ablation Only (No AOPC Faithfulness or Multi-Seed Stability)** *(Corrects `papers.csv` tag `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`, which overstates global comorbidity inversion as quantitative XAI faithfulness).*

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (`pp. 7–8`):** Screening rather than diagnostic utility; control group of normal-ECG/normal-TTE/normal-CXR patients is easier than real-world chest pain mimics (STEMI, early repolarization, LVH, PE) and likely overestimates AUC; small preliminary MIMIC external cohort (`n = 140`); accuracy drops when clinical history entries are missing or ECG image is cropped/over-brightened.
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:**
  - Strong late-fusion comparator (`ECG + Clinical + Smoking` with calibration/Brier and web deployment, `p-Cal`), yet leaves key FYDP gaps (`PG2–PG5`): omits Lab assays (`0` biomarkers modeled) and rich Lifestyle predictors (`PG4`), relies on ECG report images + independent late logistic regression rather than 1D+tabular Transformer cross-modal attention (`PG2, PG5`), lacks differential-diagnosis cohorts, and omits AOPC XAI faithfulness/stability (`PG3, RQ3`) and DCA (`RQ4`).
  - **Discrepancy vs `papers.csv`:**
    1. **`XAIEvaluated` overstatement:** `papers.csv` tags `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`, whereas P0021 only runs qualitative SHAP force plots + Grad-CAM++ + global comorbidity inversion (`AUC 0.894 -> 0.874`), with zero AOPC deletion/insertion or stability evaluation.
    2. Clarifies PDF `p. 2` ambiguity: despite mentioning lab collection, the final `16`-dim tabular vector contains **zero laboratory values** (`papers.csv` `Modalities` correctly omitted Labs). Also documents `Table 2` vs `Fig. 2` internal AUC/sample-size discrepancies (`0.894` / `n=1302` vs `0.851` / `n=1304`).
