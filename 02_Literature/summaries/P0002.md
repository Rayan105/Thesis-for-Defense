# P0002 — Multimodal fusion of EHR and ECG based on deep learning for predicting new-onset coronary heart disease in cancer patients

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Zhang2026_P0002`
- **DOI:** `10.3389/fcvm.2026.1834641` (p. 1)
- **PubMed ID (PMID):** `not reported`

- **Venue & Year:** *Frontiers in Cardiovascular Medicine*, `13:1834641`, published `17 June 2026` (received 19 March 2026, revised 05 May 2026, accepted 29 May 2026, p. 1)
- **Reading Status:** Full-Text Verified (`pp. 1–12`)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Single-center retrospective observational cohort at the Affiliated Tumor Hospital of Chongqing University / Chongqing University Cancer Hospital, Chongqing, China (`January 2010–December 2020`, pp. 1, 4 §3.1, 5 Fig. 3, 11). South Asian / Bangladeshi representation: **none / not reported** (Chinese oncology inpatient cohort; race/ethnicity distribution `not reported`).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** `1,262` cancer patients in the final cohort (p. 1, p. 4 §3.1, p. 5 Fig. 3). From `1,454` patients with cancer in admission notes, `131` were excluded (`"without admission and discharge diagnosis of CHD"`, p. 4 §3.1 / p. 5 Fig. 3—ambiguous phrasing given §2.1 p. 2 requires no CHD at admission); of `1,323` remaining (`723` Ca-CHD, `600` Non-Ca-CHD), `1` Ca-CHD (no treatment protocol), `28` Non-Ca-CHD (no cancer stage), and `32` Non-Ca-CHD (no treatment protocol) were excluded (p. 4 §3.1, p. 5 Fig. 3).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** `722` new-onset cancer-associated CHD (`Ca-CHD`, `57.2%`) vs `540` `Non-Ca-CHD` (`42.8%`) (p. 1, p. 4 §3.1, p. 6 Table 1). Stage stratification: `696` early-stage (`Stage II`: `373` Ca-CHD + `323` Non-Ca-CHD, `55.2%`) and `566` late-stage (`Stage III`: `21 + 6 = 27`; `Stage IV`: `328 + 211 = 539`; `44.8%`) (p. 1, p. 6 Table 1).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** New-onset coronary heart disease (`Ca-CHD`) during hospitalization or follow-up, diagnosed per clinical guidelines from symptoms, ECG alterations, and cardiac biomarkers (p. 2 §2.1). Exact ICD-9/ICD-10 code lists, follow-up duration, and fixed prediction horizon (`1-year`, `5-year`, etc.): `not reported`.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (4 data streams; 3 of 4 FYDP target modalities + Clinical Text):**
  - `12-lead ECG` waveforms (*"the last available ECG during hospitalization"*, p. 2 §2.1).
  - `Clinical Text` (Chinese admission clinical notes: chief complaints and physical health checkup text, pp. 2–3, Fig. 1).
  - `Clinical/EHR` structured records (demographics, personal history, physical exam, cardiac ultrasound report, cancer type/stage, comorbidities, medications; `105` structured features stated on p. 4 §3.2 vs `n=78` shown in Fig. 2B p. 3).
  - `Laboratory/Biomarkers` (hematology, lipids, liver/renal panels, `cTnI`, `CKMB`, `BNP`, coagulation tests `D-dimer`, `PT`, `APTT`, `FDP`; p. 2 §2.2, p. 7 Table 2).
  - **Excluded target modality:** `Lifestyle` (`none / not reported` — despite passing mention of "personal history" and BMI in Table 1 p. 6, no smoking, diet, or physical activity lifestyle variables are modeled or reported).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `12-lead ECG` denoised via Daubechies D6 (`db6`) wavelet removing `D1 (250–500 Hz)`, `D2 (125–250 Hz)`, `D3 (62.5–125 Hz)`, and `A10 (0–0.4875 Hz)` (pp. 2–3 §2.2; frequency bands imply `1,000 Hz` sampling, though native Hz is not stated). Pan-Tompkins R-peak/QRS detection segmented each heartbeat into `600` points (`200` left, `399` right of R-peak; `0.6 s` P-QRS-T window) and normalized across 12 leads (p. 3 §2.2).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Cases missing key variables or incomplete follow-up were excluded (`pp. 2, 4–5`). Structured variables with `< 50%` missingness were imputed with `MICE` (multiple imputation by chained equations); variables with `≥ 50%` missingness were excluded (p. 2 §2.2). No missing-modality robustness testing is reported.
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - Proposed `CNN-LSTM`: Chinese text $\rightarrow$ `BERT` (`Bert-as-Service`, Tencent AI Lab, p. 2 §2.2) $\rightarrow$ `Bi-LSTM (128 units)` + `Attention` (`128-dim` vector); structured lab/EHR $\rightarrow$ 2-layer `MLP` (`128`, `64` ReLU + `Dropout 0.3`, `64-dim` vector); 12-lead ECG $\rightarrow$ `1D-CNN` (`Conv1D(32, k=7) -> MaxPool(2) -> Conv1D(64, k=5) -> MaxPool(2) -> Flatten`, `128-dim` vector) (p. 3 Fig. 2B, pp. 3–4 §2.3).
  - Comparison baselines: `7` traditional ML classifiers (`LR`, `DT`, `KNN`, `SVM`, `RF`, `AdaBoost`, `GBDT`, p. 5 §3.4, p. 8 Table 3).
  - **Critical omission:** No unimodal deep baselines (`ECG-only CNN`, `Text-only Bi-LSTM`, `EHR/Lab-only MLP`) and no alternative fusion baselines (early/late/Transformer cross-attention) are evaluated; feature inputs given to the `7` ML baselines are not specified.
- **Fusion Strategy:** Intermediate feature concatenation (`128` text + `64` lab + `128` ECG = `320-dim` vector) passed sequentially through Dense ReLU $\rightarrow$ `Dropout (0.3)` $\rightarrow$ `Conv1D (k=3)` $\rightarrow$ `MaxPooling1D (2)` $\rightarrow$ `Conv1D (k=3)` $\rightarrow$ `MaxPooling1D (2)` $\rightarrow$ `LSTM (128 units)` $\rightarrow$ `Dropout (0.3)` $\rightarrow$ `Dense (64)` $\rightarrow$ `Sigmoid` (p. 3 Fig. 2C, p. 4 §2.3).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Random `3:1:1` split (`60%` train / `20%` validation / `20%` test, p. 5 §3.3); exact sample counts per split `not reported`.
- **Main Test Set Performance (p. 5 §3.4, p. 8 Table 3, p. 9 Fig. 4A):**
  - `CNN-LSTM` (Testing): Accuracy `0.970 (95% CI: 0.961–0.977)`, F1-score `0.970 (95% CI: 0.960–0.977)`, AUC `0.975 (95% CI: 0.962–0.988)`. *(Note: Training set reports identical Accuracy `0.970` and identical AUC `0.975 [0.962–0.988]`.)*
  - `RF` (best traditional ML baseline, Testing): Accuracy `0.788 (0.750–0.833)`, F1 `0.788 (0.737–0.872)`, AUC `0.788 (0.728–0.844)`.
  - `SVM` (Testing): Accuracy `0.745 (0.712–0.774)`, F1 `0.745 (0.713–0.773)`, AUC `0.771 (0.725–0.826)`.
  - Remaining ML baselines (Testing AUC): `GBDT` `0.682 (0.651–0.710)`, `AdaBoost` `0.655 (0.621–0.683)`, `KNN` `0.644 (0.617–0.667)`, `LR` `0.621 (0.593–0.646)`, `DT` `0.591 (0.563–0.616)` (p. 8 Table 3).
- **Cancer-Stage Subgroups (p. 6 §3.4, p. 9 Fig. 4D–E):**
  - Early-stage (`Stage II`): AUC `0.924`, PR-AUC (`AP`) `0.948` (`95% CIs` `not reported`).
  - Late-stage (`Stage III + IV`): AUC `0.888`, PR-AUC (`AP`) `0.921` (`95% CIs` `not reported`).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** **Qualitative curve only** — calibration curves are plotted for all 8 models (`Fig. 4C`, p. 9; p. 6 §3.4). Although §2.3 (p. 4) states *"Calibration curves, Brier score, and decision curve analysis (DCA) were applied"*, numeric **Brier score, ECE, calibration slope, and calibration intercept are `not reported`**.
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `Yes` — net benefit curves plotted across thresholds (`0.0–1.0` / `0.1–0.9`) for all 8 models (`Fig. 4B`, p. 9) and stage subgroups (`Fig. 4F`, p. 9).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `No` (no DeLong test or p-value comparing model AUCs; Mann–Whitney U and $\chi^2$/Fisher's exact tests applied only to baseline Tables 1–2, p. 4 §3.2).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:** Single-center retrospective cohort (`N=1,262` final) with ambiguous exclusion wording (`"131 without admission and discharge diagnosis of CHD"` vs §2.1 requiring no baseline CHD, pp. 2, 4–5) and an unusually elevated `57.2%` event rate (`722/1,262`), suggesting enriched/selected retrospective sampling rather than unselected consecutive incidence. No external validation cohort.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Severe temporal / diagnostic leakage:** §2.1 (p. 2) uses *"the last available ECG during hospitalization"* while defining endpoints as new-onset CHD *"during hospitalization or follow-up"*, and incorporates hospital diagnostic/cardiac ultrasound text (§2.2 p. 2), troponin (`cTnI`, $P<0.001$), and cardiovascular medications (`aspirin, clopidogrel, statin` `43.9%` vs `35.0%`, $P<0.001$, Table 1 p. 6)—creating direct risk that predictors capture the CHD diagnosis or post-diagnosis workup.
  - **Pre-split preprocessing leakage & data anomalies:** MICE imputation (`<50%` missingness) and scaling/BERT extraction are described prior to splitting (`§2.2` vs `§3.3`) with no train-only fit isolation reported. Table 2 (p. 7) exhibits extreme statistical anomalies: `BNP` is `76.23 (76.23–76.23)` (zero variance) in both groups; `PT` median `10.28` falls outside its own IQR `(10.70–11.49)`; and `TG`, `CKMB`, `cTnI`, and `SV` medians hit exact upper IQR boundaries.
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — HIGH RISK:** Lacks explicit ICD-9/10 codes, blinded adjudication, and censoring/follow-up horizon definitions (pp. 2, 4); admission/hospitalization clinical notes (`BERT`) risk circular target leakage.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Severe internal numerical inconsistencies:** In `Table 3` (p. 8), `LR` (`0.621/0.621/0.621`), `KNN` (`0.644/0.644/0.644`), and `RF` (`0.788/0.788/0.788`) report identical 3-decimal values across Accuracy, F1, and AUC on testing, while `CNN-LSTM` reports identical Training vs Testing AUC (`0.975 [0.962–0.988]`). Moreover, overall testing AUC (`0.975`) exceeds both exhaustive subgroup AUCs (`Early 0.924`, `Late 0.888`, Fig. 4D p. 9; Fig. 4 caption refers to *"early and late models"*).
  - Unimodal deep ablations (`ECG-only`, `Text-only`, `Lab-only`), numeric Brier/ECE calibration metrics, and DeLong significance tests are omitted; `Python (version 3.14)` and mismatched feature counts (`105` on p. 4 vs `n=78` in Fig. 2B p. 3) further erode reporting reliability.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Single-center retrospective design, modest sample size lacking multicenter external validation, and absence of thromboelastography (`TEG`) coagulation data (pp. 10–11).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Demonstrates multimodal fusion of `12-lead ECG`, `Clinical Text`, `EHR`, and `Lab` biomarkers (`P0002`), directly illustrating why rigorous methodological hygiene is required in our FYDP (`PG2–PG5`): omits `Lifestyle`, lacks Transformer cross-modal attention, fails to benchmark unimodal deep baselines, suffers **High PROBAST risk** from in-hospital ECG/medication temporal leakage and severe Table 2/Table 3 numerical anomalies, and presents SHAP summary plots without quantitative AOPC faithfulness or multi-seed stability checks.
