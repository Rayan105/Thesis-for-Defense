## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Retrospective secondary analysis of a public Mendeley Data repository (`Khan et al., 2021` [18], *"ECG images dataset of cardiac and COVID-19 patients"*, *Data Brief* 34:106762; p. 2, p. 35 ref [18]; note §IV.C p. 34 mis-cites `[16]`). Hospital origin, acquisition dates, patient age, sex, race/ethnicity, and comorbidities are **completely absent** in `P0028` (§IV.C p. 34 explicitly acknowledges *"lack of patient demographic metadata"*). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation in `P0028`:** `not reported` (unreported in manuscript).
- **Sample Size ($N$) & Critical Sample/Split Reconciliation:**
  - Reported dataset breakdown (§II.C, pp. 2, 4): **`929` total patients** across `4` classes yielding **`11,148` ECG images** (`12` images per patient):
    1. Myocardial Infarction (STEMI): `240` patients $\times 12 = 2,880$ images (p. 2)
    2. Abnormal Heartbeat (arrhythmias): `233` patients $\times 12 = 2,796$ images (p. 4)
    3. History of STEMI: `172` patients $\times 12 = 2,064$ images (p. 4)
    4. Normal Individuals: `284` patients $\times 12 = 3,408$ images (p. 4)
  - **CRITICAL AUDIT — Evaluated Test Size `N = 186` vs `11,148` Images:** Every confusion matrix (`Fig. 6` p. 7; `Fig. 7D` p. 8) and ST–T adversarial/XAI table (`Tables 23–24`, pp. 28–29) evaluates exactly **`N = 186` test traces** (`48 + 34 + 47 + 57 = 186`), which equals **exactly `20.0%` of the `929` patient/record counts** (`240×0.20=48`, `172×0.20=34.4`, `233×0.20=46.6`, `284×0.20=56.8`) rather than `10%` of `11,148` (`1,115`) or `10%` of `929` (`93`). - **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Static 4-class ECG diagnostic classification (`Normal`, `STEMI`, `History of STEMI`, `Abnormal Heartbeat`; pp. 2, 4). No longitudinal prediction horizon, clinical adjudication protocol, or follow-up endpoints (`not reported`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (Multi-Domain Decomposition of Single ECG Source Only; 1 of 4 FYDP Target Modalities):**
  - **Time domain:** 1D waveform vector `(N, 1000, 1)` (`Algorithm 2`, p. 14) fed to `1D-CNN` (`M1`).
  - **Time–Frequency domain:** Continuous Wavelet Transform (CWT) power scalograms $|S_i(a,b)|^2$ resized to `s × s` (`Algorithm 1`, p. 5) fed to `2D-CNN` (`M2`).
  - **Frequency domain:** Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) spectrum `(N, 128, 1)` (`Algorithm 2`, p. 14) fed to `1D-CNN Transformer` (`M3`).
  - **Excluded FYDP target modalities:** `Clinical/EHR` (`none`), `Laboratory/Biomarkers` (`none`), `Lifestyle` (`none`). *(Note: "Multimodal" in `P0028` denotes multi-domain transforms of digitized ECG images, not multi-source clinical fusion.)*
- **ECG Format & Preprocessing (`pp. 5–9`):** Raster ECG images are converted to 1D vectors by column-averaging (`x_i(t) = (1/W) Σ_w I_i(t,w)`, `Algorithm 1 Step 1` p. 5) — **not native digital voltage recordings**. Signals undergo wavelet soft-thresholding (`Eq. 3–5`, pp. 5–6) and zero-phase Butterworth `0.5–45 Hz` bandpass filtering (selected via ablation over `0.5–4.5 Hz` [F1 `0.30`] and `0.05–100 Hz` [F1 `0.89`] in `Tables 1–2` p. 9 / `Fig. 6` p. 7, and over ANC [`~0.79`] and Kalman [`~0.54–0.55`] adaptive filters in `Fig. 7` p. 8). Sampling rate `fs`, CWT mother wavelet, resized dimension `s`, and base model layers `M1–M3` (deferred to [57], p. 13) are `not reported`.
- **Class Imbalance & Missing-Modality Strategy:** `ADASYN` (`k=5` default, `Algorithm 1` p. 5) selected over `SMOTE` (`Fig. 2` p. 4) and evaluated via t-SNE (`Fig. 3` p. 5), side-by-side scalograms (`Fig. 4` p. 6), `max_pooling2d_3` intra-class cosine similarity (`Fig. 5` p. 6), and global `KL Divergence = 0.0008` (`p. 5`). **Missing-modality handling:** `not applicable / not reported` (all 3 domains derive deterministically from every ECG trace).
- **Model Families & Fusion Strategies (`26` Total Models Evaluated: `3` Unimodal + `23` Multimodal across `6` Families):**
  - Unimodal (`3`, `Table 4` p. 13): `M1 (1D-CNN)`, `M2 (2D-CNN)`, `M3 (Transformer)`.
  - Early fusion MLP (`3`, `Table 5` p. 13): feature concatenation + MLP (`Fig. 15` p. 13).
  - Post-hoc intermediate fusion (`4`, `Tables 6–7` pp. 14, 16): pretrained `M_1D`/`M_Trans` (`trainable=True`) $\rightarrow$ `Flatten -> Dense(256, ReLU) -> BatchNorm` $\rightarrow$ `Concatenate` $\rightarrow$ `Dense(512, ReLU) -> BatchNorm -> Dropout(0.25) -> Output` (`Algorithm 2` p. 14; best model **`M4: 1D-CNN + Transformer`**).
  - Cross-attention intermediate fusion (`4`, `Tables 8–9` p. 16): bidirectional MHA (`4 heads, d=128`, `M4-XA–M7-XA`).
  - Late fusion (`12` models across `3` schemes): Grid-search fixed convex weights (`Tables 11–12` pp. 17–18; best **`M7: 0.3·1D + 0.3·2D + 0.4·Transformer`**, `Fig. 21` p. 18), learned class-wise weighting + entropy gating (`Tables 13–14` p. 19), and learned per-sample attention head (`Tables 15–16` pp. 19–20).
  - *(Note: Paper counts `4 × 5 = 20` intermediate/late models plus `3` early MLP baselines = `23` multimodal architectures total, while p. 2 describes `"20 multimodal and 3 unimodal"`.)*

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:** Complete absence of patient demographics, recruitment site, age/sex distribution, and clinical history (`p. 34`). Unreconciled sample count mismatch between `11,148` images (`929 × 12`, pp. 2, 4) and `N_test = 186` traces (`20%` of `929`, `Fig. 6` p. 7; `Tables 23–24` pp. 28–29). Zero external validation cohort.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH RISK:** Predictors are raster ECG paper images converted to 1D vectors via pixel column-averaging (`Algorithm 1 Step 1` p. 5) without physical voltage (`mV`) or sampling rate (`fs` unreported) calibration; base models `M1–M3` are omitted from the manuscript (deferred to [57], p. 13).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — UNCLEAR RISK:** Relies exclusively on secondary Mendeley labels [18] without clinical/angiographic STEMI vs. History-of-STEMI adjudication criteria; expert ST–T masks ($W_{\text{SST}}$) used in XAI scoring lack an annotation protocol or inter-rater reliability (`κ`).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - **Split & leakage ambiguity:** `StratifiedShuffleSplit` (`p. 9`) lacks patient-level grouping (`GroupShuffleSplit`), split proportions conflict (`80/10/10` p. 9 vs `80/20` + `80/20` p. 13–14), `Algorithm 1` (`p. 5`) applies ADASYN to full dataset `D` prior to explicit splitting (with global `KL = 0.0008`), and `Algorithm 3` (`p. 16`) passes `X_test` inside the late-fusion grid-search pseudocode (contradicting §II.J.4 p. 17).
  - **Omitted clinical metrics & internal arithmetic anomalies:** Missing `ROC-AUC` for all intermediate/late/unimodal models (`Tables 4, 7, 9, 12, 14, 16`), missing numeric calibration and DCA, `Cohen's d = 0.84` outside its `95% CI [0.29, 0.56]` (`Table 22` p. 23) and conflicting with Abstract `d = 0.4`, and inconsistent `ΔF1` deltas.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Applied:** Gradient-based saliency maps on `M4` and `M7` (`Figs. 28–33`, pp. 24–29), specifically `Integrated Gradients + SmoothGrad` on the `1D-CNN` branch and `time * grad * input` token saliency projected via overlap-add on the `Transformer` branch (`Fig. 33`, p. 29). No `2D-CNN` or early-fusion saliency shown.
- **EAT Framework Quantitative Evaluation (`C1–C4`, pp. 26–33):**
  1. **`C1` Saliency Fidelity (Adebayo Sanity Checks [71]):** Weight randomization degrades saliency to `~1e-11` noise (`Fig. 30`, p. 26) and label shuffling flattens maps to zero (`Fig. 31`, p. 26) — demonstrated visually on single examples without layer-wise SSIM/Spearman rank curves.
  2. **`C2` Informational Grounding (`MI` & `Windowed NMI`):**
     - Raw ECG–saliency `MI` (`Algorithm 5` & `Fig. 32`, p. 27, `mutual_info_regression`): `M4` mean `MI = 0.5193` (Class 0 `0.5835`, Class 1 `0.5310`, Class 2 `0.4826`, Class 3 `0.4799`) vs `M7` mean `MI = 0.4654` (`0.4946`, `0.4239`, `0.4827`, `0.4606`; `ΔMI = +0.0539`). *(Note: §IV.B p. 34 cites Class 0 value `MI = 0.58 ± 0.03` as `M4` headline.)*
     - Per-sample median agreement with expert ST–T masks (`Table 25`, p. 29): **`Windowed NMI = 0.52` (`M4`) vs `0.47` (`M7`)** (`Δ = +0.05`; bootstrap 95% CI excludes `0` and permutation `p < 0.001` reported on pp. 30–31 without numeric CI/p values). However, hard overlap metrics are near zero: `Dice@10% = 0.023 vs 0.020`, `IoU@10% = 0.012 vs 0.011`, `Cohen's κ@10% ≈ 0.00` (`Table 25` p. 29). Numeric Adjusted MI (`AMI`) is `not reported` despite repeated claims (`pp. 1, 31, 34`).
  3. **`C3` Adversarial ST–T Perturbation Stability (`N = 186`, `Tables 23–24`, pp. 28–29):** Segment-constrained `FGSM-STT` and `PGD-STT` ($\epsilon \in \{0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05\}$) applied to the `1D` branch ST–T window (Transformer untouched):
     - `FGSM-STT`: flip-rate `1.08%–2.15%` (`Δp_true -0.004` to `-0.010`, `Table 23`; `0.011–0.022`, `Cosine 0.983–0.992`, `Dice@10% 0.922–0.957`, `IoU@10% 0.860–0.919`, `Table 24`).
     - `PGD-STT`: flip-rate `1.08%–7.53%` (`Table 23`) / `0.011–0.097` (`Table 24`), `Cosine 0.973–0.993`, `Dice@10% 0.873–0.954`, `IoU@10% 0.785–0.915`.
     - **CRITICAL XAI AUDIT GAP:** Despite text mentioning *"matched out-of-window controls"* (`p. 28`) and *"Deletion/attack tests confirm fidelity (performance declines when salient ST–T regions are removed or stressed)"* (`p. 31`), **zero deletion/insertion curves, zero AOPC scores, zero out-of-window numeric control comparisons, and zero multi-seed saliency correlations** are reported anywhere in `pp. 1–37`!

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Absence of patient demographic metadata in the Mendeley dataset (`p. 34`), non-ambulatory evaluation (`p. 34`), low-contrast History-of-STEMI edge cases (`p. 34`), computational focus without clinical deployment/trial (`pp. 28, 35`), and deferred clinician validation (`pp. 28, 35`).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & `papers.csv` Verification:**
  - **`papers.csv` Check:** Verified DOI, year (`2025`), venue (`IEEE Access`), access (`public_open`), cohort summary (`929` patients / `11,148` images), and `XAIEvaluated` (`Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`). Note two caveats against `papers.csv`: (a) `P0028` actually evaluates `23` multimodal variants (`4×5` intermediate/late + `3` early MLP) plus `3` unimodal (`26` total) rather than `20+3`, and (b) `StratifiedShuffleSplit` test results reflect `N = 186` traces (`20%` of `929`) without explaining `11,148` vs `929` or enforcing `GroupShuffleSplit`.
  - **Methodological Red Flags:** Digitized paper-image ECG conversion via column averaging (`Algorithm 1`), unresolved pre-split ADASYN/grid-search leakage in `Algorithms 1 & 3`, missing calibration/DCA/`ROC-AUC` for `M4`/`M7`, `Cohen's d = 0.84 ∉ [0.29, 0.56]`, and unmeasured deletion/insertion `AOPC`.
- **FYDP Positioning:** Directly informs `PG3` (Explainability Rigor) and `PG2` (Intermediate vs. Late Fusion benchmarking): we can adopt `P0028`'s `C1–C3` EAT concept (Adebayo sanity checks, ST–T `NMI` alignment, perturbation stability) while **surpassing `P0028`** by (1) fusing truly heterogeneous modalities (`12-lead ECG + EHR + Lab + Lifestyle`), (2) strictly enforcing patient-level splits & train-only preprocessing, (3) computing true **deletion/insertion AOPC + multi-seed SHAP/saliency stability correlation + clinician scoring**, and (4) reporting full discrimination (`ROC-AUC`, `PR-AUC`), `ECE`/`Brier` calibration, and `DCA`.
