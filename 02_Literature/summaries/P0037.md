# P0037 — Integrative Multimodal Hybrid Data Fusion for Mortality Prediction

## 1. Bibliographic Provenance
- **Citation Key:** `Abuhamad2026_P0037`
- **DOI:** `10.1038/s41598-026-36296-6` (`https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-36296-6`, p. 1)
- **PubMed ID (PMID):** `not reported` (in PDF)
- **Authors:** Husam Abuhamad, Suhaila Zainudin, Azuraliza Abu Bakar (Center for Artificial Intelligence Technology, Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia, Bangi, Selangor, Malaysia, p. 1)
- **Venue & Year:** *Scientific Reports* (2026) 16:5803 (Received: August 24, 2025; Accepted: January 12, 2026; Published online: January 20, 2026, pp. 1, 10)
- **Reading Status:** Full-Text Verified (`pp. 1–11`)

## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Single-center retrospective ICU cohort study using linked US critical-care databases from Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center (BIDMC, Boston, MA, USA): `MIMIC-IV` (`v2.2`), `MIMIC-IV-ECG` (`v1.0`), and `MIMIC-IV-Note` (`v2.2`, notes from `2008–2019`; pp. 1, 3–4, 9). **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **none / not reported** — Limitations explicitly state demographic/ethnicity subgroup fairness (`age`, `gender`, `ethnicity`) was **not** analyzed (p. 9), and `ethnicity` and `age` were omitted from the model's demographic inputs (`gender` only, p. 4, Fig. 1).
- **Sample Size ($N$):** **`21,469` Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) subjects** with complete tri-modal data (`tabular EHR + ECG + clinical notes`, p. 2, Fig. 1 p. 4, p. 5).
  - **Attrition Funnel (p. 4, Fig. 1, p. 9):** `53,150` subjects in `MIMIC-IV` (`221,260` in LabEvent box of Fig. 1) $\rightarrow$ `26,005` AKI subjects (`4,047` dead / `21,958` alive) $\rightarrow$ `21,962` AKI subjects linked to `MIMIC-IV-ECG` (`800,035` ECGs across `268,688` total subjects) $\rightarrow$ **`21,469`** AKI subjects also having `MIMIC-IV-Note` records (`380,000` records across `>380,000` unique ICU patients).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** **`3,349` dead (`15.6%`)** vs **`18,120` alive (`84.4%`)** in the `21,469`-subject multimodal cohort (p. 5). *(Pre-exclusion AKI pool of `26,005`: `4,047` dead [`15.56%`] vs `21,958` alive, p. 4.)*
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Binary **mortality prediction in ICU patients with AKI** (`alive` vs `dead`; non-CVD primary outcome, pp. 1, 4–5). **Critical Omissions:** Exact AKI diagnostic definition (`KDIGO`/`RIFLE`/`AKIN` vs ICD codes), exact mortality horizon (`in-ICU`, `in-hospital`, `28-day`, or all-time `MIMIC-IV` date-of-death), and prediction anchor time ($t_0 / \Delta t$) relative to ICU admission are `not reported` (pp. 3–5).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (3 of 4 target modalities; non-CVD outcome):**
  - **Clinical/EHR & Laboratory/Biomarkers (`MIMIC-IV`, `41,512` raw features $\rightarrow$ top `500` via Mutual Information):** Chart events (`2,226` items $\times$ `{min, max, mean, SD}` = `8,904` features), Lab events (`532` items $\times$ `{min, max, mean, SD}` = `2,128`), Demographics (`gender` only, `2` binary features in Fig. 1; `age` and `ethnicity` excluded), Procedures (`9,945` binary indicators from `47,868` unique codes), and Diagnosis history (`20,533` binary indicators from `53,134` unique codes) (p. 4, Fig. 1). Qualitative clinical groups among top MI features (`§Uni-model results`, p. 7) include hepatic (`AST`, `ALT`, `LDH`, `INR`), renal (`creatinine`, `BUN`, `phosphate`), respiratory (`RR`, `inspired oxygen`, `pO2`, `pH`), acid-base (`lactate`, `base excess`, `bicarbonate`, `total CO2`), neurological (`GCS`, `Richmond-RAS`, strength), Braden scale (`mobility`, `nutrition`), and cardiovascular (`HR`, invasive/non-invasive `BP`).
  - **12-lead ECG (`MIMIC-IV-ECG`):** Latest `10 s` 12-lead recording per AKI subject (`5,000` temporal points $\times$ `12` leads = `60,000` points, p. 4).
  - **Clinical Text (`MIMIC-IV-Note`):** Deidentified ICU notes including **discharge summaries, progress notes, radiology reports, and nursing documentation** (p. 4; Conclusion p. 9 inconsistently refers to *"radiology notes"*), stripped of headers/timestamps/physician IDs, lowercased, tokenized to `512` tokens (`['UKN']` for OOV), and encoded via pre-trained **`BERT`** (`BERT-large` in text p. 5, though producing `768-d` `[CLS]` embeddings in Fig. 1 p. 4, p. 5, Fig. 2 p. 6 — `768-d` corresponds to `BERT-base` rather than `1024-d` `BERT-large`).
  - **Excluded Target Modalities:** **Lifestyle:** `none / not reported` (apart from Braden nutrition/mobility subscores on p. 7).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `10 s`, `12` leads, `5,000` samples/lead (`500 Hz` implied by `5,000 / 10 s`, exact Hz not printed in text; shape `(5000, 12)`, pp. 4–5). Preprocessing: wavelet-transform denoising, baseline wander removal, irregular time-interval correction, and min-max normalization (p. 4).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** **Complete-case deletion only** — requires all 3 modalities (`26,005 → 21,962 → 21,469`, dropping `4,536` [`17.4%`] patients; pp. 4, 9). Authors explicitly confirm no missing-modality handling exists (`"Future work should develop robust strategies for missing modalities"`, p. 9). Tabular `Data Imputation` is depicted in Fig. 1 (p. 4) without specifying the imputation method.
- **Model Families & Baselines (pp. 5–7, Fig. 1, Table 2):**
  - **Unimodal Tabular (`MIMIC-IV`, 500 MI features):** `AdaBoost` (`100` stages), `Decision Tree`, `Gradient Boosting`, `KNN` (`k=5`), `Logistic Regression`, `Random Forest` (`100` trees), `SVM-RBF`.
  - **Unimodal ECG (`MIMIC-ECG`):** 1D CNN with `ResNet-SE` blocks (`Ours`, p. 5).
  - **Unimodal Text (`MIMIC-Note`):** Classification head atop pre-trained `BERT` (p. 5).
  - **Multimodal Fusion Strategies (Fig. 2, pp. 5–6):**
    - *Early Fusion (Fig. 2a):* Tabular `(1, 500)` $\rightarrow$ `Dense(5000, ReLU)` and Note `[CLS]` `(1, 768)` $\rightarrow$ `Dense(5000, ReLU)`, fused with ECG `(5000, 12)` via 4-head multi-head self-attention into `(5000, 14)` $\rightarrow$ `Conv1D-BN-ReLU(64)` + 4 `ResNet-SE` blocks (`64, 128, 256, 256`) $\rightarrow$ GAP $\rightarrow$ Dense output.
    - *Late Fusion (Fig. 2b, mislabeled "Fig. 2a" in text p. 6):* Tabular $\rightarrow$ `Dense(128, ReLU, Dropout 0.3)`; ECG $\rightarrow$ 2 `Conv1D-BN-ReLU` blocks (`64`, MaxPool `2`, `128`, GAP $\rightarrow$ `128-d`); Note $\rightarrow$ `Dense(128, ReLU, Dropout 0.3)`; concatenated/attention-fused (`384-d` in Fig. 2b) $\rightarrow$ `Dense(128, ReLU, Dropout 0.5)` $\rightarrow$ output.
    - *Hybrid Fusion (Fig. 2c):* Tabular (`128-d`) + ECG (`128-d`) concatenated (`256-d`) $\rightarrow$ `Dense(128, ReLU, Dropout 0.3)` $\rightarrow$ attention-fused (`256-d`) with Note (`128-d`) $\rightarrow$ `Dense(128, ReLU, Dropout 0.5)` $\rightarrow$ output.

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** 10-fold stratified CV (`8` train / `1` val / `1` test fold), repeated across `15` evaluation rounds/runs (p. 5, Table 3 caption p. 8). Fold-level MI (`500` features) performed inside training folds (p. 5). Inverse class weights applied to train and validation loss (BCE, Adam `lr=1e-3` exponential decay, `100` epochs, batch `128`, L2 `1e-4`, early stopping monitoring **validation accuracy** with patience `20`, pp. 5–7).
- **Unimodal Baselines (Table 2, p. 7 — Best & Mean ± SD):**
  - `MIMIC-IV` (Tabular): **Gradient Boosting** Acc `0.9292` (`0.9190 ± 0.0060`), AUC `0.8021` (`0.7772 ± 0.0156`), Prec `0.8922` (`0.8606 ± 0.0238`), Rec `0.6179` (`0.5715 ± 0.0303`), F1 `0.7302` (`0.6865 ± 0.0267`). **AdaBoost** AUC `0.8037` (`0.7721 ± 0.0160`), Acc `0.9195` (`0.9086 ± 0.0067`). **Random Forest** Acc `0.9227`, AUC `0.7739`. **Decision Tree** AUC `0.7719`. **KNN** AUC `0.5803`. **Logistic Regression** AUC `0.5152`, F1 `0.0714`. **SVM** Acc `0.1660`, AUC `0.5027`, Recall `0.9911` (near-trivial positive collapse).
  - `MIMIC-ECG` (`Ours`): Best Acc `0.9077` (`0.7460 ± 0.0927`), Best AUC `0.9483` (`0.7912 ± 0.0955`), Best Prec `0.9091` (`0.8501 ± 0.0569`), Best Rec `0.8928` (`0.4345 ± 0.1950`), Best F1 `0.8787` (`0.5576 ± 0.1513`).
  - `MIMIC-Note` (`BERT`): Best Acc `0.8240` (`0.7210 ± 0.0747`), Best AUC `0.8691` (`0.7867 ± 0.0747`), Best Prec `0.8750` (`0.7705 ± 0.0589`), Best Rec `0.4194` (`0.3532 ± 0.0564`), Best F1 `0.5479` (`0.4819 ± 0.0584`).
- **Multimodal Fusion Results (Table 3, p. 8 — 15 runs; Best & Mean ± SD):**
  - **Early Fusion:** Best Acc `0.9220` (`0.9081 ± 0.0142`), Best AUC `0.9460` (`0.9371 ± 0.0176`), Prec `0.7536` (`0.7730 ± 0.0990`), Rec `0.7602` (`0.6562 ± 0.1242`), F1 `0.7569` (`0.6927 ± 0.0425`). Confusion matrix (`p. 8`): `85` FP, `82` FN.
  - **Late Fusion:** Best Acc `0.9350` (`0.9183 ± 0.0090`), **Best AUC `0.9680`** (`0.9517 ± 0.0099`), Prec `0.7843` (`0.7333 ± 0.0361`), **Best Rec `0.8187`** (`0.7407 ± 0.0608`), **Best F1 `0.8011`** (`0.7358 ± 0.0401`). Confusion matrix (`p. 8`): `280` TP, `77` FP.
  - **Hybrid Fusion (claimed best overall):** **Best Acc `0.9360`** (`0.9206 ± 0.0094`), Best AUC `0.9619` (`0.9530 ± 0.0111`; text p. 8 prints `96.16%`, Table 1 p. 3 prints `0.961`), **Best Prec `0.8233`** (`0.7551 ± 0.0425`), Best Rec `0.7632` (`0.7268 ± 0.0572`), Best F1 `0.7921` (`0.7388 ± 0.0334`). Confusion matrix (`p. 8`): `261` TP, `1,742` TN, `81` FN, `56` FP (`n = 2,140`).
- **95% Confidence Intervals & PR-AUC:** `not reported` (reports `Best` and `Mean ± SD` only).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** **No (`not reported` — Internal Contradiction)** — Section *Metrics* (p. 6) defines Brier score and p. 8 claims *"highly calibrated predictions"*, yet **zero Brier scores, zero ECE values, and zero calibration plots appear anywhere in the paper** (pp. 1–11).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** One-way ANOVA + post-hoc Tukey's HSD across 15 runs comparing Early, Late, and Hybrid (p. 8): significant overall differences in AUC (`p = 0.00445`), Accuracy (`p = 0.0114`), Recall (`p = 0.0293`), and F1 (`p = 0.00443`), not Precision (`p = 0.287`). Tukey HSD shows Early differs from Hybrid/Late in AUC (`p = 0.0080`, `p = 0.0156`) and F1 (`p = 0.0084`, `p = 0.0145`), Early vs Hybrid in Acc (`p = 0.0133`), and Early vs Late in Recall (`p = 0.0347`); **Late vs Hybrid differences are NOT reported as significant**. No DeLong test or paired patient-level bootstrap test.

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:** Complete-case restriction removes `4,536 / 26,005` (`17.4%`) AKI patients lacking ECG or clinical notes (`p. 9`), introducing monitoring/severity selection bias. Single-center (`MIMIC-IV`, US ICU), no external validation, no AKI diagnostic criteria (`KDIGO`/`ICD`) stated, and no explicit guarantee against multiple ICU stays per subject (`2,140` confusion matrix total vs `21,469/10 ≈ 2,147`). Excludes `age` and `ethnicity`.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH RISK:** **Severe temporal / outcome leakage:**
  1. Tabular EHR computes `min, max, mean, SD` across **entire ICU stays** (`"during patients' ICU stays"`, p. 4) plus all binary diagnoses and procedures (`30,478` features).
  2. ECG selects the **most recent recording** (`p. 4`), which in terminal ICU deaths occurs immediately preceding/during terminal deterioration.
  3. `MIMIC-IV-Note` explicitly includes **discharge summaries** (`p. 4`), which routinely document hospital outcome/death!
  4. Fig. 1 (`p. 4`) places `Data Cleaning: Data Imputation, Outliers Removal` **before** `Random Stratified Data Split`, risking preprocessing leakage (though text p. 5 states MI selection was inside training folds).
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — HIGH RISK / UNCLEAR:** Mortality ascertainment timeframe (`in-ICU` vs `in-hospital` vs `MIMIC-IV` out-of-hospital `dod`) and AKI definition are `not reported` (pp. 3–5). Early stopping monitors **validation accuracy** (`p. 7`) under `15.6%` prevalence, misaligning with probabilistic risk calibration.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Defines Brier score on p. 6 but **omits all calibration results**; omits PR-AUC, 95% CIs, and DCA.
  - Emphasizes **single-run `"Best"` metrics** (`AUC 96.19%`, `Acc 93.60%`) and claims Hybrid outperforms Late Fusion (`pp. 2, 8–9`), whereas Late Fusion achieves higher Best AUC (`0.9680`), Best Recall (`0.8187`), Best F1 (`0.8011`), and statistically indistinguishable mean AUC (`0.9517` vs `0.9530`).
  - Internal reporting discrepancies (`BERT-large` p. 5 vs `768-d` Fig. 1–2; `"radiology notes"` p. 9 vs discharge/progress/radiology/nursing notes p. 4; `96.16%` p. 8 vs `96.19%` Table 3).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Single-center `MIMIC` cohort (`15.6%` mortality); complete-case selection bias (`26,005 → 21,469`); static snapshot ignoring dynamic ICU deterioration; lack of missing-modality strategies; high computational cost of ECG + BERT for edge/real-time deployment; absence of interpretability and demographic/fairness auditing across age, gender, ethnicity, and AKI severity (p. 9).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Useful architectural comparator for **tri-modal Early vs Late vs Hybrid fusion** (`ResNet-SE` ECG + `BERT` notes + tabular EHR) on `MIMIC-IV` (`PG1`, `PG2`), demonstrating how `MIMIC-IV` + `MIMIC-IV-ECG` + `MIMIC-IV-Note` can be aligned (`N = 21,469`). However, it is **non-CVD** (`AKI mortality`) and exemplifies critical methodological pitfalls our FYDP explicitly avoids (`PG2–PG5`): (1) **complete-case exclusion** with zero missing-modality tolerance (unlike `P0038`), (2) **severe end-of-stay/discharge-summary temporal leakage**, (3) **no Lifestyle modality** and zero South Asian data (`PG4`), (4) **omitted Brier calibration** (defined yet unreported), DCA, PR-AUC, and 95% CIs, and (5) **zero XAI or faithfulness auditing** (`PG3`, `PG5`).
