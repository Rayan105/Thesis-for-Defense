## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Population-based biobank discovery in the United Kingdom (`UK Biobank`, application `65275`, instance 2, `pp. 5, 16`), with external polygenic risk score (PRS) validation across three independent biobanks (`p. 7`): `Indiana Biobank` (USA), `EPIC-Norfolk` (UK), and `British Women's Heart and Health Study (BWHHS)` (UK).
  - **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** **None / Explicitly Excluded in GWAS & PRS.** Page 6 explicitly states *"GWAS was restricted to individuals with European ancestry to minimize confounding"*; Indiana Biobank was filtered to European samples clustering with 1000 Genomes Europeans (`p. 7`); EPIC-Norfolk and BWHHS are UK cohorts; and `p. 16` Limitation 6 explicitly notes *"all the genetic analyses were performed on individuals of European ancestry, and the specific impact of ancestry on M-REGLE and U-REGLE was not explored."* (Exact ethnic composition of the initial UKB VAE pretraining split is `not reported` in main PDF).
- **Sample Size ($N$):**
  - *UK Biobank (`p. 5`):* Raw extracted (April 17, 2023): `50,469` 12-lead ECG and `55,041` PPG samples. Post-QC (`0.1–99.9` percentile cutoffs): **`48,259`** samples for 12-lead ECG and **`41,282`** samples for Lead I ECG + PPG (`p. 5`).
  - *UKB European-ancestry GWAS subset (train + val, `pp. 11, 14`):* **`n = 38,697`** for 12-lead ECG (`p. 11`); **`n = 33,192`** for Lead I ECG + PPG (`p. 11`; `p. 14` also notes `37,144` total for Lead I ECG + PPG and **`27,213`** for Lead I ECG + PPG + spirogram).
  - *External PRS Cohorts (`p. 7`):* **Indiana Biobank** `N = 4,030` European-ancestry (`778` Afib cases, `3,252` controls); **EPIC-Norfolk** `N = 21,556` (`3,554` Afib cases, `18,002` controls); **BWHHS** exact sample size `not reported` in main PDF.
- **Event Count & Prevalence:**
  - *UK Biobank test set:* Exact Afib/CVD event counts and prevalence are in online `Table S17` (`not reported` in main PDF; `Fig. 2D p. 9` shows AUPRC baseline prevalence ~0.07–0.08 for Afib, ~0.46 for hypertension/CVD).
  - *Indiana Biobank:* `778 / 4,030` (`19.31%`) Afib (`p. 7`).
  - *EPIC-Norfolk:* `3,554 / 21,556` (`16.49%`) Afib (`p. 7`).
  - *BWHHS:* Exact counts `not reported` in main PDF (`pp. 7–8, 14`).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Dual focus: (1) unsupervised GWAS discovery on latent electrophysiological waveform factors (`pp. 3–6, 11–12`), and (2) downstream PRS and embedding-based cross-sectional/prevalent-and-incident disease prediction across `9` phenotypes (`Angina`, `Afib`, `Cardiovascular disease`, `Myocardial infarction`, `Hypertension`, `Coronary artery disease`, `Diabetes type 2`, `Stroke`, `All-cause mortality`, `p. 9 Fig. 2C–D`, `p. 10`), `22` ECG/PPG-derived traits (`31` total traits, `p. 13`), plus `ever smoking` (`p. 10`). Afib defined via `ICD-9 (427.3, 427.31, 427.32)` / `ICD-10 (I48.x)` in Indiana Biobank, Hospital Episode Statistics `ICD-10 I48.0/1/2/9/X` in EPIC-Norfolk, and GP/ECG review in BWHHS (`p. 7`); UKB definitions reside in unbundled `Table S17` (`p. 10`). Prediction horizon is `not reported`.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included:**
  - **12-lead ECG** (UKB field 20205, at-rest median complex waveforms, `pp. 4–5`) and **Single-lead ECG (Lead I)**.
  - **PPG** (UKB field 4205, median heartbeat waveform, `p. 5`).
  - **Spirogram** (UKB field 3066, secondary 3-modality experiment with Lead I ECG + PPG, `pp. 6, 14`).
  - **Genomics:** GWAS via `REGENIE` (`p. 6`) + ElasticNet PRS (`p. 7`).
  - **Clinical / Lifestyle / Biomarkers (Comparison/Covariates Only — NOT fused into VAE):** GWAS adjusted for `age, sex, BMI, smoking status, genotyping array, top 15 genetic PCs` (`p. 6`). Downstream comparison evaluated whether M-REGLE embeddings add to `289` "known features" (age, sex, obesity, lipid metabolic panel, activity, socioeconomic factors, `p. 10, Table S22`), and `ever smoking` appeared as a predicted phenotype (`p. 10`).
- **ECG & PPG Format & Sampling:**
  - 12-lead ECG median heartbeat complex: `600` points per lead (`12 × 600` stacked channels in M-REGLE), FIR bandpass filtered `0.05–40 Hz`, QC dropping waveforms outside `0.1–99.9` percentiles (`pp. 4–5`).
  - PPG median heartbeat: `100` points (unfiltered); concatenated sequentially with Lead I ECG into a `700`-point 1-channel vector (`12` latent dims in M-REGLE vs `8` ECG + `4` PPG in U-REGLE; multi-channel upsampled variant tested and inferior in `Table S5`, `p. 6`).
  - Amplitude scaling: divided by `90th percentile` of training-set absolute max values (`~[-1, 1]`, `p. 5`).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Complete-modality intersection only (`p. 16` explicitly notes missing-data imputation/handling was not explored).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - `M-REGLE` (Early Fusion): Single 1D convolutional $\beta$-VAE (1D Conv + MaxPool + FC bottleneck $\rightarrow$ mirrored FC + Upsample + ConvTranspose decoder, Adam optimizer, max `1,200` epochs, `100` random seeds tested for robustness, `p. 5`) generating `96` latent dims (12-lead ECG), `12` dims (Lead I ECG + PPG), or `15` dims (ECG + PPG + Spirogram), followed by PCA orthogonalization (`pp. 3–6`).
  - `U-REGLE` (Intermediate Fusion Baseline): Separate unimodal $\beta$-VAEs (`8` dims/lead for 12-lead; `8` ECG + `4` PPG; max `300` epochs) concatenated prior to PCA (`pp. 3–6, 15`).
  - Additional Baselines: Multimodal & Unimodal `PCA` and non-variational Convolutional Autoencoders (`CAE`, `pp. 4–6, 11`). Downstream phenotype/PRS models use 5-fold CV `ElasticNet` (`l1_ratio` tuned on training set, `p. 7`).
- **Fusion Strategy:** Unsupervised **early fusion** (stacking 12 ECG leads as channels or concatenating Lead I ECG + PPG [+ spirogram] at input before joint $\beta$-VAE encoding and PCA orthogonalization, `pp. 4–6, 15`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Patient-level **static hash on UK Biobank ENCODED IDs (`EID`)**: `70%` train / `20%` validation / `10%` test (`p. 5, p. 8`).
- **Waveform Reconstruction (`pp. 8, 10`):** M-REGLE reduced validation reconstruction MSE vs U-REGLE by **`72.5%`** on 12-lead ECG (`p. 8`) and **`20.23%`** on Lead I ECG + PPG (`p. 10`).
- **GWAS Discovery & Statistical Power (`pp. 3, 11–12, 14`):**
  - *12-lead ECG (`n = 38,697`, `p. 11`):* M-REGLE discovered `262` GWS hits (`+31.0%`, 62 more than U-REGLE) and `142` loci (`+19.3%`, 23 more than U-REGLE; `122 [85.92%]` replicated in GWAS Catalog); $E[\chi^2] = 112.48 \pm 2.05$ vs U-REGLE $92.2 \pm 1.83$ (`+22.0%`, `pp. 3, 11`).
  - *Lead I ECG + PPG (`n = 33,192`, `p. 11–12`):* M-REGLE discovered `103` GWS hits (`+15.7%`, 14 more than U-REGLE) and `61` loci (`+13.0%`, 7 more than U-REGLE; `59 [96.72%]` known); $E[\chi^2] = 51.66 \pm 0.98$ vs U-REGLE $44.37 \pm 0.88$ (`+16.4%`, `pp. 3, 12`).
  - *Lead I ECG + PPG + Spirogram (`n = 27,213`, `p. 14`):* $E[\chi^2] = 22.93 \pm 0.49$ vs U-REGLE $19.58 \pm 0.41$ (`p. 14`).
- **Downstream Afib & CVD Prediction (`pp. 9–10, 13–14`):**
  - *UKB 12-lead ECG Afib PRS (`10%` held-out test set, `p. 13`, `Fig. 6 p. 14`):* M-REGLE AUROC = **`0.59` (`95% CI: 0.582–0.599`)** vs U-REGLE **`0.57` (`95% CI: 0.562–0.578`)**; M-REGLE AUPRC = **`0.10` (`95% CI: 0.094–0.103`)** vs U-REGLE **`0.09` (`95% CI: 0.084–0.092`)** (`p. 13`).
  - *UKB Lead I ECG + PPG ElasticNet Phenotype Prediction (`pp. 9–10, Fig. 2C–D`):* M-REGLE matched or exceeded U-REGLE across all 9 phenotypes (`Fig. 2C–D`), significantly outperforming U-REGLE on AUROC for hypertension, CVD, and ever smoking, and on AUPRC for Afib, CVD, MI, hypertension, and all-cause mortality (`p. 10`; exact numeric values reside in unbundled `Tables S18–S21`). Adding M-REGLE embeddings to `289` known clinical/lifestyle features outperformed known features alone (`p. 10`, unbundled `Tables S23–S27`).
  - *External Biobank Afib PRS Validation (`pp. 13–14`):* M-REGLE significantly outperformed U-REGLE for Afib AUROC in Indiana Biobank (`Table S45`), and for Afib AUROC, AUPRC, and top 5% prevalence in EPIC-Norfolk (`Tables S46–S47`) and BWHHS (AUPRC & top 5% prevalence, `Table S48`); exact external AUROC/AUPRC values reside in unbundled Supplemental Tables (`not reported` in main PDF).
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** `No` (`not reported`).
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** **Yes** — paired nonparametric bootstrapping (`100` repetitions, `95% CI`, `pp. 8–10, 13–14`) and Bonferroni correction (`p < 0.05` across `31` phenotypes, `p. 13`).

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** Large population cohort (`UK Biobank`, `48,259` / `41,282`), clean participant `EID`-hash `70/20/10` splits (`p. 5`), and three external PRS validation cohorts (`Indiana Biobank`, `EPIC-Norfolk`, `BWHHS`, `p. 7`); however, GWAS/PRS analyses strictly excluded non-European ancestry (`pp. 6–7, 16`), limiting generalizability to South Asian/diverse cohorts, and only complete-modality subjects were retained (`p. 16`).
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — LOW RISK:** Waveform scaling (`90th` percentile abs max) was fit strictly on the training split (`p. 5`), ElasticNet CV was fit on training data (`p. 7`), and resting waveforms/SNPs contain no post-outcome leakage.
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — LOW / UNCLEAR RISK:** External cohorts provide explicit `ICD-9/10` / HES / GP-ECG definitions (`p. 7`), whereas UKB phenotype definitions reside in unbundled `Table S17` (`p. 10`).
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — UNCLEAR RISK:** While split hygiene, 100-seed robustness (`p. 5`), 95% bootstrap CIs (`p. 13`), and multi-cohort external validation (`pp. 13–14`) are rigorous for genomic discovery, clinical prediction assessment is limited by: (1) missing calibration (`Brier/ECE/calibration curves`) and DCA, (2) relegation of most numeric test AUROC/AUPRC tables (including external cohorts and 289 clinical-feature comparisons) to unbundled online supplements, and (3) combining `train + val` splits for GWAS SNP discovery (`pp. 6, 11`) prior to training ElasticNet PRS weights on `train` (though `10% test` and all 3 external cohorts remained completely held out).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **Unclear Risk**

## 6. Explainability (XAI) Rigor Audit
- **XAI Methods Actually Applied:**
  - **Latent-space traversal / coordinate perturbation (`pp. 10–11, Fig. 3`):** Identified latent dimensions (`4, 6, 10`) most separating Afib vs non-Afib, selected maximally separated healthy/unhealthy sample pairs, interpolated in `20` steps along a single coordinate, and visually inspected decoded waveforms (dimension 4 $\rightarrow$ QT prolongation + PPG notch disappearance; dimension 10 $\rightarrow$ ECG amplitude reduction; replicated from VAE zero-mean prior $\pm 2\text{ SD}$ in `Fig. S29`, `p. 11`).
  - **Phenotypic correlation scan (`p. 10`):** Residualized covariate correlations between embeddings and `10,602` UKB phenotypes (`1,989` Bonferroni-significant pairs).
  - **Zero** quantitative XAI faithfulness evaluation (`AOPC` deletion/insertion, comprehensiveness, sufficiency), **zero** attribution stability metrics (the 100-seed experiment tested VAE reconstruction loss and latent permutation/sign consistency, `pp. 5, 16`, not explanation faithfulness), and **zero** clinician review.

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations (`p. 16`):** Methodological proof-of-concept rather than maximal GWAS tuning; median heartbeat waveforms omit beat-to-beat/arrhythmic rhythm dynamics; complete-modality requirement drops partial-modality individuals; 12-lead ECG is multiview rather than truly distinct modalities; $\beta$ and scaling factor were not jointly grid-searched; **European-ancestry restriction** (`p. 16`); VAE latents are non-identifiable up to permutation/sign reversal.
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:**
  - Demonstrates strong quantitative gains of early/joint multimodal representation learning over intermediate/unimodal fusion on paired `ECG + PPG` (`-20.23%` reconstruction MSE, `+13.0%` GWAS loci, improved Afib PRS AUPRC), while highlighting key gaps our FYDP targets: unsupervised $\beta$-VAE yields modest supervised Afib PRS discrimination (`AUROC 0.59`, `p. 13`), omits raw 10-s beat-to-beat arrhythmia information, lacks EHR+Lab+Lifestyle cross-modal Transformer fusion (`PG1, PG4, PG5`), excludes South Asian populations, and lacks quantitative XAI faithfulness (`PG3`) and clinical calibration/DCA (`RQ4`).
  - **Discrepancy vs `papers.csv`:**
    1. **`XAIEvaluated` error (Major):** `papers.csv` tags `Yes (quantitative faithfulness)`, whereas P0023 performs only qualitative latent-dimension waveform decoding (`pp. 10–11, Fig. 3`) and Bonferroni correlation scans (`p. 10`) with zero perturbation faithfulness metrics.
    2. **Clinical/Lifestyle modality nuance:** `papers.csv` `Modalities` lists `12-lead ECG; Single-lead ECG; PPG/Wearable; Spirogram; Genomics`; `289` clinical/lifestyle features and `ever smoking` are tested only downstream via ElasticNet comparisons (`p. 10`, unbundled `Tables S22–S27`), not fused inside the M-REGLE multimodal VAE encoder.
