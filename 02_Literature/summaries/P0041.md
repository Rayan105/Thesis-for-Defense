## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (Review Scope across 85 Studies):** Predominantly **unimodal ECG** spanning single-lead (smartwatch, patch, e-skin, handheld), 2-, 3-, 6-, and 12-lead ECGs, plus `1` contactless mmWave radar study (`Zhao et al. 2024`, pp. 10, 13–22). Very sparse multimodal exceptions appear in Table 1: `Ramesh et al. 2021` (ECG + PPG, p. 15), `Lee et al. 2023` (heart sound/PCG + ECG, p. 17), `Chen et al. 2025` (ECG + BERT age/gender metadata, p. 34), `Khan 2020` (sensor + clinical datasets, p. 21), and `Wang et al. 2019` (demographic data + 6-lead ECG, p. 11). **Lifestyle modality:** `none / not reported` (except 2 mental-stress chest-sensor studies on `WESAD`/`SWELL`, p. 21). ## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI / AMSTAR-2 Risk of Bias Audit
- **Formal Risk-of-Bias Appraisal in `P0041`:** **`None` / `not reported`** — despite PROSPERO registration (`CRD42023478008`) and PRISMA adherence (p. 3), `P0041` did **not** apply `QUADAS-2`, `PROBAST`, or `PROBAST-AI` to any of its `85` included studies.
- **AMSTAR-2 & Meta-Analytic Methodological Critique of `P0041`:**
  - *Strengths:* Prospective PROSPERO protocol (`CRD42023478008`), 8-database librarian-guided search (`1952–2025`), dual independent screening/extraction, author email outreach recovering `5` extra confusion matrices, and appropriate bivariate HSROC (`metandi`) pooling accounting for sensitivity-specificity correlation (pp. 3–5, 23–24).
  - *Critical Weaknesses:* (1) **Complete absence of RoB assessment (`QUADAS-2` / `PROBAST`)** across the 85 studies (AMSTAR-2 critical flaw); (2) pooling only `27/85` (`31.8%`) confusion-matrix studies and collapsing heterogeneous multi-class beat-level problems (including intra-patient MIT-BIH splits) into binary `normal vs abnormal` without checking patient-level split leakage likely inflates pooled sensitivity (`97.12%`) and DOR (`878.09`); (3) English-only, peer-reviewed journal-only restriction without Deeks' funnel publication bias testing (p. 25); and (4) minor internal inconsistencies (`31.8%` vs `35.3%` external validation; `1` vs `4` encrypted studies; `84` rows in Table 1 vs `85` stated; `Phukan 2024` duplicated).
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **Not Applicable (Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis)** — Review itself omits formal RoB appraisal (`QUADAS-2`/`PROBAST`), while documenting that **`64.7%`** of included primary studies rely solely on internal single-dataset validation (`High Risk of Bias` prevalence in primary ECG-DL literature, p. 29).
