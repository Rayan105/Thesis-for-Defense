## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics

  - `MIMIC-III` (ICU EHR/physiological measurements, Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston, USA, PhysioNet, `pp. 4, 24`).
  - `CAIR-CVD-2025` (Mendeley Data `https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/d9scg7j8fp`, `pp. 5, 24`).
  - `UCI Heart Disease` (UCI Machine Learning Repository ID 45, US/European cohorts, `pp. 5, 24`).
  - **South Asian / Bangladeshi representation:** The PDF text itself does **not** explicitly name Bangladesh or South Asia anywhere (`not reported` in PDF prose, `pp. 1–25`; `p. 21 §6.10` mentions only *"cross-cultural interpretability"* while authors are based in Tamil Nadu, India, `p. 1`). **However, external provenance verification** of the cited Mendeley URL (`https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/d9scg7j8fp`, DOI `10.17632/d9scg7j8fp.1`) confirms that **`CAIR-CVD-2025` is a Bangladeshi cardiovascular risk dataset** (*"CAIR-CVD-2025: An Extensive Cardiovascular Disease Risk Assessment Dataset from Bangladesh"*, collected at Jamalpur Medical College Hospital, Bangladesh, `N = 1,529` full records). Critically, P0022 neither discloses this Bangladeshi origin nor evaluates Bangladesh/South Asian demographic subgroups, and inaccurately describes `CAIR-CVD-2025` as *"gathered using wearable devices and periodic health evaluations"* (`p. 5 §3.1`) when the source repository is cross-sectional tabular clinical/biochemical/lifestyle data.

  - `UCI Heart Disease`: **`300` records** (`p. 6, Fig. 1`).

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (3 of 4 target modalities via scalar/tabular features; NO raw ECG waveforms):**
  - **Clinical/EHR:** Age, gender/sex, systolic blood pressure (`SBP`), diastolic blood pressure (`DBP`), BMI, weight (`pp. 5, 15, 21`).
  - **Laboratory/Biomarkers:** Total cholesterol, glucose, and troponin (`Table 5 p. 15`, `Table 8 p. 21`, `Fig. 18 p. 23`).
  - **Lifestyle:** **Smoking only** (rank 10 in `Table 5, p. 15`; `Fig. 7, p. 15`); diet, physical activity, and alcohol are not modeled in reported feature tables.
  - **Wearable / Cardiac Scalar Proxies (NO raw ECG/PPG waveforms):** Heart Rate (`HR`), Heart Rate Variability (`HRV`), `SpO2`, and `ST-depression` (`Table 5 p. 15`, `Table 8 p. 21`, `Fig. 18 p. 23`).
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** **No raw ECG waveforms are modeled.** Only derived tabular/scalar features (`HR`, `HRV`, `ST-depression`) appear in feature tables and dashboard plots (`pp. 15, 21, 23`). Note also that `p. 5 §3.1` claims cross-dataset harmonization retained only *"clinically common cardiovascular features common across datasets"*, leaving unexplained how `SpO2`, `HRV`, and `Troponin` appear in `Table 5`/`Table 8`/`Fig. 18` when `UCI Heart Disease` and `CAIR-CVD-2025` lack continuous wearable streams and troponin.
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** K-Nearest Neighbor (`KNN`) imputation (`Eq. 6, p. 9`; `Algorithm 1, p. 11`) plus sliding-window alignment (`CDSS`) of length `T` (`Eq. 5, p. 9`).
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - *Feature Optimization:* Enhanced Quantum-Inspired Binary Grey Wolf Optimizer (`EQI-BGWO`, classical simulation with 2D Q-bit probability vectors, `30` agents, `50` iterations, objective $J = w_1\cdot\text{AUC}(q) + w_2\cdot(1-\text{Var}_{cv}(q)) - w_3\cdot|q|/d$, `Eqs. 9–11 pp. 9–10`, `§5.4 p. 12`).
  - *Proposed Model:* `QST-Trans` — 4-layer encoder-only spatio-temporal transformer (`4` heads, `128` hidden dim, `0.1` dropout) with temporal positional encoding, gated multi-head self-attention mask $G$ (`Eq. 12, p. 10`), and Kolmogorov–Arnold Network (`KAN`) feed-forward layers (`Eq. 13, p. 10`), `2.9M` trainable parameters (`Table 3, p. 13`). Trained in a simulated 5-client `FedAvg` federated setup (`100` rounds, `5` local epochs/round, Adam `lr = 0.001`, batch size `32`, `pp. 11–13 Table 2`).
  - *Benchmarked Baselines (`§5.3 p. 12`, `Table 4 p. 14`, `Fig. 13 p. 19`):* `FedCure-X` (`FedAvg`, `FedProx`), `Deep FM`, `XGBoost`, `LSTM-Attention` (plus `Bi-LSTM` and `Standard Transformer` in `Table 3 p. 13` / `Fig. 19 p. 24`; `CardioTabNet` and `ASCENDgpt` are named in `§5.3 p. 12` but **omitted** from `Table 4`!). **Missing Ablation:** Unimodal baselines (`Clinical-only` vs `Wearable-only`) are `not reported` in `Table 6 (p. 19)`.
- **Fusion Strategy:** Early/intermediate synchronization (`CDSS`) concatenating harmonized clinical and wearable time-window streams prior to `EQI-BGWO` selection and joint spatio-temporal Transformer (`QST-Trans`) encoding (`pp. 8–10, Fig. 5`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor

## 5. Validation Design & PROBAST-AI Risk of Bias Audit
- **Domain 1 (Participants) — HIGH RISK:** Unexplained aggressive subsampling (`500` MIMIC-III, `400` of `1,529` CAIR-CVD-2025, `300` UCI in `Fig. 1, p. 6`); mischaracterization of the Bangladeshi tabular `CAIR-CVD-2025` dataset as continuous wearable data (`p. 5`); absence of demographic tables or Bangladeshi subgroup performance.
- **Domain 2 (Predictors) — HIGH RISK (Pre-Split SMOTE & Normalization Leakage Contradiction):**
  - `§3.1 (p. 5)`, `Fig. 5 (p. 8)`, `§4.2 (p. 9)`, and `Algorithm 1 (p. 11)` explicitly specify running **CDSS windowing, KNN imputation, global Z-score normalization, SMOTE synthetic oversampling, and EQI-BGWO feature selection (optimizing AUC) across each dataset BEFORE model splitting/training** — a textbook **pre-split data leakage** pipeline that directly contradicts the post-hoc assertion in `§5.2 (p. 12)` (*"normalization, SMOTE balancing and feature selection were done on the training subset alone"*). Even `§5.2` fails to confirm train-only KNN imputation/CDSS windowing, and cross-cohort feature availability (`HRV`, `SpO2`, `Troponin`) is unreconciled with `CAIR-CVD-2025`/`UCI`.
- **Domain 3 (Outcome) — HIGH RISK:** Unresolved contradiction between binary classification (`Eq. 4 p. 10`, `Fig. 1 p. 6`) and 3-class confusion matrix (`Fig. 11 p. 18`), undefined prediction horizon (`10-year` in `Fig. 16` vs `24-hour` in `Fig. 17`), and unverified label harmonization across ICU records and outpatient screening cohorts.
- **Domain 4 (Analysis) — HIGH RISK:**
  - Severe internal reporting contradictions across tables, figures, and text (`AUC 0.99` vs `1.000`; `AP = 1.00`; `F1 0.98` vs `0.97`; `XGBoost` values across `Table 4`, `Fig. 13`, and `Fig. 19`; `70/15/15` split vs `5-fold CV`; `"2,530 iterations"` typo on `p. 16` vs `50` max iterations on `p. 9, 12`; `"over 4,060"` gap percentage on `p. 5`).
  - Complete omission of promised SDs, 95% CIs, model-comparison p-values, calibration (`p. 21`), DCA, and unimodal ablations; `§6.1 (p. 14)` and `§7 (p. 24)` further admit portions of testing occurred under preliminary/controlled conditions.
- **Overall PROBAST Risk of Bias:** **High Risk**
