## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Highlights that existing ECG Transformers over-rely on CNN stems, standard NLP sinusoidal positional encodings, and generic MHSA blocks unsuitable for repetitive multi-beat/multi-lead ECG physiology (§3.6, pp. 27–28); LLMs suffer from hallucination, overconfidence, lack of UQ, opaque external RAG knowledge bases, omission of patient metadata (age, sex, noise), regulatory barriers, and severe lack of population diversity across Middle Eastern, South Asian, and African cohorts (§3.6 p. 28, §4.2 p. 32, §4.4 p. 34, §5 p. 35).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:**
  - Directly supports our FYDP rationale (`PG1–PG3`, `PG5`) and South Asian generalizability framing: **explicitly documents (p. 34 §4.4)** that major benchmarks (PTB-XL, MIMIC-IV, Chapman-Shaoxing) lack South Asian populations.
  - Exposes methodological gaps relevant to our Transformer fusion design (`O2`): standard sinusoidal positional encodings and NLP MHSA are suboptimal for ECG without waveform-aware modifications (§3.6, §4.1.2–4.1.3), and nearly all surveyed architectures stop at unimodal ECG or 2-modality ECG+text/CXR/EMR without integrating Lab assays or Lifestyle predictors (`PG2`, `PG4`) or auditing XAI faithfulness (`PG3`, `RQ3`).
  - **Discrepancy vs `papers.csv`:** None on `DOI`, `Year`, `Authors`, or `XAIEvaluated` (`No`). Clarifies `papers.csv` `Modalities` (`12-lead ECG; Single-lead ECG; Clinical Text; Clinical/EHR`) by noting that surveyed papers also include wrist pulse/PPG (`ICMT-Net`), SpO2/PSG (`Fayyaz et al.`), and CXR (`MoRE`), with zero primary modeling performed.
