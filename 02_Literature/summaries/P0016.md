## 2. Study Design & Cohort Characteristics
- **Setting & Country:** Claims an *"institutional cardio-oncology cohort (`N = 1,200`)"* from *"a tertiary cardio-oncology center"* (pp. 5–6), supplemented by public datasets exclusively for modality pretraining (`UK Biobank Cardiac MRI`, `EchoNet-Dynamic`, `EchoNet-LVH`, `PTB-XL`) and auxiliary validation (`MIMIC-IV/MIMIC-Echo`; pp. 2–5, Tables 1–4). **Critical Provenance Anomaly:** Hospital name, country, recruitment dates, IRB protocol ID, and baseline cohort demographics are **completely omitted**, and p. 11 explicitly declares `Ethics Approval and Consent to Participate: Not applicable` and `Data Availability: The public databases have been used as mentioned already inside the manuscript`. South Asian / Bangladeshi representation: **none / not reported**.
- **Sample Size ($N$):** Claimed `1,200` longitudinal chemotherapy patients (anthracyclines, trastuzumab, or platinum regimens, pp. 1, 3, 6). Public pretraining cohorts: `UK Biobank CMR` (`>40,000` studies), `EchoNet-LVH` (`>13,000` images/videos), `PTB-XL` (`21,837` ECGs / `18,885` patients), `MIMIC-IV` (`~380,000` ICU admissions; Tables 1–4 p. 3).
- **Event Count & Prevalence:** **Not reported** for the `N=1,200` cohort (Introduction p. 1 cites general literature CIC incidence of `5–25%`; weighted BCE loss uses minority weight `w1 = 1.8` vs `w0 = 1.0`, p. 6, without stating actual case counts or cycle counts).
- **Endpoint Definition & Horizon:** Chemotherapy-induced cardiotoxicity (`CIC`) per ESC/ASE guidelines: `≥10%` absolute LVEF decline to `<53%` or new-onset symptomatic heart failure (p. 6). Longitudinal multi-cycle forecasting horizon (`ŷ^{(t+Δ)}`, p. 6): predicts cardiotoxicity `1–2` chemotherapy cycles (`"average of two chemotherapy cycles"`, pp. 1, 7, 10) prior to clinical LVEF decline detection.

## 3. Modality & Technical Architecture
- **Modalities Included (3 of 4 target modalities + 1 cardiac imaging modality; 4 total):**
  - `12-lead ECG` time series (`E_i`, `10 s` at `500 Hz`, input `(batch, 12, 5000)`, pp. 4, 7 Table 5).
  - `Echocardiography` ultrasound videos (`V_i`, resampled to `25 fps`, `224×224`, `(batch, 1, 224, 224)` in Fig. 2 & Table 5, pp. 4, 7).
  - `Clinical/EHR` variables (`C_i`: age, sex, BMI, comorbidities, baseline CV risk, cumulative dose, prior cardiac history, chemo regimen, pp. 3, 6).
  - `Laboratory/Biomarkers` (`B_i`: troponin I/T, BNP, NT-proBNP, CK-MB; `C_i` + `B_i` combined into `20` tabular features, pp. 3, 4, 6, 7).
  - **Excluded target modality:** `Lifestyle`: `none / not reported`.
- **ECG Format & Sampling:** `10 s` 12-lead ECG at `500 Hz`, bandpass filtered (`0.5–40 Hz`), per-sequence z-score normalized, aligned within `±7-day` windows around each chemotherapy cycle, with scaling/jitter/random lead dropout augmentations (pp. 4–5, 7 Table 5).
- **Missing-Modality Strategy:** Multi-part pipeline (pp. 4–5, 7): (1) drop biomarker variables with `>20%` missingness; (2) cycle-aware forward/backward propagation within patient trajectory; (3) binary missingness masks & modality masking tokens in the fusion transformer; (4) restricted KNN imputation on low-missingness non-critical clinical variables only; (5) attention masking for missing imaging rather than synthetic generation.
- **Model Families & Baselines:**
  - **ECG Encoder:** `Conv1D(12->32 [k7,s2] -> 64 [k5,s2] -> 128 [k5,s2])` $\rightarrow$ proj `256` + positional encoding + 4-layer, 8-head Temporal Transformer (`dim=256`, Fig. 2 p. 4, Table 5 p. 7).
  - **Echo Encoder:** Truncated `ResNet-18` (`256×14×14` $\rightarrow$ `196` patches $\rightarrow$ `256`-dim) + 4-layer, 8-head ViT (`dim=256`, Fig. 2 p. 4, Table 5 p. 7).
  - **Clinical/Biomarker Encoder:** 3-layer MLP (`20->64->128->256`, ReLU, dropout `0.3`, BatchNorm, pp. 4, 6–7).
  - **Cycle Aggregator:** Sequential temporal transformer encoder across chemotherapy cycles (`p. 6`).
  - **Baselines (Table 9 p. 9):** `Random Forest (Clinical+Biomarkers)`, `Multimodal LSTM Fusion`, `Perceiver-IO`, `Unified Multimodal Transformer`, `Cross Modal Attention Transformer` *(note mismatch with Table 6 p. 8 listing "Echo with EHR Fusion" and "Multimodal Deep Learning" instead of Random Forest and LSTM)*.
- **Fusion Strategy:** Cross-Attention Fusion Transformer (`CAFT`: 4 layers, 8 heads, `dim=256`) receiving 3 modality tokens `(3, 256)` $\rightarrow$ concat `768` $\rightarrow$ linear `256` $\rightarrow$ `[256->128->64->1]` Sigmoid classifier trained with Weighted BCE (`w1=1.8`) (Fig. 2 p. 4, Table 5 p. 7; Eq. 6 p. 6 writes `MHCA(Z_E, [Z_V; Z_C])`).

## 4. Quantitative Results & Evaluation Rigor
- **Data Split:** Claims patient-level, temporally consistent splitting without pretraining data overlap (p. 3), AND states both a `70% / 15% / 15%` stratified train/val/test split AND `5-fold stratified cross-validation` on the `N=1,200` cohort (pp. 5–6).
- **Primary Discrimination & Comparison (Table 9 p. 9; Abstract p. 1; Table 11 p. 9):**
  - **ChemoCardioNet:** ROC-AUC = `0.92 ± 0.01`, Accuracy = `0.86 ± 0.02`, Sensitivity = `0.88 ± 0.02`, Specificity = `0.85 ± 0.02`, F1-score = `0.86 ± 0.01` (Table 9 p. 9; outperforms baselines: Random Forest AUC `0.81±0.02`, LSTM `0.83±0.01`, Perceiver-IO `0.84±0.02`, Unified Transformer `0.85±0.03`, Cross-Modal Attention Transformer `0.88±0.01`).
  - **Early Lead Time:** Flags `82%` of CIC events $\ge 2$ cycles in advance vs `46%` by troponin criteria and `39%` by GLS decline (pp. 9–10).
  - **Ablations (Table 11 p. 9):** Full = `0.92`; w/o Cross-Attention Fusion (simple concat) = `0.87`; w/o ECG = `0.86`; w/o Biomarkers = `0.85`; w/o Echo = `0.84`. Individual unimodal baseline metrics are `not reported` numerically despite abstract/discussion claims (`8–12%` / `6–10%` gain, pp. 1, 10).
- **CRITICAL INTERNAL METRIC CONTRADICTIONS:**
  - **Table 10 ("Metrics with confidence interval", p. 9)** reports completely irreconcilable numbers for the same model: **AUC = `0.997` (`95% CI: 0.995–0.999`)**, Accuracy = `0.976` (`0.967–0.984`), Precision = `0.922` (`0.888–0.950`), Recall = `0.983` (`0.967–0.996`), F1 = `0.951` (`0.935–0.966`), Brier-score = `0.083` (`0.080–0.087`).
  - **Fig. 4 (p. 8)** displays a ROC curve labeled **`AUC = 1.00`** and Precision-Recall curve **`AP = 0.992`**!
- **Calibration Evaluated:** `Yes (contradictory/anomalous)` — Table 10 reports `Brier = 0.083 (0.080–0.087)` (p. 9) while text on p. 10 reports **`Brier score = 0.06`**. Fig. 4 calibration curve (p. 8) shows an unrealistic step jump (`0.00` observed probability for predicted `0.05–0.45`, jumping to `0.62` at `0.55` and `1.00` for `0.65–0.95`). `ECE`: `not reported`.
- **Decision Curve Analysis (DCA):** Claimed in Fig. 3 right panel (p. 4) as positive net benefit across `10–60%` (p. 8) or `10–40%` (p. 10), **yet Fig. 3 plots a malformed y-axis from `-80` to `0`** with all curves overlapping flat at `0` until `Treat all` plunges to `-80` at threshold `0.8–1.0`.
- **Statistical Significance Testing:** `No` (`not reported` — no DeLong test, paired bootstrap p-value, or hypothesis test reported).

## 7. Limitations & Relevance Critique
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Notes future work needs multi-center external validation, continuous wearable data, and federated learning (p. 10); acknowledges exact Shapley calculation is intractable for Transformers so Deep SHAP approximation was used (p. 8).
- **Independent Reviewer Critique & FYDP Positioning:** Conceptually aligned with our FYDP (`4` modalities including `12-lead ECG`, `Clinical EHR`, `Biomarkers`, plus `Echo`; Cross-Attention Transformer + Deep SHAP + Calibration + DCA; `PG1–PG5`). However, **severe reporting and data-integrity red flags** (unverified `N=1,200` cohort under `"Ethics: Not applicable"` on p. 11, mutually contradictory `AUC 0.92 / 0.997 / 1.00`, `Brier 0.083 / 0.06`, malformed negative-axis DCA plot, absent Lifestyle modality, and **zero quantitative XAI faithfulness/AOPC**) preclude treating its numerical benchmarks as reliable evidence and reinforce why our FYDP must enforce transparent cohort provenance, reproducible splits, DeLong testing, and genuine AOPC perturbation/stability audits (`PG3`, `PG5`).
